5. **Kākāpō were transferred from Rakiura Island to other locations because they were at risk from feral cats.**

> ✅**TRUE**
> 
> 原文中_Unfortunately, predation by feral cats on Rakiura Island led to a rapid decline in kākāpō numbers. As a result, during 1980‑97, the surviving population was evacuated to three island sanctuaries_翻译：Rakiura 岛上野猫捕食鸮鹦鹉，于是把幸存鸟转移到别的保护区。题目表述与原文完全一致。（**词汇evacuated/sanctuaries不熟导致**）
