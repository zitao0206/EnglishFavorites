###  表示“很常见｜很不常见”

- It's very common / widespread. 这十分普遍。
- It's very common. 这很常见。
- It's not common at all. 这一点都不常见。
- It's quite unusual. 这相当罕见。
- It's rare. 这很少见。

**Examples**
1. It’s very common for young people to work overtime here.
在这里年轻人加班是很常见的事。
2. This kind of bird is quite rare in this area.
这种鸟在该地区十分少见。
