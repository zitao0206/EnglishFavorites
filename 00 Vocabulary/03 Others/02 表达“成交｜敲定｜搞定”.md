### 表达“成交｜敲定｜搞定”

- close a deal：成交，达成交易（通用，口语&书面均可）
- close on sth：敲定；即将达成（侧重谈判最后阶段）
- lock the deal：锁定交易，敲定（强调结果不会再变动）
- seal the deal：一锤定音，敲定（决定性一步，语气更强）
- conclude the deal：达成交易（非常正式，多用于官方/合同）

**Examples**

1. We managed to close a deal after long‑hour talks.
2. The two sides are close on a new trade agreement.
3. His favourable offer helped us lock the deal.
4. A final face‑to‑face meeting sealed the deal.
5. Both companies concluded the deal last Friday.
