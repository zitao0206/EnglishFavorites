### woodland
[ˈwʊdlənd]

**Desp**
n. land covered with trees; a small forest.
林地

**Usages**
- woodland area 林地区域
- ancient woodland 古林地

**Examples**
1. Much of the original woodland has been cleared for farming. 大部分原始林地已被开垦为农田。
2. The woodland is home to many bird species. 这片林地是许多鸟类的家园。
3. They walked through the woodland trail. 他们走过林地小径。
