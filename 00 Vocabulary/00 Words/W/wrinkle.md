### wrinkle
[ˈrɪŋkl]

**Description**
n. a line on the skin or fabric.
皱纹

**Usages**
- facial wrinkles 面部皱纹
- wrinkle cream 抗皱霜

**Examples**
1. She has wrinkles around her eyes. 她眼角有皱纹。
2. This cream helps reduce wrinkles. 这款面霜有助于减少皱纹。
3. Iron the shirt to remove the wrinkles. 熨一下衬衫去除褶皱。
