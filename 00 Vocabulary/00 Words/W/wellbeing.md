### wellbeing
[ˌwelˈbiːɪŋ]

**Desp**
n. the state of being comfortable, healthy, or happy.
幸福；健康；福祉

**Usages**
- physical and mental wellbeing 身心健康
- promote wellbeing 促进福祉
- emotional wellbeing 情绪健康

**Examples**
1. Exercise is essential for our overall wellbeing. 锻炼对我们的整体健康至关重要。
2. The company cares about employees' mental wellbeing. 这家公司关心员工的心理健康。
3. A balanced diet contributes to long-term wellbeing. 均衡饮食有助于长期的幸福安康。
