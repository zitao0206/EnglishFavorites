### wary
[ˈweəri]

**Desp**
adj. cautious and alert to possible danger or difficulty.
谨慎的，小心的

**Extension**
同义词：cautious

**Usages**
- be wary of 对……谨慎
- wary of strangers 对陌生人警惕

**Examples**
1. Be wary of online scams. 要当心网络诈骗。
2. She was wary of his intentions. 她对他的意图很警惕。
3. Investors are growing wary of the market. 投资者对市场越来越谨慎。
