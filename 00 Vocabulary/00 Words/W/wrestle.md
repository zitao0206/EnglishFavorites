### wrestle
[ˈresl]

**Desp**
v/n. to fight by holding and trying to throw someone; to struggle with a difficult problem or decision.
摔跤，搏斗；努力解决，与……搏斗

**Usages**
- wrestle with a problem 苦苦思考一个问题
- wrestle with sb 与某人摔跤
- wrestle for power 争夺权力

**Examples**
1. The two boys wrestled on the grass. 两个男孩在草地上摔跤。
2. She has been wrestling with this decision for weeks. 几周来她一直在为这个决定而纠结。
3. He wrestled with his conscience. 他内心挣扎，备受良心折磨。
