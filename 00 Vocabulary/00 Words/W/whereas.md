### whereas
[weəˈræz]

**Desp**
conj. but in contrast; while on the other hand.
然而，反之，鉴于

**Usages**
- A..., whereas B... A……，而B……
- whereas in contrast 反之
- （正式对比连词）

**Examples**
1. Some people like coffee, whereas others prefer tea. 有些人喜欢咖啡，而另一些人更喜欢茶。
2. The north is mountainous, whereas the south is flat. 北部多山，而南部平坦。
3. She is outgoing, whereas her brother is quiet. 她外向，而她弟弟内向。
