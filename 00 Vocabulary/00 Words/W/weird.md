### weird
[wɪrd]

**Desp**
adj. Very strange and unusual, often in a way that is difficult to explain.
奇怪的，怪异的

**Usages**
- a weird feeling 奇怪的感觉
- weird weather 反常的天气
- something weird 奇怪的事

**Examples**
1. Something weird happened last night. 昨晚发生了奇怪的事。
2. He has a weird sense of humor. 他有一种古怪的幽默感。
3. I felt a weird sensation in my arm. 我胳膊上感到一种奇怪的感觉。
