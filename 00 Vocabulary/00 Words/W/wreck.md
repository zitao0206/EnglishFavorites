### wreck
[rɛk]

**Desp**
n. a vehicle or ship that has been badly damaged or destroyed; an accident in which a vehicle is destroyed.
v. to destroy or badly damage something.
n. 残骸，失事（车辆、船只）；v. 破坏，毁坏

**Extension**
同义词：destroy, ruin, demolish
反义词：restore, repair, preserve
名词形式：wreckage（残骸，碎片）

**Usages**
- a car wreck 车祸，撞车
- a shipwreck 海难，沉船
- wreck havoc 造成严重破坏
- train wreck 火车失事；一团糟的局面

**Examples**
1. He survived the car wreck with only minor injuries. 他在车祸中幸存，只受了轻伤。
2. The storm wrecked dozens of homes along the coast. 暴风雨摧毁了沿海数十栋房屋。
3. Her life became a complete wreck after the divorce. 离婚后她的生活变得一塌糊涂。
4. The wreckage of the plane was found in the mountains. 飞机残骸在山中被发现。
