### quantify
[ˈkwɒntɪfaɪ]

**Desp**
v. to measure or express the amount or size of something.
测定…的数量，量化

**Usages**
- quantify the impact 量化影响
- quantify the risk 量化风险

**Examples**
1. It is hard to quantify the damage caused by the disaster. 很难量化这场灾难造成的损失。
2. We need to quantify the costs before making a decision. 做决定前我们需要量化成本。
