### quote
[kwəʊt]

**Desp**
n/v. to repeat words from a source; a quotation.
n. 引语，引文 v. 引述，引证

**Usages**
- quote from … 引用自…
- a famous quote 名言

**Examples**
1. She quoted a famous line from the movie. 她引用了电影中的一句名言。
2. Can I quote you on that? 我可以引用你这句话吗？
3. The article begins with a quote from Einstein. 文章开头引用了爱因斯坦的话。
