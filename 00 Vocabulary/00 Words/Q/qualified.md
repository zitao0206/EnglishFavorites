### qualified
[ˈkwɒlɪfaɪd]

**Desp**
adj. Having the necessary skills, knowledge, or experience to do a job.
合格的，有资格的；胜任的

**Usages**
 - be qualified to do sth 有资格做某事
- a qualified teacher 合格的教师
- be qualified for 有资格担任……
- highly qualified 资历很高的

**Examples**
1. She is a highly qualified engineer. 她是一位资历很高的工程师。
2. He is qualified for the position. 他有资格担任这个职位。
3. We need qualified staff to run the program. 我们需要合格的人员来运营这个项目。
4. He is qualified for the position. 他胜任这个岗位。
5. Only certified engineers are qualified to sign the report. 只有持证工程师才有资格签署这份报告。
6. She is qualified to teach at university level. 她有资格在大学任教。
