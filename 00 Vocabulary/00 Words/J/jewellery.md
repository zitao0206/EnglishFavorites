### jewellery
[ˈdʒuːəlri]

**Desp**
n.【U】Objects worn for decoration, such as rings and necklaces.
珠宝首饰（不可数）

**Usages**
- a piece of jewellery 一件珠宝
- wear jewellery 佩戴首饰
- gold jewellery 金饰

**Examples**
1. She wore beautiful jewellery at the party. 她在派对上戴着漂亮的珠宝。
2. The thief stole all her jewellery. 小偷偷走了她所有的首饰。
