### jam
[dʒæm]

**Desp**
v./n. To push or pack something tightly into a space; to become stuck.
塞；挤；卡住；堵塞

**Usages**
- jam sth into a bag 把某物塞进包里
- traffic jam 堵车
- be jammed with people 挤满了人

**Examples**
1. He jammed his clothes into the suitcase. 他把衣服塞进手提箱。
2. The bus was jammed with passengers. 公交车里挤满了乘客。
3. We were stuck in a traffic jam for an hour. 我们堵车堵了一个小时。
