### jog
[dʒɒɡ]

**Desp**
n/v. to run slowly and steadily for exercise.
慢跑
- jogging n. 慢跑锻炼

**Usages**
- go for a jog 去慢跑
- jog every morning 每天早晨慢跑
- jog one's memory 唤起某人的记忆
- jogging shoes 跑步鞋、跑鞋、慢跑步鞋
- go jogging 去跑步、去慢跑、去小跑、慢跑

**Examples**
1. I go for a jog in the park every morning. 我每天早上去公园慢跑。
2. He jogs five kilometers a day to keep fit. 他每天慢跑五公里保持健康。
3. This photo jogged my memory of that summer. 这张照片唤起了我对那个夏天的记忆。
