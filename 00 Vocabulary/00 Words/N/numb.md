### numb
[nʌm]

**Desp**
adj. Deprived of the power of physical sensation; unable to feel. v. To make numb.
麻木的，失去知觉的；使麻木

**Usages**
- numb with cold 冻僵
- numb with shock 震惊到麻木
- go numb 变麻木

**Examples**
1. My fingers went numb from the freezing cold. 我的手指因严寒而冻麻木了。
2. She was numb with shock after hearing the news. 听到消息后她震惊到麻木了。
3. The dentist numbed the area before extracting the tooth. 牙医在拔牙前对该区域进行了麻醉。
