### nonsense
[ˈnɒnsns]

**Desp**
n. words or ideas that are silly or have no meaning; foolish behavior.
胡说，废话；愚蠢的行为

**Usages**
- talk nonsense 胡说八道
- absolute nonsense 一派胡言
- put up with no nonsense 不容忍胡闹

**Examples**
1. Don't talk nonsense! 别胡说八道！
2. The report is complete nonsense. 这份报告完全是一派胡言。
3. The teacher won't put up with any nonsense in class. 老师不允许课堂上出现任何胡闹行为。
