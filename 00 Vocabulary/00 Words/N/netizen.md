### netizen
[ˈnetɪzn]

**Desp**
n. a person who uses the internet, especially habitually or actively.
网民，网络公民（net + citizen）

**Usages**
- Chinese netizens 中国网民
- netizen backlash 网民抵制
- active netizen 活跃网民

**Examples**
1. Many netizens expressed outrage at the video. 许多网民对该视频表示愤怒。
2. Chinese netizens are among the most active in the world. 中国网民是世界上最活跃的网民之一。
3. The post went viral and was shared by millions of netizens. 该帖子走红，被数百万网民转发。
