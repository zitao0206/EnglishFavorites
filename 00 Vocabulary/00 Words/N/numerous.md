### numerous
[ˈnuːmərəs]

**Desp**
adj. many; existing in large numbers.
adj. 众多的，许多的

**Usages**
- numerous occasions 无数场合
- numerous studies 大量研究
- too numerous to list 多得数不清

**Examples**
1. Numerous studies support this theory. 大量研究支持这一理论。
2. She has travelled to numerous countries. 她去过许多国家。
3. The restaurant has received numerous complaints. 这家餐厅收到了大量投诉。
