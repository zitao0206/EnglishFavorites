### newsfeed


**Desp**
n. a stream of regularly updated posts or news shown on a website or app.
新闻信息流

**Usages**
- scroll through the newsfeed 刷信息流
- social media newsfeed 社交媒体信息流

**Examples**
1. My newsfeed is full of food and travel photos. 我的信息流满是美食和旅行照片。
2. I usually scroll through my newsfeed before going to bed. 我睡前常常刷信息流。
