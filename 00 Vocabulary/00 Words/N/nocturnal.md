### nocturnal
[nɒkˈtɜːnl]

**Desp**
adj. active at night.
夜间活动的

**Extension**
反义词：diurnal /daɪˈɜːnl/ 白天活动的

**Usages**
- nocturnal bird 夜行鸟类
- nocturnal animal 夜行动物

**Examples**
1. The kakapo is a nocturnal, flightless parrot that is critically endangered and one of New Zealand's unique treasures. 鸮鹦鹉是一种夜行、不会飞的鹦鹉，极度濒危，是新西兰独特的珍宝之一。
2. Owls are nocturnal creatures. 猫头鹰是夜行动物。
3. Nocturnal animals have excellent night vision. 夜行动物有出色的夜视能力。
