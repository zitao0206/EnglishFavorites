### nuance
[ˈnjuːɑːns]

**Desp**
n. a very small difference in meaning, sound, color or feeling.
细微差别

**Usages**
- cultural nuance 文化细微差别
- subtle nuance 微妙的差别

**Examples**
1. Translation often loses the nuances of the original. 翻译常常失去原文的细微差别。
2. Understanding cultural nuances is key to doing business abroad. 理解文化细微差别是在海外经商的关键。
