### overtake
[ˌəʊvəˈteɪk]

**Desp**
v. to go past a vehicle or person that is going more slowly; to become greater than someone or something. 超过，赶上；突然降临

**Usages**
- overtake a car 超车
- overtake in sales 在销量上超越
- be overtaken by events 被形势所迫

**Examples**
1. Never overtake on a bend. 切勿在弯道超车。
2. China has overtaken the US in electric vehicle sales. 中国在电动汽车销量上已超过美国。
3. Fatigue overtook him after the long journey. 长途旅行之后他疲惫不堪。
