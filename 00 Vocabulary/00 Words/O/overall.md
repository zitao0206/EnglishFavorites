### overall
[ˌəʊvərˈɔːl]

**Desp**
adj. including everything; total or general.
总体的，全面的，全部的

**Usages**
- overall situation 总体形势
- overall performance 整体表现

**Examples**
1. The overall quality of the product is quite good. 产品的整体质量相当不错。
2. Overall, the project was a success. 总的来说，这个项目是成功的。
