### obsession
[əbˈseʃn]

**Desp**
n. an idea or feeling that occupies the mind completely.
痴迷，执念

**Usages**
- an obsession with sth 对某事的痴迷
- become an obsession 成为一种执念

**Examples**
1. Discover your newest obsession at Costco. 在好市多找到你的新心头好。
2. With him, gambling is an obsession. 对于他来说赌博是一种执念。
3. Her obsession with cleanliness makes her tired. 她对清洁的执念让她很累。
