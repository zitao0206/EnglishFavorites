### outperform
[ˌaʊtpəˈfɔːm]

**Desp**
v. to do better than someone or something, especially in performance or results.
胜过，优于（业绩、性能、数据）

**Usages**
- outperform the market 跑赢市场

**Examples**
1. The fund outperformed the market for three years in a row. 该基金连续三年跑赢市场。
2. Our new chip outperforms all competitors. 我们的新芯片性能优于所有竞争对手。
