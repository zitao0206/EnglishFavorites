### oath
[oʊθ]

**Desp**
n. a solemn promise, often invoking a divine witness; a formal declaration.
誓言，誓约；宣誓

**Usages**
- take an oath 宣誓
- swear an oath 发誓
- break an oath 违背誓言
- under oath 在宣誓之下（必须说真话）

**Examples**
1. The witness took an oath to tell the whole truth. 证人宣誓说出全部真相。
2. He broke his oath of loyalty to the king. 他违背了对国王的忠诚誓言。
3. Soldiers swear an oath to protect their country. 士兵宣誓保卫祖国。
