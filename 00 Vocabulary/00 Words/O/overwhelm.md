### overwhelm
[ˌoʊvərˈwelm]

**Description**
v. to affect strongly; to defeat completely.
v. 使不知所措；（洪水般）淹没；彻底击败

**Extension**
形容词：overwhelming 压倒性的

**Usages**
- be overwhelmed by 被…淹没/压垮
- overwhelming majority 压倒性多数
- an overwhelming victory 大胜

**Examples**
1. She was overwhelmed by the amount of work. 工作量让她不堪重负。
2. The team was overwhelmed by stronger opponents. 球队被更强的对手彻底击溃。
3. An overwhelming majority voted for reform. 压倒性多数投票支持改革。
