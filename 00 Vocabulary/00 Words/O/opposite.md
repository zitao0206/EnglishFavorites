### opposite
[ˈɒpəzɪt]

**Desp**
adj. completely different; facing something. prep. across from.
完全相反的，不同的；对面的

**Usages**
- the opposite direction 相反方向
- exactly the opposite 恰恰相反

**Examples**
1. They went in the opposite direction. 他们朝相反的方向走了。
2. His opinion is the opposite of mine. 他的观点与我的完全相反。
3. The bank is opposite the supermarket. 银行在超市对面。
