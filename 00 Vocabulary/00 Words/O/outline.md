### outline
[ˈaʊtlaɪn]

**Desp**
n/v. A general description or plan of something without details; to describe the main points.
提纲，概要；轮廓；（v.）概述

**Usages**
- an outline of the plan 计划的概要
- write an outline 写提纲
- outline the main points 概述要点

**Examples**
1. She gave me a brief outline of the project. 她向我简要介绍了项目的概要。
2. Write an outline before you start the essay. 写文章之前先列个提纲。
3. The CEO outlined the company's future plans. 首席执行官概述了公司的未来规划。
