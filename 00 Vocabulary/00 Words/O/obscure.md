### obscure
[əbˈskjʊr]

**Desp**
adj. not well known; hard to understand; v. to hide.
adj. 鲜为人知的；费解的 v. 使模糊，掩盖

**Usages**
- an obscure poet 无名诗人
- obscure rules 晦涩难懂的规则
- obscure the view 挡住视线

**Examples**
1. He quoted an obscure philosopher. 他引用了一位鲜为人知的哲学家。
2. The regulations are written in obscure language. 这些法规用语晦涩难懂。
3. Clouds obscured the mountain peak. 云雾遮住了山峰。
