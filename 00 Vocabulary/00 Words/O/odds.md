### odds
[ɒdz]

**Desp**
n. The probability that something will happen.
可能性，几率

**Usages**
- What are the odds? 几率有多大？/太不可思议了。
- against all odds 克服重重困难
- the odds of winning 获胜的几率

**Examples**
1. What are the odds of running into you twice in one day! 一天碰见你两次，太不可思议了！
2. Against all odds, the small team won the championship. 克服重重困难，这支小球队赢得了冠军。
3. The odds of winning the lottery are extremely small. 中彩票的几率极小。
