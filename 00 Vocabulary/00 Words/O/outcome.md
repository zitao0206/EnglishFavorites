### outcome
[ˈaʊtkʌm]

**Desp**
n. the final result of a process, meeting, or activity.
结果，结局；成果

**Usages**
- the outcome of the election 选举结果
- a positive outcome 积极的结果
- regardless of the outcome 无论结果如何

**Examples**
1. We are waiting for the outcome of the negotiations. 我们正在等待谈判结果。
2. The outcome of the match surprised everyone. 比赛的结果让所有人惊讶。
3. Whatever the outcome, we must accept it. 无论结果如何，我们都必须接受。
