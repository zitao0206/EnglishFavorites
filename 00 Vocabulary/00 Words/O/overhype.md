### overhype
[ˌəʊvəˈhaɪp]

**Description**
v. to promote or praise something excessively.
过度炒作，大肆吹捧

- hype v.（通过传媒）大肆宣传；使（人）兴奋，使（人）活跃

**Usages**
- overhype a product 过度炒作产品
- Don't overhype this game. 不要炒作这款游戏。
- overhyped expectations 被吹捧过高的期望

**Examples**
1. Don't overhype this game. 不要炒作这款游戏。
2. The media overhyped the movie before its release. 影片上映前媒体对其大肆吹捧。
3. The product failed because it was overhyped. 这款产品失败的原因是被过度炒作。
