### overrule
[ˌəʊvəˈruːl]

**Description**
v. (formal, law) to change a decision or reject an objection, especially with higher authority.
否决，驳回

**Usages**
- objection overruled 反对无效
- overrule a decision 推翻一项决定
- overrule an objection 驳回反对意见

**Examples**
1. 'Objection overruled,' said the judge. 法官说："反对无效。"
2. The higher court overruled the previous verdict. 高等法院驳回了先前的判决。
3. The manager's decision was overruled by the board. 经理的决定被董事会否决了。
