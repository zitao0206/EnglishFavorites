### obligate
[ˈɑːblɪɡeɪt]

**Description**
v. to make someone legally or morally bound to do something.
v. （在法律或道义上）强制，使有义务

**Extension**
形容词：obligatory 义务的，强制的
近义词：oblige 迫使

**Usages**
- be obligated to do 有义务做
- feel obligated 感到有义务

**Examples**
1. You are obligated to repay the loan. 你有义务偿还贷款。
2. I felt obligated to attend the ceremony. 我觉得有义务出席仪式。
3. The law obligates companies to protect user data. 法律强制公司保护用户数据。
