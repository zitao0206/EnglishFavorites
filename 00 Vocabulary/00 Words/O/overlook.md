### overlook
[ˌəʊvəˈlʊk]

**Desp**
v. To fail to notice or consider something; to ignore a mistake or fault.
忽视，忽略；俯瞰；宽恕

**Usages**
- overlook a mistake 忽视错误
- overlook the sea 俯瞰大海
- cannot be overlooked 不容忽视

**Examples**
1. It's easy to overlook small details when you're in a hurry. 匆忙时很容易忽略小细节。
2. The hotel room overlooks the ocean. 酒店房间俯瞰大海。
3. We can't overlook the importance of this issue. 我们不能忽视这个问题的重要性。
