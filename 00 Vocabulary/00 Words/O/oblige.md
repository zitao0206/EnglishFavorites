### oblige
[əˈblaɪdʒ]

**Desp**
v. to force someone to do something; to do a favor.
v. 迫使；（被动）不得不；帮忙

**Extension**
形容词：obliged 感激的，不得不的

**Usages**
- be obliged to do 不得不做
- oblige sb with a favor 帮某人一个忙
- much obliged 多谢

**Examples**
1. The law obliges employers to provide insurance. 法律强制雇主提供保险。
2. I felt obliged to attend the wedding. 我觉得不得不去参加婚礼。
3. Could you oblige me with a pen? 能麻烦借我一支笔吗？
