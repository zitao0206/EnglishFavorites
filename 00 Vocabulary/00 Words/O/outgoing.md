### outgoing
[ˈaʊtɡoʊɪŋ]

**Desp**
adj. friendly, sociable, and energetic in company.
外向的，好交际的；即将离任的

**Extension**
反义词：introverted, reserved
名词：outgoingness

**Usages**
- an outgoing personality 外向的性格
- outgoing president 即将离任的总统
- outgoing and friendly 外向友好

**Examples**
1. She is outgoing and makes friends easily. 她性格外向，很容易交到朋友。
2. The outgoing CEO handed over the company in good shape. 即将离任的CEO把公司交接得井井有条。
3. Outgoing people tend to do well in sales. 外向的人往往在销售领域表现出色。
