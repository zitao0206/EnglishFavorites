### deploy
[dɪˈplɔɪ]

**Desp**
v. to move forces or resources into position for action; to use effectively.
v. 部署，调度；有效运用

**Extension**
名词：deployment 部署

**Usages**
- deploy troops 部署军队
- deploy resources 调配资源
- deploy new technology 应用新技术

**Examples**
1. The army deployed troops along the border. 军队在边境沿线部署了部队。
2. The company deployed new software across all offices. 公司在所有办公室部署了新软件。
3. We must deploy our resources wisely. 我们必须明智地调配资源。
