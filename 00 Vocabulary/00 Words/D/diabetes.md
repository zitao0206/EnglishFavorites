### diabetes
[ˌdaɪəˈbiːtiːz]

**Desp**
n. A disease in which the body cannot control the level of sugar in the blood.
糖尿病

**Usages**
- type-1/type-2 diabetes 一型/二型糖尿病
- diabetic adj. 糖尿病的
- diabetic diet 控糖饮食

**Examples**
1. He was diagnosed with type-2 diabetes last year. 他去年被诊断出二型糖尿病。
2. A diabetic diet helps control blood sugar. 控糖饮食有助于控制血糖。
3. Diabetes runs in her family. 她家有糖尿病家族史。
