### discourage
[dɪsˈkɜːrɪdʒ]

**Description**
v. To make someone lose confidence or willingness to do something; to try to prevent something.
使气馁，使灰心；劝阻，阻止

**Usages**
- discourage sb from doing sth 劝阻某人做某事
- feel discouraged 感到气馁
- discourage smoking 劝阻吸烟

**Examples**
1. Don't let failure discourage you. 别让失败使你气馁。
2. Parents should discourage children from watching too much TV. 父母应劝阻孩子看太多电视。
3. The rain discouraged us from going out. 下雨使我们不想出门。
