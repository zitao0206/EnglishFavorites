### digestion
[daɪˈdʒestʃən]

**Desp**
n. the process of breaking down food in the body.
消化

**Usages**
- aid digestion 助消化
- good for digestion 对消化有益
- digestive system 消化系统

**Examples**
1. Grapes are good for our digestion. 葡萄有助消化。
2. Eating fruits helps aid our digestion. 吃水果促进消化。
3. A walk after meals improves digestion. 饭后散步有助于消化。
