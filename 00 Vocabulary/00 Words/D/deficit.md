### deficit
[ˈdefɪsɪt]

**Desp**
n. the amount by which something is less than what is needed; a loss or shortage.
亏损，赤字，不足额

【反】surplus  [ˈsɜːpləs] n. 盈余；顺差；剩余量

**Usages**
- trade deficit 贸易逆差
- budget deficit 财政赤字

**Examples**
1. The country has a large trade deficit. 该国存在巨大的贸易逆差。
2. The company reported a deficit of two million dollars. 该公司报告了200万美元的亏损。
3. The government finally achieved a budget surplus. 政府最终实现了预算盈余。
