### double-cross
[ˌdʌbl ˈkrɒs]

**Description**
v. to betray or cheat someone who trusts you.
出卖，欺骗（背叛信任自己的人）

**Usages**
- double-crossing adj. 两面三刀的
- double-crossing bastard 两面三刀的小人
- double-cross one's partner 出卖同伙

**Examples**
1. He double-crossed his partners and took all the money. 他出卖了同伙，独吞了钱。
2. Nobody trusts that double-crossing bastard. 没人相信那个两面三刀的小人。
3. They planned to double-cross the buyers at the deal. 他们打算在交易中欺骗买家。
