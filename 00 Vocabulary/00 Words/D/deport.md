### deport
[dɪˈpɔːt]

**Description**
v. to force a person to leave a country.
驱逐出境，遣返

**Usages**
- deport sb to + 国家 把某人驱逐至某国
- be deported 被驱逐

**Examples**
1. The government deported the illegal immigrants to their home country. 政府将非法移民遣返回他们的祖国。
2. He was deported after overstaying his visa. 他因签证逾期而被驱逐出境。
