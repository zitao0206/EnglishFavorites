### deduct
[dɪˈdʌkt]

**Desp**
v. to take away an amount or part from a total.
扣除，减去

**Usages**
- deduct from salary 从工资中扣除
- deduct tax 扣税
- deduct costs 扣除成本

**Examples**
1. The cost will be deducted from your salary. 这笔费用将从你的工资中扣除。
2. Tax is deducted automatically from my paycheck. 税款会自动从我的工资中扣除。
3. You can deduct business expenses from your taxable income. 你可以从应税收入中扣除商务开支。
