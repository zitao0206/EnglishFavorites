### diminish
[dɪˈmɪnɪʃ]

**Desp**
v. to become or make smaller; to reduce.
v. 减少，减小，贬低

**Usages**
- diminish in value 贬值
- diminish the risk 降低风险
- diminishing returns 收益递减

**Examples**
1. The pain will diminish over time. 疼痛会随时间减轻。
2. Nothing can diminish her achievements. 什么也无法贬低她的成就。
3. The drug's effect gradually diminishes. 药效逐渐减弱。
