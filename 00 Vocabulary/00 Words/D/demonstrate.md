### demonstrate
[ˈdemənstreɪt]

**Description**
v. to show clearly that something is true or exists; to show how something works; to take part in a public protest.
证明，论证；演示；游行示威

**Usages**
- demonstrate the use of 演示……的使用
- demonstrate that 证明……
- demonstrate against 游行反对

**Examples**
1. The teacher demonstrated how to use the equipment. 老师演示了如何使用设备。
2. The results demonstrate that the method works. 结果表明这种方法有效。
3. Thousands demonstrated against the new law. 数千人游行反对新法律。
