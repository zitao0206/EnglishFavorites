### dedicate
[ˈdedɪkeɪt]

**Desp**
v. to give all your energy, time, or effort to something; to say that a book or song was written for someone.
献身于，致力于；题献

**Usages**
- dedicate oneself to 献身于
- dedicate time to 把时间用于
- dedicate a book to sb 把书献给某人

**Examples**
1. She dedicated her life to helping the poor. 她把一生奉献给帮助穷人。
2. He dedicated the book to his parents. 他把这本书献给他的父母。
3. We should dedicate more resources to education. 我们应该把更多资源投入到教育上。
