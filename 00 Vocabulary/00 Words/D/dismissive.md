### dismissive
[dɪsˈmɪsɪv]

**Desp**
adj. showing that you think someone or something is not important.
轻蔑的，鄙视的，不屑一顾的

**Usages**
- be dismissive of 对…不屑一顾

**Examples**
1. He was dismissive of my suggestions. 他对我的建议不屑一顾。
2. Her dismissive tone made me feel small. 她轻蔑的语气让我觉得自己很渺小。
