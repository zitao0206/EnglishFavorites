### distract


**Desp**
v. To prevent someone from concentrating on something.
使分心，使注意力转移

**Usages**
- distract sb from sth 使某人从…分心
- be easily distracted 容易分心
- distracting noise 干扰的噪音

**Examples**
1. Don't distract me while I'm working. 我工作时别打扰我。
2. The phone constantly distracts him from his studies. 手机不断让他从学习中分心。
