### deposit
[dɪˈpɑːzɪt]

**Desp**
n. a sum paid into an account or as security; a layer of material; v. to put down or pay in.
n. 存款；押金；沉积物 v. 存放，沉积

**Usages**
- make a deposit 存钱；付押金
- a security deposit 押金
- mineral deposits 矿藏

**Examples**
1. I made a deposit at the bank this morning. 我今天早上在银行存了钱。
2. The landlord kept the deposit for damages. 房东以损坏为由扣押了押金。
3. The river deposits rich soil along its banks. 河流沿岸沉积了肥沃的土壤。
