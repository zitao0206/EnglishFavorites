### decorate
[ˈdekəreɪt]

**Desp**
v. to make something look more attractive by adding ornaments; to paint or wallpaper a room.
装饰，布置；装修

**Usages**
- decorate a room 装修房间
- decorate for Christmas 为圣诞节布置
- decorate with flowers 用花装饰

**Examples**
1. They decorated the living room for the party. 他们为派对布置了客厅。
2. The Christmas tree was decorated with lights and ornaments. 圣诞树上装饰着灯和挂饰。
3. She loves decorating — she changes the curtains every season. 她热爱装饰——每季都换窗帘。
