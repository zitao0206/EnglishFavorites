### dolphin
[ˈdɒlfɪn]

**Desp**
n. A highly intelligent sea mammal.
海豚

**Usages**
- a pod of dolphins 一群海豚
- dolphin show 海豚表演

**Examples**
1. The dolphin is an intelligent animal. 海豚是高智商动物。
2. We saw dolphins jumping near the boat. 我们看到海豚在船边跳跃。
3. Dolphins communicate with clicks and whistles. 海豚用咔嗒声和哨声交流。
