### dizzy
[ˈdɪzi]

**Desp**
adj. having or involving a sensation of spinning around and losing one's balance.
头晕的，眩晕的

**Usages**
- feel dizzy 感到头晕
- dizzy spells 头晕发作
- make sb dizzy 让人头晕

**Examples**
1. She felt dizzy after standing up too quickly. 她站起来太快感到头晕。
2. The roller coaster made him dizzy. 过山车让他头晕目眩。
3. He suffers from dizzy spells during hot weather. 他在炎热天气时会头晕发作。
