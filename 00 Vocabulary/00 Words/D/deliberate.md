### deliberate
[dɪˈlɪbərət]

**Desp**
adj./v. done on purpose, intentional; done slowly and carefully; to think or discuss carefully.
故意的，蓄意的；从容谨慎的；（v.）仔细考虑

**Usages**
- a deliberate lie 蓄意的谎言
- deliberate on 仔细思考
- a deliberate decision 深思熟虑的决定

**Examples**
1. It was a deliberate attempt to mislead us. 这是蓄意误导我们。
2. He spoke in a slow, deliberate manner. 他说话缓慢而从容。
3. The jury deliberated for two days. 陪审团审议了两天。
