### degenerate
[dɪˈdʒenəreɪt]

**Desp**
v. to become worse in quality; adj. morally corrupt.
v. 恶化，退化，蜕变 adj. 堕落的

**Extension**
名词：degeneration 退化，恶化

**Usages**
- degenerate into chaos 演变成混乱
- degenerate into violence 演变为暴力

**Examples**
1. The debate degenerated into a shouting match. 辩论演变成了一场骂战。
2. His vision degenerated with age. 他的视力随年龄退化。
3. The protest degenerated into riots. 抗议演变成了骚乱。
