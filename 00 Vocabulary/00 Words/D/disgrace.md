### disgrace
[dɪsˈɡreɪs]

**Desp**
n. Loss of reputation or respect; shame. v. To bring shame upon.
耻辱，丢脸；使蒙羞

**Usages**
- a public disgrace 公开的耻辱
- in disgrace 处于丢脸状态
- fall into disgrace 蒙受耻辱
- disgrace oneself 使自己丢脸

**Examples**
1. The scandal brought disgrace on the entire family. 丑闻使整个家族蒙羞。
2. He resigned in disgrace after the fraud was exposed. 欺诈被曝光后他黯然辞职。
3. It would be a disgrace to accept such terms. 接受这样的条件是一种耻辱。
