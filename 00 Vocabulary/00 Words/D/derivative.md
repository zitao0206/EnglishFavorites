### derivative
[dɪˈrɪvətɪv]

**Desp**
n./adj. a value measuring the rate of change of a function; derived from something else.
导数；衍生的

**Usages**
- first-derivative 一阶导数
- second-derivative 二阶导数
- financial-derivative 金融衍生品

**Examples**
1. He trades derivatives. 他做衍生品交易。
2. The first derivative tells you the slope of the curve. 一阶导数给出曲线的斜率。
3. Financial derivatives can be highly risky. 金融衍生品风险可能很高。
