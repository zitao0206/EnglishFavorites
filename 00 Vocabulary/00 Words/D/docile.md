### docile
[ˈdəʊsaɪl]

**Description**
adj. quiet and easy to control ≈ gentle / tame.
驯服的，温顺的

**Usages**
- a docile animal 温顺的动物
- a docile horse 驯良的马

**Examples**
1. The horse is docile and easy to ride. 这匹马很温顺，容易骑。
2. Despite its size, the dog is quite docile. 尽管体型大，这只狗却很温顺。
