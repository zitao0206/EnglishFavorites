### diameter
[daɪˈæmɪtə(r)]

**Desp**
n. the distance across a circle through its centre.
直径

**Usages**
- in diameter 直径为
- trunk diameter 树干直径

**Examples**
1. The tree has a trunk diameter of 1.5 metres. 这棵树的树干直径为1.5米。
2. The pipe is 10 cm in diameter. 管道直径10厘米。
3. Measure the diameter of the circle. 测量这个圆的直径。
