### despair
[dɪˈspeə(r)]

**Description**
n/v. complete loss of hope.
n. 绝望 v. 绝望，丧失信心

**Usages**
- in despair 绝望地
- drive sb to despair 使某人绝望

**Examples**
1. He gave up in despair. 他绝望地放弃了。
2. Her despair was written all over her face. 她满脸绝望。
3. Don't lose hope; despair is the enemy of progress. 不要失去希望；绝望是进步的敌人。
4. Don't let yourself despair, this will only make matters worse.
