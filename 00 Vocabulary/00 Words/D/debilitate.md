### debilitate
[dɪˈbɪlɪteɪt]

**Desp**
v. to make someone or something physically or mentally weak.
使虚弱，使衰弱

**Usages**
- a debilitating illness 使人衰弱的疾病
- debilitate the economy 削弱经济
- be debilitated by 因……而衰弱

**Examples**
1. The disease debilitated him for years. 这种疾病使他虚弱了好几年。
2. Years of war debilitated the country's economy. 连年战争削弱了该国的经济。
3. He was debilitated by the long illness. 长期患病使他身体衰弱。
