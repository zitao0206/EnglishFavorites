### denounce
[dɪˈnaʊns]

**Desp**
v. to publicly criticize something as wrong.
v. 谴责，抨击；告发

**Extension**
名词：denunciation 谴责

**Usages**
- denounce the attack 谴责袭击
- denounce sb as a traitor 痛斥某人为叛徒

**Examples**
1. The government denounced the coup in strong terms. 政府以最强措辞谴责政变。
2. She publicly denounced the policy as unfair. 她公开抨击该政策不公。
3. He was denounced by his former colleagues. 他被以前的同事告发了。
