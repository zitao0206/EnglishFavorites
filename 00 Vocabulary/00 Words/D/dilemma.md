### dilemma
[dɪˈlemə]

**Desp**
n. a situation in which you have to make a difficult choice between two things.
困境，进退两难

**Usages**
- be in a dilemma 处于进退两难的境地
- a moral dilemma 道德困境
- face a dilemma 面临两难选择

**Examples**
1. She faced the dilemma of choosing between career and family. 她面临在事业和家庭之间选择的困境。
2. The doctor was caught in a moral dilemma. 医生陷入了道德困境。
3. We're in a dilemma: stay or leave? 我们进退两难：留下还是离开？
