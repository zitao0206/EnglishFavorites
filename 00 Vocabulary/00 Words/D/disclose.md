### disclose
[dɪsˈkləʊz]

**Desp**
v. to make something known publicly; to reveal.
揭露，公开，透露

**Usages**
- disclose information 披露信息
- refuse to disclose 拒绝透露

**Examples**
1. The company refused to disclose its financial details. 该公司拒绝披露其财务细节。
2. He disclosed the plan to the public. 他向公众公开了这个计划。
