### discipline
[ˈdɪsəplɪn]

**Desp**
n. training to obey rules; controlled behavior; a subject; v. to punish.
n. 纪律；自律；学科 v. 惩戒，训练

**Extension**
形容词：disciplined 有纪律的

**Usages**
- self-discipline 自律
- maintain discipline 维持纪律
- academic disciplines 学科

**Examples**
1. Learning an instrument requires discipline. 学习乐器需要自律。
2. The teacher kept discipline in the classroom. 老师维持了课堂纪律。
3. He was disciplined for breaking the rules. 他因违规而受到处分。
