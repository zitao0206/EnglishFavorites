### distinctive
[dɪˈstɪŋktɪv]

**Desp**
adj. having a special quality that makes something easy to recognize.
adj. 独特的，与众不同的

**Extension**
名词：distinction 区别，卓越

**Usages**
- a distinctive style 独特的风格
- a distinctive feature 显著特征

**Examples**
1. The building has a very distinctive design. 这座建筑设计非常独特。
2. Her distinctive voice made her famous. 她独特的嗓音使她成名。
3. The sauce has a distinctive smoky flavor. 这种酱汁有一种独特的烟熏味。
