### disruptive
[dɪsˈrʌptɪv]

**Desp**
adj. causing trouble and preventing something from continuing as normal; innovative in a way that changes an industry.
破坏性的，扰乱性的；（商业）颠覆性的

**Usages**
- disruptive behavior 扰乱行为
- disruptive technology 颠覆性技术
- disruptive innovation 颠覆式创新

**Examples**
1. The student was sent out for disruptive behavior. 那名学生因扰乱课堂秩序被请出教室。
2. Smartphones were a disruptive technology. 智能手机是一项颠覆性技术。
3. Disruptive innovation can reshape entire industries. 颠覆式创新可以重塑整个行业。
