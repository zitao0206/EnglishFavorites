### domestic
[dəˈmestɪk]

**Desp**
adj. relating to a country's internal affairs; relating to home or family; (of animals) kept by people.
国内的；家庭的，家用的；驯养的

**Usages**
- domestic market 国内市场
- domestic flight 国内航班
- domestic chores 家务活
- domestic consumption 国内消费

**Examples**
1. The company focuses on the domestic market. 公司专注于国内市场。
2. Domestic flights are cheaper than international ones. 国内航班比国际航班便宜。
3. They share domestic responsibilities equally. 他们平等分担家务。
