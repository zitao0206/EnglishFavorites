### dramatic
[drəˈmætɪk]

**Description**
adj. very sudden, noticeable, or exciting; related to drama.
adj. 戏剧性的，引人注目的；巨大的

**Extension**
副词：dramatically 戏剧性地，急剧地

**Usages**
- a dramatic increase 急剧增长
- a dramatic change 剧变
- dramatic effect 戏剧性效果

**Examples**
1. The policy had a dramatic impact on sales. 该政策对销售额产生了巨大影响。
2. Prices fell dramatically last year. 去年价格急剧下跌。
3. She made a dramatic entrance in a red gown. 她穿着红色礼服戏剧性地登场。
