### defuse
[ˌdiːˈfjuːz]

**Desp**
v. to make a dangerous or tense situation calmer; to remove the fuse from a bomb.
缓和，平息；拆除（炸弹的）引信

**Usages**
- defuse the tension 缓和紧张气氛
- defuse a crisis 化解危机
- defuse a bomb 拆除炸弹

**Examples**
1. Her sense of humor helped defuse the tension. 她的幽默感缓和了紧张气氛。
2. The negotiators worked to defuse the crisis. 谈判人员努力化解这场危机。
3. The bomb squad successfully defused the bomb. 拆弹小组成功拆除了炸弹。
