### deplete
[dɪˈpliːt]

**Description**
v. to use up or reduce the amount of something.
耗尽，消耗

**Extension**
名词形式：depletion

**Usages**
- deplete natural food sources 耗尽天然食物来源
- depleted resources 枯竭的资源

**Examples**
1. Introduced animals depleted the kakapo's natural food sources. 引入的动物耗尽了鸮鹦鹉的天然食物来源。
2. Overfishing has depleted the fish stocks. 过度捕捞已耗尽了鱼类资源。
3. The prolonged drought depleted the water supply. 长期干旱消耗了供水。
