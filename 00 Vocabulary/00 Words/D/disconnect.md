### disconnect
[ˌdɪskəˈnekt]

**Desp**
v. to break the connection between things; to unplug.
断开连接；拔掉

**Usages**
- disconnect the charger 拔掉充电器
- disconnect from the Internet 断开网络
- disconnect the power 切断电源

**Examples**
1. Please disconnect the charger. 请拔掉充电器。
2. The call was disconnected suddenly. 电话突然断线了。
3. Remember to disconnect the power before repairs. 维修前记得切断电源。
