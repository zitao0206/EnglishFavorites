### deem
[diːm]

**Desp**
v. to consider or judge something in a particular way.
v. 认为，视为

**Usages**
- be deemed to be 被视为
- deem it necessary 认为有必要
- be deemed unfit 被认为不合适

**Examples**
1. The project was deemed a success. 这个项目被认为是成功的。
2. He was deemed unfit to stand trial. 他被认为不适合出庭受审。
3. The rules deem this behavior unacceptable. 按规定这种行为是不可接受的。
