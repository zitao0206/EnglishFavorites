### discrete
[dɪˈskriːt]

**Desp**
adj. Separate and distinct; individually separate.
离散的，分立的

**Usages**
- discrete-data 离散数据
- discrete-math 离散数学
- discrete-signals 离散信号

**Examples**
1. The chart represents discrete data points, not a continuous line. 这张图展示的是离散数据点，而非连续曲线。
2. Discrete math is a core course for CS students. 离散数学是计算机专业的核心课程。
3. The system is built from discrete elements. 该系统由分立单元构成。
