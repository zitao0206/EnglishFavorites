### disrupt

**Description**
v. to interrupt or disturb something.
扰乱，打乱，中断

**Usages**
- disrupt the market 颠覆市场
- disrupt traffic 扰乱交通
- disrupt a class 扰乱课堂

**Examples**
1. The storm disrupted our travel plans. 暴风雨打乱了我们的出行计划。
2. New technology is disrupting traditional industries. 新技术正在颠覆传统行业。
3. Please don't disrupt the class. 请勿扰乱课堂秩序。
