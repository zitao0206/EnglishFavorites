### deform
[dɪˈfɔːrm]

**Desp**
v. to spoil the shape or appearance of something; to become distorted.
使变形，使成畸形

**Extension**
反义词：restore
名词：deformation
形容词：deformed
（用户原文 deforme，标准拼法为 deform）

**Usages**
- deform under pressure 在压力下变形
- a deformed bone 畸形的骨骼
- permanently deform 永久变形

**Examples**
1. The plastic frame deformed in the hot sun. 塑料框架在烈日下变形了。
2. Heat can permanently deform the metal. 高温会使金属永久变形。
3. The disease deformed his spine over time. 这种病久而久之使他的脊柱变形了。
