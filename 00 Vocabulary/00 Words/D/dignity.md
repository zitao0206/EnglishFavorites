### dignity
[ˈdɪɡnəti]

**Desp**
n. the state of being worthy of respect.
尊严，高贵；人格

**Usages**
- with dignity 有尊严地
- human dignity 人的尊严

**Examples**
1. She handled the criticism with dignity. 她尊严地面对批评。
2. Everyone deserves to live with dignity. 每个人都应有尊严地生活。
3. He kept his dignity even in defeat. 即使失败他也保持尊严。
