### deprive
[dɪˈpraɪv]

**Desp**
v. To take something away from someone; to prevent from having.
剥夺，使丧失

**Usages**
- deprive sb of sth 剥夺某人的某物
- be deprived of 被剥夺了

**Examples**
1. The prisoners were deprived of sleep. 囚犯被剥夺了睡眠。
2. Worrying deprives you of happiness. 忧虑会让你失去快乐。
3. He was deprived of his license. 他被吊销了执照。
