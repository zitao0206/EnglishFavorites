### deprive
[dɪˈpraɪv]

**Desp**
v. To take something away from someone; to prevent from having.
剥夺，使丧失

- deprivation [ˌdeprɪˈveɪʃn] n. 匮乏，剥夺，丧失

**Usages**
- deprive sb of sth 剥夺某人的某物
- be deprived of 被剥夺了
- sleep deprivation 睡眠剥夺
- sensory deprivation 感官剥夺
- economic deprivation 经济匮乏

**Examples**
1. The prisoners were deprived of sleep. 囚犯被剥夺了睡眠。
2. Worrying deprives you of happiness. 忧虑会让你失去快乐。
3. He was deprived of his license. 他被吊销了执照。
4. Sleep deprivation is a major threat to health issues. 睡眠剥夺严重危害健康。
5. The children suffered from deprivation during the war. 战争期间孩子们饱受匮乏之苦。
