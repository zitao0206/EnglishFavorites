### decency
[ˈdiːsnsi]

**Desp**
n. proper and respectful behaviour.
正派，体面，庄重，礼貌；教养

**Usages**
- a sense of decency 羞耻心，教养
- common decency 基本的礼节

**Examples**
1. He had the decency to apologize. 他有教养，知道道歉。
2. Common decency requires us to help. 基本的礼节要求我们提供帮助。
3. She behaved with decency and honesty. 她为人正派诚实。
