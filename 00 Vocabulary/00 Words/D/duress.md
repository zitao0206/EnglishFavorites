### duress
[dʊˈres]

**Desp**
n. threats or coercion used to force someone to do something.
胁迫，强迫（法律术语）

**Extension**
同义词：coercion, pressure
常用搭配：under duress（被迫）

**Usages**
- under duress 在胁迫下
- sign under duress 被迫签署
- signs of duress 受胁迫的迹象

**Examples**
1. He signed the contract under duress. 他是在胁迫下签署合同的。
2. A confession made under duress is not admissible in court. 胁迫下的供词在法庭上不可采信。
3. She claimed she cooperated only under duress. 她声称自己只是被迫才配合的。
