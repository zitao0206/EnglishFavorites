### descent
[dɪˈsent]

**Desp**
n. The act of going down; a slope; family origin.
下降，降落；下坡；血统，出身

**Usages**
- steep descent 陡坡，急降
- descent of Chinese blood 中国血统
- be of... descent 具有……血统

**Examples**
1. The plane began its descent into the valley. 飞机开始向山谷下降。
2. The hikers struggled down the steep descent. 徒步者们艰难地走下陡坡。
3. She is of Chinese descent. 她有中国血统。
