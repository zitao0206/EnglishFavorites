### decisive
[dɪˈsaɪsɪv]

**Desp**
adj. able to make decisions quickly and firmly; determining a result.
果断的，决断的，决定性的

**Usages**
- a decisive victory 决定性的胜利
- decisive action 果断行动

**Examples**
1. She is a decisive leader who acts quickly. 她是一位行动迅速、果断的领导者。
2. The last goal was decisive in the match. 最后一个进球在比赛中起到了决定性作用。
