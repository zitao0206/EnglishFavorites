### dispute
[dɪˈspjuːt]

**Description**
n. an argument or disagreement. v. to argue about or question something.
n. 争论，争端；v. 争论，对…提出质疑

**Usages**
- territorial dispute 领土争端
- labor dispute 劳资纠纷

**Examples**
1. The two nations are locked in a territorial dispute. 两国陷入领土争端。
2. They disputed the accuracy of the report. 他们对报告的准确性提出质疑。
3. The dispute was finally settled peacefully. 争端最终和平解决。
