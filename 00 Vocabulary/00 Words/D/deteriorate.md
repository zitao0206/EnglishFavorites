### deteriorate
[dɪˈtɪriəreɪt]

**Desp**
v. to become worse.
v. 恶化，变坏

**Extension**
名词：deterioration 恶化

**Usages**
- health deteriorates 健康恶化
- relations deteriorate 关系恶化
- deteriorating conditions 恶化的状况

**Examples**
1. His health deteriorated rapidly last winter. 去年冬天他的健康迅速恶化。
2. The building has deteriorated since 2010. 自2010年以来这座建筑日渐破败。
3. Relations between the two countries continue to deteriorate. 两国关系持续恶化。
