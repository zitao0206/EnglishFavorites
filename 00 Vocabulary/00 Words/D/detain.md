### detain
[dɪˈteɪn]

**Desp**
v. to keep someone in custody; to delay someone.
拘留，扣押，扣留；耽搁

**Usages**
- be detained for questioning 被拘留审问

**Examples**
1. The police detained the suspect for questioning. 警方拘留了嫌疑人进行讯问。
2. He was detained at the airport for several hours. 他在机场被扣留了几个小时。
