### definite
[ˈdefɪnət]

**Desp**
adj. certain, clear, and not likely to change.
明确的，肯定的；确定的

**Usages**
- a definite answer 明确的答复
- a definite improvement 明显的改善
- definite plans 确定的计划

**Examples**
1. I need a definite answer by Friday. 我需要你在周五前给出明确答复。
2. There has been a definite improvement in her health. 她的健康状况有了明显好转。
3. We have no definite plans for the weekend. 我们周末还没有确定计划。
