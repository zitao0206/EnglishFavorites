### diagnose
[ˈdaɪəɡnəʊz]

**Desp**
v. to identify an illness or the cause of a problem.
诊断，找出原因

**Usages**
- be diagnosed with 被诊断患有
- diagnose the problem 诊断问题

**Examples**
1. The doctor diagnosed him with diabetes. 医生诊断他患有糖尿病。
2. Engineers are trying to diagnose the system failure. 工程师正试图找出系统故障的原因。
