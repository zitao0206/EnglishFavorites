### deprivation
[ˌdeprɪˈveɪʃn]

**Desp**
n. The state of not having something that you need.
匮乏，剥夺，丧失

**Usages**
- sleep deprivation 睡眠剥夺
- sensory deprivation 感官剥夺
- economic deprivation 经济匮乏

**Examples**
1. Sleep deprivation is a major threat to health issues. 睡眠剥夺严重危害健康。
2. The children suffered from deprivation during the war. 战争期间孩子们饱受匮乏之苦。
