### displace
[dɪsˈpleɪs]

**Description**
v. to force out of place; to take the place of.
v. 使离开原位；取代；迫使（人们）背井离乡

**Extension**
名词：displacement 取代，流离失所

**Usages**
- displaced people 流离失所者
- displace jobs 取代工作岗位

**Examples**
1. Thousands were displaced by the flooding. 数千人因洪水背井离乡。
2. Robots displaced many factory workers. 机器人取代了许多工厂工人。
3. The rock displaced a lot of water. 那块岩石排开了大量的水。
