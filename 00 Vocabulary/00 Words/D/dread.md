### dread
[dred]

**Description**
v/n. to feel very worried or frightened about something; a strong feeling of fear or worry.
惧怕，担忧；（n.）恐惧，忧虑

**Usages**
- dread doing sth 害怕做某事
- live in dread of 生活在……的恐惧中
- a dread of failure 对失败的恐惧

**Examples**
1. I dread going to the dentist. 我害怕去看牙医。
2. She lives in dread of losing her job. 她生活在失业的恐惧之中。
3. The thought of the exam filled him with dread. 一想到考试他就充满恐惧。
