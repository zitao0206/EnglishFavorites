### disrespectful
[dɪsrɪˈspektf(ə)l]

**Desp**
adj. showing a lack of respect.
无礼的，不尊重人的

**Usages**
- be disrespectful to sb 对某人无礼

**Examples**
1. It's disrespectful to interrupt your teacher. 打断老师是不礼貌的。
2. He was disrespectful to his elders. 他对长辈无礼。
3. Disrespectful comments hurt people. 无礼的评论会伤人。
