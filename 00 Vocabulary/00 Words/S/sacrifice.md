### sacrifice
[ˈsækrɪfaɪs]

**Description**
n./v. giving up something valuable for a good reason; to give up something for the sake of another.
牺牲；献祭；（v.）牺牲，舍弃

**Usages**
- make sacrifices 作出牺牲
- sacrifice for 为……牺牲
- at the sacrifice of 以牺牲……为代价

**Examples**
1. Parents make many sacrifices for their children. 父母为孩子作出许多牺牲。
2. He sacrificed his career to care for his mother. 为了照顾母亲，他牺牲了自己的事业。
3. She achieved success at the sacrifice of her health. 她以牺牲健康为代价取得了成功。
