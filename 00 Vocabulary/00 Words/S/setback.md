### setback
[ˈsetbæk]

**Desp**
n. A difficulty or problem that delays or prevents progress, or makes things worse than before.
挫折，阻碍；倒退

**Usages**
- suffer a setback 遭受挫折
- a major setback 重大挫折
- overcome setbacks 克服挫折

**Examples**
1. The team suffered a major setback when their star player got injured. 球队明星球员受伤，遭受了重大挫折。
2. Losing the deal was a setback, but not the end of the world. 失去这笔交易是个挫折，但并非世界末日。
3. She faced many setbacks before finally succeeding. 她在最终成功前遭遇了许多挫折。
