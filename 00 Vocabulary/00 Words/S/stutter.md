### stutter
[ˈstʌtə(r)]

**Description**
v. to speak with difficulty, repeating sounds.
口吃，结结巴巴说话

**Usages**
- have a stutter 有口吃
- stutter out 结结巴巴说出

**Examples**
1. He stutters when he's nervous. 他紧张的时候会口吃。
2. She stuttered out her thanks. 她结结巴巴地道了谢。
3. His stutter disappears when he sings. 他唱歌时口吃就消失了。
