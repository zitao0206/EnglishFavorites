### secretary
[ˈsekrəteri]

**Desp**
n. a person employed by an organization to assist with clerical and administrative tasks; a senior official of a department or organization.
秘书；（政府部门）部长，书记

**Usages**
- a company secretary 公司秘书
- the Secretary of State （美国）国务卿
- a personal secretary 私人秘书

**Examples**
1. The secretary scheduled the meeting for next Tuesday. 秘书把会议安排在了下周二。
2. She works as a secretary at a law firm. 她在律师事务所当秘书。
3. The Secretary of Defense held a press conference. 国防部长召开了新闻发布会。
