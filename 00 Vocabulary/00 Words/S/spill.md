### spill


**Desp**
v. to cause or allow liquid to flow over the edge of a container.
洒出，泼出，溢出

**Usages**
- spill the soup 洒了汤
- spill the beans 泄密，说漏嘴
- spill over 溢出，波及

**Examples**
1. Don't spill the soup. 别把汤洒出来。
2. He spilled coffee all over his shirt. 他把咖啡洒了一衬衫。
3. The argument spilled over into the next day. 争吵一直延续到第二天。
