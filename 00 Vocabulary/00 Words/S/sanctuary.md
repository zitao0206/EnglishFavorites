### sanctuary
[ˈsæŋktʃuəri]

**Desp**
n. a place of refuge or protection; a nature reserve.
庇护所，保护区

**Usages**
- island sanctuaries 岛屿保护区
- wildlife sanctuary 野生动物保护区

**Examples**
1. As a result, during 1980-97, the surviving population was evacuated to three island sanctuaries. 结果，在1980至97年间，幸存种群被转移到了三个岛屿保护区。
2. The wildlife sanctuary protects endangered species. 这个野生动物保护区保护濒危物种。
3. The church offered sanctuary to refugees. 教堂为难民提供了庇护。
