### superior
[suːˈpɪəriə(r)]

**Desp**
adj./n. Better in quality or ability than others; thinking you are better than others; a person of higher rank.
出众的，优秀的，优越的；上级的；高傲的；（n.）上级

**Usages**
- be superior to 优于，胜过
- superior quality 上乘品质
- immediate superior 直接上级

**Examples**
1. This product is superior to its competitors. 这款产品优于其竞争对手。
2. She has a superior attitude toward her colleagues. 她对同事态度高傲。
3. I'll have to ask my superior about this. 这件事我得问问我的上级。
