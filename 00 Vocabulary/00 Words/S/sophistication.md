### sophistication
[səˌfɪstɪˈkeɪʃn]

**Desp**
n. The quality of being experienced and worldly; the state of being complex and advanced.
老练世故；复杂先进，精密

**Usages**
- technical sophistication 技术复杂度
- the sophistication of his writing 他文笔的精妙
- sophistication in design 设计的精密

**Examples**
1. The sophistication of his writing takes time to appreciate. 他文笔的精妙需要花时间品味。
2. The device impresses users with its technical sophistication. 该设备以其技术精密程度令用户惊叹。
3. She handled the negotiation with great sophistication. 她以极老练的手法处理了谈判。
