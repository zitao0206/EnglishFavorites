### security
[sɪˈkjʊərəti]

**Desp**
n. The state of being safe and protected; measures taken to protect against crime or danger.
安全，保障；保安措施；证券

**Usages**
- national security 国家安全
- job security 工作保障
- security guard 保安

**Examples**
1. The airport has strict security checks. 机场有严格的安检措施。
2. He works as a security guard at night. 他晚上做保安工作。
3. The wall gives us a sense of security. 这堵墙给了我们安全感。
