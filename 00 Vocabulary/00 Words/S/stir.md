### stir
[stɜː]

**Desp**
v/n. To mix by moving a spoon around; to move slightly.
搅拌；搅动；激起

**Usages**
- stir the coffee 搅拌咖啡
- stir up 激起，煽动
- cause a stir 引起轰动

**Examples**
1. Stir the coffee before you drink it. 喝咖啡之前搅拌一下。
2. She stirred the soup with a spoon. 她用勺子搅了搅汤。
3. His speech stirred up a heated debate. 他的演讲引发了激烈的争论。
