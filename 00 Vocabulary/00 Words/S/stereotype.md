### stereotype
[ˈsteriətaɪp]

**Desp**
n. a fixed oversimplified idea about a group of people.
老套，模式化的见解

**Usages**
- break the stereotype 打破刻板印象
- gender stereotype 性别刻板印象

**Examples**
1. The movie is full of stereotypes about teenagers. 这部电影充满对青少年的刻板印象。
2. We should break the stereotype that boys don't cry. 我们应该打破"男孩不哭"的刻板印象。
3. She doesn't fit the stereotype of a CEO. 她不符合人们眼中CEO的刻板形象。
