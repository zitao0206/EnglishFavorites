### scrape
[skreɪp]

**Desp**
v. to rub against a rough surface, causing damage or injury.
刮除，擦伤，刮坏

**Usages**
- scrape off 刮掉
- scrape one's knee 擦破膝盖
- scrape the paint off 刮掉油漆

**Examples**
1. I scraped my knee when I fell off my bike. 我从自行车上摔下来擦伤了膝盖。
2. She scraped the mud off her boots. 她把靴子上的泥刮掉。
3. He scraped his car against the wall. 他的车蹭到了墙。
