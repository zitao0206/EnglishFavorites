### spark
[spɑːrk]

**Desp**
n. a small flash of light; an initial stimulus that ignites something. v. To trigger or set in motion.
火花；触发，引发

**Usages**
- spark interest 激发兴趣
- spark a debate 引发辩论
- a spark of hope 一线希望

**Examples**
1. The teacher's question sparked a lively discussion. 老师的问题引发了热烈的讨论。
2. A single spark can start a massive fire. 一点火星就能引发一场大火。
3. Her speech sparked renewed interest in the project. 她的演讲重新激发了人们对项目的兴趣。
