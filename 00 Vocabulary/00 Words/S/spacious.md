### spacious
[ˈspeɪʃəs]

**Desp**
adj. having plenty of room; large in area.
adj. 宽敞的，开阔的

**Extension**
名词：spaciousness 宽敞

**Usages**
- a spacious apartment 宽敞的公寓
- a spacious living room 宽敞的客厅

**Examples**
1. The new apartment is bright and spacious. 新公寓明亮宽敞。
2. They rented a spacious office downtown. 他们在市中心租了一间宽敞的办公室。
3. The car has a spacious interior. 这辆车内部空间很宽敞。
