### self-esteem

**Desp**
n. confidence in one's own worth; self-respect.
自尊，自信

**Usages**
- high self-esteem 高度自尊、自信
- boost self-esteem 提升自信心

**Examples**
1. Success helps build self-esteem. 成功有助于建立自信。
2. Regular exercise can boost your self-esteem. 经常锻炼能提升你的自信心。
3. Children need praise to develop healthy self-esteem. 孩子需要表扬来培养健康的自尊。
