### stain
[steɪn]

**Desp**
v./n. to mark something; a mark that is hard to remove.
v. 沾染，染色 n. 污渍

**Usages**
- a coffee stain 咖啡渍
- stain the wood 给木头上色

**Examples**
1. The red wine stained the tablecloth. 红酒染污了桌布。
2. There's a stain on your shirt. 你衬衫上有污渍。
3. The wooden floor was stained dark brown. 木地板被染成深棕色。
