### surge
[sɜːrdʒ]

**Description**
v/n. to rise quickly; a sudden large increase.
v. 激增，涌动 n. 激增；浪涌

**Usages**
- a surge in demand 需求激增
- prices surge 价格飙升
- a power surge 电涌

**Examples**
1. Demand for masks surged during the pandemic. 疫情期间口罩需求激增。
2. The crowd surged forward when the gates opened. 大门一开人群蜂拥向前。
3. There was a surge in online shopping last year. 去年网购出现激增。
