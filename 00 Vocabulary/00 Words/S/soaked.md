### soaked
[soʊk]

**Desp**
adj. very wet.
湿透的
v. 浸泡；使湿透，浸湿；

**Usages**
- get soaked 被淋透
- be soaked through 浑身湿透
- soak v. 浸泡，浸湿

**Examples**
1. She forgot her umbrella; as a result, she got soaked in the rain. 她忘带伞，被雨淋透。
2. My clothes were soaked through. 我的衣服湿透了。
3. Soak the beans overnight before cooking. 豆子要先浸泡一夜再煮。
