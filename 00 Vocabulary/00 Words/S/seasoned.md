### seasoned
[ˈsiːznd]

**Desp**
adj. having a lot of experience in a particular activity.
经验丰富的；调过味的

**Usages**
- a seasoned traveler 经验丰富的旅行者
- a seasoned veteran 久经沙场的老将
- seasoned with salt 用盐调味的

**Examples**
1. She is a seasoned journalist with 20 years of experience. 她是一位有20年经验的资深记者。
2. The seasoned captain guided the ship through the storm. 经验丰富的船长带领船只穿过了暴风雨。
3. The meat was well seasoned. 这肉调味调得很好。
