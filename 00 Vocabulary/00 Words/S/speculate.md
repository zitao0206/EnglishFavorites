### speculate
[ˈspekjəleɪt]

**Description**
v. To form a theory or conjecture about a subject without firm evidence.
推测，猜测；投机

**Usages**
- speculate about/on sth 推测某事
- speculate in stocks 炒股投机

**Examples**
1. Scientists can only speculate about the origin of the universe. 科学家只能推测宇宙的起源。
2. She speculated that the delay was caused by bad weather. 她推测延误是恶劣天气造成的。
3. Investors who speculated in real estate lost heavily. 在房地产上投机的投资者损失惨重。
