### sour
[ˈsaʊə]

**Desp**
adj. having a sharp, acid taste; (of milk) spoiled.
酸的；变馊的

**Usages**
- sour taste 酸味
- go sour 变酸，变质
- sour grapes 酸葡萄（吃不到葡萄说葡萄酸）

**Examples**
1. This lemon is very sour. 这个柠檬很酸。
2. The milk has gone sour. 牛奶变酸了。
3. He dismissed the prize as sour grapes. 他说那个奖是酸葡萄心理，不屑一顾。
