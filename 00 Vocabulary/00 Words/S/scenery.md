### scenery
[ˈsiːnəri]

**Description**
n. the natural features of an area considered as a whole; (uncountable) landscape.
自然风光，风景（整体景色，不可数）

**Usages**
- a scenic spot 景点
- mountain scenery 山景
- nature scenery 自然风光

**Examples**
1. The mountain scenery is breathtaking. 山景美得令人窒息。
2. We stopped to enjoy the scenery along the way. 我们停下来欣赏沿途风景。
3. This area is famous for its natural scenery. 这一带以自然风光闻名。
