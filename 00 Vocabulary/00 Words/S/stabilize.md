### stabilize
[ˈsteɪbəlaɪz]

**Description**
v. to become or make steady.
v. （使）稳定，稳固

**Extension**
名词：stability 稳定
形容词：stable 稳定的

**Usages**
- stabilize prices 稳定物价
- stabilize the economy 稳定经济
- stabilize the patient 使病人情况稳定

**Examples**
1. Doctors managed to stabilize the patient. 医生们使病人的病情稳定了下来。
2. The government took steps to stabilize prices. 政府采取措施稳定物价。
3. The housing market finally stabilized. 房市终于企稳了。
