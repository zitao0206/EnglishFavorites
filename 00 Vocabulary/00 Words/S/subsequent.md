### subsequent
[ˈsʌbsɪkwənt]

**Desp**
adj. coming after something in time.
adj. 随后的，后来的

**Extension**
反义词：previous 先前的
名词：subsequence 随后发生的事

**Usages**
- subsequent events 后续事件
- subsequent to 在…之后

**Examples**
1. The theory was disproved by subsequent research. 该理论被后来的研究推翻了。
2. Subsequent chapters deal with practical applications. 随后几章讲实际应用。
3. He resigned subsequent to the scandal. 丑闻之后他辞职了。
