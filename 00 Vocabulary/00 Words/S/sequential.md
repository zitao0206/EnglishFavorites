### sequential
[sɪˈkwenʃl]

**Desp**
adj. following in a logical order or sequence; happening one after another.
连续的，按顺序的

**Usages**
- sequential steps 按顺序的步骤
- sequential numbers 连续编号
- sequential processing 顺序处理

**Examples**
1. The tasks must be done in sequential order. 这些任务必须按顺序完成。
2. The pages are numbered sequentially. 页面按顺序编号。
3. Data is processed in sequential steps. 数据按顺序步骤处理。
