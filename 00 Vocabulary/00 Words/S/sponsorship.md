### sponsorship
[ˈspɒnsəʃɪp]

**Desp**
n. financial support given to a person, event or organization.
赞助，资助；发起

**Usages**
- corporate sponsorship 企业赞助
- sponsorship deal 赞助协议

**Examples**
1. The marathon relies heavily on corporate sponsorship. 马拉松比赛很大程度上依赖企业赞助。
2. She received a sponsorship from the university for her research. 她获得了大学对其研究的资助。
