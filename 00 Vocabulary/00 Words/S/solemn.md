### solemn
[ˈsɑːləm]

**Desp**
adj. Formal and dignified; not cheerful or smiling; serious.
庄严的，肃穆的；严肃的

**Usages**
- solemn ceremony 庄严的仪式
- solemn promise 郑重的承诺
- solemn expression 严肃的表情

**Examples**
1. The president took the oath with a solemn expression. 总统表情严肃地宣誓就职。
2. They held a solemn memorial for the fallen soldiers. 他们为阵亡将士举行了庄严的纪念仪式。
3. He made a solemn promise never to drink again. 他郑重承诺再也不喝酒了。
