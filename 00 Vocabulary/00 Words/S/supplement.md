### supplement
[ˈsʌplɪmənt]

**Desp**
n/v. something added to improve or complete something; a pill or product added to your diet; to add something extra.
n. 补充物；增刊；膳食补充剂；v. 补充，增补

**Usages**
- vitamin supplements 维生素补充剂
- a supplement to... ……的补充
- supplement one's income 补贴收入

**Examples**
1. She takes vitamin supplements every day. 她每天服用维生素补充剂。
2. He supplements his income by teaching online. 他靠在线教学补贴收入。
3. The article is a supplement to the report. 这篇文章是对报告的补充。
