### smuggle
[ˈsmʌɡl]

**Desp**
v. to take goods or people into or out of a country secretly and illegally.
走私，偷偷夹带

**Usages**
- smuggle proprietary chips across the border 走私专利芯片过境
- smuggle goods 走私货物

**Examples**
1. He was arrested for smuggling drugs across the border. 他因走私毒品过境被捕。
2. They tried to smuggle the chips out of the country. 他们试图将芯片偷偷带出国。
