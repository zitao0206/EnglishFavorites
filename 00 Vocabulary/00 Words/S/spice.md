### spice
[spaɪs]

**Desp**
n./v. A substance from plants used to give flavor to food; excitement or interest; to add flavor.
香料，调味品；趣味，刺激；（v.）给……调味；为……增添趣味

**Usages**
- a spice of 一丝……
- add spice to 为……增添趣味
- spice up 使更有趣

**Examples**
1. This dish needs more spice. 这道菜需要多加些香料。
2. Variety adds spice to life. 多样化给生活增添趣味。
3. She spiced up the speech with some jokes. 她在演讲中加入了一些笑话，使它更有趣。
