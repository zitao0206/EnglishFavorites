### succumb
[səˈkʌm]

**Description**
v. to yield to pressure or temptation; to die from the effects of something.
屈服（+ to）

**Usages**
- succumb to pressure 屈服于压力
- succumb to disease 死于疾病

**Examples**
1. The tree succumbed to the disease. 这棵树死于疾病。
2. He succumbed to temptation and ate the cake. 他没抵挡住诱惑吃了蛋糕。
3. Many soldiers succumbed to their wounds. 许多士兵因伤死亡。
