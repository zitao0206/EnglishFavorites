### stab
[stæb]

**Description**
v. to thrust a knife or other pointed weapon into someone/something.
刺，戳，捅，扎
n. A thrust with a pointed weapon; an attempt.

**Usages**
- stab sb in the chest 刺入某人胸口
- stab wound 刀伤
- have a stab at sth 试做某事

**Examples**
1. He was stabbed in the arm during the fight. 他在打斗中被扎伤了手臂。
2. The victim had several stab wounds. 受害者身上有多处刀伤。
3. Let me have a stab at the crossword. 让我试着做做这个填字游戏。
