### surplus
[ˈsɜːpləs]

**Desp**
n. an amount that is more than what is needed. adj. more than what is needed.
n. 过剩，剩余；adj. 过剩的，多余的

**Usages**
- trade surplus 贸易顺差
- surplus food 剩余食物

**Examples**
1. The country enjoys a trade surplus with its neighbors. 该国与邻国保持贸易顺差。
2. The surplus grain was donated to charity. 剩余粮食被捐给了慈善机构。
