### sensitive
[ˈsensətɪv]

**Description**
adj. Easily affected or damaged by something; aware of and understanding other people's feelings; needing careful handling.
敏感的；体恤的；机密的

**Usages**
- be sensitive to light 对光敏感
- a sensitive topic 敏感话题
- sensitive data 敏感数据

**Examples**
1. Her skin is very sensitive to sunlight. 她的皮肤对阳光非常敏感。
2. He is a sensitive and caring person. 他是一个体贴细心的人。
3. Please avoid that sensitive topic at dinner. 晚餐时请避开那个敏感话题。
