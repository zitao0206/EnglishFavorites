### stimulate
[ˈstɪmjuleɪt]

**Desp**
v. to encourage activity or growth; to make someone excited or interested.
刺激，激励；促进；激发

**Usages**
- stimulate the economy 刺激经济
- stimulate interest 激发兴趣
- stimulate growth 促进增长

**Examples**
1. The government cut taxes to stimulate the economy. 政府减税以刺激经济。
2. Good teachers stimulate students' curiosity. 好老师会激发学生的好奇心。
3. Exercise stimulates blood circulation. 运动促进血液循环。
