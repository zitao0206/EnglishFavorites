### sunbathe
[ˈsʌnbeɪð]

**Desp**
v. to lie in the sun to get a tan.
晒日光浴

**Usages**
- sunbathe on the beach 在海滩上晒日光浴

**Examples**
1. She loves sunbathing on the beach. 她喜欢在海滩上晒日光浴。
2. We sunbathed by the pool all afternoon. 我们整个下午都在泳池边晒日光浴。
3. Don't sunbathe too long without sunscreen. 不要不涂防晒霜就长时间晒日光浴。
