### specific
[spəˈsɪfɪk]

**Desp**
adj. Clearly defined or identified; relating to a particular subject.
具体的，明确的；特定的

**Usages**
- specific details 具体细节
- for a specific purpose 为特定目的
- be specific about sth 对某事说明确

**Examples**
1. Can you be more specific about what you need? 你能更具体地说明你需要什么吗？
2. The tool was designed for a specific purpose. 这个工具是为特定目的设计的。
3. Each student has specific learning needs. 每个学生都有特定的学习需求。
