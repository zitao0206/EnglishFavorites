### suspend
[səˈspend]

**Desp**
v. to hang something up; to stop temporarily; to bar someone from a job or school.
v. 悬挂；暂停；停职，停学

**Extension**
名词：suspension 暂停，停职

**Usages**
- suspend operations 暂停运营
- be suspended from school 被停学
- suspend judgment 暂缓判断

**Examples**
1. Flights were suspended due to the storm. 由于暴风雨，航班暂停了。
2. The student was suspended for fighting. 那名学生因打架被停学。
3. The lamp was suspended from the ceiling. 灯吊挂在天花板上。
