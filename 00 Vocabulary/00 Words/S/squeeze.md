### squeeze
[skwiːz]

**Desp**
v. To press something firmly; to force into a small space.
挤压，榨取；挤入

**Usages**
- squeeze out 挤出
- squeeze into 挤进
- squeeze lemon juice 挤柠檬汁

**Examples**
1. Squeeze some lemon juice into the water. 往水里挤点柠檬汁。
2. He squeezed the toothpaste tube. 他挤了挤牙膏管。
3. Five people squeezed into the tiny car. 五个人挤进了那辆小车。
