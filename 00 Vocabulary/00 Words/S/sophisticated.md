### sophisticated
[səˈfɪstɪkeɪtɪd]

**Desp**
adj. Having or showing much worldly experience; complex and refined.
老练的，精密的，复杂的

**Usages**
- sophisticated technology 尖端技术
- sophisticated taste 高雅品味
- a sophisticated audience 有鉴赏力的观众

**Examples**
1. The software uses sophisticated algorithms to detect fraud. 该软件使用复杂算法来检测欺诈。
2. She has sophisticated taste in art and music. 她在艺术和音乐方面有高雅的品味。
3. The system is too sophisticated for casual users. 该系统对普通用户来说太复杂了。
