### sophisticated
[səˈfɪstɪkeɪtɪd]

**Desp**
adj. Having or showing much worldly experience; complex and refined.
老练的，精密的，复杂的

- sophistication [səˌfɪstɪˈkeɪʃn] n. 老练世故；复杂先进，精密

**Usages**
- sophisticated technology 尖端技术
- sophisticated taste 高雅品味
- a sophisticated audience 有鉴赏力的观众
- technical sophistication 技术复杂度
- the sophistication of his writing 他文笔的精妙
- sophistication in design 设计的精密

**Examples**
1. The software uses sophisticated algorithms to detect fraud. 该软件使用复杂算法来检测欺诈。
2. She has sophisticated taste in art and music. 她在艺术和音乐方面有高雅的品味。
3. The system is too sophisticated for casual users. 该系统对普通用户来说太复杂了。
4. The sophistication of his writing takes time to appreciate. 他文笔的精妙需要花时间品味。
5. The device impresses users with its technical sophistication. 该设备以其技术精密程度令用户惊叹。
6. She handled the negotiation with great sophistication. 她以极老练的手法处理了谈判。
