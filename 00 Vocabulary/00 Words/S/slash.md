### slash
[slæʃ]

**Description**
v. to cut something violently; to reduce greatly; n. a cut mark.
v. 砍，劈；大幅削减 n. 砍痕；斜线

**Usages**
- slash prices 大幅降价
- slash the budget 大砍预算
- slash costs 削减成本

**Examples**
1. The company slashed its workforce by half. 公司裁员一半。
2. Prices were slashed for the summer sale. 夏季大甩卖大幅降价。
3. He slashed the tire with a knife. 他用刀割破了轮胎。
