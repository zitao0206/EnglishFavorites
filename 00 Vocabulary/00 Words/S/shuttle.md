### shuttle
[ˈʃʌtl]

**Desp**
n/v. A vehicle that travels regularly between two places; to travel back and forth.
航天飞机；班车；往返运送

**Usages**
- space shuttle 航天飞机
- shuttle bus 班车，摆渡车
- shuttle between A and B 在两地之间往返

**Examples**
1. The space shuttle landed safely this morning. 航天飞机今晨安全着陆。
2. A free shuttle bus runs between the airport and the hotel. 机场和酒店之间有免费班车。
3. He shuttles between Beijing and Shanghai every week. 他每周往返于北京和上海之间。
