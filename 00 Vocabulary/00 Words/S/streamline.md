### streamline
[ˈstriːmlaɪn]

**Desp**
v. to make a system or process more efficient by removing unnecessary steps; to shape for less resistance.
使（机构、流程）效率更高，精简；使成流线型

**Extension**
同义词：optimize, simplify, rationalize
形容词：streamlined

**Usages**
- streamline the process 精简流程
- streamline operations 精简运营
- a streamlined design 流线型设计

**Examples**
1. The company streamlined its operations to cut costs. 公司精简了运营以削减成本。
2. We need to streamline the approval process. 我们需要简化审批流程。
3. The new car has a streamlined body for better fuel efficiency. 新车的流线型车身提高了燃油效率。
