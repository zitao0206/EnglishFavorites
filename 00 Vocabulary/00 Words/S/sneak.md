### sneak
[sniːk]

**Description**
v. to go quietly and secretly; to take secretly.
v. 偷偷地走，溜；偷偷拿

**Usages**
- sneak into the cinema 溜进电影院
- sneak a look 偷看一眼
- sneak out 溜出去

**Examples**
1. He sneaked out of the house without waking anyone. 他悄悄溜出家门，没吵醒任何人。
2. She sneaked a glance at her phone during class. 她上课时偷瞄了一眼手机。
3. Don't sneak food before dinner. 晚饭前别偷吃东西。
