### spam
[spæm]

**Desp**
n. irrelevant or inappropriate messages sent to many people; v. to flood someone with messages.
垃圾消息；狂发消息，刷屏

**Usages**
- phone spam 手机垃圾短信
- social-media spam 社交媒体刷屏
- spam filter 垃圾邮件过滤器

**Examples**
1. Ignore that spam, it's all fake-product ads. 别理那条垃圾消息，全是虚假产品广告。
2. My inbox is full of spam. 我的收件箱里全是垃圾邮件。
3. He kept spamming the group chat with memes. 他不停地往群里刷表情包。
