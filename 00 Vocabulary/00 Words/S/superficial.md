### superficial
[ˌsuːpərˈfɪʃl]

**Description**
adj. only on the surface; not deep or thorough.
adj. 表面的，肤浅的，浅薄的

**Extension**
名词：superficiality 肤浅

**Usages**
- a superficial wound 表皮伤
- superficial knowledge 肤浅的知识
- a superficial analysis 蜻蜓点水的分析

**Examples**
1. The cut was superficial and needed no stitches. 伤口很浅，不需要缝合。
2. He has only a superficial knowledge of economics. 他对经济学只有肤浅的了解。
3. The report gives a superficial analysis of the problem. 这份报告对问题的分析流于表面。
