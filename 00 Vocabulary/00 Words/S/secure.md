### secure
[sɪˈkjʊr]

**Desp**
adj. safe from risk; firm; v. to obtain; to make safe.
adj. 安全的；稳固的 v. 获得；保护，扣紧

**Extension**
名词：security 安全
名词：security 证券（复数）

**Usages**
- secure a job 找到一份工作
- a secure income 稳定的收入
- secure a loan 取得贷款

**Examples**
1. She secured a place at a top university. 她获得了一所顶尖大学的录取名额。
2. Make sure the ropes are secure. 确保绳子拴牢了。
3. We must secure the borders against smuggling. 我们必须保卫边境、打击走私。
