### slack
[slæk]

**Desp**
adj. not tight; not busy; lazy; n. loose part; v. to slacken.
adj. 松弛的；萧条的；懈怠的 n. 松弛部分 v. 放松

**Usages**
- slack demand 需求疲软
- cut sb some slack 放某人一马
- slack off 偷懒

**Examples**
1. Sales are slack during the winter months. 冬季几个月销售疲软。
2. The rope was too slack to hold the tent. 绳子太松，固定不住帐篷。
3. Don't slack off before the deadline. 截止日期前别松懈。
