### simultaneous
[ˌsaɪmlˈteɪniəs]

🕒 Updated: 2026-08-16
📅 Created: 2026-08-16
📌 ReviewCount: 1

**Desp**
adj. happening, done, or existing at the same time.
同时发生的，同步的

**Usages**
- a simultaneous interpreting device 同声传译设备
- simultaneous translation 同声传译
- simultaneous events 同时发生的事件

**Examples**
1. The conference provided simultaneous interpreting devices for all delegates. 大会为所有代表提供了同声传译设备。
2. There were three simultaneous explosions in the city. 城里同时发生了三起爆炸。
3. The two launches were simultaneous. 两次发射是同步进行的。
