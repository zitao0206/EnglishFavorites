### sweep
[swiːp]

**Desp**
v. to clean with a brush; to move quickly; n. a wide movement.
v. 打扫；掠过，席卷 n. 打扫；广泛搜索

**Usages**
- sweep the floor 扫地
- sweep across the country 席卷全国
- sweep aside 漠视，扫除

**Examples**
1. She swept the crumbs off the table. 她把面包屑从桌上扫掉。
2. The new fashion swept across Europe. 新风尚席卷欧洲。
3. The team swept the series four-nil. 球队以4比0横扫系列赛。
