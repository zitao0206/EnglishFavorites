### sentimental
[ˌsentɪˈmentl]

**Description**
adj. Based on or expressing feelings, especially tender feelings.
情感上的，多愁善感的，感伤的

**Usages**
- sentimental value 情感价值
- sentimental about 对……念念不忘
- get sentimental 变得多愁善感

**Examples**
1. The gold necklace has great sentimental value. 这条金项链饱含情感意义。
2. She kept the letters for sentimental reasons. 出于感情原因她保留了那些信。
3. He got sentimental about his old school. 他对母校念念不忘。
