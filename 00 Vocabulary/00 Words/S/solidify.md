### solidify
[səˈlɪdɪfaɪ]

**Description**
v. to make or become hard or solid; to make more definite or secure.
凝固，固化；巩固，加强

**Usages**
- solidify a relationship 巩固关系
- solidify one's position 巩固地位
- solidify an agreement 确立协议

**Examples**
1. The partnership solidified after months of negotiation. 经过数月谈判，合作关系得以巩固。
2. The liquid solidifies at room temperature. 该液体在室温下凝固。
3. Winning the championship solidified his reputation as a great coach. 赢得冠军巩固了他作为伟大教练的声誉。
