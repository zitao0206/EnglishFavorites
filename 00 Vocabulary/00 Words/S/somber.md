### somber
[ˈsɒmbə(r)]

**Desp**
adj. sad and serious; dark in color or appearance.
阴郁的，忧郁的；昏暗的（= sombre）

**Usages**
- a somber mood 阴郁的气氛
- somber colors 暗淡的颜色
- a somber tone 沉重的语气

**Examples**
1. The room fell into a somber silence. 房间里陷入一片阴郁的寂静。
2. He wore a somber expression throughout the meeting. 整个会议期间他都表情阴沉。
3. The film has a somber, melancholic tone. 这部电影基调阴郁忧伤。
