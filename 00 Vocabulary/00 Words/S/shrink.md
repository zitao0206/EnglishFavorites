### shrink
[ʃrɪŋk]

**Desp**
v. To become or make smaller; to move back from fear.
收缩，退缩，缩小

**Usages**
- shrink the market 市场萎缩
- shrink from responsibility 逃避责任
- shrink in size 尺寸缩小

**Examples**
1. The market for print newspapers continues to shrink. 纸质报纸的市场持续萎缩。
2. The sweater shrank after washing. 毛衣洗后缩水了。
3. She never shrinks from a challenge. 她从不畏惧挑战。
