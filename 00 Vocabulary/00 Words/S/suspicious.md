### suspicious
[səˈspɪʃəs]

**Desp**
adj. making one feel that someone/something is involved in wrongdoing; distrustful.
可疑的；多疑的，怀疑的

**Usages**
- be suspicious of sb/sth 怀疑某人/某事
- a suspicious package 可疑包裹
- highly suspicious 非常可疑

**Examples**
1. I am suspicious of her true motives. 我对她真正的动机心存怀疑。
2. He became suspicious when the stranger kept asking about his schedule. 当那陌生人不断打听他的日程时，他起了疑心。
3. The police found a suspicious package at the station. 警方在车站发现了一个可疑包裹。
