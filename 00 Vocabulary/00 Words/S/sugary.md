### sugary
[ˈʃʊɡəri]

**Desp**
adj. Containing a lot of sugar; too sweet.
含糖的，甜的

**Usages**
- sugary drinks 含糖饮料
- sugary snacks 甜味零食
- avoid sugary food 避免含糖食物

**Examples**
1. I try to avoid sweets because I'm on a diet. 我在节食，尽量不吃甜食。
2. Sugary drinks are bad for your teeth. 含糖饮料对牙齿不好。
3. This cake is too sugary for me. 这块蛋糕对我来说太甜了。
