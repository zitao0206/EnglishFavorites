### spit
[spɪt]

**Desp**
vt./vi. to force liquid or food out of the mouth.
吐出；吐痰

**Usages**
- spit out 吐出
- spit at sb 朝某人吐唾沫
- spit in public 在公共场合吐痰

**Examples**
1. Don't spit in public. 不要在公共场合吐痰。
2. He spat out the bone. 他把骨头吐出来。
3. She spat at him in anger. 她气得朝他啐了一口。
