### strengthen
[ˈstreŋθn]

**Desp**
v. to make something stronger; to become stronger.
使变强，加强；（情感、决心）更加坚强

**Usages**
- strengthen the relationship 加强关系
- strengthen one's resolve 坚定决心

**Examples**
1. Exercise can strengthen your body. 锻炼可以强健体魄。
2. The crisis strengthened their friendship. 这场危机加深了他们的友谊。
