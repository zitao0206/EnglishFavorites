### snap
[snæp]

**Desp**
v. to break suddenly; to suddenly lose control or become angry; to take a photo. n. a sudden break; a photo.
折断，断裂；突然失去冷静，崩溃；拍照

**Usages**
- snap at sb 对某人大吼
- snap a photo 拍照

**Examples**
1. He finally snapped after months of stress. 数月压力之后，他终于情绪崩溃。
2. The branch snapped in the strong wind. 树枝在强风中折断了。
3. She snapped a few photos of the sunset. 她拍了几张日落的照片。
