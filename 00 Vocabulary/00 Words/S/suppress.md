### suppress
[səˈpres]

**Desp**
v. to stop something by using force or authority; to prevent something from being shown, expressed, or known.
镇压，压制；抑制，忍住；封锁（信息）

**Extension**
反义词：express（表达）、encourage（鼓励）
同义词：subdue, restrain, inhibit
其他词形：suppression n. 镇压；抑制

**Usages**
- suppress a rebellion 镇压叛乱
- suppress one's anger/feelings 压抑怒火/情绪
- suppress information/evidence 封锁信息/证据
- suppress a smile/laughter 忍住笑

**Examples**
1. The government suppressed the rebellion with force. 政府用武力镇压了叛乱。
2. She could barely suppress her laughter. 她几乎忍不住笑出声来。
3. He suppressed the urge to quit his job. 他压下了辞职的冲动。
4. The company tried to suppress the bad news. 公司试图封锁这个坏消息。
