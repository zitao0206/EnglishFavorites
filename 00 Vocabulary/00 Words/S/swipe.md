### swipe
[swaɪp]

**Desp**
v. to move your finger across a screen; to pass a card through a reader; to steal something.
滑动（屏幕）；刷（卡）；偷窃

**Usages**
- swipe a card 刷卡
- swipe the screen 滑动屏幕
- swipe left/right 向左/右滑动

**Examples**
1. Swipe your card to enter the building. 刷卡进入大楼。
2. She swiped right on the app. 她在应用上向右滑动了。
3. He swiped my wallet when I wasn't looking. 他趁我不注意偷走了我的钱包。
