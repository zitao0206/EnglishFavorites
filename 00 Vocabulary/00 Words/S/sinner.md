### sinner
[ˈsɪnə(r)]

**Description**
n. a person who has done wrong or broken religious/moral law.
罪人

**Usages**
- condemned sinner 遭天谴的罪人
- doomed sinner 注定毁灭的罪人
- a repentant sinner 悔过的罪人

**Examples**
1. In the story, the condemned sinner begged for mercy. 故事里，遭天谴的罪人乞求宽恕。
2. He called himself a sinner and prayed for forgiveness. 他自称罪人，祈求宽恕。
3. Even a sinner deserves a second chance. 即使是罪人也该有第二次机会。
