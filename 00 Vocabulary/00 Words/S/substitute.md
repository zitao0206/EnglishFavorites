### substitute
[ˈsʌbstɪtjuːt]

**Description**
n/v. A thing used instead of another; to use one thing in place of another.
替代品；替代

**Usages**
- substitute A for B 用A代替B
- a substitute for sth 某物的替代品
- substitute teacher 代课老师

**Examples**
1. We can substitute rice for bread. 我们可以用米饭代替面包。
2. This drink is a healthy substitute for soda. 这款饮品是汽水的健康替代品。
3. Honey can substitute for sugar in most recipes. 在大多数食谱中蜂蜜可以替代糖。
