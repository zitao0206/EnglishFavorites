### snow

**Desp**
n. frozen water falling in flakes; v. to fall as snow.
n. 雪；v. 下雪

**Usages**
- snow heavily / snow hard 雪下得很大
- It stopped snowing 雪停了
- a heavy snow 一场大雪

**Examples**
1. It's snowing. 正在下雪。
2. It was snowing hard back then. 那时候雪下得很大。
3. It stopped snowing, so we went out to play. 雪停了，我们出去玩。
