### skyrocket
[ˈskaɪrɒkɪt]

**Desp**
v. to rise very quickly and suddenly. n. a firework that shoots high into the sky.
飞速上涨，猛涨；冲天火箭

**Usages**
- prices skyrocket 物价飞涨

**Examples**
1. Housing prices have skyrocketed in recent years. 近年来房价飞涨。
2. The company's stock skyrocketed after the announcement. 消息公布后该公司股价飙升。
