### substantial
[səbˈstænʃl]

**Desp**
adj. large in amount or importance; solidly built.
adj. 大量的，可观的；坚固的

**Extension**
名词：substance 物质，实质

**Usages**
- a substantial amount 大量
- substantial evidence 充分的证据
- a substantial meal 丰盛的一餐

**Examples**
1. She earned a substantial sum from the sale. 她从这次出售中赚了一大笔钱。
2. There is substantial evidence for the theory. 有充分的证据支持这一理论。
3. They made substantial changes to the design. 他们对设计做了大幅改动。
