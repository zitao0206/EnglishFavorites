### seaside
[ˈsiːsaɪd]

**Desp**
n/adj. The area by the sea; of or near the sea.
海边；海滨的

**Usages**
- at the seaside 在海边
- a seaside town 海滨小镇
- seaside resort 海滨度假胜地

**Examples**
1. We spent the summer at the seaside. 我们在海边度过了夏天。
2. They live in a small seaside town. 他们住在一个海滨小镇。
