### snooze
[snuːz]

**Desp**
v. To sleep lightly for a short time; n. a short light sleep.
打盹，小睡

**Usages**
- hit the snooze button 按贪睡按钮
- take a snooze 打个盹
- snooze through 睡过去

**Examples**
1. He snoozed on the sofa after lunch. 他午饭后在沙发上打了个盹。
2. I hit the snooze button three times this morning. 今天早上我按了三次贪睡按钮。
3. Don't snooze through your alarm. 别睡过头错过闹钟。
