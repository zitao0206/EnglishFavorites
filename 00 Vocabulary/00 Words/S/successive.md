### successive
[səkˈsesɪv]

**Desp**
adj. following one after another without interruption.
adj. 连续的，接连的

**Extension**
动词：succeed 接续，成功
名词：succession 连续，继任

**Usages**
- successive years 连续几年
- successive governments 历届政府

**Examples**
1. The team won three successive championships. 这支球队连续三次夺得冠军。
2. Prices rose for the third successive month. 物价连续第三个月上涨。
3. Successive governments failed to reform the system. 历届政府都未能改革这一体制。
