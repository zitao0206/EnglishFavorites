### stem
[stem]

**Description**
v. to stop or restrain; n. the main stalk of a plant.
v. 遏制，阻止；起源于 n. 茎，干；词干

**Usages**
- stem the flow of 遏制…的流动
- stem from 源于
- the stem of a flower 花茎

**Examples**
1. The government acted to stem the crisis. 政府采取行动遏制危机。
2. Many problems stem from poor planning. 许多问题源于规划不善。
3. She cut the stems at an angle. 她把花茎斜着剪断。
