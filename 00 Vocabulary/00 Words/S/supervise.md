### supervise
[ˈsuːpərvaɪz]

**Description**
v. to be in charge of a person or activity and make sure that things are done correctly, safely, etc.
监督，管理，指导

**Usages**
- supervise the project 监督项目
- supervise employees 监督员工
- supervise closely 密切监督

**Examples**
1. She supervises a team of ten engineers. 她管理着一个十人工程师团队。
2. New staff must be supervised during their first month. 新员工第一个月必须有人监督。
3. He supervised the construction of the bridge. 他监督了这座桥的建造。
