### sissy
[ˈsɪsi]

**Desp**
n. a cowardly or weak person; a boy seen as unmanly. adj. cowardly, weak.
n. 胆小鬼，怯懦的人；adj. 女人气的，柔弱的

**Usages**
- stop being a sissy 别当胆小鬼

**Examples**
1. Don't call him a sissy just because he doesn't like sports. 别因为他不喜欢运动就叫他胆小鬼。
2. He thought crying was a sissy thing to do. 他认为哭是懦弱的表现。
