### subtle
[ˈsʌtl]

**Desp**
adj. Not obvious or easy to notice; delicate and understated; clever and indirect.
微妙的；不易察觉的；巧妙的

**Usages**
- a subtle difference 细微差别
- subtle humor 含蓄的幽默
- subtle changes 微妙的变化

**Examples**
1. There is a subtle difference between the two words. 这两个词之间有细微差别。
2. She made a subtle gesture to get my attention. 她做了个细微的手势来引起我的注意。
3. The flavor is subtle and delicate. 味道微妙而细腻。
