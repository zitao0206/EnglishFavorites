### specialize
[ˈspeʃəlaɪz]

**Desp**
v. to spend most of your time studying or working on a particular subject or skill.
专门研究，专攻；专门从事

**Usages**
- specialize in 专攻……
- specialize in heart surgery 专攻心脏外科
- a specialized skill 专业技能

**Examples**
1. She specializes in corporate law. 她专攻公司法。
2. The restaurant specializes in seafood. 这家餐厅专做海鲜。
3. He decided to specialize in data science. 他决定专攻数据科学。
