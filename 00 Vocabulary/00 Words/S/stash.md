### stash
[stæʃ]

**Desp**
v/n. To store something secretly in a safe place; a hidden store of something.
藏匿，私藏；藏匿物

**Usages**
- stash sth under the floor 藏在地板下
- secret stash 私藏物品
- a stash of cash 藏匿的现金

**Examples**
1. He stashed the money under the floor. 他把钱藏在地板下。
2. Police found a large stash of bribes. 警方发现了大批藏匿的贿款。
3. She kept a secret stash of chocolate in her desk. 她在抽屉里私藏了一些巧克力。
