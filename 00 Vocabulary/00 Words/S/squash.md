### squash
[skwɒʃ]

**Desp**
v./n. to press something flat; to crush; to suppress or stop something.
压扁，压碎，挤，塞；镇压，制止

**Usages**
- squash a spider with the book 用书拍死蜘蛛
- squash the rebellion 镇压叛乱
- squash into the car 挤进车里

**Examples**
1. She wants to squash a spider with the book. 她想用这本书拍死蜘蛛。
2. The government moved to squash the rumor. 政府出手制止谣言。
3. We all squashed into the tiny elevator. 我们全都挤进了狭小的电梯。
