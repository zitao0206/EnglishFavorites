### session
[ˈseʃn]

**Description**
n. a period of time spent doing a particular activity; a formal meeting; a period of court or parliament meetings.
一场，一节（活动）；会议；开庭期

**Usages**
- a training session 培训课
- a Q&A session 问答环节
- a workout session 一次锻炼

**Examples**
1. We have a training session this afternoon. 我们今天下午有培训课。
2. The conference will open with a keynote session. 会议将以主题演讲开场。
3. He recorded the practice session. 他录下了练习过程。
