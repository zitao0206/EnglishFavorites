### snore
[snɔː(r)]

**Desp**
n/v. to breathe noisily while sleeping.
打鼾，打呼噜

**Usages**
- snore loudly 打鼾很响
- a loud snore 响亮的鼾声

**Examples**
1. He often snores when he sleeps. 他睡觉时经常打鼾。
2. Her snoring kept everyone awake. 她的鼾声吵得大家睡不着。
