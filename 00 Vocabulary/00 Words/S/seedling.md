### seedling
[ˈsiːdlɪŋ]

**Desp**
n. a young plant grown from a seed.
幼苗

**Usages**
- plant seedlings 种植幼苗
- seedling stage 幼苗期

**Examples**
1. The seedlings were planted in rows. 幼苗被成行种植。
2. Strong winds damaged many seedlings. 大风损坏了许多幼苗。
3. Seedlings need regular watering. 幼苗需要定期浇水。
