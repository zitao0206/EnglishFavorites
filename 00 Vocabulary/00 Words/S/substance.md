### substance

**Desp**
n. a material with particular properties.
物质

**Usages**
- chemical substances 化学物质
- harmful substances 有害物质

**Examples**
1. Water is a common substance. 水是一种常见物质。
2. The lab tests the substance for safety. 实验室检测该物质的安全性。
3. Some substances are harmful to health. 有些物质对健康有害。
