### strive
[straɪv]

**Desp**
v. to try very hard to achieve something.
v. 努力，奋斗，力求

**Usages**
- strive for perfection 力求完美
- strive to do 努力做
- strive against adversity 与逆境抗争

**Examples**
1. We constantly strive to improve our service. 我们不断努力改进服务。
2. She strove for excellence in everything. 她事事力求卓越。
3. The team is striving to meet the deadline. 团队正努力赶上截止日期。
