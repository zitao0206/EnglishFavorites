### stakeholder
[ˈsteɪkhəʊldə(r)]

**Desp**
n. a person or group with an interest or concern in something.
利益相关者

**Usages**
- engage stakeholders 让利益相关者参与
- stakeholder engagement 利益相关者参与

**Examples**
1. …and ensure stakeholders continue to be fully engaged in the preservation of the species. ……并确保利益相关者继续充分参与该物种的保护工作。
2. Stakeholders include local communities, government and NGOs. 利益相关者包括当地社区、政府和非政府组织。
3. The project requires stakeholder support. 该项目需要利益相关者的支持。
