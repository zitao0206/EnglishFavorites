### snack
[snæk]

**Desp**
n./v. A small amount of food eaten between meals.
零食，点心；吃零食

**Usages**
- a light snack 点心，小吃
- snack on fruit 吃水果当零食
- midnight snack 宵夜

**Examples**
1. I had a quick snack before the meeting. 开会前我快速吃了点东西。
2. She snacks on nuts during work. 她工作期间吃坚果当零食。
3. Let's grab a snack at the café. 我们去咖啡馆吃点东西吧。
