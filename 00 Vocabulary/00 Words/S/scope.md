### scope
[skəʊp]

**Desp**
n. the extent or range of something.
范围，范畴，边界

**Usages**
- scope of work 工作范围
- beyond the scope of … 超出…的范围

**Examples**
1. This question is outside the scope of our discussion. 这个问题不在我们讨论的范围内。
2. We need to define the scope of work first. 我们需要先确定工作范围。
3. The project's scope keeps expanding. 项目范围不断扩大。
