### skeptical
[ˈskeptɪkl]

**Desp**
adj. having doubts about something that other people believe; not easily convinced.
怀疑的，持怀疑态度的

**Usages**
- be skeptical of/about 对……持怀疑态度
- remain skeptical 仍持怀疑
- a skeptical look 怀疑的眼神

**Examples**
1. I'm skeptical about his claims. 我对他的说法持怀疑态度。
2. Scientists remain skeptical of the results. 科学家们对这些结果仍持怀疑态度。
3. She gave me a skeptical look. 她怀疑地看了我一眼。
