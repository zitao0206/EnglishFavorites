### hesitant
[ˈhezɪtənt]

**Description**
adj. uncertain about doing something; slow to act.
adj. 犹豫的，迟疑的

**Extension**
动词：hesitate 犹豫
名词：hesitation 犹豫

**Usages**
- hesitant about doing 对做…犹豫
- a hesitant reply 迟疑的回答

**Examples**
1. She was hesitant about accepting the offer. 她对是否接受这份工作很犹豫。
2. He gave a hesitant answer at the interview. 面试时他给出了迟疑的回答。
3. Don't be hesitant to ask questions. 有问题尽管问，别犹豫。
