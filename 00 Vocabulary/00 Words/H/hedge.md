### hedge
[hedʒ]

**Desp**
n/v. a way of protecting against financial loss or risk; a row of bushes.
n/v. 对冲（风险），防范风险；手段，防范措施；树篱

**Usages**
- hedge against inflation 对冲通胀
- hedge fund 对冲基金

**Examples**
1. Gold is often used to hedge against inflation. 黄金常被用来对冲通胀。
2. The fund uses options to hedge its risks. 该基金利用期权对冲风险。
