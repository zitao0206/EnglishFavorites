### hazard
[ˈhæzəd]

**Desp**
n/v. a danger or risk; to risk something or make a guess.
危险，隐患；（v.）冒险；大胆猜测

**Usages**
- a health hazard 健康隐患
- fire hazard 火灾隐患
- at hazard 处于危险中

**Examples**
1. Smoking is a serious health hazard. 吸烟是严重的健康隐患。
2. The broken stairs are a safety hazard. 破损的楼梯是安全隐患。
3. I'd hazard a guess that prices will rise. 我斗胆猜测价格会上涨。
