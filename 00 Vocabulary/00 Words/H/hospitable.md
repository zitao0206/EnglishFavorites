### hospitable
[hɒˈspɪtəbl]

**Desp**
adj. friendly and welcoming to guests or strangers.
好客的，热情友善的

**Usages**
- hospitable hosts 好客的主人
- be hospitable to guests 对客人热情
- hospitality n. 殷勤，好客

**Examples**
1. The locals were extremely hospitable to us. 当地人对我们极为热情好客。
2. They are famous for their hospitality. 他们以好客闻名。
3. It's a hospitable town full of friendly people. 这是一座充满友善居民的宜居好客小镇。
