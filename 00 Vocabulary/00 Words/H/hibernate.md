### hibernate
[ˈhaɪbəneɪt]

**Desp**
v. to spend the winter in a sleeping state.
冬眠，蛰伏

**Usages**
- go into hibernation 进入冬眠
- hibernate in winter 冬天冬眠

**Examples**
1. Bears hibernate in winter. 熊在冬天冬眠。
2. Some animals go into hibernation to save energy. 有些动物通过冬眠来节省能量。
