### hawkish
[ˈhɔːkɪʃ]

**Desp**
adj. favoring strong or aggressive action, especially in politics or economics.
鹰派的，主张强硬的（政治）

- dovish [ˈdʌvɪʃ] adj . 鸽派的；主和的

**Usages**
- hawk n. 鹰；dove n. 鸽（鸽派）
- hawkish stance/politics 强硬立场

**Examples**
1. The new governor takes a hawkish stance on trade. 新任州长在贸易问题上采取强硬立场。
2. Hawkish policies often lead to higher interest rates. 鹰派政策往往导致利率上升。
