### heart-to-heart
[ˌhɑːrt tə ˈhɑːrt]

**Desp**
n. an honest and intimate conversation between two people; adj. frank and sincere.
n. 促膝谈心，坦诚的交谈；adj. 推心置腹的

**Extension**
同义词：heart-to-heart talk, intimate conversation

**Usages**
- have a heart-to-heart 促膝谈心
- a heart-to-heart conversation 坦诚的对话
- a heart-to-heart talk with a friend 与朋友推心置腹的谈话

**Examples**
1. We had a heart-to-heart about our relationship problems. 我们就感情问题进行了一次坦诚的交谈。
2. Sometimes a heart-to-heart talk can resolve misunderstandings. 有时一次交心的谈话能化解误会。
3. Mom sat me down for a heart-to-heart about my future. 妈妈让我坐下来，就我的前途进行了一次推心置腹的谈话。
