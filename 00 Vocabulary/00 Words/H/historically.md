### historically

**Desp**
adv. throughout history; from a historical point of view.
在历史上，从历史角度

**Usages**
- historically significant 具有历史意义的

**Examples**
1. Historically, the region was part of a larger empire. 历史上该地区曾是一个大帝国的疆域。
2. The building is historically important. 这座建筑具有重要历史意义。
3. Historically, prices rise during festivals. 从历史上看，节日期间物价会上涨。
