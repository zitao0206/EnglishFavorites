### hack
[hæk]

**Desp**
v. to secretly get into a computer system; to cut roughly. n. a person who hacks computers.
入侵（计算机系统）；乱砍；黑客

**Usages**
- hack into a system 入侵系统
- hacker n. 黑客

**Examples**
1. Hackers tried to hack into the bank's system. 黑客试图入侵银行系统。
2. He was arrested for hacking government websites. 他因入侵政府网站被捕。
