### harsh
[hɑːʃ]

**Desp**
adj. Unpleasantly rough, severe, or cruel; too strong or bright.
严厉的；刺耳的；严酷的

**Usages**
- harsh criticism 严厉的批评
- harsh conditions 严酷的环境
- harsh punishment 严厉的惩罚

**Examples**
1. He faced harsh criticism from the media. 他遭到媒体的严厉批评。
2. The soldiers endured harsh conditions in the desert. 士兵们在沙漠中忍受着严酷的环境。
3. Don't be too harsh on yourself. 别对自己太苛刻。
4. His voice was harsh and unmusical. 他的声音刺耳难听。
