### hostile
[ˈhɑːstl]

**Desp**
adj. unfriendly and aggressive; opposing something.
adj. 怀有敌意的，不友好的；强烈反对的

**Extension**
名词：hostility 敌意

**Usages**
- hostile attitude 敌对态度
- hostile environment 恶劣环境
- be hostile to 对…怀有敌意

**Examples**
1. The reporter received a hostile reception. 那名记者受到了不友好的接待。
2. Some villagers were hostile to the new project. 一些村民强烈反对新项目。
3. He grew up in a hostile environment. 他在恶劣的环境中长大。
