### humble
[ˈhʌmbl]

**Desp**
adj. modest; low in rank or importance.
谦逊的；低下的，卑微的

**Usages**
- humble attitude 谦逊的态度
- a humble background 卑微的出身

**Examples**
1. He remained humble despite his success. 尽管成功了，他依然谦逊。
2. She comes from a humble background. 她出身卑微。
3. Keep a humble attitude and keep learning. 保持谦逊的态度，不断学习。
