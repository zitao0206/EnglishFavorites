### highlight
[ˈhaɪlaɪt]

**Desp**
v. to emphasize; to mark text with a bright color; n. the best part.
v. 强调，突出；以亮色标注 n. 最精彩的部分

**Usages**
- highlight the importance of 强调…的重要性
- the highlight of the trip 旅程的亮点
- highlight key points 标出要点

**Examples**
1. The report highlights the risks of inaction. 报告强调了不作为的风险。
2. The highlight of the concert was the finale. 音乐会的高潮是终曲。
3. She highlighted the key sentences in yellow. 她用黄色标出了关键句子。
