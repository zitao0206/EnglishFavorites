### humiliate
[hjuːˈmɪlieɪt]

**Desp**
v. To make someone feel ashamed or foolish, especially in public.
羞辱，使丢脸，使蒙羞

**Usages**
- humiliate sb 羞辱某人
- be humiliated 被羞辱
- suffer humiliation 蒙受羞辱

**Examples**
1. The teacher humiliated him in front of the whole class. 老师在全班面前羞辱了他。
2. He was humiliated by his defeat. 他因失败而蒙羞。
3. She suffered public humiliation at the press conference. 她在记者会上蒙受了公开羞辱。
