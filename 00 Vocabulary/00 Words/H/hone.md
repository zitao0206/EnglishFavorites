### hone
[hoʊn]

**Desp**
v. to sharpen a blade; to develop and improve a skill over time.
磨砺，磨练（技能）；把……磨锋利

**Extension**
同义词：sharpen, polish, refine

**Usages**
- hone one's skills 磨练技能
- hone in on... 聚焦于……
- hone one's craft 打磨手艺

**Examples**
1. She honed her English skills by watching movies. 她通过看电影磨练英语水平。
2. Years of practice honed his craft. 多年的练习让他的手艺炉火纯青。
3. The team honed in on the root cause of the problem. 团队聚焦于问题的根本原因。
