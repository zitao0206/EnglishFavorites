### hypothesis
[haɪˈpɑːθəsɪs]

**Desp**
n. A supposition or proposed explanation made on the basis of limited evidence as a starting point for further investigation.
假设，假说
【复】hypotheses

**Usages**
- test a hypothesis 验证假设
- propose a hypothesis 提出假说
- the null hypothesis 零假设

**Examples**
1. The experiment was designed to test their hypothesis. 实验旨在验证他们的假设。
2. Scientists proposed a new hypothesis to explain the phenomenon. 科学家提出了一个新假说来解释这一现象。
3. Further research is needed to confirm the hypothesis. 需要进一步研究来确认这一假设。
