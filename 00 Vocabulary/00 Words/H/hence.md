### hence
[hens]

**Desp**
adv. for this reason; therefore.
因此，所以

**Usages**
- hence the name 因此得名
- hence + 名词/结论 由此
- hence we ruled out the suspect 因此我们排除了这名嫌疑人

**Examples**
1. The evidence is insufficient, hence we ruled out the suspect. 证据不足，因此我们排除了这名嫌疑人。
2. He felt deep mistrust, hence his secret stash. 他深感不信任，所以才私藏物品。
3. The town lies on the coast, hence its fishing culture. 小镇位于海边，因此形成了渔业文化。
