### hinder
[ˈhɪndər]

**Desp**
v. To create difficulties for someone or something, resulting in delay or obstruction.
阻碍，妨碍

**Usages**
- hinder sb from doing sth 阻碍某人做某事
- hinder progress 阻碍进展

**Examples**
1. Lack of funding hindered the research project. 资金不足阻碍了研究项目。
2. Heavy rain hindered the rescue efforts. 暴雨妨碍了救援工作。
3. Don't let fear hinder you from pursuing your dreams. 别让恐惧阻碍你追求梦想。
