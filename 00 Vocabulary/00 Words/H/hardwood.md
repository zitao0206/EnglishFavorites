### hardwood
[ˈhɑːdwʊd]

**Desp**
n. the wood from broad-leaved trees, typically hard and dense.
硬木

**Extension**
反义词：softwood

**Usages**
- hardwood tree 硬木树
- hardwood floor 硬木地板

**Examples**
1. Oak and elm are common hardwoods. 橡树和榆树是常见的硬木。
2. The floor is made of solid hardwood. 地板由实心硬木制成。
3. Hardwood is more durable than softwood. 硬木比软木更耐用。
