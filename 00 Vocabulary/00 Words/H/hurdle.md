### hurdle
[ˈhɜːrdl]

**Desp**
n. An obstacle or difficulty; a frame or fence in a race. v. To overcome an obstacle.
障碍，难关；跨栏

【同】obstacle [ˈɑːbstək(ə)l] n. 障碍，阻碍；障碍物

**Usages**
- overcome a hurdle 克服障碍
- clear a hurdle 跨过栏/越过障碍
- a major hurdle 主要难关

**Examples**
1. Funding remains the biggest hurdle for the project. 资金仍然是该项目最大的障碍。
2. She cleared every hurdle on her way to the championship. 她在通往冠军的路上克服了每一个障碍。
3. The team must hurdle several legal obstacles before launching. 团队在启动前必须克服几个法律障碍。
