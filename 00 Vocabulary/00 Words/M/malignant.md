### malignant
[məˈlɪɡnənt]

**Desp**
adj. harmful and likely to get worse (especially of a tumor).
恶性的，有害的

**Usages**
- malignant tumor 恶性肿瘤
- malignant cells 恶性细胞，癌细胞
- a malignant influence 恶劣的影响

**Examples**
1. The tumor's cells grow very fast. 癌细胞生长速度很快。
2. The malignant tumor had already spread. 恶性肿瘤已经扩散了。
3. Doctors caught the malignant growth early. 医生及早发现了恶性病灶。
