### mature
[məˈtjʊə(r)]

**Desp**
adj. fully developed; v. to reach an advanced stage of development.
成熟的；v. 成熟

**Extension**
反义词：immature
名词形式：maturity

**Usages**
- mature tree 成熟的树
- mature market 成熟市场

**Examples**
1. The mature elm tree stood over 30 metres tall. 那棵成熟的榆树高达30多米。
2. She is mature for her age. 她比同龄人成熟。
3. The technology has matured over the past decade. 这项技术在过去十年中已趋成熟。
