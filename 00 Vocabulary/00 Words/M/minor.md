### minor
[ˈmaɪnə(r)]

**Desp**
adj. lesser in importance, size or seriousness; under the legal age. n. a person under the legal age of adulthood.
adj. 较小的，次要的；未成年的；n. 未成年人

**Usages**
- minors 未成年人
- legal minor 法定未成年人
- a minor problem 小问题

**Examples**
1. The website is not suitable for minors. 该网站不适合未成年人。
2. It's just a minor problem, don't worry. 这只是个小问题，别担心。
3. She suffered only minor injuries in the accident. 她在事故中只受了轻伤。
