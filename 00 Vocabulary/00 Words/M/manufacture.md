### manufacture
[ˌmænjuˈfæktʃə(r)]

**Desp**
v. to make goods in large quantities using machines.
制造，生产

**Usages**
- manufactured-product 工业产品，制品
- manufacture daily goods 生产日用品
- manufacture cars 制造汽车

**Examples**
1. The company manufactures daily goods. 这家公司生产日用品。
2. These parts are manufactured in Germany. 这些零件产自德国。
3. The factory manufactures over a thousand cars a month. 这家工厂每月制造一千多辆汽车。
