### marginal
[ˈmɑːrdʒɪnl]

**Desp**
adj. small and not important; at or near the edge.
adj. 微小的，不重要的；边缘的

**Extension**
名词：margin 边缘，利润
动词：marginalize 边缘化

**Usages**
- marginal improvement 微小的改进
- marginal profits 微薄利润
- marginal cost 边际成本

**Examples**
1. The reform had only a marginal effect on growth. 改革对增长的推动微乎其微。
2. The difference between the two products is marginal. 两种产品差别很小。
3. They live on the marginal fringes of society. 他们生活在社会的边缘地带。
