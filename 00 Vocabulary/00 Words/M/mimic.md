### mimic
[ˈmɪmɪk]

**Desp**
vt. to copy the way someone speaks or acts; to imitate exactly.
模仿，摹拟

【近】imitate [ˈɪmɪteɪt] v. 模仿，仿效；仿造，伪造

**Usages**
- mimic sb's voice 模仿某人的声音
- mimic the behavior of... 模仿……的行为
- mimic nature 仿生，模仿自然

**Examples**
1. He can mimic the president's voice perfectly. 他能惟妙惟肖地模仿总统的声音。
2. Parrots mimic human speech. 鹦鹉会模仿人类说话。
3. The robot mimics the surgeon's hand movements. 机器人摹拟外科医生的手部动作。
