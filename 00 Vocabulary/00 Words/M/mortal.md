### mortal
[ˈmɔːtl]

**Desp**
adj. causing death; n. a human being subject to death.
致命的；n. 凡人

**Extension**
名词形式：mortality [mɔːrˈtæləti]（死亡率）

**Usages**
- adult mortality 成体死亡率
- mortal danger 致命危险

**Examples**
1. Rats were found to be a major predator of kakapo chicks and an insufficient number of chicks survived to offset adult mortality. 老鼠被发现是鸮鹦鹉雏鸟的主要捕食者，存活的雏鸟数量不足以抵消成体的死亡率。
2. All mortals must die. 凡人皆有死。
3. He received a mortal wound in battle. 他在战斗中受了致命伤。
