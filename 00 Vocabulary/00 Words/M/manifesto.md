### manifesto
[ˌmænɪˈfestəʊ]

**Desp**
n. A public statement explaining the aims, beliefs, or plans of a person or group.
宣言，声明

**Usages**
- a political manifesto 政治宣言
- issue a manifesto 发表宣言
- the party's manifesto 政党的纲领

**Examples**
1. The party published its election manifesto. 该党发布了竞选宣言。
2. The Communist Manifesto was written in 1848. 《共产党宣言》写于1848年。
3. Their manifesto calls for radical reform. 他们的宣言呼吁进行彻底改革。
