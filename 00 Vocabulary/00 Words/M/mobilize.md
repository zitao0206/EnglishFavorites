### mobilize
[ˈmoʊbəlaɪz]

**Desp**
v. to organize people or resources for action.
v. 动员，调动

**Extension**
名词：mobilization 动员

**Usages**
- mobilize support 动员支持
- mobilize resources 调动资源
- mobilize public opinion 动员舆论

**Examples**
1. The charity mobilized thousands of volunteers. 慈善机构动员了数千名志愿者。
2. The government mobilized troops for the rescue. 政府调动军队参加救援。
3. Social media helped mobilize public support. 社交媒体帮助动员了公众支持。
