### monopolize
[məˈnɑːpəlaɪz]

**Description**
v. to have complete control of a market or thing.
v. 垄断；独占

**Extension**
名词：monopoly 垄断

**Usages**
- monopolize the market 垄断市场
- monopolize the conversation 垄断话语/滔滔不绝

**Examples**
1. The company monopolized the software market for years. 这家公司垄断软件市场多年。
2. He monopolized the conversation all evening. 他整晚都垄断着谈话。
3. Laws prevent firms from monopolizing key industries. 法律防止企业垄断关键行业。
