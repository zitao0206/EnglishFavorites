### monologue
[ˈmɑːnəlɔːɡ]

**Desp**
n. a long speech by one speaker, especially in a play, film, or conversation.
独白，长篇独白（mono- 单一 + -logue 话语）

**Usages**
- deliver a monologue 发表独白
- an internal monologue 内心独白
- a dramatic monologue 戏剧独白

**Examples**
1. The actor's opening monologue moved the audience to tears. 演员的开场独白让观众感动落泪。
2. She went into a long monologue about her vacation plans. 她开始滔滔不绝地长篇大论她的度假计划。
3. Hamlet's famous monologue begins with "To be or not to be". 哈姆雷特著名的独白以"生存还是毁灭"开头。
