### monetary
[ˈmʌnɪteri]

**Description**
adj. relating to money or currency.
adj. 货币的，金融的

**Extension**
名词：money 货币

**Usages**
- monetary policy 货币政策
- monetary system 货币体系
- monetary value 货币价值

**Examples**
1. The central bank adjusted its monetary policy. 央行调整了货币政策。
2. The gift has little monetary value but great sentimental worth. 这件礼物货币价值不大但情感意义深厚。
3. They discussed reform of the international monetary system. 他们讨论了国际货币体系改革。
