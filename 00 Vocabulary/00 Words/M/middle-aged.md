### middle-aged
[ˌmɪdl ˈeɪdʒd]

**Desp**
adj. No longer young but not yet old, typically between about 40 and 60.
中年的，中老年的

**Usages**
- a middle-aged man 一位中年男子
- middle-aged spread 中年发福
- middle-aged couple 中年夫妇

**Examples**
1. A middle-aged man sat alone in the corner. 一位中年男子独自坐在角落里。
2. Many middle-aged people worry about their health. 许多中年人担心自己的健康。
3. The suspect was described as middle-aged and tall. 据描述，嫌疑人是中年人，身材高大。
