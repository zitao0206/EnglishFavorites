### maturity
[məˈtjʊərəti]

**Desp**
n. The state of being fully developed physically or mentally; the quality of behaving like an adult.
成熟

**Usages**
- emotional maturity 心智成熟
- reach maturity 达到成熟
- with maturity 随着成熟

**Examples**
1. Emotional maturity matters more than age in a relationship. 感情中，心智成熟比年龄更重要。
2. The plan needs time to reach maturity. 这个计划需要时间才能成熟。
3. She handles problems with great maturity. 她处理问题非常成熟。
