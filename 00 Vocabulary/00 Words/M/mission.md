### mission
[ˈmɪʃn]

**Desp**
n. An important job or task, especially one involving travel; the purpose of an organization.
任务，使命；代表团；天职

**Usages**
- a mission statement 使命宣言
- accomplish a mission 完成使命
- a peacekeeping mission 维和任务

**Examples**
1. The astronauts completed their mission successfully. 宇航员们成功完成了任务。
2. Our mission is to make quality education accessible to all. 我们的使命是让优质教育惠及所有人。
3. She was sent on a diplomatic mission to Europe. 她被派往欧洲执行外交任务。
