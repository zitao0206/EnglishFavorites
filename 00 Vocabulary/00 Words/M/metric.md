### metric
[ˈmetrɪk]

**Desp**
n. a standard of measurement; a measure used in business, tech or finance.
衡量标准，维度（商业、科技、金融）

**Usages**
- benchmark metric 基准指标
- key metric 核心指标

**Examples**
1. Customer satisfaction is a key metric for the company. 客户满意度是公司的核心指标。
2. The team tracks several metrics to measure performance. 团队跟踪多项指标来衡量绩效。
