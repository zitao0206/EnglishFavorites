### mean

**Desp**
adj. unkind or cruel.
刻薄的

**Usages**
- a mean remark 刻薄的言论
- be mean to sb 对某人刻薄

**Examples**
1. Don't be mean to your little sister. 别对你妹妹刻薄。
2. That was a mean thing to say. 说那种话太刻薄了。
3. He can be mean when he's angry. 他生气时可能会很刻薄。
