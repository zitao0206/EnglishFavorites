### moody
[ˈmuːdi]

**Description**
adj. having moods that change quickly and often; bad-tempered, sulky.
情绪不好的，喜怒无常的

**Usages**
- a moody person 喜怒无常的人
- moody and unstable 情绪不稳

**Examples**
1. Her mother was moody and unstable. 她母亲喜怒无常，情绪不稳定。
2. He gets moody when he is tired. 他累了就闹情绪。
3. Don't be so moody—cheer up! 别这么情绪化，高兴点！
