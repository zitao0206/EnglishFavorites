### mold
[moʊld]

**Description**
v. to shape or form something; n. a hollow container used to shape material; a distinctive type.
v. 塑造，影响；n. 模具；类型，气质

**Extension**
同义词：shape, form
形容词：molded
（英式拼写：mould）

**Usages**
- mold a person's character 塑造一个人的品格
- break the mold 打破常规
- a mold for casting 铸造模具

**Examples**
1. Parents play a key role in molding their children's values. 父母在塑造孩子价值观方面起关键作用。
2. She broke the mold by becoming the first female CEO. 她成为首位女CEO，打破了常规。
3. The clay is pressed into a mold to form the shape. 黏土被压入模具成型。
