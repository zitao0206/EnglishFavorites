### mastermind
[ˈmɑːstəmaɪnd]

**Description**
n. a person who plans and organizes a complex scheme, especially a criminal one.
策划者，主谋，智囊

**Usages**
- the mastermind behind sth 幕后主谋
- the mastermind of the frame-up 栽赃案的幕后主谋
- a criminal mastermind 犯罪主谋

**Examples**
1. He was the mastermind of the frame-up. 他是栽赃案的幕后主谋。
2. Police finally identified the mastermind behind the robbery. 警方最终确认了抢劫案的幕后主谋。
3. She is the mastermind of the company's success. 她是公司成功背后的智囊。
