### messy
[ˈmesi]

**Desp**
adj. untidy or dirty; complicated or difficult to deal with.
脏乱的，凌乱的；棘手的

**Usages**
- in a mess 一团糟
- messy room 凌乱的房间
- a messy divorce 棘手的离婚

**Examples**
1. His room is always messy. 他的房间总是乱糟糟的。
2. The project got into a mess. 项目陷入一团糟。
3. The negotiations were long and messy. 谈判漫长而棘手。
