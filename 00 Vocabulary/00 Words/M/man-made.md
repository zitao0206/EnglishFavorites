### man-made
[ˈmænmeɪd]

**Desp**
adj. Made by people rather than occurring naturally.
人造的，人工的

**Usages**
- man-made island 人造岛
- man-made fiber 人造纤维
- man-made lake 人工湖

**Examples**
1. The city built a man-made island. 这座城市建了一座人工岛。
2. Man-made fibers are cheaper than wool. 人造纤维比羊毛便宜。
3. It's a man-made lake, not a natural one. 这是人工湖，不是天然的。
