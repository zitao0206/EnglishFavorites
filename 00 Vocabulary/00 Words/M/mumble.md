### mumble
[ˈmʌmbl]

**Description**
v. To speak quietly and unclearly.
咕哝，含糊地说话

**Usages**
- mumble an apology 含糊地道了歉
- mumble to oneself 自言自语

**Examples**
1. He mumbled something under his breath. 他低声咕哝了几句。
2. Don't mumble—speak clearly. 别含含糊糊的，说清楚。
3. She mumbled an apology and left. 她含糊地道了歉就走了。
