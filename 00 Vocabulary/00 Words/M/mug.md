### mug
[mʌɡ]

**Desp**
v. To attack and rob someone in a public place.
行凶抢劫

**Usages**
- be mugged 遭到抢劫
- mug sb in the street 在街上抢劫某人
- mug-up for an exam 考前临时抱佛脚

**Examples**
1. He was mugged at night. 他夜里遭到抢劫。
2. She was mugged on her way home. 她在回家路上被抢劫了。
3. The gang mugged tourists near the station. 这伙人在车站附近抢劫游客。
