### mistrust
[mɪsˈtrʌst]

**Description**
v/n. to lack trust or confidence in someone; a feeling of not trusting.
不信任，怀疑

**Usages**
- mistrusting guy 多疑的人
- deep mistrust 深深的不信任
- mistrust sb's motives 怀疑某人的动机

**Examples**
1. He felt deep mistrust of his partners. 他对合作伙伴深感不信任。
2. Her mistrust made cooperation difficult. 她的多疑让合作变得困难。
3. The public's mistrust of the media is growing. 公众对媒体的不信任与日俱增。
