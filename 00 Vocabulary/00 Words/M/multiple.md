### multiple
[ˈmʌltɪpl]

**Desp**
adj. Involving many things, people, events, etc.; several.
多个的，多样的

**Usages**
- multiple choices 多种选择
- multiple tasks 多项任务
- multiple times 多次

**Examples**
1. She has won multiple awards. 她多次获奖。
2. The system can handle multiple users at once. 该系统可以同时处理多个用户。
3. He made multiple attempts to pass the exam. 他多次尝试通过考试。
