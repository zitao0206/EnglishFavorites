### acknowledge
[əkˈnɒlɪdʒ]

**Desp**
v. To accept or admit that something is true; to thank someone for something they have done.
承认；致谢；确认收到

**Usages**
- acknowledge a mistake 承认错误
- acknowledge receipt 确认收到
- be widely acknowledged as 被公认为

**Examples**
1. He acknowledged that he had made a mistake. 他承认自己犯了错误。
2. She is widely acknowledged as a leader in her field. 她被公认为所在领域的领军人物。
3. Please acknowledge receipt of this email. 请确认收到这封邮件。
