### antisemitism
[ˌæntiˈsemɪtɪzəm]

**Desp**
n. hostility toward Jewish people.
反犹太主义，排犹主义

**Usages**
- fight against antisemitism 反对反犹太主义

**Examples**
1. Antisemitism has caused enormous suffering in history. 反犹太主义在历史上造成了巨大苦难。
2. We must speak out against antisemitism. 我们必须公开反对反犹太主义。
