### anticipate
[ænˈtɪsɪpeɪt]

**Desp**
v. to expect something and prepare for it.
预期，预料，预计

**Usages**
- anticipate a problem 预见问题
- as anticipated 如所预料

**Examples**
1. We anticipate a rise in demand next quarter. 我们预计下季度需求会上升。
2. The results were better than anticipated. 结果比预期的要好。
