### appropriate
[əˈproʊpriət]

**Desp**
adj. suitable or right for a particular situation.
合适的，恰当的

**Usages**
- be appropriate for 适合……
- appropriate action 恰当的行动
- It is appropriate to do 做……是合适的

**Examples**
1. Wear appropriate clothes for the interview. 面试要穿合适的衣服。
2. The school took appropriate action. 学校采取了恰当的行动。
3. It is not appropriate to laugh at others. 嘲笑别人是不合适的。
