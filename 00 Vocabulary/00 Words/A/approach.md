### approach
[əˈproʊtʃ]

**Desp**
n. A way of dealing with something; v. to move closer to someone or something; to deal with a problem.
方法，途径；接近；处理

**Usages**
- a new approach 新方法
- approach a problem 处理问题
- an approach to learning 学习方法

**Examples**
1. We need a different approach to this problem. 我们需要用不同的方法处理这个问题。
2. The plane approached the runway. 飞机接近跑道。
3. He approached the manager about a raise. 他就加薪问题找了经理。
