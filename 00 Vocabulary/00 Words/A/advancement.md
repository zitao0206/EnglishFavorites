### advancement
[ədˈvɑːnsmənt]

**Desp**
n. progress; development; promotion to a higher position.
进步，进展，技术革新；晋升

**Usages**
- technological advancement 技术进步
- career advancement 职业晋升

**Examples**
1. The advancement of AI has changed many industries. 人工智能的进步改变了许多行业。
2. She is eager for career advancement. 她渴望职业晋升。
