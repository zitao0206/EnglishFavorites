### awkward
[ˈɔːkwəd]

**Desp**
adj. causing embarrassment; difficult to handle.
令人尴尬的，为难的；笨拙的

**Usages**
- awkward silence 尴尬的沉默
- an awkward question 棘手的问题
- feel awkward 感到尴尬

**Examples**
1. There was an awkward silence after his joke. 他的笑话之后是一阵尴尬的沉默。
2. She felt awkward at the party. 她在聚会上感到局促不安。
