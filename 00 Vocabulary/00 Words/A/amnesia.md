### amnesia
[æmˈniːziə]

**Desp**
n. loss of memory.
健忘症，记忆缺失

**Usages**
- temporary amnesia 暂时性失忆
- suffer from amnesia 患失忆症

**Examples**
1. After the accident, he suffered from total amnesia. 事故后他完全失忆了。
2. The patient's amnesia made it hard to recognize his family. 失忆让病人认不出家人。
