### astronaut
[ˈæstrənɔːt]

**Desp**
n. a person who is trained to travel and work in a spacecraft.
宇航员，航天员

**Extension**
同义词：cosmonaut（俄）、taikonaut（中国航天员专用）
词根：astro-（星，太空）+ naut（航行者）

**Usages**
- a trained astronaut 受训宇航员
- astronaut training 宇航员训练
- become an astronaut 成为宇航员

**Examples**
1. She dreamed of becoming an astronaut since childhood. 她从小就梦想成为宇航员。
2. The astronauts spent six months on the space station. 宇航员们在空间站度过了六个月。
3. China's taikonauts have walked in space. 中国航天员已经进行过太空行走。
