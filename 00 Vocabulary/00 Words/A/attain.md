### attain
[əˈteɪn]

**Description**
v. to reach or achieve a goal or level.
v. 达到，获得，实现

**Extension**
名词：attainment 成就，造诣

**Usages**
- attain a goal 达成目标
- attain the highest level 达到最高水平
- attain success 获得成功

**Examples**
1. She attained the rank of professor at thirty-five. 她35岁就当上了教授。
2. The country attained independence in 1960. 该国于1960年获得独立。
3. Few runners attain that level of endurance. 很少有跑者能达到那种耐力水平。
