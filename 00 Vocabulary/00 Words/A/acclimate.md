### acclimate
[ˈækləmeɪt]

**Desp**
v. To become accustomed to a new climate or environment; to adapt.
适应（新环境、新气候）；使服水土

**Usages**
- acclimate to sth 适应某事
- acclimate oneself to 使自己适应

**Examples**
1. It took her several weeks to acclimate to the high altitude. 她花了几周时间才适应高海拔。
2. New employees need time to acclimate to the company culture. 新员工需要时间适应公司文化。
3. The plants were gradually acclimated to colder temperatures. 这些植物被逐渐适应了更低的温度。
