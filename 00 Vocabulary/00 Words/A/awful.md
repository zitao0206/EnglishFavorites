### awful
[ˈɔːfl]

**Desp**
adj. very bad or unpleasant; (used for emphasis) very great.
糟糕的，可怕的；极度的

**Usages**
- awful weather 糟糕的天气
- an awful experience 可怕的经历
- feel awful 感觉糟透了

**Examples**
1. The weather was awful all week. 整个星期天气都很糟糕。
2. I feel awful about what happened. 我对所发生的事感到非常难过。
3. That was an awful movie. 那部电影糟透了。
