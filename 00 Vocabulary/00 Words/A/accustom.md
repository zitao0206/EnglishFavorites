### accustom
[əˈkʌstəm]

**Description**
v. to make someone used to something.
v. 使习惯于

**Extension**
形容词：accustomed 习惯的

**Usages**
- accustom oneself to 使自己习惯于
- be accustomed to 习惯于
- get accustomed to 逐渐习惯

**Examples**
1. It took weeks to accustom myself to the night shift. 我花了几周才习惯夜班。
2. She is accustomed to working under pressure. 她习惯了在压力下工作。
3. You must accustom your ears to the local accent. 你必须让自己的耳朵习惯当地口音。
