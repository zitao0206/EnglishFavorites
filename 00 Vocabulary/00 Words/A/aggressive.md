### aggressive
[əˈɡresɪv]

**Desp**
adj. Forceful and ready to attack; very determined to succeed; (of a disease) developing quickly.
好斗的，挑衅的；积极进取的；攻势的

**Usages**
- aggressive behavior 攻击性行为
- an aggressive marketing strategy 激进的营销策略
- aggressive driving 危险驾驶

**Examples**
1. The dog became aggressive when strangers came near. 陌生人靠近时狗变得有攻击性。
2. The company is pursuing an aggressive growth strategy. 公司正推行激进的增长战略。
3. His aggressive style upset his teammates. 他咄咄逼人的风格让队友不满。
