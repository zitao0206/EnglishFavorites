### autonomy
[ɔːˈtɒnəmi]

**Desp**
n. The right or ability of a group or person to govern itself or make their own decisions.
自治，自治权；自主，自主权

**Usages**
- regional autonomy 区域自治
- grant autonomy 给予自主权
- a high degree of autonomy 高度自治

**Examples**
1. The region was granted autonomy from the central government. 该地区获得了中央政府的自治权。
2. Teachers need more autonomy in the classroom. 教师需要在课堂上有更多自主权。
3. The company gives its branches considerable autonomy. 公司给予分支机构相当大的自主权。
