### assess
[əˈses]

**Desp**
v. to judge or evaluate the value, quality or importance of something (= evaluate).
评估，评定

**Usages**
- assess the situation 评估形势
- assess the risk 评估风险

**Examples**
1. Teachers assess students' progress every term. 老师每学期评估学生的进步。
2. We need to assess the risks before investing. 投资前我们需要评估风险。
