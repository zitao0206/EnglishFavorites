### affiliate
[əˈfɪlieɪt]

**Description**
v. to attach to a larger organization; n. a related organization.
v. 使隶属于；n. 附属机构，分公司

**Extension**
名词：affiliation 附属关系

**Usages**
- be affiliated with 隶属于
- an affiliate company 附属公司
- network affiliates 联播台

**Examples**
1. The hospital is affiliated with the university. 这家医院隶属于该大学。
2. The channel is an NBC affiliate. 这个频道是NBC的联播台。
3. The research center affiliates with several labs. 该研究中心与多个实验室有隶属关系。
