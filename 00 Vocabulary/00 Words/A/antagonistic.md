### antagonistic
[ænˌtæɡəˈnɪstɪk]

**Desp**
adj. showing opposition or hostility toward someone or something.
敌对的，对立的，怀有敌意的

**Usages**
- be antagonistic to/towards 对…怀有敌意

**Examples**
1. The two countries have long been antagonistic to each other. 两国长期以来互相对立。
2. He became antagonistic when criticized. 被批评时他变得充满敌意。
