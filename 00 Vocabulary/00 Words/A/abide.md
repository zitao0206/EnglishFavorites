### abide
[əˈbaɪd]

**Description**
v. to accept and act according to a rule or decision; to tolerate something.
遵守；忍受，容忍

**Usages**
- abide by the rules 遵守规则
- abide by the law 遵守法律
- can't abide 无法忍受

**Examples**
1. All members must abide by the club's rules. 所有成员都必须遵守俱乐部的规则。
2. If you join the competition, you must abide by its rules. 如果你参加比赛，就必须遵守比赛规则。
3. I can't abide such rudeness. 我无法忍受这样的无礼。
