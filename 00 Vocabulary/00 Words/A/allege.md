### allege
[əˈledʒ]

**Desp**
v. to state something as a fact without proof.
声称，指控（无证据地断言）

**Usages**
- alleged adj. 据称的，被指控的
- allegedly adv. 据说
- alleged practice 被指控的行为

**Examples**
1. The report alleges that the company hid the risks. 该报告指控该公司隐瞒了风险。
2. He is alleged to have cheated in the exam. 据称他在考试中作弊。
3. The alleged practice has drawn widespread criticism. 被指控的行为引起了广泛批评。
