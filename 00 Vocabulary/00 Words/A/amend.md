### amend
[əˈmend]

**Description**
v. to change a law or document to improve it.
v. 修改，修订（法律、文件）

**Extension**
名词：amendment 修正案

**Usages**
- amend the constitution 修改宪法
- amend a contract 修改合同
- amend one's ways 改过自新

**Examples**
1. The law was amended to cover online sales. 该法律经修订后涵盖线上销售。
2. They agreed to amend the contract terms. 他们同意修改合同条款。
3. Parliament passed several amendments to the bill. 议会通过了对该法案的多项修正。
