### ambassador
[æmˈbæsədə(r)]

**Desp**
n. a senior official sent to represent a country abroad; a person who represents something.
大使，使者

**Usages**
- brand ambassador 品牌大使

**Examples**
1. She served as ambassador to France. 她曾任驻法国大使。
2. The singer became a brand ambassador for the company. 这位歌手成为了该公司的品牌大使。
