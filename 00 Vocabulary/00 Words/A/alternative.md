### alternative
[ɔːlˈtɜːrnətɪv]

**Desp**
n. another possible choice; adj. available as another option.
n. 可供选择的事物，替代方案；adj. 替代的

**Usages**
- an alternative to ...的替代品
- alternative energy 替代能源
- have no alternative 别无选择

**Examples**
1. We need to find an alternative solution. 我们需要找到替代方案。
2. Solar power is an alternative source of energy. 太阳能是一种替代能源。
3. You have no alternative but to comply. 你除了服从别无选择。
