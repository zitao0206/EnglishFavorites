### assimilate
[əˈsɪməleɪt]

**Desp**
v. to become part of a group or culture; to absorb and understand information.
融入，同化；吸收

**Usages**
- assimilate into local culture 融入当地文化
- assimilate new knowledge 吸收新知识
- be assimilated by 被……同化

**Examples**
1. Immigrants gradually assimilate into local culture. 移民慢慢融入当地文化。
2. It takes time to assimilate all this new information. 消化这些新信息需要时间。
3. The conquered tribe was assimilated into the empire. 被征服的部族被帝国同化了。
