### all-time
[ˈɔːl taɪm]

**Desp**
adj. the best, worst, highest or lowest that has ever been; of all time.
空前的，有史以来的

**Usages**
- all-time high 历史最高
- all-time record 历史纪录

**Examples**
1. The stock hit an all-time high yesterday. 该股票昨天创下历史新高。
2. This is the all-time best-selling album of the decade. 这是这十年来有史以来最畅销的专辑。
