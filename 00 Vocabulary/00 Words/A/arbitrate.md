### arbitrate
[ˈɑːbɪtreɪt]

**Desp**
v. To officially settle an argument or dispute between two people or groups.
仲裁，公断；调停

**Usages**
- arbitrate a dispute 仲裁争端
- arbitrate between two parties 在双方之间调停
- arbitrate in a case 在案件中担任仲裁

**Examples**
1. An independent committee was set up to arbitrate the dispute. 成立了一个独立委员会来仲裁这一争端。
2. The UN tried to arbitrate between the two countries. 联合国试图在两国之间进行调停。
3. They agreed to arbitrate the matter rather than go to court. 他们同意通过仲裁解决此事，而不是诉诸法庭。
