### aesthetic
[esˈθetɪk]

**Desp**
adj. Relating to beauty and art; n. a set of principles about beauty.
美学的，审美的；美感

**Usages**
- aesthetic value 审美价值
- aesthetic appeal 美感
- an aesthetic sense 审美感

**Examples**
1. The building has great aesthetic value. 这座建筑有很高的审美价值。
2. The design balances function and aesthetics. 这个设计平衡了功能与美感。
3. She has a keen aesthetic sense. 她有敏锐的审美感。
