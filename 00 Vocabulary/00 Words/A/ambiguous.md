### ambiguous
[æmˈbɪɡjuəs]

**Description**
adj. having more than one possible meaning; unclear.
adj. 模棱两可的，有歧义的

**Extension**
名词：ambiguity 歧义
反义词：unambiguous 明确的

**Usages**
- an ambiguous answer 模棱两可的回答
- ambiguous wording 含糊的措辞

**Examples**
1. His reply was deliberately ambiguous. 他的回答故意含糊其辞。
2. The contract's wording is ambiguous. 合同的措辞有歧义。
3. She gave an ambiguous smile. 她露出了一个让人捉摸不透的微笑。
