### atypical
[ˌeɪˈtɪpɪkl]

**Desp**
adj. Not typical; unusual or unrepresentative.
非典型的，不具代表性的

**Usages**
- atypical symptoms 非典型症状
- an atypical case 非典型病例
- 反义：typical

**Examples**
1. The patient showed atypical symptoms. 患者表现出非典型症状。
2. This is an atypical example, so don't draw conclusions. 这是个不具代表性的例子，别下结论。
