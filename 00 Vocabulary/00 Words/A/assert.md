### assert
[əˈsɜːrt]

**Description**
v. to state firmly and confidently; to insist on.
v. 断言，坚称；维护，坚持

**Extension**
名词：assertion 断言

**Usages**
- assert oneself 坚持己见，显示权威
- assert one's rights 维护自己的权利
- assert that ... 断言…

**Examples**
1. He asserted that the data was accurate. 他坚称数据是准确的。
2. She learned to assert herself in meetings. 她学会了在会议上坚持己见。
3. Employees should assert their legal rights. 员工应维护自己的合法权利。
