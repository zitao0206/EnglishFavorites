### adequate
[ˈædɪkwət]

**Description**
adj. enough for a purpose; satisfactory but not great.
adj. 足够的，充分的；尚可的

**Extension**
反义词：inadequate 不足的
名词：adequacy 充分性

**Usages**
- adequate resources 充足的资源
- adequate preparation 充分的准备

**Examples**
1. Make sure you get adequate sleep. 确保你得到充足的睡眠。
2. The room was adequate but not luxurious. 房间还算可以但不算豪华。
3. The company provides adequate training for new staff. 公司为新员工提供充分的培训。
