### artisan
[ˌɑːtɪˈzæn]

**Desp**
n. a skilled worker who makes things by hand (= craftsman).
手工艺人，工匠

**Usages**
- artisan bread 手工面包
- artisan goods 手工制品

**Examples**
1. The market is full of artisan goods made by local craftsmen. 市场上摆满了当地工匠制作的手工艺品。
2. He is an artisan who specializes in leatherwork. 他是一位专攻皮具的工匠。
