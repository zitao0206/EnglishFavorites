### align
[əˈlaɪn]

**Desp**
v. to put things in a straight line; to agree with or support someone or something.
使排成直线；使一致；与……结盟/保持一致

**Extension**
反义词：misalign（使错位、使不一致）
同义词：agree, match, coordinate
其他词形：alignment n. 对齐；结盟；aligned adj. 对齐的；一致的

**Usages**
- align with 与……一致/结盟
- align the text 对齐文本
- be perfectly aligned 完全一致
- align one's interests 使利益一致

**Examples**
1. Your relationship will be happier when your hobbies align. 当你们的爱好相契合时，你们的关系会更融洽。
2. Our interests are perfectly aligned. 我们的利益完全一致。
3. She aligned herself with the reform group. 她与改革派站在了一起。
4. Align the two edges before gluing them. 粘合前先把两条边对齐。
