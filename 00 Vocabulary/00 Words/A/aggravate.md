### aggravate
[ˈæɡrəveɪt]

**Description**
v. to make a problem worse; to annoy someone.
v. 使恶化，加重；激怒

**Extension**
名词：aggravation 恶化，恼怒

**Usages**
- aggravate the situation 使局势恶化
- aggravate an injury 加重伤情
- aggravate sb 惹恼某人

**Examples**
1. Stress can aggravate the condition. 压力会加重病情。
2. His remarks only aggravated the situation. 他的话只会让局面更糟。
3. It aggravates me when people cut in line. 有人插队时我会很恼火。
