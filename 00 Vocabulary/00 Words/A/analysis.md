### analysis
[əˈnæləsɪs]

**Desp**
n. The process of examining something carefully in order to understand it.
分析；分析报告

**Usages**
- a detailed analysis 详细的分析
- data analysis 数据分析
- in the final analysis 归根结底

**Examples**
1. The analysis of the data took several weeks. 数据分析花了好几周时间。
2. We need a thorough analysis of the market. 我们需要对市场进行彻底分析。
3. In the final analysis, it was a success. 归根结底，这是一次成功。
