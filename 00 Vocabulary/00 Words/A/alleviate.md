### alleviate
[əˈliːvieɪt]

**Desp**
v. to make pain or problems less severe.
v. 减轻，缓和

**Extension**
名词：alleviation 减轻

**Usages**
- alleviate pain 减轻疼痛
- alleviate poverty 缓解贫困
- alleviate the burden 减轻负担

**Examples**
1. The medicine helped alleviate the pain. 这种药帮助减轻了疼痛。
2. New policies aim to alleviate poverty in rural areas. 新政策旨在缓解农村贫困。
3. Jokes alleviated the tension in the room. 笑话缓和了屋里的紧张气氛。
