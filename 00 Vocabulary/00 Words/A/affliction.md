### affliction
[əˈflɪkʃn]

**Desp**
n. Pain, suffering, or illness; something that causes this.
苦难，苦恼，病痛

**Usages**
- a painful affliction 痛苦的疾病
- the afflictions of life 人生苦难
- suffer an affliction 遭受折磨

**Examples**
1. He suffered from a painful affliction for years. 他多年遭受病痛折磨。
2. She endured the afflictions of life with courage. 她以勇气承受人生的苦难。
