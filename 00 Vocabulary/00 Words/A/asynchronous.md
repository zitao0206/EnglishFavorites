### asynchronous
[eɪˈsɪŋkrənəs]

**Desp**
adj. not existing or occurring at the same time; (of communication) not requiring real-time interaction.
异步的，非同步的

**Usages**
- asynchronous communication 异步通信
- asynchronous learning 异步学习
- asynchronous work 异步工作

**Examples**
1. Email is an asynchronous form of communication. 电子邮件是一种异步通信方式。
2. Asynchronous learning allows students to study at their own pace. 异步学习允许学生按自己的节奏学习。
3. The team adopted asynchronous work to accommodate different time zones. 团队采用异步工作以适应不同时区。
