### accurate
[ˈækjərət]

**Description**
adj. correct and exact; free from error.
adj. 准确的，精确的

**Extension**
反义词：inaccurate 不准确的
名词：accuracy 准确性

**Usages**
- accurate information 准确的信息
- an accurate measurement 精确的测量

**Examples**
1. Make sure your figures are accurate. 确保你的数据准确无误。
2. The forecast is accurate to within two degrees. 天气预报误差在两度以内。
3. Her translation is accurate and natural. 她的翻译准确而自然。
