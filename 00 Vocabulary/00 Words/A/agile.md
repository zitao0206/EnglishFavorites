### agile
[ˈædʒl]

**Desp**
adj. able to move quickly and easily; able to think and react quickly; (of methods) flexible.
敏捷的，灵活的；反应快的

**Usages**
- an agile mind 敏捷的头脑
- agile development 敏捷开发
- agile movements 敏捷的动作

**Examples**
1. The agile cat jumped onto the roof. 敏捷的猫跳上了屋顶。
2. She has an agile mind for puzzles. 她头脑敏捷，擅长解谜。
3. Our team uses agile methods. 我们团队采用敏捷方法。
