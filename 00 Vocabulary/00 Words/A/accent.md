### accent
[ˈæksent]

**Desp**
n/v. the way a person pronounces words, showing where they are from; extra force given to a syllable.
口音，腔调；重音；强调

**Usages**
- a foreign accent 外国口音
- speak with an accent 说话带口音
- put the accent on 强调……

**Examples**
1. She speaks English with a slight French accent. 她说英语带一点法语口音。
2. The accent falls on the first syllable. 重音落在第一个音节上。
3. The company puts the accent on innovation. 这家公司把重点放在创新上。
