### ancestor
[ˈænsestə(r)]

**Desp**
n. A person from whom one is descended; an early form of something.
祖先，老祖；雏形

**Usages**
- worship ancestor 祭祖
- the ancestor of modern machines 现代机械的雏形
- a common ancestor 共同祖先

**Examples**
1. This tool is the ancestor of modern machines. 这件工具是现代机械的雏形。
2. They return home every spring to worship their ancestors. 他们每年春天回乡祭祖。
3. Humans and apes share a common ancestor. 人类和猿类有共同的祖先。
