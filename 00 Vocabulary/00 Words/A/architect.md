### architect
[ˈɑːkɪtekt]

**Desp**
n. A person who designs buildings; the designer or creator of an idea or plan.
建筑师，设计师；缔造者

**Usages**
- a famous architect 著名建筑师
- the architect of reform 改革的设计者
- landscape architect 景观设计师

**Examples**
1. The building was designed by a famous architect. 这座建筑出自一位著名建筑师之手。
2. He is regarded as the architect of the company's success. 他被视为公司成功的缔造者。
