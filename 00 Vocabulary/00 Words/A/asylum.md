### asylum
[əˈsaɪləm]

**Desp**
n. protection given by a country to refugees.
庇护，避难

**Usages**
- seek asylum 寻求庇护
- political asylum 政治庇护
- grant asylum 给予庇护
- apply for asylum 申请避难

**Examples**
1. The refugees sought asylum in a neighboring country. 难民在邻国寻求庇护。
2. He was granted political asylum. 他获得了政治庇护。
3. Thousands apply for asylum every year. 每年有数千人申请避难。
