### analogue
[ˈænəlɒɡ]

**Desp**
n. something that is similar to something else; a thing comparable to another.
相似物，类似事情

**Extension**
形容词：analogous
美式拼写：analog

**Usages**
- analogue of ……的类似物
- analogue model 类比模型

**Examples**
1. The brain is sometimes described as an analogue of a computer. 大脑有时被描述为计算机的类似物。
2. This is the closest analogue to the original species. 这是最接近原物种的类似物。
3. Analog clocks use an analogue display. 指针式时钟使用模拟显示。
