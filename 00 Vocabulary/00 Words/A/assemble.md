### assemble
[əˈsembl]

**Description**
v. to gather together; to put parts together.
v. 集合，聚集；组装，装配

**Extension**
名词：assembly 集会，装配
反义词：disassemble 拆卸

**Usages**
- assemble a team 组建团队
- assemble furniture 组装家具
- the assembly line 装配线

**Examples**
1. The students assembled in the hall. 学生们在礼堂集合。
2. He assembled the bookshelf in an hour. 他一小时就装好了书架。
3. A crowd assembled outside the courthouse. 人群聚集在法院外。
