### authoritative
[əˈθɔːrəteɪtɪv]

**Desp**
adj. trusted as accurate and reliable; commanding and confident.
权威的，可信的；专断的，命令式的

**Extension**
反义词：tentative, unreliable
名词：authority
副词：authoritatively

**Usages**
- an authoritative source 权威来源
- an authoritative report 权威报告
- an authoritative tone 命令式的口吻

**Examples**
1. This dictionary is an authoritative reference for English learners. 这本词典是英语学习者的权威参考。
2. She cited an authoritative study on climate change. 她引用了一项关于气候变化的权威研究。
3. He spoke in an authoritative tone that demanded obedience. 他以命令式的口吻说话，要求别人服从。
