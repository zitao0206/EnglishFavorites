### austerity
[ɒˈsterəti]

**Desp**
n. strict economy; a plain, simple way of living.
紧缩，压缩（财政）；简朴，朴素，节俭的生活方式

**Usages**
- budget austerity 紧缩政策
- austerity measures 紧缩措施

**Examples**
1. The government introduced budget austerity to cut debt. 政府推出财政紧缩以减少债务。
2. They lived a life of austerity in the monastery. 他们在修道院里过着简朴的生活。
3. Austerity measures are unpopular with voters. 紧缩措施不受选民欢迎。
