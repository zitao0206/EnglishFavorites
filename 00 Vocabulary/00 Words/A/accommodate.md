### accommodate
[əˈkɑːmədeɪt]

**Desp**
v. to provide room for; to adapt to; to do a favor.
v. 容纳；使适应；为…提供方便

**Extension**
名词：accommodation 住宿；适应

**Usages**
- accommodate 200 guests 可容纳200位客人
- accommodate sb's needs 照顾某人的需求

**Examples**
1. The hall can accommodate five hundred people. 这个大厅能容纳五百人。
2. We'll try to accommodate your schedule. 我们会尽量配合你的时间安排。
3. The hotel accommodates guests with disabilities. 这家酒店为残障客人提供便利。
