### age-restricted
[ˈeɪdʒ rɪˌstrɪktɪd]

**Description**
adj. restricted or limited to people of a certain age.
年龄受限的，限年龄的

**Usages**
- age-restricted content 年龄受限内容
- age-restricted games 限制级游戏

**Examples**
1. The video is age-restricted and requires ID verification. 该视频有年龄限制，需要身份验证。
2. Age-restricted products cannot be sold to minors. 限年龄产品不能卖给未成年人。
