### unpack
[ʌnˈpæk]

**Desp**
v. to take things out of a container; to analyze something in depth.
打开；取出；深入剖析，仔细探讨

**Usages**
- unpack the luggage 打开行李
- unpack a complex idea 剖析复杂概念
- unpack what sb really meant 解读某人真正的意思

**Examples**
1. I just arrived and haven't unpacked yet. 我刚刚到，还没收拾行李。
2. Can you unpack what he really meant? 你能解读他真正的意思吗？
3. The lecture unpacks the causes of the crisis in detail. 讲座深入剖析了这场危机的成因。
