### Uygur
[ˈwiːɡʊə]

**Desp**
n. a member of a Turkic ethnic group living mainly in Xinjiang, China.
维吾尔族

**Usages**
- Uygur ethnic group 维吾尔族

**Examples**
1. The Uygur ethnic group is one of the many ethnic groups in China. 维吾尔族是中国众多民族之一。
2. She is a Uygur singer who loves her hometown. 她是一位热爱家乡的维吾尔族歌手。
