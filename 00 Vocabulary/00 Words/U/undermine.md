### undermine
[ˌʌndərˈmaɪn]

**Desp**
v. To gradually weaken, damage, or lessen the effectiveness of something.
破坏，削弱，暗中损坏

**Usages**
- undermine confidence 削弱信心
- undermine authority 削弱权威
- undermine trust 破坏信任

**Examples**
1. Constant criticism can undermine a child's self-esteem. 不断的批评会削弱孩子的自尊心。
2. The scandal undermined public trust in the government. 丑闻破坏了公众对政府的信任。
3. Don't let rumors undermine your confidence before the exam. 别让谣言在考前削弱你的信心。
