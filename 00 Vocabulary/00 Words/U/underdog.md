### underdog

**Desp**
n. a person or team expected to lose; the weaker side.
弱者，不被看好的一方；逆袭故事

**Usages**
- underdog story 逆袭故事
- root for the underdog 支持弱者

**Examples**
1. She always stands up for the underdog. 她总是为弱势一方发声。
2. Everyone loves an underdog story. 人人都爱逆袭故事。
3. The underdog team won the final. 不被看好的队伍赢得了决赛。
