### utter
[ˈʌtə(r)]

**Desp**
adj. complete or total. 完全的，彻底的；
v. to say something aloud. 说出

**Usages**
- utterly adv. 完全地
- utter nonsense 一派胡言

**Examples**
1. The meeting was a complete/utter waste of time. 这次会议完全是浪费时间。
2. She didn't utter a single word during the meeting. 会议期间她一个字都没说。
3. He was utterly exhausted after the race. 比赛后他彻底精疲力竭。
