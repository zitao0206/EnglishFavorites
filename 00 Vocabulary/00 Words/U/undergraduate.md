### undergraduate 
[ˌʌndərˈɡrædʒuət]

**Desp**
n. A university student who has not yet received a bachelor's degree.
大学本科生，在校大学生

**Usages**
- undergraduate student 本科生
- undergraduate degree 学士学位
- an undergraduate course 本科课程

**Examples**
1. She's an undergraduate at Harvard University. 她是哈佛大学的本科生。
2. The course is open to all undergraduates. 这门课程对所有本科生开放。
3. He decided to pursue an undergraduate degree in computer science. 他决定攻读计算机科学学士学位。
