### undergo
[ˌʌndərˈɡoʊ]

**Desp**
v. to experience or be subjected to something, especially something difficult or unpleasant.
经历，经受（变化、手术等）

**Usages**
- undergo surgery 接受手术
- undergo changes 经历变化
- undergo training 接受训练

**Examples**
1. The patient will undergo surgery tomorrow morning. 病人明早将接受手术。
2. The company is undergoing a major restructuring. 公司正在经历重大重组。
3. She underwent months of training for the marathon. 她为马拉松接受了数月训练。
