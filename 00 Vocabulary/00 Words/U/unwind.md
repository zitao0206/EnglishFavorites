### unwind
[ˌʌnˈwaɪnd]

**Desp**
v. To relax and stop feeling anxious; to undo something that is wound around.
放松，松弛；解开，展开

**Usages**
- unwind after work 下班后放松
- unwind with music 用音乐放松
- unwind the rope 解开绳子

**Examples**
1. A hot bath helps me unwind after a long day. 漫长一天后，洗个热水澡能让我放松下来。
2. She listens to soft music to unwind. 她听轻音乐来放松。
3. He slowly unwound the bandage. 他慢慢解开绷带。
