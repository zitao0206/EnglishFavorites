### upbringing
[ˈʌpbrɪŋɪŋ]

**Desp**
n. The way in which a child is cared for and taught by their parents.
养育，教养；成长环境

**Usages**
- a strict upbringing 严格的教养
- a good upbringing 良好的教养
- influence one's upbringing 影响某人的成长

**Examples**
1. She had a strict religious upbringing. 她从小受到严格的宗教教育。
2. His upbringing shaped his character. 他的成长环境塑造了他的性格。
3. A loving upbringing is crucial for children. 充满爱的养育对儿童至关重要。
