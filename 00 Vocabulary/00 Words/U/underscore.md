### underscore
[ˌʌndərˈskɔːr]

**Desp**
v. to emphasize or show that something is important; to draw a line under a word.
强调，凸显；在……下划线

**Usages**
- underscore the importance of 强调……的重要性
- underscore the need for 凸显对……的需求
- serve to underscore 起到强调作用

**Examples**
1. The incident underscores the need for better security. 这一事件凸显了加强安保的必要性。
2. His success underscores the value of hard work. 他的成功强调了努力的价值。
3. Let me underscore this point. 让我强调这一点。
