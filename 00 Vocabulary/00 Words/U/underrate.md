### underrate
[ˌʌndərˈreɪt]

**Desp**
v. to rate or evaluate too low; to underestimate.
低估，轻视

**Usages**
- underrate the difficulty 低估难度
- underrate the importance 低估重要性
- be underrated 被低估

**Examples**
1. People often underrate the importance of sleep. 人们经常低估睡眠的重要性。
2. Her contributions to the team have been underrated. 她对团队的贡献被低估了。
3. Don't underrate your own abilities. 不要低估你自己的能力。
