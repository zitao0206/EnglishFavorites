### pivotal
[ˈpɪvətl]

**Desp**
adj. of crucial importance in relation to the development or success of something.
关键的，核心的，举足轻重的

**Usages**
- pivotal role 关键作用
- pivotal moment 关键时刻
- a pivotal figure 核心人物

**Examples**
1. She played a pivotal role in the negotiation. 她在谈判中发挥了关键作用。
2. That decision was a pivotal moment in the company's history. 那个决定是公司历史上的关键时刻。
3. He is a pivotal figure in modern architecture. 他是现代建筑界的核心人物。
