### permanent
[ˈpɜːrmənənt]

**Desp**
adj. lasting or intended to last indefinitely without change.
永久的，持久的

**Usages**
- permanent damage 永久性损伤
- permanent residence 永久居留权
- a permanent job 固定工作

**Examples**
1. The accident caused permanent damage to his hearing. 事故对他的听力造成了永久性损伤。
2. She applied for permanent residence in Canada. 她申请了加拿大的永久居留权。
3. They are looking for a permanent solution to the problem. 他们在寻找解决该问题的永久方案。
