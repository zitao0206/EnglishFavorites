### personnel
[ˌpɜːsəˈnel]

**Desp**
n. the people who work for an organization.
人员，员工，全体人员

**Usages**
- personnel department 人事部门
- military personnel 军事人员

**Examples**
1. All personnel must attend the safety training. 所有人员都必须参加安全培训。
2. The company hired new personnel for the project. 公司为该项目招聘了新人员。
