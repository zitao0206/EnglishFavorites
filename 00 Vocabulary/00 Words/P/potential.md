### potential
[pəˈtenʃl]

**Desp**
adj. Possible or likely in the future; n. the ability to develop or become something.
潜在的，可能的；潜力，潜能

**Usages**
- potential customers 潜在客户
- realize one's potential 发挥潜力
- market potential 市场潜力

**Examples**
1. This product has great market potential. 这款产品有巨大的市场潜力。
2. She has the potential to become a leader. 她有成为领导者的潜力。
3. We need to identify potential risks. 我们需要识别潜在风险。
