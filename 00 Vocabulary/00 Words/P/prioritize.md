### prioritize
[praɪˈɔːrətaɪz]

**Desp**
v. to designate or treat something as more important than other things.
优先处理，按重要性排序

**Usages**
- prioritize tasks 确定任务优先级
- prioritize health 把健康放在首位
- prioritize quality over speed 优先考虑质量而非速度

**Examples**
1. You need to prioritize your tasks before the deadline. 你需要在截止日期前确定任务的优先级。
2. The company prioritizes innovation above all else. 公司将创新置于一切之上。
3. It's important to prioritize your mental health. 把心理健康放在首位很重要。
