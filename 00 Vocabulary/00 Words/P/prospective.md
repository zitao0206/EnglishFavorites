### prospective
[prəˈspektɪv]

**Desp**
adj. expected or expecting to be something particular in the future.
预期的，未来的，可能的

**Usages**
- prospective buyers 潜在买家
- prospective students 未来学生/准学生

**Examples**
1. The company is interviewing prospective employees this week. 公司本周在面试潜在的雇员。
2. Prospective students are encouraged to visit the campus. 鼓励未来的学生参观校园。
3. They held a meeting with prospective investors. 他们与潜在投资者举行了一次会议。
