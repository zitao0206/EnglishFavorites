### punch
[pʌntʃ]

**Desp**
v. to hit someone or something hard with your fist; to make a hole in something.
拳打；猛击；打洞

**Usages**
- punch a hole 打孔
- punch sb in the face 一拳打在某人脸上
- punch the air 挥拳庆祝

**Examples**
1. He punched a hole in the ticket with the machine. 他用机器在票上打了个孔。
2. She punched him on the nose. 她一拳打在他鼻子上。
3. The crowd punched the air in excitement. 人群兴奋地挥拳欢呼。
