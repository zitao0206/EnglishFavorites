### paper-folding
[ˈpeɪpə ˈfəʊldɪŋ]

**Desp**
n. the art or craft of folding paper into shapes.
折纸

**Usages**
- art of paper-folding 折纸艺术
- origami craft 折纸手工
- paper-folding skills 折纸技巧

**Examples**
1. The art of paper-folding has a long history in China. 折纸艺术在中国历史悠久。
2. Kids learned paper-folding in the craft class. 孩们在手工课上学了折纸。
3. He can make a crane with paper-folding. 他能用折纸折出一只鹤。
