### possess
[pəˈzes]

**Desp**
v. To have or own something; to have a particular quality or ability.
拥有，占有；具备（品质）

**Usages**
- possess great talent 拥有极高的天赋
- possess legal rights 拥有合法权利
- be possessed of 具有……

**Examples**
1. He possesses a remarkable memory. 他拥有惊人的记忆力。
2. The company possesses the patent for this technology. 这家公司拥有这项技术的专利。
3. She possesses all the qualities of a good leader. 她具备优秀领导者的所有品质。
