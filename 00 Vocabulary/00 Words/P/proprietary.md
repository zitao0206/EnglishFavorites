### proprietary
[prəˈpraɪətri]

**Desp**
adj. owned by a particular company; protected by patent or copyright.
专有的，独家的，专利的

**Usages**
- proprietary technology 专有技术
- proprietary formula 独家配方

**Examples**
1. The company protects its proprietary technology carefully. 该公司谨慎保护其专有技术。
2. This is a proprietary formula that no competitor can copy. 这是竞争对手无法复制的独家配方。
