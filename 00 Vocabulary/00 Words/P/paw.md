### paw
[pɔː]

**Desp**
n. The foot of an animal with claws or nails.
（动物）爪子

**Usages**
- a dog's paw 狗爪子
- paw prints 爪印
- paw at 用爪子抓

**Examples**
1. The cat licked its paw. 猫舔了舔自己的爪子。
2. The puppy left paw prints on the floor. 小狗在地板上留下爪印。
