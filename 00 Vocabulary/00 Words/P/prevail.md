### prevail
[prɪˈveɪl]

**Desp**
v. to win or gain control; to be common or widespread.
获胜，占上风；盛行，流行

**Usages**
- Truth will prevail. 真理终将获胜。

**Examples**
1. Truth will prevail in the end. 真理终将获胜。
2. Justice prevailed over evil. 正义战胜了邪恶。
3. This custom still prevails in rural areas. 这种习俗在农村地区仍然盛行。
