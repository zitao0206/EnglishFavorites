### prominent
[ˈprɒmɪnənt]

**Description**
adj. important, famous, or noticeable; projecting outward.
显著的，突出的

**Extension**
副词：prominently
名词形式：prominence

**Usages**
- prominent feature 显著特征
- prominent role 重要角色

**Examples**
1. The tree's prominent trunk made it a local landmark. 这棵树突出的树干使它成为当地地标。
2. She is a prominent scientist in the field. 她是该领域著名的科学家。
3. The issue was prominently featured in the news. 这个问题在新闻中被显著报道。
