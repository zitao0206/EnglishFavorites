### persistence
[pərˈsɪstəns]

**Desp**
n. the quality of continuing to do something despite difficulty; the fact of continuing to exist.
坚持，毅力；持续

**Usages**
- persistence and patience 坚持与耐心
- with persistence 坚持不懈地
- the persistence of a habit 习惯的顽固

**Examples**
1. Her persistence finally paid off. 她的坚持最终得到了回报。
2. Success requires persistence. 成功需要毅力。
3. He showed great persistence in learning English. 他在学英语方面表现出了极大的坚持。
