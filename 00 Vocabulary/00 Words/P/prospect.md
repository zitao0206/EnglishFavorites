### prospect
[ˈprɒspekt]

**Desp**
n. the possibility of future success; v. to search for mineral deposits.
前景，前途；v. 勘探

**Extension**
其他形式：prospective (adj.)

**Usages**
- good prospects 前景良好
- prospect for gold 勘探金矿

**Examples**
1. Job prospects in the tech industry remain strong. 科技行业的就业前景依然强劲。
2. They prospected for oil in the region. 他们在该地区勘探石油。
3. The prospect of promotion motivated him. 晋升的前景激励了他。
