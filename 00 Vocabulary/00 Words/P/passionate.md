### passionate
[ˈpæʃənət]

**Desp**
adj. having or showing very strong feelings or enthusiasm.
热情的，充满激情的；情欲强烈的

**Extension**
反义词：indifferent, apathetic
名词：passion
副词：passionately

**Usages**
- be passionate about... 对……充满热情
- a passionate speech 充满激情的演讲
- passionate supporter 热情的支持者

**Examples**
1. She is passionate about environmental protection. 她对环保事业充满热情。
2. He delivered a passionate speech at the rally. 他在集会上发表了慷慨激昂的演讲。
3. You need to be passionate about your work to succeed. 要成功，你必须对工作充满热情。
