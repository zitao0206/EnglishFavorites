### perpetual
[pərˈpetʃuəl]

**Desp**
adj. never ending or changing; continuous.
adj. 永久的，持续不断的

**Extension**
动词：perpetuate 使长久存在

**Usages**
- perpetual motion 永动（机）
- perpetual noise 持续不断的噪音
- a perpetual state of change 永远在变的状态

**Examples**
1. The lake is covered in a perpetual mist. 湖面常年笼罩着雾气。
2. He lives in a perpetual state of anxiety. 他长期处于焦虑状态。
3. The two sides are locked in perpetual conflict. 双方陷入无休止的冲突。
