### pitfall
[ˈpɪtfɔːl]

**Desp**
n. a hidden or unsuspected danger or difficulty.
隐患，陷阱，易犯的错误

**Usages**
- common pitfalls 常见陷阱
- avoid pitfalls 避免陷阱
- the pitfalls of ...的隐患

**Examples**
1. The guide highlights common pitfalls in starting a business. 该指南强调了创业中的常见陷阱。
2. One of the pitfalls of remote work is isolation. 远程工作的隐患之一是孤立感。
3. Investors should be aware of the pitfalls before committing. 投资者在投入前应了解其中的风险。
