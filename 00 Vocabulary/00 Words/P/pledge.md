### pledge
[pledʒ]

**Desp**
v/n. to make a serious promise; a promise.
v. 承诺，保证；抵押 n. 誓言，保证

**Usages**
- pledge to do 承诺做
- make a pledge 作出保证
- pledge allegiance 宣誓效忠

**Examples**
1. The government pledged to cut carbon emissions. 政府承诺减少碳排放。
2. He made a pledge to give up smoking. 他保证戒烟。
3. They pledged $10 million to the charity. 他们向该慈善机构认捐了1000万美元。
