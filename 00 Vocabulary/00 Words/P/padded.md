### padded
[ˈpædɪd]

**Desp**
adj. filled or covered with soft material; (of animals' feet) having fleshy pads.
带软垫的；有厚肉垫的

**Usages**
- padded jacket 棉袄
- padded feet 有肉垫的脚
- padded envelope 缓冲信封

**Examples**
1. Cats have padded feet. 猫长有肉垫。
2. He wore a padded jacket in winter. 他冬天穿棉袄。
3. The parcel arrived in a padded envelope. 包裹装在缓冲信封里送达。
