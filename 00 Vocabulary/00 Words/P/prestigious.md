### prestigious
[preˈstɪdʒəs]

**Desp**
adj. Respected and admired because of its importance or high quality.
有声望的，享有盛誉的

**Usages**
- a prestigious university 名牌大学
- a prestigious award 享有盛誉的奖项
- a prestigious firm 知名企业

**Examples**
1. She studied at a prestigious university. 她在一所名牌大学学习。
2. He won a prestigious literary award. 他获得了一个享有盛誉的文学奖。
3. The firm is one of the most prestigious in the country. 这家公司是该国最有声望的公司之一。
