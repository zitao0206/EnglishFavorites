### perusal
[pəˈruːzl]

**Description**
n. the act of reading something carefully; 
细读，翻阅（→ peruse v. 精读）
v. peruse 精读

**Usages**
- after careful perusal 经审阅后
- for your perusal 供你审阅
- for further perusal 供进一步细读

**Examples**
1. The document is attached for your perusal. 文件随附供你审阅。
2. After careful perusal of the contract, she signed it. 仔细审阅合同后，她签了字。
