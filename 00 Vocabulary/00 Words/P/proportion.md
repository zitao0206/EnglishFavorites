### proportion
[prəˈpɔːʃn]

**Desp**
n. a part or share of a whole; the relationship between the size of two things.
比例；部分；均衡

**Usages**
- in proportion to 与……成比例
- a large proportion of 很大一部分
- out of proportion 不成比例

**Examples**
1. A large proportion of the budget goes to education. 预算的很大一部分用于教育。
2. The room is well proportioned. 这个房间比例很协调。
3. Her reaction was out of proportion to the situation. 她的反应与当时的情况不成比例。
