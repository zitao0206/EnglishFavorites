### placement
[ˈpleɪsmənt]

**Desp**
n. the act of putting something in a particular place; a job position or a period of practical work.
安置，布置；职位安排，实习

**Usages**
- job placement 就业安置
- product placement 植入式广告
- a work placement 工作实习

**Examples**
1. The school offers job placement services. 学校提供就业安置服务。
2. The placement of the ads was strategic. 广告的投放位置很有策略。
3. She did a work placement at a bank. 她在一家银行做实习。
