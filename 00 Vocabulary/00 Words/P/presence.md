### presence
[ˈprezns]

**Desp**
n. the fact of being present.
出席，参加；存在

**Usages**
- in the presence of … 在…面前
- online presence 线上存在感

**Examples**
1. Your presence is required at the meeting. 你需要出席会议。
2. He felt her presence in the room. 他感觉到她在房间里。
3. The company has a strong online presence. 这家公司有很强的线上影响力。
