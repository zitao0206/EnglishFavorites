### procedure
[prəˈsiːdʒə(r)]

**Desp**
n. an official or established way of doing something.
程序，步骤，流程，手续

**Usages**
- standard-procedure 标准流程
- operating-procedure 操作步骤
- follow the procedure 按流程办事

**Examples**
1. Please follow the standard procedure when filing a complaint. 投诉时请遵循标准流程。
2. The operating procedure is explained in the manual. 操作步骤在说明书中有说明。
3. Applying for a visa involves a lot of procedure. 申请签证手续繁多。
