### presume
[prɪˈzjuːm]

**Desp**
v. to think that something is true because it is likely, although you are not certain.
假定，推测；擅自做某事

**Usages**
- presume that 推测……
- be presumed dead 被推定死亡
- presume to do sth 擅自做某事

**Examples**
1. I presume you've heard the news. 我想你已经听说这个消息了。
2. The missing hikers are presumed dead. 失踪的徒步者被推定已死亡。
3. I won't presume to tell you what to do. 我不会擅自告诉你该怎么做。
