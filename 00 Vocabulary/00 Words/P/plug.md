### plug
[plʌɡ]

**Desp**
v. to connect something to a power supply or network; n. 插头.
插入，接入

**Usages**
- plug into a socket 插入插座
- plug-in the network 接入网络
- Plug your phone into the charger. 把手机插到充电器上。

**Examples**
1. Plug your phone into the charger. 把你的手机插到充电器上。
2. She plugged the lamp into the socket. 她把台灯插头插入插座。
3. You need to plug in the network cable first. 你需要先接入网线。
