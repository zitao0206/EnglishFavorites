### phase
[feɪz]

**Desp**
n. a stage in a process.
阶段

**Usages**
- phase out 逐渐淘汰，逐步减少
- phase in 逐步引入

**Examples**
1. The project is in its final phase. 项目处于最后阶段。
2. The company will phase out old products. 公司将逐步淘汰旧产品。
3. New rules will be phased in over two years. 新规将在两年内逐步实施。
