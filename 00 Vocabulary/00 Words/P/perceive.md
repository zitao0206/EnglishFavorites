### perceive
[pərˈsiːv]

**Desp**
v. To notice something that is not easy to notice; to understand or view something in a particular way.
察觉，感知；认为，理解

**Usages**
- perceive the difference 察觉差异
- perceive sb as 认为某人是……
- be perceived as 被视为……

**Examples**
1. Cats can perceive things humans cannot. 猫能感知到人类感知不到的东西。
2. The change was too subtle to perceive. 这个变化太微妙，难以察觉。
3. He is perceived as a strong leader. 他被视为一位强有力的领导者。
