### paralyze
[ˈpærəlaɪz]

**Desp**
v. to make someone unable to move; to make a system or organization unable to operate normally. 使瘫痪，使麻痹；使无法正常运转

**Usages**
- be paralyzed with fear 吓得动弹不得
- paralyze the economy 使经济瘫痪
- a paralyzing blow 毁灭性打击

**Examples**
1. He was paralyzed from the waist down in the accident. 他在事故中腰部以下瘫痪。
2. The city was paralyzed by the heavy snow. 大雪使城市陷入瘫痪。
3. She stood paralyzed with fear. 她吓得站在那里动弹不得。
