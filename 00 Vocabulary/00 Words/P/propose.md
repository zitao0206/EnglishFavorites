### propose
[prəˈpəʊz]

**Description**
v. to suggest a plan or idea for people to consider; to ask someone to marry you.
提议，建议；求婚

**Usages**
- propose a plan 提出计划
- propose to do sth 提议做某事
- propose to sb 向某人求婚

**Examples**
1. She proposed a new approach to the problem. 她提出了解决这个问题的新方法。
2. I propose we take a short break. 我建议我们休息一下。
3. He proposed to her on the beach. 他在海滩上向她求婚了。
