### profound
[prəˈfaʊnd]

**Desp**
adj. very great, deep, or intense.
adj. 深刻的，深远的；渊博的

**Extension**
副词：profoundly 深深地

**Usages**
- a profound impact 深远的影响
- a profound question 深刻的问题
- profound knowledge 渊博的知识

**Examples**
1. The war had a profound effect on society. 战争对社会产生了深远的影响。
2. She has a profound understanding of chemistry. 她对化学有深刻的理解。
3. The book left a profound impression on me. 这本书给我留下了深刻的印象。
