### precarious
[prɪˈkeəriəs]

**Desp**
adj. not secure or certain; dangerously unstable.
不稳定的，岌岌可危的

**Extension**
副词：precariously
名词形式：precariousness

**Usages**
- precarious position 岌岌可危的处境
- precarious situation 不稳定的局势

**Examples**
1. The species is in a precarious position despite conservation efforts. 尽管有保护措施，该物种仍岌岌可危。
2. He earned a precarious living as a freelance writer. 他作为自由撰稿人过着不稳定的生活。
3. The ladder was balanced precariously against the wall. 梯子摇摇欲坠地靠在墙上。
