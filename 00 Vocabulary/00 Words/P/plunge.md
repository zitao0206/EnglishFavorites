### plunge
[plʌndʒ]

**Desp**
v/n. to fall or move suddenly and quickly; to decrease sharply; to push something into something else. 骤降，猛跌；纵身跳入；使陷入；n. 跳水；骤降

**Usages**
- plunge into 纵身跳入；陷入
- take the plunge 冒险一试，下定决心
- prices plunged 价格暴跌

**Examples**
1. The stock market plunged by 10% yesterday. 昨天股市暴跌了10%。
2. He plunged into the river to save the child. 他纵身跳入河中救那个孩子。
3. The company took the plunge and entered the new market. 这家公司大胆冒险，进入了新市场。
