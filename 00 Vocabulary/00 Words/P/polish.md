### polish
[ˈpɑːlɪʃ]

**Desp**
v. to make something shine by rubbing; to improve something.
v. 擦亮，抛光；润色，改进 n. 上光剂；优雅

**Usages**
- polish your shoes 擦鞋
- polish an essay 润色文章
- polish up your skills 提升技能

**Examples**
1. He polished his shoes before the interview. 面试前他把鞋擦得锃亮。
2. You should polish your presentation before Friday. 周五前你应该把演示润色一下。
3. She polished her English by watching films. 她通过看电影提升英语水平。
