### prudent
[ˈpruːdnt]

**Desp**
adj. acting with care and thought for the future; wise.
adj. 谨慎的，精明的，深谋远虑的

**Extension**
名词：prudence 审慎

**Usages**
- a prudent decision 审慎的决定
- prudent investment 稳健的投资

**Examples**
1. It would be prudent to save some money. 存点钱是明智的。
2. She made a prudent choice by waiting. 她选择等待是明智之举。
3. A prudent investor avoids unnecessary risks. 精明的投资者会避免不必要的风险。
