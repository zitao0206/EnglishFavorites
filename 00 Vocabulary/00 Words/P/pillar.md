### pillar
[ˈpɪlə(r)]

**Desp**
n. A tall upright support; an important member of a group.
支柱，栋梁；柱子

**Usages**
- a pillar of the community 社区栋梁
- pillar industry 支柱产业
- a pillar of strength 精神支柱

**Examples**
1. He is a pillar of the community. 他是社区栋梁。
2. Tourism is a pillar industry of the city. 旅游业是该市的支柱产业。
3. She was a pillar of strength for the family. 她是全家的精神支柱。
