### perseverance
[ˌpɜːsəˈvɪərəns]

**Desp**
n. continued effort to do or achieve something despite difficulties.
毅力，坚持不懈

**Usages**
- lack of perseverance 缺乏毅力
- with perseverance 凭着毅力
- show perseverance 表现出毅力

**Examples**
1. My biggest issue is lack of perseverance. 我最大的问题是缺乏毅力。
2. Her success came from perseverance and hard work. 她的成功来自坚持不懈和努力工作。
3. With perseverance, he finally passed the exam. 凭着毅力，他终于通过了考试。
