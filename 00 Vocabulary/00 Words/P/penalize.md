### penalize
[ˈpiːnəlaɪz]

**Desp**
v. To punish someone for breaking a rule; to put someone at a disadvantage.
处罚，惩罚

**Usages**
- penalize sb for sth 因某事处罚某人
- be heavily penalized 受到重罚
- penalize unfair competition 惩罚不正当竞争

**Examples**
1. The referee penalized the team for a foul. 裁判因犯规处罚了该队。
2. Companies that pollute will be penalized. 污染环境的企业将受到处罚。
3. The new law penalizes late tax filers. 新法律惩罚逾期报税者。
