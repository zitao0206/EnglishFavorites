### penalty
[ˈpenəlti]

**Desp**
n. A punishment for breaking a law, rule, or agreement; (sports) a disadvantage given for breaking the rules.
处罚，惩罚；罚金；点球

**Usages**
- a heavy penalty 重罚
- death penalty 死刑
- pay the penalty 付出代价

**Examples**
1. The penalty for speeding is a heavy fine. 超速的处罚是高额罚款。
2. The team conceded a penalty in the last minute. 这支球队在最后一分钟被判罚点球。
3. He paid the penalty for his carelessness. 他为自己的粗心付出了代价。
