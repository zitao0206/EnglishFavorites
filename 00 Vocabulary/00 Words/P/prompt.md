### prompt
[prɑːmpt]

**Desp**
adj. Done quickly and without delay; v. to cause someone to do something; n. a message that tells you what to do.
迅速的，及时的；促使，激起；提示

**Usages**
- a prompt reply 及时回复
- prompt sb to do sth 促使某人做某事
- be prompted by 由……引起

**Examples**
1. Thank you for your prompt response. 感谢你的及时回复。
2. The news prompted him to change his plans. 这个消息促使他改变了计划。
3. What prompted you to ask that? 是什么促使你问那个问题的？
