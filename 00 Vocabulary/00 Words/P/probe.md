### probe
[prəʊb]

**Desp**
v./n. to investigate; an investigation.
探查，调查，探测

**Usages**
- probe into … 深入调查…
- a police probe 警方调查

**Examples**
1. Police probed into the cause of the fire. 警方调查了火灾起因。
2. The probe found no evidence of fraud. 调查没有发现欺诈证据。
3. Doctors probed the wound carefully. 医生小心地探查伤口。
