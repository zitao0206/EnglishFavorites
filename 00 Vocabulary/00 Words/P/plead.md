### plead
[pliːd]

**Desp**
v. To beg earnestly for something; to make an urgent appeal.
恳求，祈求；（法律）申辩

**Usages**
- plead for mercy 恳求宽恕
- plead with sb to do sth 恳求某人做某事
- plead guilty/not guilty 认罪/不认罪

**Examples**
1. She pleaded for mercy. 她恳求宽恕。
2. He pleaded with her to stay. 他恳求她留下来。
3. The defendant pleaded not guilty. 被告不认罪。
