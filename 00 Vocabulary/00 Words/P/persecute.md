### persecute
[ˈpɜːsɪkjuːt]

**Desp**
v. to treat someone cruelly or unfairly because of race, religion or beliefs.
（因种族、信仰、观点）迫害，残害

**Usages**
- persecute minorities 迫害少数群体

**Examples**
1. The regime persecuted anyone who opposed it. 该政权迫害任何反对它的人。
2. They were persecuted for their religious beliefs. 他们因宗教信仰而受到迫害。
