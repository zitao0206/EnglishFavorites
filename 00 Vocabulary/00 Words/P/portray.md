### portray
[pɔːˈtreɪ]

**Desp**
v. to describe or show someone or something in a certain way.
描绘，刻画；描述，塑造

**Usages**
- portray sb as … 把某人描绘成…
- portray a character 塑造角色

**Examples**
1. The movie portrays him as a hero. 电影把他塑造成英雄。
2. The novel portrays life in the 19th century. 小说描绘了19世纪的生活。
3. She portrayed a teacher in the play. 她在剧中饰演一位老师。
