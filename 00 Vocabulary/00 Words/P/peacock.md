### peacock
[ˈpiːkɒk]

**Desp**
n. A large male bird with beautiful long tail feathers.
（雄）孔雀

**Usages**
- as proud as a peacock 像孔雀一样骄傲
- a peacock's tail 孔雀的尾巴

**Examples**
1. The peacock spread its magnificent tail. 孔雀展开了华丽的尾羽。
2. He was as proud as a peacock after winning. 获胜后他像孔雀一样趾高气扬。
