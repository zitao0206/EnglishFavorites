### perish
[ˈperɪʃ]

**Desp**
v. to die, especially in a sudden or violent way; to be destroyed completely.
死亡，丧生，毁灭，消亡

**Usages**
- perish in the disaster 在灾难中丧生
- perish from hunger 死于饥饿
- perish in flames 葬身火海

**Examples**
1. Many people perished in the disaster. 很多人在这场灾难中丧生。
2. Hundreds of flowers perished in the frost. 数百株花朵在霜冻中枯死了。
3. The ancient civilization perished thousands of years ago. 这一古老文明数千年前就消亡了。
