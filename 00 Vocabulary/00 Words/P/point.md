### point
[pɔɪnt]

**Desp**
n. a particular detail, idea, purpose or spot; the sharp end of something
v. to direct your finger toward something; to indicate or suggest something
n. 观点；要点；意义；地点；尖端
v. 指；指向；表明

**Extension**
形容词：pointed 尖锐的；直截了当的
派生词：pointless[ˈpɔɪntləs] adj. 无意义的；point‑blank adv. 直截了当地

**Usages**
- the point 核心要点
- make a point 提出一个观点
- there is no point (in doing sth) 做某事没有意义
- point out sth 指出
- point to sth/be pointing to 指向；表明/ 正指向，正在表明
- point at sth 指着（实物）
- on point 恰到好处，一针见血
- to the point 切中要害
- off the point 离题
- the Pointless Competition无谓的竞争

**Examples**
1. I don’t understand your point. 我不明白你的观点。
2. There is no point in waiting any longer. 再等下去没有意义。
3. He pointed out several serious problems. 他指出了几个严重的问题。
4. All the evidence points to human‑caused climate change. 所有证据都指向人为造成的气候变化。
5. Her analysis was on point. 她的分析一针见血。
