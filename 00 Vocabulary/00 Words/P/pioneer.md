### pioneer
[ˌpaɪəˈnɪə(r)]

**Desp**
n. a person who is among the first to do something.
拓荒者，开拓者，先锋

**Usages**
- a pioneer in AI research 人工智能研究的先驱

**Examples**
1. The company led the use of silicon chips. 这家公司开创了硅芯片的使用。
2. She is a pioneer in the field of genetics. 她是遗传学领域的先驱。
3. Early pioneers crossed the mountains on foot. 早期拓荒者徒步翻越山脉。
