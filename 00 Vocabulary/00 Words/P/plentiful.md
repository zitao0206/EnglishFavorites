### plentiful
[ˈplentɪfl]

**Desp**
adj. existing in or yielding plenty; abundant.
丰富的，大量的

**Extension**
反义词：scarce

**Usages**
- food is plentiful 食物充足
- plentiful supply 丰富的供应

**Examples**
1. Kakapo breed in summer and autumn, but only in years when food is plentiful. 鸮鹦鹉在夏秋两季繁殖，但只在食物充足的年份。
2. Fish were plentiful in the river. 河里鱼类丰富。
3. Cheap labor was plentiful in the industrial era. 工业时代廉价劳动力充足。
