### pessimistic
[ˌpesɪˈmɪstɪk]

**Desp**
adj. expecting bad outcomes; gloomy.
adj. 悲观的

**Extension**
名词：pessimism 悲观
反义词：optimistic 乐观的

**Usages**
- be pessimistic about 对…悲观
- a pessimistic view 悲观的看法

**Examples**
1. He is pessimistic about the economy. 他对经济形势很悲观。
2. Don't be so pessimistic about your chances. 别对自己的机会这么悲观。
3. Analysts gave a pessimistic forecast for the market. 分析师对市场做出了悲观预测。
