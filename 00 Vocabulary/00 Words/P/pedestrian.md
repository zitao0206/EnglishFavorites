### pedestrian
[pəˈdestriən]

**Desp**
n. a person walking on the road; adj. of or for walkers.
行人；adj. 行人的，步行的

**Usages**
- pedestrian crossing 人行横道
- pedestrian street 步行街
- pedestrian bridge 天桥

**Examples**
1. Pedestrians should use the crossing. 行人应走人行横道。
2. This street is closed to cars; it's pedestrian only. 这条街禁止汽车通行，只允许步行。
