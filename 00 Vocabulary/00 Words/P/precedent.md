### precedent
[ˈpresɪdənt]

**Desp**
n. an earlier event or decision that is used as an example for later situations.
先例，前例

**Usages**
- set a precedent 开创先例
- follow a precedent 遵循先例

**Examples**
1. The ruling set a legal precedent for future cases. 该判决为今后案件开创了法律先例。
2. There is no precedent for such a decision. 这样的决定没有先例。
