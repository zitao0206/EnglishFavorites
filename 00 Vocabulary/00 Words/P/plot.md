### plot
[plɒt]

**Description**
n. the events of a story; v. to draw a graph or map.
n. 剧情；v. 绘制

**Usages**
- a surprising plot 出人意料的剧情
- price-plot 绘制价格走势图
- plot the data 绘制数据图

**Examples**
1. The movie has a surprising plot. 这部电影剧情出人意料。
2. We plotted the sales figures on a graph. 我们把销售数据绘制成图表。
3. The novel's plot twists kept readers guessing. 小说的剧情反转让读者猜个不停。
