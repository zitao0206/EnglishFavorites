### vibrate
[ˈvaɪbreɪt]

**Description**
v. to shake with small quick movements.
v. 震动，颤动

**Extension**
名词：vibration 震动

**Usages**
- the phone vibrates 手机震动
- vibrate with rage 气得发抖

**Examples**
1. My phone vibrates when I get a message. 收到消息时我的手机会震动。
2. The floor vibrated as the train passed. 火车经过时地板震动。
3. The guitar strings vibrated after he plucked them. 他拨动后吉他弦震颤着。
