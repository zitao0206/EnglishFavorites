### vigorous
[ˈvɪɡərəs]

**Description**
adj. strong, active, and energetic; forceful.
有力的，精力充沛的；剧烈的

**Usages**
- vigorous exercise 剧烈运动
- a vigorous debate 激烈的辩论
- vigorous growth 蓬勃增长

**Examples**
1. Regular vigorous exercise keeps you healthy. 经常剧烈运动有益健康。
2. The proposal sparked a vigorous debate. 该提议引发了激烈的辩论。
3. The economy showed vigorous growth. 经济呈现强劲增长。
