### vital
[ˈvaɪtl]

**Desp**
adj. Extremely important and necessary; full of energy.
至关重要的；充满活力的

**Usages**
- play a vital role 起至关重要的作用
- vital to/for 对……至关重要
- vital signs 生命体征

**Examples**
1. Sleep is vital to good health. 睡眠对健康至关重要。
2. She plays a vital role in the team. 她在团队中发挥着至关重要的作用。
3. The nurses checked his vital signs. 护士检查了他的生命体征。
