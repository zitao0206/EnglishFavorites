### vegetarian
[ˌvedʒəˈteəriən]

**Desp**
adj. eating only plants; n. a person who does not eat meat.
素食的；n. 素食者

**Usages**
- entirely vegetarian 完全素食的
- vegetarian diet 素食饮食

**Examples**
1. They are entirely vegetarian, with their diet including the leaves, roots and bark of trees as well as bulbs, and fern fronds. 它们完全是素食的，食物包括树叶、树根、树皮以及球茎和蕨类叶片。
2. She became a vegetarian for health reasons. 她出于健康原因成为素食者。
3. The restaurant offers a vegetarian menu. 这家餐厅提供素食菜单。
