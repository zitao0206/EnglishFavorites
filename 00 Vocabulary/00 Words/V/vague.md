### vague
[veɪɡ]

**Description**
adj. not clear or definite; indistinct.
adj. 模糊的，不明确的；含糊其辞的

**Extension**
副词：vaguely 隐约地

**Usages**
- a vague idea 模糊的想法
- a vague answer 含糊的回答
- vague memories 模糊的记忆

**Examples**
1. She gave a vague answer to my question. 她对我的问题给了个含糊的回答。
2. I have a vague memory of the house. 我对那栋房子只有模糊的记忆。
3. The instructions were vague and confusing. 指示含糊不清，令人困惑。
