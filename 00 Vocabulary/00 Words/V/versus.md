### versus
[ˈvɜːsəs]

**Desp**
prep. Used to compare two things or to say that two people or teams are competing against each other.
与……相对；对抗（常缩写为 vs.）

**Usages**
- China versus Japan 中国队对日本队
- nature versus nurture 先天与后天
- online versus offline 线上与线下

**Examples**
1. Tonight's game is the Lakers versus the Warriors. 今晚的比赛是湖人队对勇士队。
2. It's a debate of quality versus quantity. 这是一场质量与数量的辩论。
3. The court case is Smith versus Johnson. 这起诉讼案是史密斯诉约翰逊。
