### vacuity
[vəˈkjuːəti]

**Description**
n. emptiness; lack of content, ideas or law.
空虚，空洞，缺失

**Usages**
- vacuity of law 法律空白，法律漏洞
- intellectual vacuity 思想的空洞
- a vacuity of ideas 思想的贫乏

**Examples**
1. Lawyers exploited the vacuity of law in this area. 律师们钻了该领域法律空白的空子。
2. The article was criticized for its intellectual vacuity. 这篇文章因思想空洞而受到批评。
3. He stared into the vacuity of the empty room. 他凝视着空荡房间的虚无。
