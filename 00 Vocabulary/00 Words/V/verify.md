### verify
[ˈverɪfaɪ]

**Description**
v. to check that something is true or accurate.
v. 核实，查证，验证

**Extension**
名词：verification 核实
形容词：verifiable 可验证的

**Usages**
- verify the facts 核实事实
- verify your identity 验证身份
- verify the results 验证结果

**Examples**
1. Please verify that all doors are locked. 请核实所有门都锁好了。
2. The bank needs to verify your identity. 银行需要核实你的身份。
3. The experiment verified the hypothesis. 实验证实了这个假说。
