### vicious
[ˈvɪʃəs]

**Description**
adj. violent and cruel; (of a circle) self-perpetuating.
adj. 恶毒的，凶残的；恶性的

**Extension**
名词：viciousness 恶毒

**Usages**
- a vicious attack 恶毒的攻击
- a vicious circle 恶性循环
- a vicious dog 恶犬

**Examples**
1. He was the victim of a vicious attack. 他遭到恶毒袭击。
2. Poverty can become a vicious circle. 贫困可能演变成恶性循环。
3. That dog looks vicious. 那条狗看起来很凶。
