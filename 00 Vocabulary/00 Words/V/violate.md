### violate
[ˈvaɪəleɪt]

**Desp**
v. to break a law or agreement; to disturb.
v. 违反，违背；侵犯，亵渎

**Extension**
名词：violation 违反

**Usages**
- violate the law 违法
- violate a contract 违约
- violate sb's privacy 侵犯隐私

**Examples**
1. The company violated environmental regulations. 该公司违反了环保法规。
2. He was fined for violating the speed limit. 他因超速被罚款。
3. The hackers violated users' privacy. 黑客侵犯了用户的隐私。
