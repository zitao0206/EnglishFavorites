### verdict
[ˈvɜːdɪkt]

**Desp**
n. An official decision made by a jury or judge; a conclusion or opinion.
裁决，判决；（最终）定论

**Usages**
- final verdict 最终定论
- reach a verdict 作出裁决
- a guilty verdict 有罪裁决

**Examples**
1. The jury reached a verdict of not guilty. 陪审团作出无罪裁决。
2. The final verdict on the case surprised everyone. 此案的最终判决令所有人吃惊。
3. The critics' verdict on the film was mixed. 影评人对这部电影的评价毁誉参半。
