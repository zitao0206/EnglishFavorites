### expire
[ɪkˈspaɪə(r)]

**Desp**
v. to come to an end or stop being valid; to die.
到期，失效；断气，死亡

**Usages**
- expire in a month 一个月后到期
- the contract expires 合同到期
- expire on 到期于……

**Examples**
1. My passport expires next year. 我的护照明年到期。
2. The offer expires at midnight tonight. 这个优惠今晚午夜截止。
3. Your subscription has expired. 你的订阅已经过期。
