### execution
[ˌeksɪˈkjuːʃn]

**Description**
n. the act of carrying out a plan.
执行，落实，实施

**Usages**
- execution plan 执行计划
- the execution of a task 任务的执行

**Examples**
1. The plan is great, but the execution is flawed. 方案很棒，但是执行有缺陷。
2. Good ideas need good execution. 好点子需要好执行。
3. The execution of the project took two years. 项目落地花了两年。
