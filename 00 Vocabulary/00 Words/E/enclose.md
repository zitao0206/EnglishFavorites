### enclose
[ɪnˈkləʊz]

**Desp**
v. to put something inside a letter or parcel; to surround something.
随函附上；围住，包围

**Usages**
- enclose a photo 附上一张照片
- enclose a copy 附上副本
- be enclosed by walls 被围墙围住

**Examples**
1. Please enclose your resume with the application letter. 请随申请信附上你的简历。
2. I enclose a copy of the contract for your records. 随函附上合同副本一份供你存档。
3. The garden is enclosed by a tall hedge. 花园被一道高树篱围了起来。
