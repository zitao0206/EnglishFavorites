### ego
[ˈiːɡəʊ]

**Desp**
n. a person's sense of self-importance.
自我，自尊心

**Usages**
- big-ego / huge-ego 自大、极强的自尊心
- put one's ego aside 放下身段，放下自我执念

**Examples**
1. Don't let your ego stop you from asking questions. 不要碍于自尊而不敢提问。
2. He has a huge ego and never admits mistakes. 他自尊心极强，从不认错。
3. Put your ego aside and listen to feedback. 放下自我，倾听反馈。
