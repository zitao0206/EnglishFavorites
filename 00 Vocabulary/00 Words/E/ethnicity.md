### ethnicity
[eθˈnɪsəti]

**Desp**
n. the fact of belonging to a particular ethnic group.
种族，民族

**Usages**
- nationality n. 国籍
- ethnicity and race 民族与种族

**Examples**
1. People of all ethnicities live in this city. 这座城市生活着各个民族的人。
2. The survey asked about age, gender and ethnicity. 调查询问了年龄、性别和民族。
