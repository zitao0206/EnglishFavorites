### embark
[ɪmˈbɑːrk]

**Description**
v. to board a ship or plane; to start something new.
v. 上船，登机；开始，从事

**Usages**
- embark on a new career 开始新事业
- embark on a journey 开始旅程
- embark for Shanghai 乘船前往上海

**Examples**
1. She embarked on a new career in teaching. 她开始了教书的新生涯。
2. Passengers embarked at Southampton. 乘客在南安普敦登船。
3. The company is embarking on a major expansion. 公司正着手大规模扩张。
