### exclude
[ɪkˈskluːd]

**Desp**
v. to keep out; to leave out.
v. 排除在外，不包括

**Extension**
名词：exclusion 排除
形容词：exclusive 独有的
反义词：include 包括

**Usages**
- exclude sb from 把某人排除在外
- exclude the possibility 排除可能性

**Examples**
1. They excluded him from the meeting. 他们没让他参加会议。
2. The price excludes delivery charges. 此价格不含运费。
3. We can't exclude the possibility of rain. 我们不能排除下雨的可能。
