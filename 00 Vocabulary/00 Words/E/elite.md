### elite
[eɪˈliːt]

**Description**
n. A group of people with the highest status or ability in a society or organization.
精英

**Usages**
- elite team 精锐团队
- forces elite 精锐部队
- the ruling elite 统治精英

**Examples**
1. The mission was carried out by an elite team. 这次行动由一支精锐团队执行。
2. Only the elite of the army joined the special forces. 只有部队的精英才能加入特种部队。
3. The party is controlled by a small elite. 该党派由一小撮精英掌控。
