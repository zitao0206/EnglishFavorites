### envelope
[ˈenvələʊp]

**Desp**
n. a flat paper container used for sending letters or documents.
信封；封套

**Usages**
- seal an envelope 封上信封
- a stamped envelope 贴好邮票的信封
- push the envelope 挑战极限

**Examples**
1. She put the letter into an envelope. 她把信装进信封。
2. Don't forget to write the address on the envelope. 别忘了在信封上写地址。
3. The design team is pushing the envelope of technology. 设计团队正在挑战技术的极限。
