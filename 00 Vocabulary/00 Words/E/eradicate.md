### eradicate
[ɪˈrædɪkeɪt]

**Desp**
v. to destroy completely; to put an end to.
根除，消灭

**Extension**
名词形式：eradication

**Usages**
- eradicate from 从……根除
- eradication program 根除计划

**Examples**
1. Cats were eradicated from Little Barrier Island in 1980, and possums were eradicated from Codfish Island by 1986. 1980年猫被从小屏障岛根除，到1986年负鼠从科德菲什岛根除。
2. The disease has been eradicated in most countries. 这种疾病已在大多数国家被根除。
3. Efforts to eradicate poverty continue worldwide. 消除贫困的努力在全球持续进行。
