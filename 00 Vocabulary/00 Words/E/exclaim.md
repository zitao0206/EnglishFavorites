### exclaim
[ɪkˈskleɪm]

**Desp**
v. To say something suddenly and loudly because of surprise, anger, or excitement.
惊叫，呼喊；大声说出

**Usages**
- exclaim in surprise 惊讶地喊叫
- exclaim with delight 高兴地欢呼
- exclaim that... 大声说……

**Examples**
1. "What a beautiful view!" she exclaimed. “多美的景色啊！”她惊呼道。
2. He exclaimed in surprise at the news. 听到这个消息他惊讶地叫了起来。
3. The children exclaimed with delight when they saw the gifts. 孩子们看到礼物时高兴地欢呼起来。
