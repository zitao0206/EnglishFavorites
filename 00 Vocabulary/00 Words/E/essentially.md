### essentially
[ɪˈsenʃəli]

**Description**
adv. used to say what the most important aspect of something is; basically.
本质上，根本上；基本上

**Usages**
- essentially the same 本质上相同
- essentially different 本质不同
- essentially a matter of 本质上是……的问题

**Examples**
1. The two plans are essentially the same. 这两个计划本质上相同。
2. Essentially, the problem is lack of funding. 本质上，问题是缺乏资金。
3. His answer was essentially a refusal. 他的回答基本上就是拒绝。
