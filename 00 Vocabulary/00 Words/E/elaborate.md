### elaborate
[ɪˈlæbərət]

**Desp**
v. To explain in detail; adj. detailed and complicated.
v. 详述；adj. 复杂的，详尽的

**Usages**
- elaborate on sth 详细说明
- an elaborate design 精心的设计
- an elaborate plan 周密的计划

**Examples**
1. Could you elaborate on your proposal? 你能详细说明一下你的提案吗？
2. They prepared an elaborate meal for the guests. 他们为客人准备了一顿丰盛的饭菜。
