### enlighten
[ɪnˈlaɪtn]

**Desp**
v. to give someone more knowledge or understanding of something.
启发，开导，使领悟

**Usages**
- enlighten sb on/about sth 就某事开导某人
- enlightening experience 有启发性的经历
- enlightened opinion 开明的观点

**Examples**
1. Can you enlighten me on how the system works? 你能给我讲讲这个系统是怎么运作的吗？
2. The teacher enlightened the students about the dangers of smoking. 老师开导学生认识吸烟的危害。
3. The documentary was truly enlightening. 这部纪录片真让人受益匪浅。
