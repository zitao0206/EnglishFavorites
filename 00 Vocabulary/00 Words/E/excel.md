### excel
[ɪkˈsel]

**Desp**
v. to be very good at something.
擅长，突出，胜过

**Usages**
- excel at / be good at 擅长
- excel in 在…方面出色

**Examples**
1. She excels at public speaking. 她擅长公开演讲。
2. The team excels in customer service. 该团队在客户服务方面很出色。
