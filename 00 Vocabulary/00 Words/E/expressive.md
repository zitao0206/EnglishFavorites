### expressive
[ɪkˈspresɪv]

**Desp**
adj. Effectively conveying meaning or feeling; full of expression.
富有表现力的，富于表情的

**Usages**
- expressive eyes 富有表情的眼睛
- expressive language 表达性语言
- highly expressive 富有表现力的

**Examples**
1. She has very expressive eyes that convey every emotion. 她有一双很有表现力的眼睛，能传达各种情感。
2. Music is one of the most expressive art forms. 音乐是最具表现力的艺术形式之一。
3. His expressive face made him a great actor. 他富有表情的脸使他成为一名出色的演员。
4. She has wonderfully expressive eyes.
