### explode
[ɪkˈsploʊd]

**Desp**
v. to burst or shatter violently and noisily as a result of internal pressure; to increase suddenly, sharply, and without control.
爆炸；激增；爆发

**Usages**
- explode with anger 勃然大怒
- explode in popularity 人气暴涨
- explode a myth 打破神话

**Examples**
1. The bomb was timed to explode at midnight. 炸弹被定时在午夜爆炸。
2. Sales of the new phone exploded after the launch event. 新手机发布会后销量暴涨。
3. She exploded with anger when she heard the news. 她听到消息后勃然大怒。
