### ethnic
[ˈeθnɪk]

**Desp**
adj. relating to a group with a shared culture or origin.
adj. 民族的，种族的

**Extension**
名词：ethnicity 族裔身份

**Usages**
- ethnic group 族裔群体
- ethnic food 民族风味菜肴
- ethnic conflict 族裔冲突

**Examples**
1. The city is home to many ethnic groups. 这座城市聚居着许多族裔群体。
2. We tried ethnic food at the festival. 我们在节日上品尝了民族风味菜肴。
3. The survey asked about ethnicity. 调查询问了族裔身份。
