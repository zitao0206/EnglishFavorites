### estate
[ɪˈsteɪt]

**Description**
n. a large area of land with a big house; all of the money and property that someone owns.
房地产，财产；庄园

**Usages**
- real estate 房地产
- a country estate 乡间庄园
- personal estate 个人财产

**Examples**
1. He works in real estate. 他从事房地产行业。
2. The family owns a large estate in the countryside. 这个家族在乡下拥有一座大庄园。
3. After her death, her estate was divided among her children. 她去世后，遗产分给了孩子们。
