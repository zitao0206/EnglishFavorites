### extract
[ɪkˈstrækt]

**Desp**
v. to remove or take out; n. a substance taken from something.
v. 提取，提取；摘录 n. 提取物，摘录

**Extension**
名词：extraction 提取，开采

**Usages**
- extract data 提取数据
- extract a tooth 拔牙
- vanilla extract 香草精

**Examples**
1. The dentist extracted the bad tooth. 牙医拔掉了那颗坏牙。
2. They extract oil from the seeds. 他们从种子里提取油脂。
3. She read an extract from her new novel. 她朗读了新小说的一段节选。
