### enhance
[ɪnˈhæns]

**Desp**
v. to improve the quality or value of something.
v. 提高，增强，增进

**Extension**
名词：enhancement 增强

**Usages**
- enhance quality 提升质量
- enhance one's reputation 提升声誉
- enhance the user experience 改善用户体验

**Examples**
1. The software was enhanced with new features. 软件增加了新功能得到增强。
2. Good lighting enhances the room's appearance. 好的照明能提升房间的观感。
3. Reading widely enhances your vocabulary. 广泛阅读能扩充词汇量。
