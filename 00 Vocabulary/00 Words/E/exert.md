### exert
[ɪɡˈzɜːrt]

**Desp**
v. to use power or influence; to make an effort.
v. 施加（影响、压力）；努力，尽力

**Extension**
名词：exertion 努力，费力

**Usages**
- exert influence 施加影响
- exert pressure on 对…施压
- exert oneself 尽力，努力

**Examples**
1. Parents exert a strong influence on children. 父母对孩子有很强的影响。
2. The media exerts pressure on politicians. 媒体对政客施加压力。
3. You must exert yourself to pass the exam. 你必须全力以赴才能通过考试。
