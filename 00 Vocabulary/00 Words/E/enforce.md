### enforce
[ɪnˈfɔːrs]

**Description**
v. to make people obey a law or rule.
v. 执行，实施（法律）；强迫

**Extension**
名词：enforcement 执行，强制

**Usages**
- enforce the law 执法
- enforce a rule 执行规定
- strictly enforce 严格执行

**Examples**
1. Police are responsible for enforcing the law. 警察负责执法。
2. The school strictly enforces its dress code. 学校严格执行着装规定。
3. The new policy will be enforced from May. 新政策将从五月起实施。
