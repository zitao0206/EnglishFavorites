### evacuate
[ɪˈvækjueɪt]

**Description**
v. to move people from a place of danger to a safer place.
撤离，转移

**Extension**
同义词：transfer
名词形式：evacuation
execute [ˈeksɪkjuːt] v. 执行，实施；处决

**Usages**
- evacuate to 转移至
- be evacuated 被撤离

**Examples**
1. As a result, during 1980-97, the surviving population was evacuated to three island sanctuaries. 结果，在1980至97年间，幸存种群被转移到了三个岛屿保护区。
2. The village was evacuated due to flooding. 因洪水，村庄被疏散。
3. Residents were evacuated to higher ground. 居民被转移到地势较高的地方。
