### entitle
[ɪnˈtaɪtl]

**Desp**
v. to give someone the right to have or do something; to give a title to a book, film, etc.
使有权利，使有资格；给……命名

**Usages**
- be entitled to 有权享有……
- entitle sb to sth 使某人有权获得某物
- a book entitled 名为……的书

**Examples**
1. Employees are entitled to paid leave. 员工有权享受带薪休假。
2. The ticket entitles you to a free drink. 凭此票可免费获得一杯饮料。
3. She wrote a book entitled "The Silent Sea". 她写了一本名为《寂静之海》的书。
