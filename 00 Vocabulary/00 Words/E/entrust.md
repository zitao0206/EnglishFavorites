### entrust
[ɪnˈtrʌst]

**Desp**
v. to give someone responsibility for a task or object, trusting them to handle it.
委托，交付，交由……保管/办理

**Usages**
- entrust sb with sth 委托某人某事
- entrust sth to sb 把某事交托给某人
- entrust him with the confidential files 托付他保管机密文件

**Examples**
1. The boss entrusted him with the confidential files. 老板托付他保管机密文件。
2. She entrusted her savings to the bank. 她把积蓄交由银行保管。
3. Parents entrust the school with their children's education. 家长把孩子的教育托付给学校。
