### endorse
[ɪnˈdɔːrs]

**Description**
v. to publicly support or approve of something; to appear in an advertisement saying you like a product.
支持，赞同；代言；背书

**Usages**
- endorse a candidate 支持候选人
- endorse a product 代言产品
- fully endorse 完全赞同

**Examples**
1. The senator endorsed the new policy. 参议员支持新政策。
2. The athlete endorses a sports brand. 这位运动员代言一个运动品牌。
3. I fully endorse your proposal. 我完全赞同你的提议。
