### exacerbate
[ɪɡˈzæsəbeɪt]

**Desp**
v. to make a problem or situation worse.
使恶化，加剧

**Usages**
- exacerbate the problem 加剧问题

**Examples**
1. The delay only exacerbated the situation. 延误只会使局势恶化。
2. Poor diet can exacerbate health problems. 不良饮食会加剧健康问题。
