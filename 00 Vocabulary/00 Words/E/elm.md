### elm
[elm]

**Desp**
n. a large tree with rough leaves, common in northern regions.
榆树

**Usages**
- elm tree 榆树
- elm wood 榆木

**Examples**
1. The disease destroyed millions of elm trees. 这种疾病毁掉了数百万棵榆树。
2. Elm wood is used for making furniture. 榆木用于制作家具。
3. The elm avenue was planted two centuries ago. 这条榆树大道种于两个世纪前。
