### energize
[ˈɛnərdʒaɪz]

**Desp**
v. to give someone energy and enthusiasm; to supply with electrical or other energy.
v. 使充满活力，激励；供能

**Extension**
名词：energy（能量，活力）
形容词：energetic（精力充沛的）
反义词：drain, exhaust, deplete

**Usages**
- energize someone 激励某人
- energize the team 激励团队
- feel energized 感到精力充沛

**Examples**
1. The coach's speech energized the whole team. 教练的讲话激励了整个团队。
2. A brisk walk will energize you in the morning. 早晨快走一会儿会让你精力充沛。
3. She felt energized after a good night's sleep. 睡了个好觉后她感到活力满满。
