### exotic
[ɪɡˈzɑːtɪk]

**Description**
adj. from a distant foreign country; strikingly unusual.
adj. 异国的，奇异的

**Usages**
- exotic animals 异域动物
- exotic flavors 异国风味
- exotic destinations 异域旅行地

**Examples**
1. The restaurant serves exotic dishes from Southeast Asia. 这家餐厅供应东南亚的异国风味菜肴。
2. The garden is full of exotic plants. 花园里种满了奇花异草。
3. They travel to exotic destinations every year. 他们每年都去异国情调的目的地旅行。
