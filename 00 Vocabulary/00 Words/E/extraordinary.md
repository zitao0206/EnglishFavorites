### extraordinary
[ɪkˈstrɔːdnri]

**Desp**
adj. very unusual or remarkable.
极好的，杰出的，异常的

**Usages**
- extraordinary achievement 突破性发展

**Examples**
1. The team made extraordinary achievements this year. 团队今年取得了突破性发展。
2. She has an extraordinary talent for music. 她有非凡的音乐天赋。
3. It's extraordinary how quickly technology changes. 技术变化之快令人惊叹。
