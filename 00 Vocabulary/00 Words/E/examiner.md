### examiner
[ɪɡˈzæmɪnə(r)]

**Desp**
n. a person who examines or tests someone's knowledge or ability.
考官，审查者

**Usages**
- IELTS examiner 雅思考官
- examiner's assessment 考官评分

**Examples**
1. The examiner asked him to describe his hometown. 考官让他描述一下自己的家乡。
2. Examiners look for coherence and lexical resource. 考官关注连贯性和词汇资源。
3. The examiner graded the speaking test. 考官给口语考试打了分。
