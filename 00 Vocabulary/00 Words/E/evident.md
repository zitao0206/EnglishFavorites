### evident
[ˈevɪdənt]

**Desp**
adj. Easy to see, notice, or understand; obvious.
明显的，显而易见的

**Usages**
- it is evident that... 很明显……
- become evident 变得明显
- self-evident 不言自明的

**Examples**
1. It was evident that she was tired. 很明显她累了。
2. His talent became evident at an early age. 他的天赋在很小的时候就显现出来了。
3. The truth is self-evident. 真相是不言自明的。
