### erupt
[ɪˈrʌpt]

**Desp**
v. to burst out suddenly (of a volcano); to happen suddenly and violently (of violence, anger, etc.).
（火山）喷发；爆发，突然发生

**Usages**
- erupt violently 猛烈喷发
- erupt in anger 勃然大怒
- violence erupted 爆发暴力冲突

**Examples**
1. The volcano erupted without warning. 火山毫无征兆地喷发了。
2. Violence erupted in the city after the match. 比赛结束后城市里爆发了暴力冲突。
3. The audience erupted in applause. 观众爆发出热烈的掌声。
