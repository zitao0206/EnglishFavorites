### entourage
[ˌɑːntəˈrɑːʒ]

**Description**
n. a group of people attending or surrounding an important person; surroundings.
随从，随行人员；（引申）周围，环境

**Usages**
- among his entourage 在他的随从中
- a large entourage 一大批随从
- the president's entourage 总统的随行人员

**Examples**
1. Among his entourage was a retired general. 他的随从中有一位退役将军。
2. The star arrived with a large entourage of assistants. 这位明星带着一大群助理随从抵达。
3. She traveled with a small entourage of close friends. 她带着几位密友组成的小随从出行。
