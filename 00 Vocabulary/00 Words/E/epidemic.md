### epidemic
[ˌepɪˈdemɪk]

**Desp**
n. a widespread outbreak of disease; a rapid spread of a problem.
n. 流行病；（坏事的）泛滥

**Extension**
形容词：epidemiological 流行病学的

**Usages**
- an epidemic of flu 流感疫情
- an epidemic of obesity 肥胖症的蔓延

**Examples**
1. The flu epidemic spread rapidly across the country. 流感疫情迅速蔓延全国。
2. Obesity has reached epidemic proportions. 肥胖已呈泛滥之势。
3. Vaccines helped contain the epidemic. 疫苗帮助控制了疫情。
