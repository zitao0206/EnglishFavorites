### endure
[ɪnˈdʊr]

**Desp**
v. to suffer patiently; to last a long time.
v. 忍受，忍耐；持续存在

**Extension**
名词：endurance 忍耐力

**Usages**
- endure hardship 忍受苦难
- endure the pain 忍受疼痛

**Examples**
1. She endured years of hardship alone. 她独自熬过了多年的艰难岁月。
2. These traditions have endured for centuries. 这些传统延续了数百年。
3. I can't endure the noise any longer. 这噪音我再也受不了了。
