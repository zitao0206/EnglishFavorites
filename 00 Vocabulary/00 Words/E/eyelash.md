### eyelash
[ˈaɪlæʃ]

**Desp**
n. one of the short hairs growing on the edge of the eyelid.
睫毛

**Usages**
- long eyelashes 长睫毛
- fake eyelashes 假睫毛

**Examples**
1. Camels have long eyelashes to keep sand out of their eyes. 骆驼长睫毛阻挡沙子入眼。
2. She blinked her long eyelashes. 她眨着长长的睫毛。
