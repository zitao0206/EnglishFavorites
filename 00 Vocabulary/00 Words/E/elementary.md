### elementary
[ˌelɪˈmentri]

**Desp**
adj. simple and basic; relating to the early stages of education.
简单的，基础的；初级的

**Usages**
- elementary school 小学
- elementary level 初级水平
- elementary mathematics 基础数学

**Examples**
1. The question is quite elementary. 这个问题很简单。
2. She teaches at an elementary school. 她在小学教书。
3. This is elementary mathematics. 这是基础数学。
