### enviable
[ˈenviəbl]

**Description**
adj. causing envy; very desirable.
adj. 令人羡慕的

**Extension**
动词：envy 羡慕
名词：envy 羡慕

**Usages**
- an enviable position 令人羡慕的地位
- an enviable reputation 令人艳羡的声誉

**Examples**
1. She has an enviable ability to stay calm. 她有一种令人羡慕的保持冷静的能力。
2. The company enjoys an enviable reputation for quality. 这家公司享有令人艳羡的质量声誉。
3. He is in the enviable position of having two job offers. 他手握两份录用通知，令人羡慕。
