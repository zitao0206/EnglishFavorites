### efficient
[ɪˈfɪʃnt]

**Desp**
adj. achieving maximum productivity with minimum wasted effort or expense.
高效的，有效率的

**Usages**
- efficient at one's job 工作高效
- efficient use of resources 资源高效利用
- highly efficient 高效的

**Examples**
1. The new system is much more efficient than the old one. 新系统比旧系统高效得多。
2. She is very efficient at managing her time. 她在时间管理方面非常高效。
3. We need a more efficient way to process the data. 我们需要一种更高效的方式来处理数据。
