### extinct
[ɪkˈstɪŋkt]

**Desp**
adj. no longer in existence; (of a volcano) no longer active.
adj. 灭绝的，绝种的；（火山）不再活跃的

**Extension**
动词：extinguish 熄灭，扑灭
名词：extinction 灭绝

**Usages**
- an extinct species 灭绝物种
- become extinct 灭绝
- extinct volcanoes 死火山

**Examples**
1. Dinosaurs have been extinct for millions of years. 恐龙已灭绝数百万年。
2. Many languages are becoming extinct. 许多语言正在消亡。
3. Scientists try to protect species from extinction. 科学家努力保护物种免于灭绝。
