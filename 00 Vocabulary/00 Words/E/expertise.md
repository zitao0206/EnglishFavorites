### expertise
[ˌekspɜːˈtiːz]

**Desp**
n. special skill or knowledge that you get from experience or training.
专业知识，专业技能；专长

**Usages**
- technical expertise 技术专长
- expertise in 在……方面的专长
- share expertise 分享专业经验

**Examples**
1. The team has decades of expertise in AI. 这个团队在人工智能领域有数十年的专业经验。
2. We need someone with financial expertise. 我们需要一位有财务专长的人。
3. She shared her expertise with the new employees. 她与新人分享了自己的专业经验。
