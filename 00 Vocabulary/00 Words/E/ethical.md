### ethical
[ˈeθɪkl]

**Desp**
adj. relating to moral principles; morally right and acceptable.
伦理的，道德的；合乎道德的

**Usages**
- ethical issues 伦理问题
- ethical standards 道德标准
- an ethical dilemma 伦理困境

**Examples**
1. The doctor faced an ethical dilemma. 医生面临一个伦理困境。
2. It is not ethical to lie to your customers. 对客户撒谎是不道德的。
3. The company follows strict ethical standards. 公司遵循严格的道德标准。
