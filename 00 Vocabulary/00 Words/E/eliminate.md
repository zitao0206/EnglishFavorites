### eliminate
[ɪˈlɪmɪneɪt]

**Desp**
v. to completely remove or get rid of something.
消除，排除；淘汰

**Usages**
- eliminate poverty 消除贫困
- eliminate the risk 排除风险
- be eliminated from 从……中被淘汰

**Examples**
1. The government aims to eliminate poverty. 政府的目标是消除贫困。
2. Automation will eliminate many routine jobs. 自动化将淘汰许多常规工作。
3. Our team was eliminated in the first round. 我们队第一轮就被淘汰了。
