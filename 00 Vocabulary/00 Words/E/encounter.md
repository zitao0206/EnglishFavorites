### encounter
[ɪnˈkaʊntər]

**Desp**
v. to meet or experience something unexpectedly; n. a meeting.
v. 遭遇，偶然遇见 n. 相遇，遭遇

**Usages**
- encounter difficulties 遇到困难
- encounter problems 遭遇问题
- a chance encounter 偶遇

**Examples**
1. We encountered several problems during the trip. 旅途中我们遇到了几个问题。
2. I encountered an old friend at the airport. 我在机场偶遇了一位老朋友。
3. Their first encounter was at a conference. 他们的初次相识是在一次会议上。
