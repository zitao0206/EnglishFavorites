### fallible
[ˈfæləbl]

**Desp**
adj. able to make mistakes or be wrong.
会犯错误的；难免出错的

**Usages**
- human beings are fallible 人非圣贤，孰能无过
- a fallible memory 不可靠的记忆
- fallible judgment 可能出错的判断

**Examples**
1. All human beings are fallible. 人非圣贤，孰能无过。
2. The system is fallible and needs regular checks. 这个系统可能会出错，需要定期检查。
3. Even experts are fallible in their judgments. 即便是专家，判断也难免出错。
