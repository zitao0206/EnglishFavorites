### fuss
[fʌs]

**Description**
n. unnecessary excitement or worry about something unimportant.
大惊小怪，紧张不安，小题大做

**Usages**
- make a fuss 小题大做
- make a fuss over sth 为某事大惊小怪
- without fuss 不慌不忙

**Examples**
1. He made a fuss over a small mistake. 他为一点小错小题大做。
2. Don't make a fuss about it—it's nothing. 别大惊小怪，没什么大不了。
3. She got into the car without any fuss. 她不慌不忙地上了车。
