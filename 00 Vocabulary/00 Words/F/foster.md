### foster
[ˈfɔːstər / ˈfɑːstər]

**Description**
v. to encourage development; to care for a child not one's own.
v. 培养，促进；寄养，抚育

**Usages**
- foster creativity 培养创造力
- foster a child 寄养孩子
- foster cooperation 促进合作

**Examples**
1. Good teachers foster a love of learning. 好老师培养对学习的热爱。
2. The program aims to foster innovation. 该项目旨在促进创新。
3. They fostered three children over ten years. 十年间他们寄养了三个孩子。
