### fascinate
[ˈfæsɪneɪt]

**Description**
v. to attract and hold someone's interest strongly.
v. 迷住，深深吸引

**Extension**
形容词：fascinating 迷人的
名词：fascination 魅力，着迷

**Usages**
- be fascinated by 被…迷住
- be fascinated with 对…着迷

**Examples**
1. The magician's performance fascinated the children. 魔术师的表演让孩子们着迷。
2. I've always been fascinated by ancient history. 我一直对古代历史深深着迷。
3. Her smile fascinated everyone in the room. 她的微笑迷住了屋里的每个人。
