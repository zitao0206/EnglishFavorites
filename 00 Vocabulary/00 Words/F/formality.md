### formality
[fɔːˈmæləti]

**Desp**
n. a formal or official procedure; something done as a matter of custom rather than necessity.
正式礼节，例行公事

**Usages**
- just a formality 只是走个形式
- a mere formality 例行公事
- go through the formalities 办理手续

**Examples**
1. Sometimes gifts are just a formality. 有时候送礼只是走个形式而已。
2. The interview was a mere formality; the job was already his. 面试只是走过场，那份工作已经是他的了。
3. We had to go through the customs formalities. 我们必须办理海关手续。
