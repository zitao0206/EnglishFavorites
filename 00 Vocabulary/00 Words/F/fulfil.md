### fulfil
[fʊlˈfɪl]

**Description**
v. to achieve or satisfy something; to perform a duty.
v. 实现，满足；履行（职责）

**Extension**
形容词：fulfilling 令人满足的
名词：fulfillment 实现，满足

**Usages**
- fulfil one's potential 发挥潜能
- fulfil a promise 履行承诺
- fulfil the requirements 满足要求

**Examples**
1. She finally fulfilled her dream of becoming a pilot. 她终于实现了当飞行员的梦想。
2. The company failed to fulfil its contractual obligations. 公司未能履行合同义务。
3. This job doesn't fulfil me anymore. 这份工作再也无法让我有满足感。
