### fatigue
[fəˈtiːɡ]

**Desp**
n. extreme tiredness; v. to make someone very tired.
疲劳，疲乏；使疲劳

**Usages**
- mental fatigue 精神疲劳
- muscle fatigue 肌肉疲劳
- suffer from fatigue 感到疲劳

**Examples**
1. Driving for hours causes fatigue. 连续开车数小时会导致疲劳。
2. She was overcome by fatigue. 她疲惫不堪。
3. Eye fatigue is common among office workers. 眼疲劳在上班族中很常见。
