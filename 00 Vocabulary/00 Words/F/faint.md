### faint
[feɪnt]

**Desp**
adj/v. very slight or weak; not clear; to suddenly become unconscious.
adj. 微弱的，模糊的；头晕的；v. 晕倒

**Usages**
- a faint smile 淡淡的微笑
- faint voice 微弱的声音
- faint from hunger 饿得晕倒

**Examples**
1. There was a faint smell of coffee in the air. 空气中有一股淡淡的咖啡味。
2. She felt faint from the heat. 她热得快要晕倒了。
3. He fainted when he saw the blood. 他看到血就晕了过去。
