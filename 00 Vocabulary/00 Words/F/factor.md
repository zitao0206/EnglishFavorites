### factor
[ˈfæktə]

**Desp**
n. one of the things that influence a result.
因素

**Usages**
- a major factor 主要因素
- risk factor 风险因素
- a deciding factor 决定性因素

**Examples**
1. Lack of sleep is a major factor in health problems. 睡眠不足是健康问题的一大诱因。
2. Price was the deciding factor for me. 价格对我来说是决定性因素。
3. Several factors led to the crisis. 几个因素导致了这场危机。
