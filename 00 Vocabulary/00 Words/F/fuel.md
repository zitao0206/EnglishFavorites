### fuel
[fjuːəl]

**Desp**
n. material burned to produce energy. v. to increase or make stronger; to supply with fuel.
n. 燃料；v. 加剧，助长；加油

**Usages**
- fuel inflation 加剧通货膨胀
- fuel demand 燃料需求

**Examples**
1. The rumor fueled panic among investors. 谣言加剧了投资者的恐慌。
2. Rising demand fueled the rapid growth of the industry. 不断增长的需求推动了该行业的快速发展。
3. The plane needs more fuel for the long flight. 长途飞行需要给飞机加更多燃料。
