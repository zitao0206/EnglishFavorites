### forthcoming
[ˌfɔːrθˈkʌmɪŋ]

**Desp**
adj. about to happen; willing to give information.
adj. 即将到来的；乐于提供信息的

**Usages**
- the forthcoming elections 即将到来的选举
- be forthcoming about 乐于透露

**Examples**
1. Details of the forthcoming album have been released. 即将发行的专辑的细节已经公布。
2. She was surprisingly forthcoming about her plans. 她对自己的计划出人意料地坦率。
3. No further details are forthcoming at present. 目前没有更多细节。
