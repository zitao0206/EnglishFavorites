### formulate
[ˈfɔːmjuleɪt]

**Desp**
vt. to create, invent, or prepare something carefully; to express in a clear way.
制定，规划，构想；系统阐述

**Usages**
- formulate a plan 制定计划
- formulate a policy 制定政策
- formulate an idea 构想一个想法

**Examples**
1. The committee formulated a new policy. 委员会制定了一项新政策。
2. We need to formulate a clear strategy. 我们需要制定一个清晰的策略。
3. He formulated his thoughts before speaking. 他在开口前理清了思路。
