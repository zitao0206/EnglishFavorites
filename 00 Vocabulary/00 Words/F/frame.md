### frame
[freɪm]

**Desp**
n. 框架；v. 陷害，诬陷，构陷

**Usages**
- frame sb for bribery 诬陷某人受贿
- frame sb for murder 栽赃某人谋杀
- a photo frame 相框

**Examples**
1. They tried to frame him for bribery. 他们企图栽赃他受贿。
2. He claimed he had been framed by his business rivals. 他声称自己被商业对手构陷了。
3. She hung the picture in a wooden frame. 她把画装在木框里挂了起来。
