### fan
[fæn]

**Desp**
n. A machine or object that moves air; a person who admires someone.
风扇，扇子；迷，粉丝

**Usages**
- electric fan 电扇
- fan-shaped adj. 扇形的
- a big fan of 是……的铁杆粉丝

**Examples**
1. Turn on the electric fan, it's hot. 太热了，打开电扇吧。
2. She waved a fan to cool herself. 她扇着扇子纳凉。
3. I'm a big fan of this band. 我是这支乐队的铁杆粉丝。
