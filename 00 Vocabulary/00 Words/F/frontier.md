### frontier
[ˈfrʌntɪə(r)]

**Desp**
n. the border of a country; the edge of what is known or developed.
边境，边界；前沿，尖端

**Usages**
- the frontier of science 科学前沿
- new frontier 新领域

**Examples**
1. AI is the new frontier of technology. 人工智能是科技的新前沿。
2. The frontier between the two countries is heavily guarded. 两国之间的边境戒备森严。
