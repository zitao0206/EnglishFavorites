### frugal
[ˈfruːɡl]

**Description**
adj. careful about spending money or using resources; economical.
节俭的，节约的，省钱的

**Usages**
- a frugal life 节俭的生活
- frugal meal 简单的一餐
- be frugal with money 用钱节省

**Examples**
1. He leads a very frugal life. 他过着十分节俭的生活。
2. She is frugal with her time as well as her money. 她既惜时也省钱。
