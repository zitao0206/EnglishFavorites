### fur
[fɜː]

**Desp**
n. the thick soft hair covering the body of some animals.
（动物）软毛，毛皮

**Usages**
- a fur coat 皮草大衣
- soft fur 柔软的皮毛
- fake fur 人造毛

**Examples**
1. The cat's fur is soft and shiny. 猫的毛柔软而有光泽。
2. She wore a fur coat in winter. 她冬天穿了一件皮草大衣。
3. Many animals grow thick fur in winter. 许多动物冬天会长出厚厚的毛。
