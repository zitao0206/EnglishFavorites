### fluctuate
[ˈflʌktʃueɪt]

**Description**
v. to change frequently up and down.
v. 波动，起伏

**Extension**
名词：fluctuation 波动
形容词：fluctuating 波动的

**Usages**
- prices fluctuate 价格波动
- fluctuate between 在…之间波动
- exchange-rate fluctuations 汇率波动

**Examples**
1. Stock prices fluctuated wildly last week. 上周股价剧烈波动。
2. Her weight fluctuates by a few kilos. 她的体重在几公斤间波动。
3. The temperature fluctuates between day and night. 昼夜温差波动很大。
