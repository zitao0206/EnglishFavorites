### florist
[ˈflɒrɪst]

**Desp**
n. A person or shop that sells flowers.
花商，花店

**Usages**
- at the florist's 在花店
- buy flowers from the florist 在花店买花

**Examples**
1. I bought some flowers from the florist. 我在花店买了花。
2. She works as a florist. 她是花商。
