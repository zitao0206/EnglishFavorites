### fancy
[ˈfænsi]

**Desp**
v/adj/n. to want or like something; elaborate or expensive; imagination or liking.
v. 想要，喜欢
adj. 时髦的，奢华的；华丽的，花哨的 
n. 幻想，爱好

**Usages**
- fancy doing sth 想做某事
- a fancy hotel 豪华酒店
- take a fancy to 喜欢上

**Examples**
1. Do you fancy going for a walk? 你想去散散步吗？
2. She stayed at a fancy hotel in Paris. 她住在巴黎一家豪华酒店。
3. He took a fancy to the little dog. 他喜欢上了那只小狗。
