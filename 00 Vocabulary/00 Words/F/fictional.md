### fictional
[ˈfɪkʃənl]

**Desp**
adj. imagined and not real; from a story.
虚构的，假想的

**Usages**
- a fictional story 虚构故事
- a fictional world 虚拟世界
- fictional characters 虚构人物

**Examples**
1. All the characters in the novel are fictional. 小说中所有人物都是虚构的。
2. The game is set in a fictional world of magic. 游戏设定在一个魔幻的虚拟世界。
3. This is a fictional account of real events. 这是对真实事件的虚构演绎。
