### fast-paced
[ˌfæst ˈpeɪst]

**Desp**
adj. moving or changing rapidly; full of quick action.
快节奏的，节奏紧凑的

**Extension**
反义词：slow-paced, leisurely

**Usages**
- a fast-paced lifestyle 快节奏的生活方式
- fast-paced environment 快节奏的环境
- a fast-paced thriller 节奏紧凑的惊悚片

**Examples**
1. She enjoys the fast-paced life of the big city. 她享受大城市的快节奏生活。
2. The newsroom is a fast-paced, high-pressure environment. 新闻编辑室是一个快节奏、高压力的环境。
3. It's a fast-paced movie that never gets boring. 这是一部节奏紧凑、绝不无聊的电影。
