### flourish
[ˈflʌrɪʃ]

**Desp**
v./n. to grow or develop successfully and strongly; to wave something in the air.
繁荣，兴旺；茁壮成长；挥舞

**Usages**
- flourish under 在……下蓬勃发展
- business is flourishing 生意兴隆
- flourish a pen 挥动钢笔

**Examples**
1. The company flourished during the economic boom. 经济繁荣时期这家公司蓬勃发展。
2. These plants flourish in warm climates. 这些植物在温暖的气候下生长旺盛。
3. Arts and culture flourish in this city. 这座城市文化艺术繁荣。
