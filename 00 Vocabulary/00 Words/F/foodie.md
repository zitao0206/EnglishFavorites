### foodie
[ˈfuːdi]

**Desp**
n. a person who is very interested in food and cooking.
美食家，吃货

**Usages**
- foodie culture 吃货文化
- foodie tour 美食之旅

**Examples**
1. She's a foodie who tries every new restaurant. 她是个爱尝新餐厅的吃货。
2. The city has a strong foodie culture. 这座城市美食文化浓厚。
