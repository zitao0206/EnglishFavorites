### feral
[ˈferəl]

**Description**
adj. existing in a wild or untamed state, esp. after escaping from captivity.
野生的，野化的

**Usages**
- feral cat 野猫
- feral animals 野生动物

**Examples**
1. Unfortunately, predation by feral cats on Rakiura Island led to a rapid decline in kakapo numbers. 不幸的是，拉基乌拉岛上野猫的捕食导致鸮鹦鹉数量急剧下降。
2. Feral dogs roam the streets at night. 野狗夜间在街头游荡。
3. The government launched a feral pest control program. 政府启动了野生害兽控制计划。
