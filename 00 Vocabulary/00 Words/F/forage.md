### forage
[ˈfɒrɪdʒ]

**Desp**
v. to search for food; n. food for livestock.
觅食；n. 饲料

**Usages**
- forage on the ground 在地面觅食
- forage for food 寻找食物

**Examples**
1. They forage on the ground and climb high into trees. 它们在地面觅食并攀爬到高树上。
2. The bears forage for berries in the forest. 熊在森林中寻找浆果。
3. Squirrels forage for nuts before winter. 松鼠在冬天到来前觅食坚果。
