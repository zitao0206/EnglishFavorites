### frustrate
[ˈfrʌstreɪt]

**Desp**
v. to prevent someone's plans; to make someone annoyed.
v. 挫败；使沮丧，使恼火

**Extension**
名词：frustration 挫败感
形容词：frustrated 沮丧的
形容词：frustrating 令人沮丧的

**Usages**
- be frustrated by 被…弄得沮丧
- frustrate one's plans 使计划落空

**Examples**
1. The delay frustrated everyone involved. 延误让所有参与者都很沮丧。
2. Lack of resources frustrated the team's efforts. 资源匮乏使团队的努力受挫。
3. It frustrates me that I can't help more. 我帮不上更多忙，这让我很郁闷。
