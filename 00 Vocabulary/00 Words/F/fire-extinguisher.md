### fire-extinguisher
[ɪkˈstɪŋɡwɪʃə(r)]

**Desp**
n. A device used to put out small fires.
灭火器

**Usages**
- use a fire-extinguisher 使用灭火器
- a fire-extinguisher on the wall 墙上的灭火器

**Examples**
1. There is a fire-extinguisher in every corridor. 每条走廊都配有灭火器。
2. He grabbed the fire-extinguisher and put out the flames. 他抓起灭火器扑灭了火焰。
