### flip
[flɪp]

**Desp**
v. to turn over quickly; to look through pages quickly; to become suddenly angry.
快速翻动，浏览；翻转；（俚）突然发怒

**Usages**
- flip through a magazine 翻阅杂志
- flip a coin 抛硬币

**Examples**
1. She flipped through the pages of the book. 她快速翻阅着书页。
2. He flipped the pancake in the pan. 他把锅里的煎饼翻了个面。
