### futile
[ˈfjuːtl]

**Description**
adj. producing no useful result; pointless.
adj. 徒劳的，无益的

**Extension**
名词：futility 无用，徒劳

**Usages**
- a futile attempt 徒劳的尝试
- futile resistance 无谓的抵抗

**Examples**
1. Their efforts to save the project proved futile. 他们挽救项目的努力付诸东流。
2. It's futile to argue with him. 和他争论是徒劳的。
3. She realized the search was futile. 她意识到搜寻毫无结果。
