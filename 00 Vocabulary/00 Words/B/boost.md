### boost
[buːst]

**Desp**
v/n. to increase or improve; an increase.
v. 提升，推动，宣传 n. 提振，激励

**Usages**
- boost the economy 振兴经济
- boost confidence 增强信心
- a boost to sales 对销售的提振

**Examples**
1. The tax cut will boost consumer spending. 减税将刺激消费支出。
2. The win gave the team a big confidence boost. 胜利极大地提振了球队的信心。
3. The campaign boosted sales by twenty percent. 这次促销让销售额提升了20%。
