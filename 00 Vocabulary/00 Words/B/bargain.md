### bargain


**Description**
v. To negotiate the price of something;
n. something bought at a good price.
v. 讨价还价；n. 便宜货

**Usages**
- bargain with sb 与某人讨价还价
- a real bargain 真正的便宜货
- bargain hunter 淘便宜货的人

**Examples**
1. She bargained with the seller over the price. 她和卖家讨价还价。
2. This coat was a real bargain. 这件外套真是捡到便宜了。
