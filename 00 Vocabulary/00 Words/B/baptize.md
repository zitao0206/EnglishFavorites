### baptize
[bæpˈtaɪz]

**Desp**
v. to give someone a Christian name and welcome them into the church.
v. 给…施洗礼，命名

**Extension**
名词：baptism 洗礼

**Usages**
- be baptized as a Christian 受洗成为基督徒
- baptize a baby 为婴儿施洗

**Examples**
1. The priest baptized the newborn baby. 牧师为新生儿施了洗礼。
2. She was baptized in the local church. 她在当地的教堂受洗。
3. They baptized their daughter Mary. 他们给女儿施洗并取名玛丽。
