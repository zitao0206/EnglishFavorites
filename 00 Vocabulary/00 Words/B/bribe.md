### bribe
[braɪb]

**Desp**
n./v. Something given to persuade someone to act dishonestly; to give such a thing.
贿赂，收买

**Usages**
- take/accept a bribe 收受贿赂
- bribe sb into doing sth 贿赂某人做某事
- offer a bribe 行贿

**Examples**
1. The official was arrested for accepting a bribe. 那名官员因收受贿赂被捕。
2. They tried to bribe the guard into opening the door. 他们试图贿赂保安开门。
3. He refused to take a bribe despite the pressure. 尽管有压力，他还是拒绝收受贿赂。
