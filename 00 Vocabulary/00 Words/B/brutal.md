### brutal
[ˈbruːtl]

**Description**
adj. extremely cruel, harsh, or violent; unpleasantly honest.
残忍的，野蛮的；直率无情的

**Usages**
- a brutal attack 残忍的袭击
- brutal honesty 直言不讳
- brutal winter 严酷的冬天

**Examples**
1. The brutal winter made travel almost impossible. 严酷的冬天让出行几乎不可能。
2. He gave a brutally honest assessment of the situation. 他对形势作了极为直率的评估。
3. The movie contains some brutal scenes. 这部电影有一些残酷的场面。
