### bravado
[brəˈvɑːdəʊ]

**Desp**
n. a display of false confidence or courage.
虚张声势，逞强

**Usages**
- false bravado 虚假的逞强

**Examples**
1. His bravery was just bravado; he was scared inside. 他的勇敢只是虚张声势，内心其实很害怕。
2. She hid her fear behind a mask of bravado. 她用逞强的面具掩盖恐惧。
