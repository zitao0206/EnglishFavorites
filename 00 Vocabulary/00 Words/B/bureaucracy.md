### bureaucracy
[bjʊəˈrɒkrəsi]

**Desp**
n. A system of government or management with many rules and procedures; excessive red tape.
官僚制度，官僚作风；繁文缛节

**Usages**
- government bureaucracy 政府官僚体制
- cut through bureaucracy 摆脱繁文缛节
- bureaucracy and red tape 官僚主义和文牍主义

**Examples**
1. The project was delayed by bureaucracy. 项目因官僚作风而被延误。
2. Starting a business here involves a lot of bureaucracy. 在这里创业要应付大量的繁文缛节。
3. The new policy aims to reduce bureaucracy. 新政策旨在减少官僚程序。
