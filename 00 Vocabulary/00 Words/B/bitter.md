### bitter
[ˈbɪtə]

**Desp**
adj. Having a sharp, unpleasant taste; feeling angry and unhappy.
苦的；痛苦的，苦涩的

**Usages**
- bitter taste 苦味
- a bitter experience 痛苦的经历
- bitter cold 严寒
- **sweets and bitters** 苦与甜（人生)
- bittersweet adj. 苦乐参半的，又苦又甜的

**Examples**
1. The medicine tastes bitter. 这药很苦。
2. Dark chocolate is more bitter than milk chocolate. 黑巧克力比牛奶巧克力更苦。
3. He felt bitter about the failure. 他对那次失败感到愤愤不平。

**Mak sentences**
1. Life is always bitter-sweet.
