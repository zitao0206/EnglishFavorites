### befall

**Desp**
v. to happen to someone, especially something bad.
降临，发生于…身上（多指灾难厄运）

**Usages**
- misfortune befell sb 不幸降临到某人头上
- a tragedy befall sb 悲剧降临到某人身上

**Examples**
1. A great misfortune befell his family. 一场巨大的不幸降临到他家人身上。
2. We prayed that no harm would befall us. 我们祈祷灾祸不要降临到我们身上。
3. Something terrible has befallen him. 有可怕的事情发生在他身上了。
