### brew
[bruː]

**Desp**
v. to make beer or tea; (of trouble) to develop; n. a drink.
v. 酿造（啤酒）；冲泡（茶、咖啡）；酝酿 n. 冲泡的饮料

**Extension**
名词：brewery 啤酒厂

**Usages**
- brew tea 泡茶
- brew trouble 酝酿麻烦
- a fresh brew of coffee 新煮的咖啡

**Examples**
1. She brewed a pot of tea for her guests. 她为客人沏了一壶茶。
2. Trouble had been brewing for months. 麻烦已经酝酿好几个月了。
3. This brewery has brewed beer since 1850. 这家啤酒厂自1850年起酿造啤酒。
