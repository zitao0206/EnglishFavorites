### bustle
[ˈbʌsl]

**Desp**
n/v. Busy and noisy activity; to move around busily.
繁忙，喧嚣；忙碌地移动

**Usages**
- the bustle of the city 城市的喧嚣
- bustle about 忙来忙去
- bustling street 繁华的街道

**Examples**
1. I miss the bustle of the city. 我怀念城市的喧嚣热闹。
2. She bustled about preparing dinner. 她忙着准备晚餐。
3. The market is always bustling with people. 市场里总是人来人往，热闹非凡。
