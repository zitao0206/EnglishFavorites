### burnout
[ˈbɜːrnaʊt]

**Desp**
n. The state of being extremely tired or ill because of overwork; the point at which something stops working.
精疲力竭；职业倦怠；烧毁

**Usages**
- suffer from burnout 遭遇倦怠
- job burnout 职业倦怠
- prevent burnout 防止倦怠

**Examples**
1. Many teachers suffer from burnout. 许多教师遭遇职业倦怠。
2. She took a break to avoid burnout. 她休息了一下以避免倦怠。
3. Burnout is common among doctors. 倦怠在医生中很常见。
