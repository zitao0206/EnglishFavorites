### barrier
[ˈbæriə(r)]

**Desp**
n. something that prevents progress or movement.
障碍

**Usages**
- language barrier 语言障碍
- trade barrier 贸易壁垒
- break down barriers 打破障碍

**Examples**
1. The language barrier made communication difficult. 语言障碍使交流变得困难。
2. Removing trade barriers benefits everyone. 消除贸易壁垒对每个人都有好处。
3. She overcame many barriers to achieve her goal. 她克服了重重障碍实现目标。
