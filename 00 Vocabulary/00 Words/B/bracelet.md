### bracelet
[ˈbreɪslət]

**Desp**
n. A piece of jewelry worn around the wrist.
手链，手镯

**Usages**
- beaded bracelet 珠串手链
- a delicate gold bracelet 一条精致的金手链
- wear a bracelet 戴手链

**Examples**
1. She wears a delicate gold bracelet. 她戴着一条精致的金手链。
2. He bought her a beaded bracelet as a gift. 他给她买了一条珠串手链作礼物。
3. The bracelet was engraved with her name. 手链上刻着她的名字。
