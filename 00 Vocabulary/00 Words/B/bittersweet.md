### bittersweet
[ˈbɪtərswiːt]

**Description**
adj. Arousing pleasure tinged with sadness; both bitter and sweet to the taste.
苦乐参半的，又苦又甜的

**Usages**
- bittersweet memories 苦乐参半的回忆
- a bittersweet ending 苦乐参半的结局
- bittersweet chocolate 苦甜巧克力

**Examples**
1. Graduation day was a bittersweet moment for all of us. 毕业典礼对我们所有人来说是一个苦乐参半的时刻。
2. She has bittersweet memories of her childhood home. 她对童年的家有苦乐参半的回忆。
3. The film's bittersweet ending left the audience in tears. 电影苦乐参半的结局让观众泪流满面。
