### biased
[ˈbaɪəst]

**Desp**
adj. unfairly preferring one person or view.
adj. 有偏见的，偏袒的

**Extension**
名词：bias 偏见

**Usages**
- be biased against 对…有偏见
- biased reporting 有偏见的报道
- gender bias 性别偏见

**Examples**
1. The article is clearly biased against the company. 这篇文章明显对该公司有偏见。
2. Judges must remain unbiased. 法官必须保持不偏不倚。
3. He argued that the report was politically biased. 他认为该报告带有政治偏见。
