### backdoor
[ˈbækdɔː(r)]

**Desp**
n./adj. a door at the back of a building; a secret or indirect way of doing something; a hidden way into a computer system.
后门；秘密途径；（计算机）后门程序

**Usages**
- a backdoor way in 迂回的进入方式
- backdoor access 后门访问
- a backdoor deal 幕后交易

**Examples**
1. He slipped in through the backdoor. 他从后门溜了进去。
2. The hackers installed a backdoor in the system. 黑客在系统中安装了后门程序。
3. They tried to get the contract through the backdoor. 他们试图通过幕后途径拿到这份合同。
