### bluff
[blʌf]

**Desp**
v./n. to try to deceive someone by pretending to be stronger or more confident than you are.
虚张声势，吓唬

**Usages**
- call sb's bluff 戳穿某人的虚张声势

**Examples**
1. He was bluffing when he said he would quit. 他说要辞职是在虚张声势。
2. She called his bluff and he backed down. 她戳穿了他的虚张声势，他就退缩了。
