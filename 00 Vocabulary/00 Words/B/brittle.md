### brittle
[ˈbrɪtl]

**Description**
adj. hard but easily broken; (of emotions) fragile.
易碎的；脆弱的

**Usages**
- thin and brittle 薄而易碎
- brittle bones 脆弱的骨头
- a brittle temper 易怒的脾气

**Examples**
1. The pastry is thin and brittle. 点心薄而易碎。
2. The dry leaves are brittle and break easily. 干树叶很脆，一掰就碎。
3. Older people often have brittle bones. 老年人骨头往往很脆。
