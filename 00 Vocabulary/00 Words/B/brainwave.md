### brainwave

**Desp**
n. an electrical signal in the brain; a sudden clever idea.
脑电波；灵光一现

**Usages**
- brainwave patterns 脑电波模式
- have a brainwave 灵光一现

**Examples**
1. The machine records your brainwave activity. 这台机器记录你的脑电波活动。
2. I had a brainwave in the shower. 我在洗澡时灵光一现。
