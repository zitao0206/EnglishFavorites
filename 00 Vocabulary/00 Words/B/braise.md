### braise
[breɪz]

**Description**
v. to cook meat or vegetables slowly in a covered pot with a small amount of liquid.
炖，焖（先煎后小火慢煮）

**Extension**
同义词：stew（炖煮，液体更多）

**Usages**
- braise beef 炖牛肉
- braised pork 红烧肉
- braise in red wine 用红酒焖

**Examples**
1. The beef is braised for three hours until tender. 牛肉炖了三个小时直到软烂。
2. She braised the pork with soy sauce and sugar. 她用酱油和糖焖烧这道猪肉。
3. First sear the meat, then braise it in broth. 先把肉煎上色，再用高汤焖炖。
