### benchmark
[ˈbentʃmɑːk]

**Desp**
n./v. A standard or point of reference used to measure or judge things.
基准，参照标准；标杆；（v.）以……为基准

**Usages**
- a benchmark for success 成功的基准
- benchmark against 以……为基准
- industry benchmark 行业基准

**Examples**
1. The company's service is the benchmark for the industry. 这家公司的服务是行业的标杆。
2. We benchmark our products against the best in the world. 我们以世界一流产品为基准来衡量自己的产品。
3. The test serves as a benchmark for student progress. 这次考试是衡量学生进步的基准。
