### betterment
[ˈbetəmənt]

**Desp**
n. The act of making something better or the state of becoming better.
改善，改进；进步

**Usages**
- for the betterment of society 为了社会的进步
- social betterment 社会改良
- economic betterment 经济发展

**Examples**
1. He works for the betterment of his community. 他为社区的改善而努力。
2. Education is key to personal betterment. 教育是个人进步的关键。
3. The fund supports the betterment of public health. 该基金支持公共卫生的改善。
