### bash
[bæʃ]

**Desp**
v. to hit hard; to criticize strongly; n. a party; a hard hit.
猛击；严厉批评；狂欢派对

**Usages**
- bash sb for sth 因某事抨击某人
- have a bash 试一试
- a birthday bash 生日派对

**Examples**
1. The media bashed the film for its weak plot. 媒体因剧情薄弱而抨击这部电影。
2. He bashed the door with his fist. 他用拳头猛击门。
3. We threw a bash for her birthday. 我们为她办了场生日派对。
