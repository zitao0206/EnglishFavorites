### ingrain
[ɪnˈɡreɪn]

**Desp**
v. deeply root; firmly establish.
使……根深蒂固；
就……原料染色；生染

ingrained adj. 根深蒂固的

动词：ingrain v. 使……根深蒂固；
**Usages**
- ingrained habits 根深蒂固的习惯
- deeply ingrained 深深扎根的

**Examples**
1. The belief is deeply ingrained in the culture. 这种信仰在该文化中根深蒂固。
2. Ingrained attitudes are hard to change. 根深蒂固的态度很难改变。
3. Dirt had become ingrained in the fabric. 污垢已渗入织物纤维。
4. Mother tried to ingrain respect for our elders in us. 母亲努力教育我们尊重老人。
