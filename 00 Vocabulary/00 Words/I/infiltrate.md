### infiltrate
[ˈɪnfɪltreɪt]

**Desp**
v. to secretly enter an organization or place in order to gain information or influence.
渗透，潜入，混入

**Usages**
- infiltrate the enterprise 渗透企业
- infiltrate an organization 潜入组织
- infiltrate into the crowd 混入人群

**Examples**
1. Spies tried to infiltrate the enterprise. 间谍企图渗透这家企业。
2. The gang infiltrated the local police department. 黑帮向当地警局告密。
3. Undercover agents infiltrated the smuggling ring. 卧底特工潜入了走私团伙。
