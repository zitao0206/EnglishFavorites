### immature
[ˌɪməˈtjʊə(r)]

**Desp**
adj. not fully developed physically or mentally; behaving like a child.
不成熟的

**Usages**
- immature behavior 不成熟的行为
- immature thinking 不成熟的想法
- an immature fruit 未成熟的果实

**Examples**
1. Quitting in anger was rather immature behavior. 赌气辞职是相当不成熟的行为。
2. His immature thinking cost the team dearly. 他不成熟的想法让团队付出了沉重代价。
3. The fruit is still immature and too sour to eat. 果子还没熟，太酸不能吃。
