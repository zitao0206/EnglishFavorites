### imperative
[ɪmˈperətɪv]

**Description**
adj. extremely important and urgent; n. something that must be done.
adj. 极重要的，紧迫的；n. 必要的事， imperative 语气

**Usages**
- it is imperative that …是当务之急
- a moral imperative 道德上的当务之急

**Examples**
1. It is imperative that we act now. 我们现在必须立刻行动。
2. Safety is our first imperative. 安全是我们的头等大事。
3. It's imperative to keep the records accurate. 保持记录准确无误至关重要。
