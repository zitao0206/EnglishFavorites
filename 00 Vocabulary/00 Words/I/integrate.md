### integrate
[ˈɪntɪɡreɪt]

**Description**
v. to combine parts into a whole; to blend into a group.
v. 使融合，使一体化；融入

**Extension**
名词：integration 一体化，融合
反义词：disintegrate 瓦解

**Usages**
- integrate ... into 使融入
- integrate theory with practice 理论联系实际
- fully integrated 完全整合的

**Examples**
1. The app integrates maps, weather and messaging. 这款应用整合了地图、天气和消息功能。
2. New immigrants try to integrate into society. 新移民努力融入社会。
3. The two companies integrated their operations. 两家公司整合了业务。
