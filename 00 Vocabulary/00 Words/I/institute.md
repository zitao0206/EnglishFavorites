### institute
[ˈɪnstɪtuːt]

**Description**
v. to start a system or policy; n. an organization for research or education.
v. 建立，制定 n. 研究所，学院

**Usages**
- institute reforms 实行改革
- institute an inquiry 展开调查
- a research institute 研究所

**Examples**
1. New safety procedures were instituted after the accident. 事故后实行了新的安全规程。
2. She works at an institute of technology. 她在一所理工学院工作。
3. The company instituted a strict dress code. 公司实行了严格的着装规定。
