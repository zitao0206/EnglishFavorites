### inflate
[ɪnˈfleɪt]

**Description**
v. to fill with air or gas; to increase prices.
v. 使充气；使（物价）上涨，膨胀

**Extension**
名词：inflation 通货膨胀
反义词：deflate 放气，紧缩

**Usages**
- inflate a tire 给轮胎充气
- inflate prices 抬高价格

**Examples**
1. We inflated the air mattress in minutes. 我们几分钟就给气垫床充好了气。
2. Demand inflated housing prices in the city. 需求推高了这座城市的房价。
3. The balloons inflated quickly. 气球很快鼓了起来。
