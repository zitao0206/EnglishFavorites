### incorporate
[ɪnˈkɔːrpəreɪt]

**Desp**
v. to include something as part of a whole.
v. 包含，吸收，把…并入

**Extension**
名词：incorporation 合并， incorporation 公司注册

**Usages**
- incorporate ... into 把…纳入
- incorporate feedback 纳入反馈

**Examples**
1. The design incorporates energy-saving features. 该设计融入了节能特性。
2. We incorporated their suggestions into the plan. 我们把他们的建议纳入了计划。
3. The company was incorporated in 2015. 这家公司于2015年注册成立。
