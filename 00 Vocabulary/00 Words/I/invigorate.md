### invigorate
[ɪnˈvɪɡəreɪt]

**Desp**
v. to give energy or vitality to someone or something.
使精力充沛，使振奋，激发

**Extension**
反义词：debilitate, drain
名词：invigoration
形容词：invigorating

**Usages**
- invigorate the economy 振兴经济
- an invigorating shower 提神的淋浴
- feel invigorated 感到精神焕发

**Examples**
1. The cold shower invigorated me after a long flight. 长途飞行后，冷水澡让我精神焕发。
2. The new policy aims to invigorate the local economy. 新政策旨在振兴当地经济。
3. A brisk walk in the morning is invigorating. 清晨快走令人神清气爽。
