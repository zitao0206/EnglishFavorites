### insolence
[ˈɪnsələns]

**Desp**
n. rude and disrespectful behavior.
n. 傲慢无礼，粗鲁

**Extension**
形容词：insolent 无礼的

**Usages**
- answer with insolence 无礼地顶嘴
- insolence towards elders 对长辈无礼

**Examples**
1. He was punished for his insolence towards the teacher. 他因对老师无礼而受罚。
2. Her insolence shocked the guests. 她的傲慢无礼令宾客震惊。
3. The boss won't tolerate insolence from staff. 老板不会容忍员工的无礼。
