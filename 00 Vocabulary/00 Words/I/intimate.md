### intimate
[ˈɪntɪmət]

**Desp**
adj. close and personal; private; detailed; v. to hint.
adj. 亲密的；私密的；精通的 v. 暗示

**Extension**
名词：intimacy 亲密

**Usages**
- an intimate friend 密友
- intimate details 隐私细节
- intimate knowledge of 精通

**Examples**
1. They shared an intimate conversation late into the night. 他们促膝长谈至深夜。
2. The café has an intimate atmosphere. 这家咖啡馆氛围温馨私密。
3. She has an intimate knowledge of the local market. 她对当地市场了如指掌。
