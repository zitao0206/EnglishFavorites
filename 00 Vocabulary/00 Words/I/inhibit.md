### inhibit
[ɪnˈhɪbɪt]

**Desp**
v. to prevent or slow down a process or action.
v. 抑制，阻止，阻碍

**Extension**
名词：inhibition 抑制，拘谨

**Usages**
- inhibit growth 抑制增长
- inhibit development 阻碍发展

**Examples**
1. Fear can inhibit creative thinking. 恐惧会抑制创造性思维。
2. The drug inhibits the spread of the virus. 这种药物抑制病毒的扩散。
3. Shyness inhibited her from speaking up. 羞怯使她不敢大声说出来。
