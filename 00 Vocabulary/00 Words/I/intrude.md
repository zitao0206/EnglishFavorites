### intrude
[ɪnˈtruːd]

**Desp**
v. to enter without invitation; to disturb.
v. 侵入，闯入；打扰

**Extension**
名词：intruder 入侵者
形容词：intrusive 侵扰的

**Usages**
- intrude on sb's privacy 侵犯隐私
- intrude into a discussion 打断讨论
- an intruder alarm 防盗警报

**Examples**
1. I don't want to intrude on your family time. 我不想打扰你们的家庭时光。
2. An intruder broke into the house at night. 一名入侵者夜间闯入了房子。
3. New buildings intrude on the mountain views. 新建筑破坏了山景。
