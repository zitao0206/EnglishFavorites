### impose
[ɪmˈpoʊz]

**Desp**
v. to force a rule or burden on someone; to take unfair advantage.
v. 强加；征收（税款）；勉强（利用）

**Extension**
名词：imposition 强加

**Usages**
- impose a tax on 对…征税
- impose restrictions 施加限制
- impose one's views on 将观点强加于

**Examples**
1. The government imposed a tax on sugary drinks. 政府对含糖饮料征税。
2. Parents shouldn't impose their wishes on their children. 父母不应把自己的意愿强加给孩子。
3. New restrictions were imposed on imports. 进口受到了新的限制。
