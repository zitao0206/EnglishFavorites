### insecure
[ˌɪnsɪˈkjʊə(r)]

**Desp**
adj. Lacking confidence or certainty; not safe or guaranteed.
缺乏信心的，无把握的，不安全的

**Usages**
- feel insecure 感到不安，缺乏自信
- an insecure job 不稳定的工作
- be insecure about... 对……没有信心

**Examples**
1. I was feeling insecure. 我缺乏安全感。
2. Many workers feel insecure about their jobs. 许多员工对自己的工作感到不安。
3. He is insecure about his English accent. 他对自己的英语口音缺乏信心。
