### implant
[ɪmˈplɑːnt]

**Desp**
v./n. to put something into a person's body by surgery; to fix an idea firmly in someone's mind.
植入，移植；灌输（观念）；（n.）植入物

**Usages**
- implant a chip 植入芯片
- implant an idea 灌输观念
- a dental implant 牙种植体

**Examples**
1. The surgeon implanted a pacemaker in his chest. 外科医生在他的胸腔内植入了心脏起搏器。
2. Parents often implant their own values in their children. 父母常常把自己的价值观灌输给孩子。
3. She got a dental implant last month. 她上个月做了牙种植。
