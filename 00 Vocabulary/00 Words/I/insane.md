### insane
[ɪnˈseɪn]

**Desp**
adj. mentally ill; extremely unreasonable or crazy.
疯狂的，精神失常的

**Usages**
- insane demands 疯狂的要求
- drive sb insane 把人逼疯
- an insane idea 疯狂的想法

**Examples**
1. I'm sick of your insane demands. 我厌烦了你那疯狂的要求。
2. The noise is driving me insane. 这噪音快把我逼疯了。
3. It was an insane plan that somehow worked. 那是个疯狂的计划，居然奏效了。
