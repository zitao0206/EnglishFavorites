### intent
[ɪnˈtent]

**Desp**
n. purpose or intention. adj. focused and determined.
n. 目的，意图，故意；adj. 专注的，坚决的

**Usages**
- intent to do sth 做某事的意图
- be intent on 专注于，一心想

**Examples**
1. He denied any intent to harm anyone. 他否认有任何伤害他人的意图。
2. She was intent on finishing the project on time. 她一心想按时完成项目。
