### ironic
[aɪˈrɑːnɪk]

**Desp**
adj. Happening in the opposite way to what is expected, often causing wry amusement.
讽刺的，具有讽刺意味的

**Usages**
- ironic that... 讽刺的是…
- it's ironic 真讽刺
- an ironic smile 讽刺的微笑

**Examples**
1. It's ironic that the fire station burned down. 消防站被烧毁了，真是讽刺。
2. She found it ironic that he became a peace activist after years in the military. 她觉得他在军中多年后成为和平活动家，颇为讽刺。
3. There was an ironic tone in his voice. 他语气中带着一丝讽刺。
