### implement
[ˈɪmplɪment]

**Description**
v. to put a plan or decision into effect; n. a tool.
v. 实施，执行 n. 工具，器具

**Extension**
名词：implementation 实施

**Usages**
- implement a plan 实施计划
- implement the policy 执行政策
- farming implements 农具

**Examples**
1. The school implemented a new grading system. 学校实行了新的评分制度。
2. We need to implement the changes by June. 我们需要在六月前落实这些改动。
3. The policy was implemented across all regions. 该政策在所有地区得到执行。
