### individual
[ˌɪndɪˈvɪdʒuəl]

**Desp**
n/adj. A single person or thing, considered separately from the group; single, separate.
n. 个人，个体；adj. 单独的，个别的

**Usages**
- individual rights 个人权利
- each individual 每一个人
- individual differences 个体差异

**Examples**
1. Each individual has a role to play in the team. 团队中每个人都有自己的角色。
2. The tax rate depends on individual income. 税率取决于个人收入。
3. We should respect individual differences. 我们应当尊重个体差异。
