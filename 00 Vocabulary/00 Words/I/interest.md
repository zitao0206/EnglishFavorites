### interest
[ˈɪntrəst]

**Desp**
n. the feeling of wanting to know or learn about something; money paid for borrowing; advantage.
兴趣；利益；利息

**Usages**
- rate of interest 利率
- conflict of interest 利益冲突
- a matter of interest 感兴趣的事

**Examples**
1. He showed great interest in photography. 他对摄影表现出极大兴趣。
2. The rate of interest has been cut again. 利率再次下调。
3. There is a conflict of interest between the two roles. 这两个角色之间存在利益冲突。
