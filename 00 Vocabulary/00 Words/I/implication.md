### implication
[ˌɪmplɪˈkeɪʃn]

**Desp**
n. a possible effect or result of something; something implied. (imply v. 暗示，意味着)
可能影响，后果；含义，暗示

**Usages**
- implications for 对…的影响
- by implication 言外之意

**Examples**
1. The new policy has major implications for small businesses. 新政策对小企业有重大影响。
2. He implied that he was not satisfied. 他暗示自己并不满意。
