### insidious
[ɪnˈsɪdiəs]

**Desp**
adj. proceeding in a gradual, subtle way, but with harmful effects; treacherous.
潜伏的，潜在的，外表无害实则有害的

**Usages**
- insidious marketing strategy 阴险的营销手段
- an insidious disease 隐匿性疾病

**Examples**
1. The insidious marketing strategy made people buy things they didn't need. 这种阴险的营销手段让人们买下了并不需要的东西。
2. High blood pressure is often an insidious disease with no obvious symptoms. 高血压常常是一种没有明显症状的隐匿性疾病。
3. Many insidious habits often lead to adverse health outcomes.
