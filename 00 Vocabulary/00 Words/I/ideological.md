### ideological
[ˌaɪdiˈɑːlədʒɪkl]

**Desp**
adj. relating to a system of ideas or beliefs.
adj. 意识形态的，思想体系的

**Extension**
名词：ideology 意识形态

**Usages**
- ideological differences 意识形态分歧
- ideological conflict 思想冲突

**Examples**
1. The two parties have deep ideological differences. 两党存在深刻的意识形态分歧。
2. The debate was ideological rather than practical. 这场争论是意识形态之争而非务实之争。
3. Ideological divides shape the election. 意识形态分歧左右着选举。
