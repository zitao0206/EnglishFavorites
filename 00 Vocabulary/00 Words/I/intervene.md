### intervene
[ˌɪntərˈviːn]

**Description**
v. to become involved in a situation to change it.
v. 干预，介入；调停

**Extension**
名词：intervention 干预

**Usages**
- intervene in a dispute 调停争端
- intervene to prevent sth 出面阻止
- government intervention 政府干预

**Examples**
1. The teacher intervened to stop the fight. 老师出面制止了打斗。
2. The central bank intervened to stabilize the currency. 央行出手干预以稳定汇率。
3. Don't intervene in their private affairs. 别干涉他们的私事。
