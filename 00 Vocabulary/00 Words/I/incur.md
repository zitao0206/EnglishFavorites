### incur
[ɪnˈkɜː(r)]

**Desp**
v. to experience something unpleasant as a result of your actions; to bring on yourself.
招致，引起；蒙受（损失、费用）

**Usages**
- incur costs 产生费用
- incur debt 负债
- incur sb's anger 招致某人的愤怒

**Examples**
1. The company incurred heavy losses last year. 这家公司去年蒙受了巨额亏损。
2. Any damage to the car will incur extra charges. 对汽车的任何损坏都会产生额外费用。
3. His remarks incurred the manager's displeasure. 他的言论招致了经理的不满。
