### imitate
[ˈɪmɪteɪt]

**Desp**
v. to copy the way someone behaves or speaks; to copy something.
模仿，仿效

**Usages**
- imitate sb's style 模仿某人的风格
- imitate the sounds 模仿声音
- imitate an accent 模仿口音

**Examples**
1. Children imitate their parents. 孩子模仿父母。
2. He can imitate the teacher's voice perfectly. 他能惟妙惟肖地模仿老师的声音。
3. The product imitates a famous brand. 这款产品仿冒了一个知名品牌。
