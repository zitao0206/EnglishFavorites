### impulsive
[ɪmˈpʌlsɪv]

**Desp**
adj. acting suddenly without thinking; rash.
冲动的，草率的

**Usages**
- impulsive decisions 冲动的决定
- an impulsive buyer 冲动型买家
- impulsive behavior 冲动行为

**Examples**
1. Don't make impulsive decisions. 不要做出冲动的决定。
2. He regretted his impulsive purchase the next day. 第二天他就后悔自己冲动的购物。
