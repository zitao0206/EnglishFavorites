### incubate
[ˈɪŋkjubeɪt]

**Desp**
v. to sit on eggs to hatch them; to develop slowly.
孵卵，孵化

**Extension**
名词形式：incubation

**Usages**
- incubate eggs 孵蛋
- play no part in incubation 不参与孵化

**Examples**
1. Males play no part in incubation or chick-rearing – females alone incubate eggs and feed the chicks. 雄性不参与孵化或育雏——雌性独自孵蛋和喂养雏鸟。
2. The bird incubates her eggs for three weeks. 这只鸟孵蛋三周。
3. The project is still in its incubation stage. 该项目仍处于孵化阶段。
