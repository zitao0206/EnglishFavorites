### intrigue
[ɪnˈtriːɡ]

**Desp**
v/n. to make someone very interested and curious; secret or underhanded scheming.
v. 激起……的好奇心；n. 阴谋，密谋

**Usages**
- intrigue sb 引起某人兴趣
- political intrigue 政治阴谋
- be intrigued by 被……深深吸引

**Examples**
1. The mystery novel intrigued me from the first page. 这本悬疑小说从第一页就引起了我的兴趣。
2. The story is full of political intrigue. 这个故事充满了政治阴谋。
3. I was intrigued by her unusual background. 她不凡的背景引起了我极大的好奇。
