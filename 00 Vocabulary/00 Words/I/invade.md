### invade
[ɪnˈveɪd]

**Desp**
v. to enter a place by force; to intrude on.
v. 侵略，侵入；侵扰

**Extension**
名词：invasion 入侵
形容词：invasive 侵入的

**Usages**
- invade a country 入侵一个国家
- invade sb's privacy 侵犯隐私

**Examples**
1. The army invaded the neighboring country. 军队入侵了邻国。
2. Paparazzi constantly invade celebrities' privacy. 狗仔队不断侵犯名人的隐私。
3. Tourists invade the small town every summer. 每年夏天游客蜂拥涌入这座小镇。
