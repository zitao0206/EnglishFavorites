### inflame
[ɪnˈfleɪm]

**Desp**
v. To provoke or intensify strong feelings; to cause inflammation in the body.
激怒，使加剧；使发炎

**Usages**
- inflame passions 激化情绪
- inflame tensions 加剧紧张局势
- inflamed tissue 发炎的组织

**Examples**
1. His speech inflamed the crowd's anger. 他的演讲激化了群众的愤怒。
2. The injury inflamed the surrounding tissue. 伤势使周围组织发炎了。
3. The new policy inflamed tensions between the two groups. 新政策加剧了两个群体之间的紧张关系。
