### impetuous
[ɪmˈpetʃuəs]

**Desp**
adj. acting or done quickly and without thought or care.
冲动的，鲁莽的，轻率的

**Extension**
反义词：cautious, deliberate, circumspect
名词：impetuosity
副词：impetuously

**Usages**
- an impetuous decision 冲动的决定
- impetuous behavior 鲁莽行为
- young and impetuous 年轻气盛

**Examples**
1. He regretted his impetuous decision to quit his job. 他后悔自己冲动辞职的决定。
2. Don't be impetuous—think it over before you act. 别鲁莽行事——先想清楚再行动。
3. His impetuous behavior often gets him into trouble. 他鲁莽的行为常常给他惹麻烦。
