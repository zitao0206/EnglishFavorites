### inspect
[ɪnˈspekt]

**Description**
v. to look at something closely and carefully, especially to check quality or condition.
检查，查看，审视，视察

**Extension**
同义词：examine, scrutinize
名词：inspection
名词（人）：inspector

**Usages**
- inspect the goods 检查货物
- inspect the scene 勘察现场
- a safety inspection 安全检查

**Examples**
1. Officials inspected the factory for safety violations. 官员检查了工厂的安全违规情况。
2. The customs officer inspected my luggage. 海关人员检查了我的行李。
3. Engineers inspect the bridge every year. 工程师每年都对这座桥进行检修。
