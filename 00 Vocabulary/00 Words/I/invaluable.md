### invaluable
[ɪnˈvæljuəbl]

**Desp**
adj. Extremely useful or valuable.
极有价值的，无价的

**Usages**
- invaluable experience 宝贵的经验
- be invaluable to 对……极为宝贵
- an invaluable asset 无价之宝

**Examples**
1. Her advice was invaluable to me. 她的建议对我极为宝贵。
2. The internship gave me invaluable experience. 这次实习给了我宝贵的经验。
3. Good teachers are an invaluable asset to society. 好老师是社会的无价之宝。
