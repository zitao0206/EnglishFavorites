### involution
[ˌɪnvəˈluːʃn]

**Desp**
n. The process of being rolled inward; in Chinese internet culture, excessive competition leading to diminishing returns.
内卷；内旋（数学/生物术语）

**Usages**
- education involution 教育内卷
- workplace involution 职场内卷
- anti-involution 反内卷

**Examples**
1. The term 'involution' has become a buzzword in Chinese social media. “内卷”一词已成为中国社交媒体的热词。
2. Workplace involution forces employees to work longer hours for the same pay. 职场内卷迫使员工以同样的薪酬工作更长时间。
3. Many young people are rejecting the culture of involution. 许多年轻人正在抵制内卷文化。
