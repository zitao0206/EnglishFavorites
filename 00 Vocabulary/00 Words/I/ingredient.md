### ingredient
[ɪnˈɡriːdiənt]

**Description**
n. one of the foods used in a recipe; a factor in a result.
n. （烹饪）原料，配料；要素，因素

**Usages**
- fresh ingredients 新鲜食材
- the key ingredient 关键因素
- active ingredients 有效成分

**Examples**
1. Check the ingredients before buying packaged food. 购买包装食品前先看配料表。
2. The secret ingredient is lemon zest. 秘密配料是柠檬皮屑。
3. Trust is an essential ingredient in friendship. 信任是友谊中不可或缺的因素。
