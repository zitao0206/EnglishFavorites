### insure
[ɪnˈʃʊr]

**Description**
v. to buy insurance protection.
v. 给…投保，为…保险

**Extension**
名词：insurance 保险
近义词：ensure 确保

**Usages**
- insure against 投保…险
- insure your house 给房子投保
- insured items 投保物品

**Examples**
1. You should insure your car against theft. 你应该给你的车投保盗抢险。
2. The painting is insured for two million dollars. 这幅画投保了2000万美元。
3. Make sure you're fully insured before the trip. 出行前务必买足保险。
