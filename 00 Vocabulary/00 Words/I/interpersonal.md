### interpersonal
[ˌɪntərˈpɜːrsənəl]

**Desp**
adj. relating to relationships or communication between people.
人际的，人与人之间的

**Extension**
同义词：relational

**Usages**
- interpersonal skills 人际交往能力
- interpersonal relationships 人际关系
- interpersonal communication 人际沟通

**Examples**
1. Good interpersonal skills are essential in the workplace. 良好的人际交往能力在职场中至关重要。
2. The workshop focuses on interpersonal communication. 这个研讨会专注于人际沟通。
3. Conflicts often stem from poor interpersonal relationships. 冲突往往源于糟糕的人际关系。
