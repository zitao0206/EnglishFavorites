### invasive
[ɪnˈveɪsɪv]

**Desp**
adj. tending to spread aggressively and harmfully; (medical) involving cutting into the body.
侵入的，入侵的

**Usages**
- Minimally-invasive surgery 微创手术
- invasive species 入侵物种
- invasive plants 入侵植物

**Examples**
1. Invasive species are driving away native species. 入侵物种正在赶走本土物种。
2. The hospital offers minimally-invasive surgery. 这家医院提供微创手术。
