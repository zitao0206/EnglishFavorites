### increment
[ˈɪŋkrəmənt]

**Desp**
n. an increase in amount or value; a regular increase in salary. adj. (incremental) 渐进的.
增长，增加额；增量

**Usages**
- annual increment 年度加薪
- incremental adj. 渐进的，递增的

**Examples**
1. His salary increased by a small increment each year. 他的薪水每年小幅增加。
2. The software improves through small increments. 该软件通过小的增量不断改进。
