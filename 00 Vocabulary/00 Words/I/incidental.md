### incidental
[ˌɪnsɪˈdentl]

**Desp**
adj. happening as a minor part of something; accidental.
adj. 附带的，次要的；偶然的

**Extension**
名词：incident 事件

**Usages**
- incidental expenses 杂项开支
- incidental music 配乐
- incidental to 附属于

**Examples**
1. The cost includes all incidental expenses. 费用包含所有杂项开支。
2. The benefits outweigh the incidental risks. 收益远超附带的风险。
3. His remark was incidental to the main discussion. 他的发言对主要讨论来说只是顺带的。
