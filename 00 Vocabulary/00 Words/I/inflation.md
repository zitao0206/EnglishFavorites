### inflation
[ɪnˈfleɪʃn]

**Desp**
n. a general rise in prices.
通货膨胀

**Usages**
- control inflation 控制通胀
- inflation rate 通胀率

**Examples**
1. Inflation is rising faster than wages. 通胀上涨速度超过工资。
2. The government took steps to control inflation. 政府采取措施控制通胀。
3. High inflation hurts people's purchasing power. 高通胀损害人们的购买力。
