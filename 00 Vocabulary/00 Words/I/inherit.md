### inherit
[ɪnˈherɪt]

**Desp**
vt. to receive money, property or traits from parents or predecessors.
继承，遗传而得

**Usages**
- inherit money from sb 从某人那里继承钱财
- inherit a trait 遗传特征

**Examples**
1. She inherited a house from her grandmother. 她从祖母那里继承了一栋房子。
2. He inherited his father's sense of humor. 他遗传了父亲的幽默感。
3. The new manager inherited a difficult situation. 新经理接手了一个棘手的局面。
