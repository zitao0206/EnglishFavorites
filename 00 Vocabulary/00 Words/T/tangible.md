### tangible
[ˈtændʒəbəl]

**Desp**
adj. real and concrete enough to be touched or measured; clear and definite.
有形的，可触摸的；切实的，明确的

**Extension**
反义词：intangible
名词：tangibility

**Usages**
- tangible benefits 实际利益
- tangible assets 有形资产
- tangible evidence 确凿的证据

**Examples**
1. The training program delivered tangible results. 培训项目带来了切实的成效。
2. Trust is an intangible asset, unlike buildings or machines. 信任与建筑或机器不同，是无形资产。
3. We need tangible evidence before drawing conclusions. 下结论之前我们需要确凿的证据。
