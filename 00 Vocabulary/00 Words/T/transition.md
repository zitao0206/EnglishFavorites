### transition
[trænˈzɪʃn]

**Description**
n. The process of changing from one state or condition to another.
过渡，转变，转型

**Usages**
- transition to 向……转型
- smooth transition 平稳过渡
- in transition 处于过渡期

**Examples**
1. I aim to transition to data science, as I want to work with AI. 我打算转向数据科学，从事AI相关工作。
2. The country is in transition to democracy. 这个国家正处在向民主过渡的时期。
3. The transition from school to work is challenging. 从学校到工作的转变很有挑战性。
