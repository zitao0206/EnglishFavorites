### trigger
[ˈtrɪɡər]

**Description**
v. to cause something to start; n. a device that fires a gun; a cause.
v. 引发，触发 n. 扳机；诱因

**Usages**
- trigger a debate 引发争论
- trigger a war 引发战争
- pull the trigger 扣动扳机

**Examples**
1. The news triggered panic in the markets. 这一消息引发了市场恐慌。
2. Stress can trigger headaches. 压力会引发头痛。
3. The incident triggered a full investigation. 该事件引发了全面调查。
