### tumble
[ˈtʌmbl]

**Desp**
v/n. to fall suddenly and helplessly; a fall.
v. 跌倒，滚落；（价格）暴跌 n. 跌倒

**Usages**
- tumble down 摔倒
- prices tumble 价格暴跌
- take a tumble 摔一跤

**Examples**
1. The child tumbled down the stairs. 孩子从楼梯上滚了下来。
2. Share prices tumbled after the announcement. 公告发布后股价暴跌。
3. He took a tumble on the icy road. 他在结冰的路上摔了一跤。
