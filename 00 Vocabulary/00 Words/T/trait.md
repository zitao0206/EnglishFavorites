### trait
[treɪt]

**Desp**
n. a particular quality in someone's character; a distinguishing feature.
特征，特质，特性

**Extension**
同义词：characteristic, quality, feature

**Usages**
- personality traits 人格特质
- inherited traits 遗传特征
- a defining trait 决定性特征

**Examples**
1. Honesty is her most admirable trait. 诚实是她最令人敬佩的品质。
2. Personality traits are shaped by both genes and environment. 人格特质由基因和环境共同塑造。
3. Ambition is a common trait among entrepreneurs. 雄心是创业者的共同特质。
