### testify
[ˈtestɪfaɪ]

**Description**
v. to give evidence in court; to prove something.
v. 作证，证明

**Extension**
名词：testimony 证词

**Usages**
- testify against 作不利于…的证明
- testify in court 出庭作证
- testify to 证明，说明

**Examples**
1. She testified against her former boss. 她出庭指证前上司。
2. He was called to testify in the trial. 他被传唤出庭作证。
3. The ruins testify to the city's ancient glory. 遗址见证了这座古城昔日的辉煌。
