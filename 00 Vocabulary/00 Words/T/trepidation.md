### trepidation
[ˌtrepɪˈdeɪʃn]

**Desp**
n. (formal) A feeling of anxiety or fear about something that will happen.
（正式）内心紧张，忧虑，惶恐

**Usages**
- with some trepidation 怀着一丝忐忑
- filled with trepidation 满心惶恐
- trepidation about/over sth 对某事的忧虑

**Examples**
1. She faced the interview with some trepidation. 她怀着一丝忐忑参加面试。
2. He opened the envelope with trepidation. 他忐忑不安地拆开了信封。
3. There was growing trepidation about the exam results. 对考试结果的忧虑与日俱增。
