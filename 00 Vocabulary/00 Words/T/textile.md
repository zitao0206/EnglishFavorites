### textile
[ˈtekstaɪl]

**Desp**
n/adj. cloth or fabric; relating to cloth.
n. 纺织品，织物 adj. 纺织的

**Usages**
- textile industry 纺织业
- textile factory 纺织厂

**Examples**
1. The textile industry is a major employer. 纺织业是吸纳就业的大户。
2. She works in a textile factory. 她在纺织厂工作。
3. The country exports textiles all over the world. 该国向全世界出口纺织品。
