### terrify
[ˈterɪfaɪ]

**Desp**
v. To make someone feel extremely frightened.
使恐惧，使惊吓

**Usages**
- be terrified of 害怕……
- terrify sb into doing sth 吓得某人做某事
- a terrifying experience 可怕的经历

**Examples**
1. The horror movie terrified the children. 恐怖片把孩子们吓坏了。
2. She is terrified of spiders. 她害怕蜘蛛。
3. He was terrified into silence. 他被吓得不敢说话。
