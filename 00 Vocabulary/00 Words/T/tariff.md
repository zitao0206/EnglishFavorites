### tariff
[ˈtærɪf]

**Desp**
n. a tax on goods coming into or going out of a country.
关税

**Usages**
- impose tariffs 加征关税
- tariff war 关税战

**Examples**
1. The US imposed new tariffs on imported steel. 美国对进口钢铁加征了新关税。
2. Higher tariffs will raise the price of goods. 更高的关税将推高商品价格。
