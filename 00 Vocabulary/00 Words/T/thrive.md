### thrive
[θraɪv]

**Desp**
v. to grow, develop, or be successful; to be healthy and happy.
茁壮成长；兴旺发达

**Usages**
- thrive on 靠……茁壮成长
- thrive in 在……中蓬勃发展
- a thriving business 兴旺的生意

**Examples**
1. Some plants thrive in the shade. 有些植物在阴凉处也能茁壮生长。
2. Her business is thriving. 她的生意蒸蒸日上。
3. He thrives on pressure. 他在压力下反而如鱼得水。
