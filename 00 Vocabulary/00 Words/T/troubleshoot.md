### troubleshoot

**Desp**
v. to find and fix problems, especially technical ones.
故障排查

**Usages**
- troubleshoot errors 排查错误
- troubleshooting guide 故障排查手册

**Examples**
1. He is good at troubleshooting software errors. 他擅长排查软件错误。
2. The manual includes a troubleshooting guide. 手册里包含故障排查指南。
