### testimonial
[ˌtestɪˈmoʊniəl]

**Desp**
n. a formal statement praising someone's qualities or work; adj. of or relating to testimony.
n. 推荐信，证明书；（顾客的）好评；adj. 表白的，纪念的

**Extension**
同义词：recommendation, endorsement
名词：testimony（证词）

**Usages**
- customer testimonials 客户好评
- a testimonial letter 推荐信
- video testimonial 视频推荐

**Examples**
1. The website displays testimonials from satisfied customers. 网站展示了满意客户的好评。
2. She wrote a testimonial for his job application. 她为他的求职申请写了一封推荐信。
3. Video testimonials are powerful marketing tools. 视频推荐是强大的营销工具。
