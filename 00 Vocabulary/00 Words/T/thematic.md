### thematic
[θɪˈmætɪk]

**Desp**
adj. relating to or relating to a particular theme or subject.
主题的，专题的

**Extension**
其他形式：thematical (adj.)
副词：thematically

**Usages**
- thematic overview 专题概述
- thematically similar 主题上相似

**Examples**
1. The speaker gave a thematical overview of environmental policies. 演讲者给出了环境政策的专题概述。
2. The two films are thematically similar, though their plots are totally different. 这两部电影在主题上很相似，尽管情节完全不一样。
3. The textbook is organized by thematic units. 这本教材按主题单元组织。
