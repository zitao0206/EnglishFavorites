### tanned
[tænd]

**Desp**
adj/v-ed skin darkened by the sun.
深褐色的；被晒成棕褐色

**Usages**
- get tanned 晒黑
- tanned skin 晒黑的皮肤

**Examples**
1. Her skin was tanned from the beach holiday. 她的皮肤在海边度假中被晒成棕褐色。
2. He came back from vacation all tanned. 他度假回来晒黑了。
3. A tanned face looks healthy. 晒成棕褐色的脸看起来很健康。
