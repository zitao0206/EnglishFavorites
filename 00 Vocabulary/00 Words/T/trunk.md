### trunk
[trʌŋk]

**Desp**
n. the main stem of a tree; the human or animal body excluding head and limbs.
树干，（人或动物的）躯干

**Usages**
- tree trunk 树干
- trunk of the body 躯干

**Examples**
1. The tree trunk has a diameter of two metres. 这根树干直径两米。
2. The elephant lifted the log with its trunk. 大象用鼻子举起了木头。
3. He has a strong, muscular trunk. 他有着强壮的肌肉躯干。
