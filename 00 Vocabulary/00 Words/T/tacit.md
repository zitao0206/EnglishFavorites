### tacit
[ˈtæsɪt]

**Desp**
adj. Understood or implied without being stated openly.
心照不宣的，默示的

**Usages**
- tacit agreement 默许，默契
- tacit approval 默许
- tacit knowledge 隐性知识

**Examples**
1. They reached a tacit agreement not to mention it. 他们达成了心照不宣的默契，不再提这件事。
2. His silence was taken as tacit approval. 他的沉默被视为默许。
3. There is a tacit understanding between them. 他们之间存在一种默契。
