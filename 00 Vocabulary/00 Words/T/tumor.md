### tumor
[ˈtjuːmə(r)]

**Desp**
n. A mass of diseased cells growing in the body.
肿瘤，肿块

**Usages**
- benign/malignant tumor 良性/恶性肿瘤
- remove a tumor 切除肿瘤
- a tumor patient 肿瘤患者

**Examples**
1. The doctors found a tumor in his lung. 医生在他的肺部发现了肿瘤。
2. Fortunately, the tumor is benign. 幸运的是，肿瘤是良性的。
3. The surgeon successfully removed the tumor. 外科医生成功切除了肿瘤。
