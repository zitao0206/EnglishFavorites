### thereby
[ˌðeəˈbaɪ]

**Desp**
adv. as a result of that action; by doing so.
因此，由此，从而

**Usages**
- thereby doing sth 由此做某事
- thereby avoiding... 从而避免……
- （正式书面连接词）

**Examples**
1. He studied hard, thereby passing the exam with ease. 他刻苦学习，从而轻松通过了考试。
2. The company cut costs, thereby increasing profits. 公司削减成本，由此提高了利润。
3. She apologized sincerely, thereby saving the friendship. 她真诚道歉，从而保住了这段友谊。
