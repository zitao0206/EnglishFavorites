### tough
[tʌf]

**Desp**
adj. difficult; strong and durable; strict.
adj. 困难的；坚韧的；强硬的

**Usages**
- a tough decision 艰难的决定
- a tough negotiation 艰苦的谈判
- be tough on 对…严厉

**Examples**
1. It was a tough decision to make. 这是个艰难的决定。
2. The steak was too tough to chew. 这块牛排韧得嚼不动。
3. The government took a tough line on crime. 政府对犯罪采取强硬立场。
