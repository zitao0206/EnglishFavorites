### torture
[ˈtɔːtʃə(r)]

**Desp**
v/n. to cause extreme physical or mental suffering.
折磨，拷打；拷问

**Usages**
- torture sb 折磨某人
- be tortured by guilt 被负罪感折磨
- mental torture 精神折磨

**Examples**
1. The prisoners were tortured for information. 囚犯被拷问以获取情报。
2. She was tortured by guilt for years. 多年来她一直被负罪感折磨。
3. The constant noise was pure torture. 持续的噪音简直是种折磨。
