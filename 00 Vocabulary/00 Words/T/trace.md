### trace
[treɪs]

**Desp**
v. To follow or find the origin of something; to draw an outline.
追踪，追溯；描绘，描摹；查找源头

**Usages**
- trace out 描绘出；梳理出
- trace out a shape 勾画出轮廓
- trace the origin of... 追溯……的源头

**Examples**
1. The police traced the call to a hotel downtown. 警方追查到电话来自市中心一家酒店。
2. Let's trace out a plan before we start. 开始之前我们先草拟一个方案。
3. Historians traced the custom back to the Tang Dynasty. 历史学家将这一习俗追溯到唐代。
