### towel
[ˈtaʊəl]

**Desp**
n. A piece of cloth used for drying; v. to dry with a towel.
n. 毛巾，抹布，纸巾；v. 用毛巾擦

**Usages**
- paper towel 纸巾
- a bath towel 浴巾
- towel off 擦干身体

**Examples**
1. He dried his hands with a towel. 他用毛巾擦干双手。
2. Please hand me a paper towel. 请递给我一张纸巾。
