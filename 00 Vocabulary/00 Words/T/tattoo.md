### tattoo
[tæˈtuː]

**Desp**
n. a picture or pattern drawn on skin with needles and ink. v. to mark skin in this way.
n. 纹身；v. 纹身，给…刺花纹

**Usages**
- get a tattoo 纹纹身
- tattoo artist 纹身师

**Examples**
1. He had a symbol on his arm. 他手臂上纹了一个符号。
2. She got a small tattoo on her wrist. 她在手腕上纹了个小纹身。
3. The tattoo artist is very popular in town. 这位纹身师在城里很受欢迎。
