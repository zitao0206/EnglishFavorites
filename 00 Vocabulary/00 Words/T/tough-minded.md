### tough-minded
[ˌtʌf ˈmaɪndɪd]

**Description**
adj. strong-willed; practical and not easily influenced by emotions.
意志坚强的，务实的，不动摇的

**Usages**
- a tough-minded leader 意志坚强的领导者

**Examples**
1. She is a tough-minded negotiator who never gives in. 她是一位意志坚强、从不妥协的谈判者。
2. Tough-minded decisions are sometimes unpopular but necessary. 务实的决定有时不受欢迎，但却是必要的。
