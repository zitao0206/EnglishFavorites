### thrill
[θrɪl]

**Desp**
n/v. A sudden feeling of excitement and pleasure; to excite someone greatly.
n. 兴奋，激动；v. 使兴奋

**Usages**
- a thrill of excitement 一阵兴奋
- thrilled to hear 听说……很兴奋
- the thrill of victory 胜利的喜悦

**Examples**
1. The roller coaster gave me a real thrill. 过山车让我真正体验到了刺激。
2. We were thrilled to hear the good news. 听到这个好消息我们兴奋不已。
3. He gets a thrill out of skydiving. 他从跳伞中获得极大的刺激感。
