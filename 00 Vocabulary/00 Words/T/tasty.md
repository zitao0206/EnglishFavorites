### tasty
[ˈteɪsti]

**Desp**
adj. having a pleasant taste; delicious.
美味可口的

**Usages**
- a tasty meal 美味的一餐
- tasty snacks 可口的零食

**Examples**
1. The soup is really tasty. 这汤真好喝。
2. She cooked a tasty dinner for us. 她为我们做了一顿美味的晚餐。
