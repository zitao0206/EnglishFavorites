### talkative

**Desp**
adj. enjoying talking a lot.
健谈的

**Usages**
- a talkative person 健谈的人

**Examples**
1. She's very talkative at parties. 她在派对上很健谈。
2. He became talkative after a glass of wine. 喝了一杯酒后他变得健谈起来。
3. My new roommate is friendly and talkative. 我的新室友友好又健谈。
