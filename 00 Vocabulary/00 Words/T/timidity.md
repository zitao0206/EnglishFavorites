### timidity
[tɪˈmɪdəti]

**Desp**
n. Lack of courage or confidence; shyness.
胆怯，怯懦

**Usages**
- overcome timidity 克服胆怯
- timid adj. 胆小的
- out of timidity 出于胆怯

**Examples**
1. Courage is the opposite of timidity. 勇气是怯懦的反面。
2. His timidity prevented him from speaking up. 他的怯懦让他不敢开口。
3. She overcame her timidity and joined the debate team. 她克服了胆怯，加入了辩论队。
