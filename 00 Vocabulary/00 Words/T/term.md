### term
[tɜːm]

**Description**
n/v. A word or phrase used to describe something; a fixed period of time; one of the periods of the school year.
术语，措辞；期限，学期；任期；v. 把……称为

**Usages**
- in the long term 从长远来看
- a technical term 专业术语
- come to terms with 接受，妥协

**Examples**
1. In the long term, this investment will pay off. 从长远来看，这项投资会有回报。
2. 'Photosynthesis' is a scientific term. “光合作用”是一个科学术语。
3. He was elected for a four-year term. 他当选后任期为四年。
4. It's something which I think affected me for a long time, and it's something that I've just recently come to terms with. 我认为这些事影响了我很长一段时间，我最近才开始正视这些事情。
