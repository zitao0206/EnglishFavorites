### toll
[təʊl]

**Desp**
n. money paid for using a road or bridge; the damage or deaths caused by something.
过路费；通行费；代价，伤亡

**Usages**
- pay a toll 支付过路费
- death toll 死亡人数

**Examples**
1. You have to pay a toll to cross the bridge. 过这座桥需要支付过路费。
2. The death toll from the earthquake is rising. 地震造成的死亡人数正在上升。
3. Years of stress took a heavy toll on his health. 多年的压力对他的健康造成了严重影响。
