### territory
[ˈterətɔːri]

**Desp**
n. land under the control of a country; an area of knowledge or activity.
领土，版图；领域，范围

**Usages**
- defend one's territory 捍卫领土
- unfamiliar territory 陌生领域
- occupied territory 被占领土

**Examples**
1. The two countries disputed the territory for decades. 两国就这片领土争端了数十年。
2. Quantum physics is unfamiliar territory for me. 量子物理对我来说是陌生领域。
3. Sales reps are assigned specific territories. 销售代表各有负责的区域。
