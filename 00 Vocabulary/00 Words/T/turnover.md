### turnover
[ˈtɜːrnoʊvər]

**Desp**
n. the rate at which employees leave a company; the total amount of business done in a period.
人员流失率，人员更替率；营业额，（货物）周转率

**Extension**
动词短语：turn over（翻转；营业额达）

**Usages**
- high staff turnover 高员工流失率
- annual turnover 年营业额
- reduce turnover 降低流失率

**Examples**
1. The company has a high turnover of staff. 这家公司员工流失率很高。
2. Our annual turnover reached ten million dollars. 我们年营业额达到了一千万美元。
3. Good benefits help reduce employee turnover. 良好的福利有助于降低员工流失率。
