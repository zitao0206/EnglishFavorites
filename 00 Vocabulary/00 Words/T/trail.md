### trail
[treɪl]

**Desp**
v. to be behind in a game or competition; to follow. n. a path; a mark or track.
v.（比分）落后；跟随，追踪；n. 小径，痕迹，踪迹

**Usages**
- trail behind 落后于
- mountain trail 山路小径

**Examples**
1. The home team trailed by two goals at half-time. 主队在半场时落后两球。
2. We followed a narrow trail through the forest. 我们沿着森林中的一条小径前行。
3. The police are on the trail of the suspect. 警方正在追踪嫌疑人。
