### tropical
[ˈtrɑːpɪkl]

**Desp**
adj. relating to the tropics; hot and humid.
adj. 热带的；湿热的

**Extension**
名词：tropics 热带地区

**Usages**
- tropical rainforest 热带雨林
- tropical climate 热带气候
- tropical fish 热带鱼

**Examples**
1. Tropical rainforests are home to countless species. 热带雨林是无数物种的家园。
2. They vacationed in a tropical island. 他们去热带岛屿度假。
3. The region has a tropical climate with heavy rains. 该地区属热带气候，降雨充沛。
