### theoretical
[ˌθiːəˈretɪkl]

**Description**
adj. concerned with or involving the theory of a subject rather than its practical application.
理论的，理论上的

**Usages**
- theoretical framework 理论框架
- theoretical physics 理论物理
- in theory = theoretically 理论上

**Examples**
1. The course covers both theoretical and practical aspects. 该课程涵盖理论和实践两个方面。
2. Her research is purely theoretical at this stage. 她的研究目前纯粹是理论层面的。
3. There is a theoretical possibility of life on Mars. 火星上存在生命是有理论可能性的。
