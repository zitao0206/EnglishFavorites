### tremble
[ˈtrembl]

**Desp**
v./n. to shake slightly in a way that you cannot control, especially because you are frightened or cold.
发抖，颤抖；担心；（n.）颤抖

**Usages**
- tremble with fear 吓得发抖
- tremble with cold 冷得发抖
- tremble at the thought 一想到就发抖

**Examples**
1. Her hands were trembling with fear. 她的手吓得直发抖。
2. He trembled at the thought of the exam. 一想到考试他就发抖。
3. There was a slight tremble in her voice. 她的声音微微发颤。
