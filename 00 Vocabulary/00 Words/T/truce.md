### truce
[truːs]

**Description**
n. an agreement to stop fighting for a period of time.
停战协定，休战

**Usages**
- tariff truce 关税休战
- call a truce 宣布休战

**Examples**
1. The two sides agreed to a truce. 双方同意休战。
2. The tariff truce brought relief to global markets. 关税休战给全球市场带来了缓解。
