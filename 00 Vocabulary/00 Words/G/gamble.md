### gamble
[ˈɡæmbl]

**Desp**
v/n. To risk money on a game or an uncertain result; a risky action taken in the hope of a good outcome.
赌博；冒险；n. 赌博，冒险

**Usages**
- gamble on stocks 在股票上冒险
- take a gamble 冒一次险
- gambling addiction 赌瘾

**Examples**
1. He gambled away all his savings. 他赌博输光了所有积蓄。
2. Starting a business is always a gamble. 创业总是一场冒险。
3. Don't gamble with your future. 不要拿你的未来去冒险。
