### gourmet
[ˈɡʊəmeɪ]

**Desp**
n. A person who enjoys and knows a lot about good food.
美食家

**Usages**
- gourmet food 美食
- gourmet restaurant 高档餐厅
- a gourmet meal 美餐

**Examples**
1. He is a gourmet who loves fine dining. 他是位喜爱精致美食的美食家。
2. The city is famous for its gourmet restaurants. 这座城市以高档餐厅闻名。
