### grain
[ɡreɪn]

**Desp**
n. seeds of crops such as wheat and rice; a tiny piece.
谷物，谷粒，颗粒

**Usages**
- a grain of rice 一粒米
- whole grains 全谷物
- a grain of sand 一粒沙

**Examples**
1. The farmers harvested the grain. 农民们收割了谷物。
2. A grain of rice fell on the floor. 一粒米掉在了地上。
3. Whole grains are good for health. 全谷物有益健康。
