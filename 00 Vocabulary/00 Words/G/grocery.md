### grocery
[ˈɡroʊsəri]

**Desp**
n. food and other goods sold in a store; a store that sells food and household goods.
食品杂货；食品杂货店

**Usages**
- grocery store 杂货店，超市
- grocery shopping 买食品杂货
- a grocery list 购物清单

**Examples**
1. I need to do the grocery shopping today. 我今天需要去买菜。
2. She works at a local grocery store. 她在当地一家杂货店工作。
3. Don't forget the grocery list. 别忘了购物清单。
