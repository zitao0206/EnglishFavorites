### generalize
[ˈdʒenrəlaɪz]

**Desp**
v. To make a general statement that is based on only a few examples; to apply a rule to a wider range.
概括，归纳；推广，普及

**Usages**
- generalize from 从……中归纳
- generalize about 对……一概而论
- generalize to 推广到

**Examples**
1. You can't generalize about all teenagers from one example. 你不能仅凭一个例子就对所有青少年一概而论。
2. It's dangerous to generalize from such a small sample. 从这么小的样本中归纳出结论是危险的。
3. The findings can be generalized to other age groups. 这些发现可以推广到其他年龄段。
