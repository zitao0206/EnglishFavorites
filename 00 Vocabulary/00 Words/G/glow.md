### glow
[ɡloʊ]

**Description**
v/n. to give out a steady warm light; a soft light.
v. 发光，发热；n. 光辉，红光

**Extension**
名词：glowing 热情洋溢的

**Usages**
- glow with pride 洋洋得意
- the glow of sunset 落日的余晖

**Examples**
1. The city lights glow at night. 城市的灯光在夜晚闪耀。
2. Her face glowed with excitement. 她兴奋得满面红光。
3. The coals glowed in the fireplace. 煤炭在壁炉里发着红光。
