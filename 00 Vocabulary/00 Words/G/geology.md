### geology
[dʒiˈɒlədʒi]

**Desp**
n. the scientific study of the earth, its rocks and physical structure.
地质学

**Usages**
- study geology 学习地质学
- the geology of the region 该地区的地质构造
- a geology professor 地质学教授

**Examples**
1. He majored in geology at university. 他大学主修地质学。
2. The geology of this area is very complex. 这个地区的地质构造非常复杂。
3. Geology helps us understand earthquakes. 地质学帮助我们理解地震。
