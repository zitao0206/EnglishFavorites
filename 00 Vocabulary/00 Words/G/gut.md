### gut
[ɡʌt]

**Desp**
n. the stomach and intestines; courage or bravery; a strong instinctive feeling.
勇气，胆量；直觉；内脏

**Usages**
- gut feeling / gut instinct 本能直觉
- have the guts to do sth 有勇气做某事

**Examples**
1. My gut feeling tells me something is wrong. 我的直觉告诉我出问题了。
2. He didn't have the guts to tell her the truth. 他没有勇气告诉她真相。
