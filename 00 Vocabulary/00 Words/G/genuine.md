### genuine
[ˈdʒenjuɪn]

**Desp**
adj. Real and exactly what it appears to be; sincere and honest.
真正的，真实的；真诚的

**Usages**
- a genuine smile 真诚的微笑
- genuine leather 真皮
- a genuine interest 真正的兴趣
- ≈ sincere 真实的，真心的

**Examples**
1. The painting is a genuine Picasso. 这幅画是毕加索的真迹。
2. She showed a genuine interest in our project. 她对我们的项目表现出真正的兴趣。
3. His apology sounded genuine. 他的道歉听起来是真诚的。
4. Only genuine refugees can apply for asylum. 只有真正的难民才可以申请政治避难。
