### gross
[ɡrəʊs]

**Desp**
adj. very unpleasant; total, before deductions.
恶劣的，反胃的，令人不适的；总的

**Usages**
- gross income 总收入，毛收入

**Examples**
1. Eww, that's gross! 呃，真恶心！
2. His gross income is 10,000 yuan a month. 他的月总收入是一万元。
3. The movie was too gross for me. 这部电影对我来说太恶心了。
