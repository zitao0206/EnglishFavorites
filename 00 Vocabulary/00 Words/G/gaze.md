### gaze
[ɡeɪz]

🕒 Updated: 2026-08-16
📅 Created: 2026-08-16
📌 ReviewCount: 1

**Desp**
v. to look steadily and intently, especially in admiration, surprise, or thought.
凝视，注视
n. A long, steady look.

**Usages**
- gaze into the abyss 凝视深渊
- gaze at sb/sth 凝视某人/某物
- meet sb's gaze 迎上某人的目光

**Examples**
1. When you gaze into the abyss, the abyss also gazes into you. 当你凝视深渊时，深渊也在凝视着你。
2. He sat by the window, gazing at the stars. 他坐在窗边，凝视着星星。
3. She returned his gaze without a word. 她一声不吭地回视他的目光。
