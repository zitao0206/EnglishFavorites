### knowledgeable

**Desp**
adj. Knowing a lot about a particular subject.
博学的，见多识广的，知识渊博的

**Usages**
- be knowledgeable about sth 精通某事
- a knowledgeable expert 知识渊博的专家

**Examples**
1. You seem quite knowledgeable on this topic. 你在这个话题上见解颇丰。
2. He is very knowledgeable about Chinese history. 他对中国历史非常了解。
3. Ask the knowledgeable staff for help. 去请教见多识广的工作人员。
