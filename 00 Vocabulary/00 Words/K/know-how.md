### know-how

**Desp**
n. practical knowledge or skill in a field.
专门技术诀窍，实操技能

**Usages**
- technical know-how 技术诀窍
- business know-how 商业技能

**Examples**
1. The company has rich technical know-how in AI development. 这家公司拥有深厚的人工智能开发技术经验。
2. You need the right know-how to fix this machine. 修这台机器需要专门技术。
3. He shared his marketing know-how with the team. 他和团队分享了营销诀窍。
