### restore

**Desp**
v. to bring something back to its original state.
修复，复原，恢复；归还，返还

**Usages**
- restore the old building 修复老建筑
- restore order 恢复秩序

**Examples**
1. The church was restored to its former glory. 教堂恢复了昔日的辉煌。
2. The police restored order after the protest. 警察在抗议后恢复了秩序。
3. The painting was carefully restored. 这幅画被精心修复。
