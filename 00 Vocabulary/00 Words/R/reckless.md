### reckless
[ˈrekləs]

**Desp**
adj. Showing a lack of care about danger and the possible results of your actions.
鲁莽的，不计后果的

**Usages**
- reckless driving 鲁莽驾驶
- reckless behavior 不计后果的行为
- reckless spending 挥霍无度

**Examples**
1. He was fined for reckless driving. 他因鲁莽驾驶被罚款。
2. Such reckless behavior could cost you your life. 这种不计后果的行为可能会让你丧命。
3. Don't make reckless decisions with your money. 不要用你的钱做鲁莽的决定。
