### rash
[ræʃ]

**Desp**
n. an area of red spots on the skin; a series of unpleasant events.
皮疹；一连串（糟糕的）事情

**Usages**
- break out in a rash 突然起疹子
- get a rash 出疹子
- viral rash 病毒性疹子
- allergic rash 过敏性皮疹

**Examples**
1. He broke out in a rash after eating seafood. 他吃了海鲜后突然起了疹子。
2. The baby has an allergic rash. 宝宝起了过敏性皮疹。
3. There has been a rash of robberies this month. 这个月接连发生了一连串抢劫案。
