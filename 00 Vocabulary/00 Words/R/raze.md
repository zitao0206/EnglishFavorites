### raze
[reɪz]

**Desp**
v. to completely destroy a building or town; to demolish.
毁坏，消除，夷为平地

**Usages**
- raze sth to the ground 将某物夷为平地

**Examples**
1. The old factory was razed to the ground. 旧工厂被夷为平地。
2. The earthquake razed entire villages. 地震摧毁了整个村庄。
