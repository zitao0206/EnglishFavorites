### regulator
[ˈreɡjuleɪtə(r)]

**Desp**
n. an official organization that controls an activity and makes sure it follows rules.
监管机构，管理机构

**Usages**
- financial regulator 金融监管机构
- data protection regulator 数据保护监管机构

**Examples**
1. The regulator fined the bank for violating data privacy rules. 监管机构因银行违反数据隐私规定而对其罚款。
2. Tech companies are under increasing pressure from regulators. 科技公司正面临监管机构越来越大的压力。
