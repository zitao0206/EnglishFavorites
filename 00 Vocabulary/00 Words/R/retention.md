### retention
[rɪˈtenʃn]

**Desp**
n. the continued possession, use, or control of something; the ability to keep or remember.
保留，保持；留存率

**Usages**
- customer retention 客户留存率
- employee retention 员工留存率
- water retention 水分滞留

**Examples**
1. Improving customer retention is key to long-term profitability. 提高客户留存率是长期盈利的关键。
2. The company has a high employee retention rate. 该公司的员工留存率很高。
3. Good sleep helps with memory retention. 良好的睡眠有助于记忆保持。
