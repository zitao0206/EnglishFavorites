### realm
[relm]

**Desp**
n. a field or domain of activity or interest; a kingdom.
领域，范围；王国

**Usages**
- in the realm of 在…领域
- beyond the realm of possibility 超出可能范围

**Examples**
1. Advances in the realm of artificial intelligence are astonishing. 人工智能领域的进展令人惊叹。
2. What he described was beyond the realm of possibility. 他所描述的已超出可能的范围。
3. The king expanded his realm through diplomacy and war. 国王通过外交和战争扩大了他的王国。
