### revenue
[ˈrevənjuː]

**Desp**
n. money that an organization receives; income.
（组织的）收入，收益；税收收入

**Usages**
- annual revenue 年收入
- tax revenue 税收收入

**Examples**
1. The company's annual revenue reached one billion dollars. 公司年收入达到十亿美元。
2. Ad revenue is the main source of income for the platform. 广告收入是该平台的主要收入来源。
