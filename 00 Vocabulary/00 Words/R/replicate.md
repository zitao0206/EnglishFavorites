### replicate
[ˈreplɪkeɪt]

**Desp**
v. to copy or repeat something exactly; (biology) to make copies of itself.
复制；重现；复制自身

**Usages**
- replicate the results 重现结果
- replicate a study 重复一项研究
- replicate DNA DNA 复制

**Examples**
1. Researchers failed to replicate the experiment's results. 研究人员未能重现该实验的结果。
2. The virus can replicate rapidly in the body. 病毒能在体内迅速复制。
3. We need to replicate this success in other markets. 我们需要在其他市场复制这一成功。
