### reaffirm
[ˌriːəˈfɜːm]

**Desp**
vt. to state something again strongly; to confirm again.
再确定，重申

**Usages**
- reaffirm one's commitment 重申承诺

**Examples**
1. The government reaffirmed its commitment to reform. 政府重申了改革的承诺。
2. She reaffirmed her decision to study abroad. 她重申了出国留学的决定。
