### responsive
[rɪˈspɑːnsɪv]

**Description**
adj. reacting quickly and positively; answering.
adj. 反应积极的，灵敏的；回应的

**Extension**
反义词：unresponsive 反应迟钝的
名词：response 回应

**Usages**
- be responsive to 对…反应积极
- a responsive design 响应式设计

**Examples**
1. The staff were friendly and responsive. 员工友好且响应迅速。
2. The engine is very responsive. 这台发动机反应非常灵敏。
3. A manager should be responsive to employees' needs. 管理者应积极回应员工的需求。
