### ridiculous
[rɪˈdɪkjələs]

**Desp**
adj. very silly or unreasonable; absurd.
荒谬的，可笑的

**Usages**
- a ridiculous idea 荒谬的想法
- look ridiculous 看起来可笑
- absolutely ridiculous 荒唐至极

**Examples**
1. That's a ridiculous suggestion. 那是个荒谬的建议。
2. He looks ridiculous in that hat. 他戴那顶帽子看起来很可笑。
3. It's ridiculous to pay that much. 付那么多钱太荒唐了。
