### restrain
[rɪˈstreɪn]

**Desp**
v. to hold back or control someone/something.
v. 抑制，遏制，管束

**Extension**
名词：restraint 抑制，约束

**Usages**
- restrain oneself 克制自己
- restraint of trade 贸易限制

**Examples**
1. He had to restrain himself from laughing. 他不得不强忍住笑。
2. Police restrained the aggressive man. 警察制服了那个有攻击性的男子。
3. She restrained her anger during the meeting. 会议期间她抑制住了怒火。
