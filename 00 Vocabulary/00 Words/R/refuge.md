### refuge
[ˈrefjuːdʒ]

**Desp**
n. shelter or protection from danger; a safe place.
n. 庇护，避难；避难所

**Extension**
动词：refugee 难民（名词）

**Usages**
- take refuge in 在…避难
- seek refuge 寻求庇护
- a wildlife refuge 野生动物保护区

**Examples**
1. They took refuge from the storm in a barn. 他们在谷仓里躲避暴风雨。
2. Thousands sought refuge in neighboring countries. 数千人在邻国寻求庇护。
3. The church became a refuge for the homeless. 教堂成了无家可归者的庇护所。
