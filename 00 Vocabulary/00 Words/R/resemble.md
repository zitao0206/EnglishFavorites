### resemble
[rɪˈzembl]

**Description**
v. To look like or be similar to someone or something.
与……相似，像

**Usages**
- resemble one's father 长得像父亲
- closely resemble 非常相似
- resemble a bird 像一只鸟

**Examples**
1. She resembles her mother in many ways. 她在很多方面都像她妈妈。
2. The building closely resembles a castle. 这座建筑非常像一座城堡。
3. His latest novel resembles his earlier works. 他最新的小说与他早期作品相似。
