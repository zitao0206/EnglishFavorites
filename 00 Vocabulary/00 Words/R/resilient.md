### resilient
[rɪˈzɪliənt]

**Desp**
adj. able to recover quickly from difficulties; strong.
有复原力的，坚韧的

**Usages**
- a resilient economy 有韧性的经济
- resilience n. 韧性

**Examples**
1. Children are often more resilient than adults. 孩子往往比大人更有韧性。
2. The economy proved resilient to the shock. 经济被证明对冲击具有韧性。
