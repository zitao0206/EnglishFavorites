### rebel
[ˈrebl / rɪˈbel]

**Desp**
n. a person who fights against authority; v. to resist control.
n. 反叛者；v. 造反，反叛

**Extension**
名词：rebellion 叛乱
形容词：rebellious 反叛的

**Usages**
- rebel against authority 反抗权威
- a teenage rebel 叛逆的青少年

**Examples**
1. The rebels attacked the government base. 叛军袭击了政府基地。
2. Teenagers often rebel against their parents. 青少年常常反叛父母。
3. He was a rebel in his youth. 他年轻时是个叛逆者。
