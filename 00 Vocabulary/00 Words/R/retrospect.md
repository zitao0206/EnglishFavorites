### retrospect
[ˈretrəspekt]

**Desp**
n. thinking about something that happened in the past.
回顾，回想（in retrospect 回想起来）

**Usages**
- in retrospect 回顾起来
- a brief retrospect 简要回顾
- with the benefit of retrospect 事后看来

**Examples**
1. In retrospect, I should have taken the job. 回想起来，我当初应该接受那份工作。
2. In retrospect, the decision was clearly wrong. 事后看来，那个决定显然是错误的。
3. The meeting was a success in retrospect. 回顾起来，这次会议办得很成功。
