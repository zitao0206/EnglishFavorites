### refine
[rɪˈfaɪn]

**Desp**
v. to improve something by making small changes; to purify a substance.
改进，完善；提炼，精炼

**Extension**
反义词：coarsen, worsen
名词：refinement
形容词：refined

**Usages**
- refine a plan 完善计划
- refine one's skills 提升技能
- refine oil 炼油

**Examples**
1. We need to refine our marketing strategy. 我们需要完善营销策略。
2. She spent years refining her technique. 她花了多年时间磨练技艺。
3. Crude oil is refined into gasoline. 原油被提炼成汽油。
