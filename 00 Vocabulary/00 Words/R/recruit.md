### recruit
[rɪˈkruːt]

**Desp**
v. to enroll someone as a member or worker. n. A new member of an organization.
招募，招聘；新成员

**Usages**
- recruit new employees 招募新员工
- recruit talent 招揽人才
- new recruits 新人/新兵

**Examples**
1. The company is recruiting new employees for its expansion. 公司正在为业务扩张招募新员工。
2. It's hard to recruit top talent in a competitive market. 在竞争激烈的市场中很难招揽顶尖人才。
3. The new recruits will start training next week. 新成员下周开始培训。
