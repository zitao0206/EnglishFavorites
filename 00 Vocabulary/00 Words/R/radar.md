### radar
[ˈreɪdɑː(r)]

**Desp**
n. a system using radio waves to detect objects; an intuitive ability to notice something.
雷达；直觉感知能力

**Usages**
- on the radar 在关注范围内
- off the radar 不受关注

**Examples**
1. The plane was detected by radar. 飞机被雷达探测到了。
2. This issue is on the company's radar. 这个问题在公司关注的范围内。
