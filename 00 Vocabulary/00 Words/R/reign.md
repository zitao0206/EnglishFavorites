### reign
[reɪn]

**Description**
n/v. the period during which a monarch rules; to rule or prevail.
n. 统治，在位期；主宰 v. 统治，盛行

**Usages**
- the reign of Queen Elizabeth 伊丽莎白女王在位时期
- reign supreme 占据至高地位，称雄

**Examples**
1. Silence reigned everywhere. 四下一片寂静。
2. AI-technology continues to reign supreme. 人工智能技术继续占据至高地位。
3. The king reigned over the country for 30 years. 这位国王统治这个国家长达30年。
