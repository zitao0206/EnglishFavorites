### reasonable

**Desp**
adj. fair, sensible, and not too expensive.
合理的

**Usages**
- a reasonable price 合理的价格
- a reasonable excuse 合理的借口

**Examples**
1. The price is reasonable and affordable. 价格合理且负担得起。
2. It's reasonable to expect some delay. 有点延迟是正常的。
3. He gave me a reasonable explanation. 他给了我一个合理的解释。
