### reunion
[ˌriːˈjuːniən]

**Desp**
n. A meeting of people who have not seen each other for a long time.
重聚，团聚；同学会

**Usages**
- family reunion 家庭团聚
- class reunion 同学聚会
- a reunion dinner 团圆饭

**Examples**
1. We had a family reunion during the Spring Festival. 春节期间我们全家团聚了。
2. I'm going to my high school class reunion this weekend. 这周末我要去参加高中同学聚会。
3. The reunion was a joyful occasion. 这次重聚充满欢乐。
