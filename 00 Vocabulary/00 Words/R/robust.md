### robust
[rəʊˈbʌst]

**Desp**
adj. strong and healthy; firm and reliable.
强健的，健壮的；稳固可靠的

**Usages**
- robust growth 强劲的增长
- a robust system 稳固可靠的系统
- robust demand 旺盛的需求

**Examples**
1. The economy showed robust growth last year. 去年经济强劲增长。
2. The software is robust and rarely crashes. 该软件稳定可靠，很少崩溃。
3. He owes his robust health to daily exercise. 他把强健的体魄归功于每天锻炼。
