### reassuring
[ˌriːəˈʃʊərɪŋ]

**Desp**
adj. Making you feel less worried or frightened.
安慰的，安心的，可靠的

**Usages**
- reassuring news 好消息，让人宽心的消息
- a reassuring smile 让人安心的微笑
- in a reassuring tone 用安抚的语气

**Examples**
1. The doctor's words were very reassuring. 医生的话让人很安心。
2. It's reassuring to know that help is on the way. 知道救援正在路上，心里踏实多了。
3. She gave the child a reassuring hug. 她给了孩子一个安心的拥抱。
