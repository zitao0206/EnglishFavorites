### reclaim
[rɪˈkleɪm]

**Description**
v. to get something back; to restore land to a useful state.
v. 收回，回收；开垦，改造（土地）

**Usages**
- reclaim lost territory 收复失地
- reclaim land from the sea 围海造田
- reclaimed water 中水（回收水）

**Examples**
1. You can reclaim your deposit at the end of the lease. 租期结束时你可以取回押金。
2. The city reclaimed the wetlands for a park. 该市把湿地改造成了公园。
3. She reclaimed her position as CEO. 她重新夺回了CEO的职位。
