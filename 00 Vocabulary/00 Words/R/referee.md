### referee
[ˌrefəˈriː]

**Description**
n. an official who enforces rules in a game; a person who gives references.
n. 裁判员；推荐人；仲裁者

**Extension**
动词：refer 提及，提交

**Usages**
- the match referee 比赛裁判
- act as a referee 担任裁判/推荐人

**Examples**
1. The referee blew the whistle for a foul. 裁判鸣哨判罚犯规。
2. He listed his professor as a referee on the application. 他在申请表中把教授列为推荐人。
3. The referee sent off two players. 裁判罚下了两名球员。
