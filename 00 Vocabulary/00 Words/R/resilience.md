### resilience
[rɪˈzɪliəns]

**Desp**
n. The ability to recover quickly from difficulties or changes.
韧性，恢复力；适应力

**Usages**
- build resilience 培养韧性
- emotional resilience 情绪韧性
- show resilience 展现出韧劲

**Examples**
1. Children show remarkable resilience after setbacks. 孩子们在挫折后表现出惊人的韧性。
2. The economy has shown great resilience. 经济表现出了很强的韧性。
3. Meditation can help build mental resilience. 冥想有助于培养心理韧性。
