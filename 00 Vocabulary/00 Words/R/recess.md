### recess
[rɪˈses]

**Desp**
n. a break between classes; a pause in proceedings.
课间休息；休会期，暂停办公

**Usages**
- during recess 课间休息时
- in recess 休会中

**Examples**
1. The kids play outside during recess. 孩子们课间在外面玩。
2. The court is in recess until Monday. 法庭休庭到周一。
3. Congress will take a summer recess. 国会将进入夏季休会期。
