### reluctant
[rɪˈlʌktənt]

**Desp**
adj. unwilling and hesitant to do something.
adj. 不情愿的，勉强的

**Extension**
名词：reluctance 不情愿
副词：reluctantly 不情愿地

**Usages**
- be reluctant to do 不愿做
- a reluctant agreement 勉强的同意

**Examples**
1. He was reluctant to admit his mistake. 他不愿承认自己的错误。
2. She reluctantly agreed to help. 她勉强同意帮忙。
3. Many students are reluctant to speak in public. 许多学生不愿当众发言。
