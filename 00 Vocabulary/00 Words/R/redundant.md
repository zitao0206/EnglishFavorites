### redundant
[rɪˈdʌndənt]

**Description**
adj. no longer needed; unnecessarily repetitive.
adj. 多余的，被裁减的；累赘的

**Extension**
名词：redundancy 冗余，裁员

**Usages**
- be made redundant 被裁员
- redundant information 冗余信息

**Examples**
1. Workers were made redundant when the factory closed. 工厂关闭时工人们被裁员了。
2. Delete any redundant words in your essay. 删掉文章里多余的词。
3. The backup system prevents data from becoming redundant. 备份系统防止数据冗余。
