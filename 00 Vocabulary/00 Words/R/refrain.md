### refrain
[rɪˈfreɪn]

**Description**
v. to hold back from doing something; n. a repeated line in a song.
v. 克制，避免 n. 副歌，重复的话

**Extension**
名词：refrain 克制（行为）

**Usages**
- refrain from doing 克制不做
- refrain from smoking 忍住不吸烟

**Examples**
1. Please refrain from smoking in the hall. 大厅内请勿吸烟。
2. She refrained from commenting on the dispute. 她避免对争议发表评论。
3. He could barely refrain from laughing. 他几乎忍不住要笑出来。
