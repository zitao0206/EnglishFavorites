### retailer
[ˈriːteɪlər]

**Desp**
n. a business that sells goods to consumers.
n. 零售商

**Extension**
动词：retail 零售
反义词：wholesaler 批发商

**Usages**
- an online retailer 线上零售商
- a major retailer 大型零售商
- retail prices 零售价

**Examples**
1. The retailer operates hundreds of stores nationwide. 这家零售商在全国经营数百家门店。
2. Online retailers have changed consumer habits. 线上零售商改变了消费习惯。
3. The supermarket is the largest retailer in the region. 这家超市是该地区最大的零售商。
