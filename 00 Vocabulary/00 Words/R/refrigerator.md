### refrigerator
[rɪˈfrɪdʒəreɪtər]

**Desp**
n. A large appliance that keeps food and drinks cold.
冰箱，冷藏柜

**Usages**
- put in the refrigerator 放进冰箱
- refrigerator door 冰箱门
- empty the refrigerator 清空冰箱

**Examples**
1. Put the milk back in the refrigerator. 把牛奶放回冰箱。
2. The refrigerator is full of leftovers. 冰箱里全是剩菜。
3. He opened the refrigerator to grab a drink. 他打开冰箱拿饮料。
