### rural
[ˈrʊərəl]

**Desp**
adj. relating to the countryside.
农村的

**Usages**
- rural areas 农村地区
- rural life 乡村生活
- rural education 农村教育

**Examples**
1. He grew up in a rural area. 他在农村长大。
2. Rural life is quiet and peaceful. 乡村生活宁静祥和。
3. The government invests in rural education. 政府投资农村教育。
