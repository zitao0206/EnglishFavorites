### rectify
[ˈrektɪfaɪ]

**Desp**
v. to correct something that is wrong.
v. 纠正，改正

**Extension**
名词：rectification 纠正

**Usages**
- rectify a mistake 纠正错误
- rectify the situation 扭转局面

**Examples**
1. We must rectify the error immediately. 我们必须立即纠正这个错误。
2. The company rectified the problem within a week. 公司一周内解决了问题。
3. It's never too late to rectify your mistakes. 改正错误永远不晚。
