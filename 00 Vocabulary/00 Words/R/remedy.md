### remedy
[ˈremədi]

**Desp**
n/v. a way of solving a problem or curing an illness; to correct or improve a situation.
n. 补救办法；药物，疗法；v. 补救，纠正

**Usages**
- a remedy for colds 治感冒的药
- an effective remedy 有效的补救措施
- remedy the situation 扭转局面

**Examples**
1. There is no easy remedy for this problem. 这个问题没有简单的解决办法。
2. Honey and lemon is a popular remedy for coughs. 蜂蜜柠檬是治疗咳嗽的常见偏方。
3. The company took steps to remedy the situation. 公司采取措施扭转局面。
