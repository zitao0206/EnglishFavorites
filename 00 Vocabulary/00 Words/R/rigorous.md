### rigorous
[ˈrɪɡərəs]

**Desp**
adj. Extremely thorough, exhaustive, and accurate; strictly applied.
严格的，严密的；严谨的

**Usages**
- rigorous testing 严格测试
- rigorous standards 严格标准
- rigorous academic training 严谨的学术训练

**Examples**
1. The new drug underwent rigorous testing before approval. 新药在获批前经过了严格测试。
2. He maintained rigorous standards in his research. 他在研究中保持着严格的标准。
3. The military training was physically and mentally rigorous. 军事训练在身心两方面都非常严格。
