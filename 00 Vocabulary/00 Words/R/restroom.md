### restroom
[ˈrestruːm]

🕒 Updated: 2026-08-16
📅 Created: 2026-08-16
📌 ReviewCount: 1

**Desp**
n. a public toilet; a bathroom in a public place.
公共厕所，卫生间

**Usages**
- the men's/women's restroom 男/女卫生间
- a restroom break 去洗手间的休息时间
- find a restroom 找卫生间

**Examples**
1. Excuse me, where is the restroom? 请问卫生间在哪里？
2. The restroom on the second floor is being cleaned. 二楼的卫生间正在打扫。
3. We stopped at a gas station for a restroom break. 我们在加油站停下上了趟厕所。
