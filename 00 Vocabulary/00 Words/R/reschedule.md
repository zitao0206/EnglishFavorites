### reschedule
[ˌriːˈʃedjuːl]

**Desp**
v. to change the time of a planned event or arrangement.
重新安排时间；改期

**Usages**
- reschedule a meeting 重新安排会议
- reschedule an appointment 重新预约
- reschedule the flight 改签航班

**Examples**
1. I need to reschedule my appointment. 我需要重新预约。
2. The meeting was rescheduled to Friday. 会议改到周五。
3. We had to reschedule the flight due to the storm. 由于暴风雨我们不得不改签航班。
