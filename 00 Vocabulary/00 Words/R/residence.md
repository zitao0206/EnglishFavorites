### residence
[ˈrezɪdəns]

**Desp**
n. the place where one lives; a house or flat.
住宅，住所，居所

**Usages**
- official residence 官邸
- permanent residence 长期居留
- place of residence 居住地

**Examples**
1. The president's official residence is the White House. 总统官邸是白宫。
2. He applied for permanent residence in Canada. 他申请了加拿大永久居留权。
