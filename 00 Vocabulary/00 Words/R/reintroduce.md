### reintroduce
[ˌriːɪntrəˈdjuːs]

**Desp**
v. to introduce again; to return a species to its native habitat.
重新引入

**Extension**
名词形式：reintroduction

**Usages**
- reintroduce a species 重新引入物种
- reintroduction program 再引入计划

**Examples**
1. The species was reintroduced to its native habitat. 该物种被重新引入其原生栖息地。
2. They plan to reintroduce wolves to the park. 他们计划将狼重新引入公园。
3. The reintroduction program has been successful. 再引入计划已取得成功。
