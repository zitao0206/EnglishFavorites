### retain
[rɪˈteɪn]

**Desp**
v. to keep or continue to have something.
保留，保存，保持

**Usages**
- retain information 记住信息
- retain customers 留住客户

**Examples**
1. The town retains its old charm. 这座小镇保持着古老的魅力。
2. It's hard to retain so much information at once. 一次记住这么多信息很难。
