### reckon
[ˈrekən]

**Description**
v. to think or calculate; to estimate.
v. 认为，估计；计算，测算

**Extension**
名词：reckoning 估算，清算

**Usages**
- reckon with 考虑到，对付
- I reckon that ... 我认为…
- be reckoned as 被认为是

**Examples**
1. I reckon we'll finish by Friday. 我估计周五之前能完成。
2. She's reckoned as one of the best coaches. 她被认为是最好的教练之一。
3. They hadn't reckoned with such strong resistance. 他们没料到会遇到这么强的抵制。
