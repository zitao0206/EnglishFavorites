### reproduction
[ˌriːprəˈdʌkʃn]

**Desp**
n. The process of producing young; a copy of something.
繁殖，生殖；复刻，再现

**Usages**
- reproduction rate 繁殖率
- a reproduction of the painting 这幅画的复刻品
- faithful reproduction 忠实再现

**Examples**
1. This painting is a reproduction of the original. 这幅画是原作的复制品。
2. The film is an accurate reproduction of 1920s Shanghai. 这部电影忠实再现了上世纪二十年代的上海。
3. Warm weather speeds up the insects' reproduction. 温暖的天气加快了昆虫的繁殖。
