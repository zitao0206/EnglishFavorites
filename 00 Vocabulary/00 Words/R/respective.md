### respective
[rɪˈspektɪv]

**Desp**
adj. belonging to each individually; particular and separate.
各自的，分别的

**Usages**
- their respective roles 他们各自的角色
- go to their respective homes 回各自的家
- respective responsibilities 各自的职责

**Examples**
1. The two teams returned to their respective locker rooms. 两支队伍回到了各自的更衣室。
2. They took on their respective responsibilities in the project. 他们在项目中承担了各自的职责。
3. The guests were shown to their respective rooms. 客人们被带到各自的房间。
