### leftover
[ˈleftəʊvə(r)]

**Desp**
n/adj. Food that has not been eaten at the end of a meal; remaining or unused.
n. 剩菜，剩饭；adj. 剩余的

**Usages**
- eat the leftovers 吃剩菜
- leftover food 剩余的食物
- leftover materials 剩余的材料

**Examples**
1. We had leftovers for dinner last night. 昨晚我们吃了剩菜当晚餐。
2. Put the leftover food in the fridge. 把剩下的食物放进冰箱。
3. She made a sandwich with the leftovers. 她用剩菜做了个三明治。
