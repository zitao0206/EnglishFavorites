### linger
[ˈlɪŋɡər]

**Desp**
v. to stay in a place longer than necessary; to remain or persist.
逗留，徘徊；（感觉、印象等）萦绕，挥之不去

**Usages**
- linger over coffee 慢慢喝咖啡磨蹭
- linger on a memory 沉浸在回忆中
- a smell that lingers 久久不散的气味

**Examples**
1. The smell of garlic lingered in the kitchen for days. 大蒜味在厨房里萦绕了好几天。
2. She lingered at the door, unwilling to say goodbye. 她在门口徘徊，不愿道别。
3. The memory of that summer still lingers. 那个夏天的记忆仍挥之不去。
