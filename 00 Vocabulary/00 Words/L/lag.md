### lag
[læɡ]

**Desp**
v. To fall behind in movement, progress, or development.
掉队，落后

**Usages**
- lag behind 落后
- time lag 时间差，时滞
- lag in development 发展滞后

**Examples**
1. He lagged behind the others on the hike. 徒步中他落在其他人后面。
2. The country lags behind in technology. 这个国家在技术上落后。
