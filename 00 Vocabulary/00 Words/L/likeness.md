### likeness
[ˈlaɪknəs]

**Description**
n. The quality of being similar in appearance or character.
相似，相像；肖像

**Usages**
- bear a likeness to 与……相似
- a family likeness 家人的相似之处
- a striking likeness 惊人的相似

**Examples**
1. She bears a strong likeness to her grandmother. 她长得非常像她奶奶。
2. There's a family likeness among the cousins. 表兄弟姐妹之间有家族相似之处。
3. The portrait is a good likeness of the queen. 这幅肖像是女王很好的写照。
