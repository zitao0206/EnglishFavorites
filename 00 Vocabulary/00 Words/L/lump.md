### lump
[lʌmp]

**Description**
v. to put things together into one group; to deal with together. n. a piece of something solid.
把…归并；肿块；一块

**Usages**
- lump oneself in with sb 将自己归入某人群
- lump together 归并在一起

**Examples**
1. Don't lump all teenagers in with the troublemakers. 别把所有青少年都和惹麻烦的人归为一类。
2. The costs were lumped together in one report. 费用被归并到一份报告中。
