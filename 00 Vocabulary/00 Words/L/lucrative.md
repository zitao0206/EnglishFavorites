### lucrative
[ˈluːkrətɪv]

**Desp**
adj. Producing much money; profitable.
利润丰厚的，赚钱的

**Usages**
- lucrative business 高收益生意
- lucrative freelance job 高薪自由职业
- a lucrative market 利润丰厚的市场

**Examples**
1. He turned his hobby into a lucrative business. 他把爱好变成了一门赚钱的生意。
2. Freelance design can be quite lucrative. 自由职业设计收入可以相当可观。
3. The company gave up the lucrative contract over ethical concerns. 出于道德考量，公司放弃了这份利润丰厚的合同。
