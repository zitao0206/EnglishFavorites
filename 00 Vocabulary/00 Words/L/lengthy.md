### lengthy
[ˈleŋθi]

**Description**
adj. very long, especially in time.
adj. 漫长的，冗长的

**Usages**
- a lengthy process 漫长的过程
- a lengthy discussion 冗长的讨论
- a lengthy report 长篇报告

**Examples**
1. The visa application is a lengthy process. 签证申请是个漫长的过程。
2. After lengthy negotiations, they reached a deal. 经过漫长的谈判，他们达成了协议。
3. He wrote a lengthy report on the incident. 他就此事写了一份长篇报告。
