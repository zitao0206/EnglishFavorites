### licence
[ˈlaɪsns]

**Desp**
n. An official document giving permission to do or own something; freedom to do something.
许可证，执照；许可，自由（美式拼作 license）

**Usages**
- a driving licence 驾照
- a business licence 营业执照
- poetic licence 诗的破格，艺术加工的自由

**Examples**
1. He got his driving licence last month. 他上个月拿到了驾照。
2. The restaurant has a licence to sell alcohol. 这家餐厅有售酒许可证。
3. As a writer, you can take some licence with the facts. 作为作家，你可以在事实上做一些艺术加工。
