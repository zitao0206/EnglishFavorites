### lexical
[ˈleksɪkl]

**Desp**
adj. relating to the words or vocabulary of a language.
词汇的，词典的

**Extension**
名词形式：lexicon

**Usages**
- lexical resource 词汇资源
- lexical diversity 词汇多样性

**Examples**
1. IELTS examiners assess lexical resource. 雅思考官评估词汇资源。
2. The lexical differences between the two dialects are minor. 两种方言在词汇上的差异很小。
3. She has a rich lexical knowledge. 她拥有丰富的词汇知识。
