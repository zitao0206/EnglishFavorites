### landscape
[ˈlændskeɪp]

**Desp**
n. All the visible features of an area of land; the distinctive features of a particular field of activity.
风景，景观；格局，全貌

**Usages**
- political landscape 政治格局
- rural landscape 乡村风景
- business landscape 商业格局

**Examples**
1. The mountain landscape was breathtaking. 山间风景令人叹为观止。
2. The political landscape has changed dramatically in recent years. 近年来政治格局发生了巨大变化。
3. Technology has reshaped the entire business landscape. 技术重塑了整个商业格局。
