### laud
[lɔːd]

**Desp**
v. to praise someone or something highly.
赞美，称颂

**Usages**
- be lauded for sth 因某事受到称赞

**Examples**
1. The critics lauded the director's new film. 评论家们盛赞这位导演的新片。
2. She was lauded for her contribution to science. 她因对科学的贡献而受到称赞。
