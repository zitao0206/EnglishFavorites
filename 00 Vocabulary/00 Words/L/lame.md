### lame
[leɪm]

**Desp**
adj. unable to walk properly; weak and unconvincing (of an excuse or argument).
跛的；蹩脚的，没说服力的

**Usages**
- a lame excuse 蹩脚的借口

**Examples**
1. That's a lame excuse for being late. 迟到用这个借口太蹩脚了。
2. His lame argument convinced no one. 他蹩脚的论证说服不了任何人。
