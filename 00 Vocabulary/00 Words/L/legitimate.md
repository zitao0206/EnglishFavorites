### legitimate
[lɪˈdʒɪtɪmət]

**Desp**
adj. legal or acceptable; reasonable; (of a child) born to married parents.
adj. 合法的；正当合理的；婚生的

**Extension**
副词：legitimately 合法地
动词：legitimize 使合法化

**Usages**
- a legitimate reason 正当理由
- legitimate business 合法生意
- legitimate concerns 合理的担忧

**Examples**
1. That's a legitimate question worth discussing. 那是个值得讨论的合理问题。
2. He runs a legitimate business with proper licenses. 他经营着一家手续齐全的合法企业。
3. The government expressed legitimate concerns about safety. 政府对安全表示了合理的担忧。
