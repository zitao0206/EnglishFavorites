### literal
[ˈlɪtərəl]

**Desp**
adj. taking words in their basic meaning, not figurative; exact.
adj. 字面上的，按字面理解的；完全按原文的

**Extension**
反义词：figurative 比喻的
副词：literally 字面上地，简直

**Usages**
- literal meaning 字面意思
- a literal translation 直译

**Examples**
1. The literal meaning of 'sweet' is 'tasting like sugar'. sweet 的字面意思是尝起来像糖。
2. The literal translation sounds odd in Chinese. 直译成中文听着很怪。
3. A literal reading of the law suggests otherwise. 按字面解读该法律则另当别论。
