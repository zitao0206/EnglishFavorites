### yawn
[jɔːn]

**Desp**
v/n. to open your mouth wide and breathe in deeply because you are tired or bored; a boring thing or event.
v. 打哈欠；n. 哈欠；乏味的事物

**Usages**
- yawn with boredom 无聊得打哈欠
- suppress a yawn 忍住哈欠
- a yawn of a film 一部乏味的电影

**Examples**
1. He yawned during the long lecture. 漫长的讲座中他打起了哈欠。
2. I couldn't stop yawning in the meeting. 开会时我忍不住一直打哈欠。
3. The movie was a yawn. 这部电影很乏味。
