### yogurt
[ˈjoʊɡərt]

🕒 Updated: 2026-08-16
📅 Created: 2026-08-16
📌 ReviewCount: 1

**Desp**
n. a dairy product made from fermented milk.
酸奶

**Usages**
- Greek yogurt 希腊酸奶
- a cup of yogurt 一杯酸奶
- frozen yogurt 冻酸奶

**Examples**
1. I have yogurt and fruit for breakfast every morning. 我每天早上吃酸奶和水果当早餐。
2. This yogurt is sugar-free and low-fat. 这款酸奶无糖且低脂。
3. She added honey to plain yogurt for flavor. 她在原味酸奶里加了蜂蜜调味。
