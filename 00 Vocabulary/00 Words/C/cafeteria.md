### cafeteria
[ˌkæfəˈtɪəriə]

**Desp**
n. A restaurant where people serve themselves or are served at a counter.
自助餐厅，食堂

**Usages**
- school cafeteria 学校食堂
- the company cafeteria 公司自助餐厅
- eat in the cafeteria 在食堂吃饭

**Examples**
1. Students gather in the school cafeteria at noon. 中午学生们聚在学校食堂里。
2. The hospital cafeteria is open all day. 医院自助餐厅全天开放。
3. We grabbed a quick lunch at the cafeteria. 我们在食堂匆匆吃了午饭。
