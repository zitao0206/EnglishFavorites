### cruise
[kruːz]

**Desp**
v. to sail for pleasure; to drive smoothly; n. a sea voyage.
v. 乘船巡游；（车辆）平稳行驶 n. 巡游，游轮度假

**Usages**
- cruise along the coast 沿海岸巡游
- go on a cruise 乘游轮旅行
- cruise at 500 km/h 以500公里时速巡航

**Examples**
1. They cruised the Caribbean last winter. 去年冬天他们乘游轮游览了加勒比海。
2. The plane cruises at 35,000 feet. 飞机在三万五千英尺高度巡航。
3. The car cruised along the highway. 汽车沿高速公路平稳行驶。
