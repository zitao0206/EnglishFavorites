### complicated
[ˈkɑːmplɪkeɪtɪd]

**Description**
adj. consisting of many interconnected parts; difficult to understand.
adj. 复杂的，难懂的

**Extension**
动词：complicate 使复杂化
名词：complexity 复杂性
近义词：complex 复杂的

**Usages**
- a complicated process 复杂的流程
- a complicated situation 复杂的局面

**Examples**
1. The instructions were too complicated to follow. 说明书太复杂，看不懂。
2. Tax laws are extremely complicated. 税法极其复杂。
3. Their relationship became complicated over time. 他们的关系随时间变得复杂。
