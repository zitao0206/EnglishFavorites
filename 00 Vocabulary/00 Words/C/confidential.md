### confidential
[ˌkɑːnfɪˈdenʃl]

**Desp**
adj. meant to be kept secret.
adj. 机密的，保密的

**Extension**
名词：confidence 信心，秘密

**Usages**
- confidential information 机密信息
- confidential documents 机密文件
- keep sth confidential 对…保密

**Examples**
1. All medical records are strictly confidential. 所有医疗记录都严格保密。
2. The report contains confidential business data. 报告包含机密的商业数据。
3. Please keep this arrangement confidential. 请对此安排保密。
