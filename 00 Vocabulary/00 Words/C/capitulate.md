### capitulate
[kəˈpɪtʃuleɪt]

**Desp**
v. to surrender or give up under pressure.
认输，屈服

**Usages**
- capitulate to pressure 屈服于压力

**Examples**
1. The company capitulated to the workers' demands. 公司屈服于工人的要求。
2. He refused to capitulate despite the threats. 尽管受到威胁，他仍拒绝屈服。
