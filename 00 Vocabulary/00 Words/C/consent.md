### consent
[kənˈsent]

**Description**
v/n. to agree to something; permission.
v. 同意，准许 n. 同意，许可

**Usages**
- consent to do 同意做
- with sb's consent 经某人同意
- informed consent 知情同意

**Examples**
1. She consented to the interview. 她同意接受采访。
2. He took the car without his father's consent. 他未经父亲同意就开了车。
3. Patients must give informed consent before surgery. 手术前病人必须签署知情同意书。
