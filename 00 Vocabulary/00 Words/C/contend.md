### contend


**Desp**
v. To claim or argue; to deal with a difficult situation (contend with).
声称，主张；不得不应对、处理

**Usages**
- contend with sth 应对，处理
- contend that 主张，认为
- contend for 争夺

**Examples**
1. Nurses often have to contend with violent patients. 护士经常需要应对暴躁的病人。
2. The lawyer contended that his client was innocent. 律师主张他的当事人无罪。
3. Three teams are contending for the championship. 三支队伍在争夺冠军。
