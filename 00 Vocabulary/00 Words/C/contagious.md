### contagious
[kənˈteɪdʒəs]

**Description**
adj. spread by contact; (of emotion) likely to spread.
adj. （疾病）接触传染的；（情绪）有感染力的

**Extension**
名词：contagion 传染

**Usages**
- a contagious disease 传染病
- contagious laughter 有感染力的笑声

**Examples**
1. COVID-19 is a highly contagious disease. 新冠是一种传染性极强的疾病。
2. Her enthusiasm is contagious. 她的热情很有感染力。
3. Yawning is contagious. 打哈欠会传染。
