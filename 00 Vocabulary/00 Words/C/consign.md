### consign
[kənˈsaɪn]

**Description**
vt. to hand something over to someone's care.
把…托付给，把…交付给

**Usages**
- consign the book to her sister 把这本书托付给她姐姐

**Examples**
1. She consigned the book to her sister before leaving. 离开前她把书托付给了姐姐。
2. The old equipment was consigned to the basement. 旧设备被送到地下室存放。
3. He consigned his luggage to the care of the hotel. 他把行李托付给酒店保管。
