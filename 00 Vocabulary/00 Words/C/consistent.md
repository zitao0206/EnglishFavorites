### consistent
[kənˈsɪstənt]

**Desp**
adj. always behaving or happening in the same way; in agreement.
一贯的，始终如一的

**Usages**
- be consistent with … 与…一致
- keep a consistent schedule 坚持规律作息

**Examples**
1. You need to keep a consistent schedule to improve your sleep. 你需要坚持规律作息改善睡眠。
2. His words are not consistent with his actions. 他言行不一。
3. Her work has been consistently excellent. 她的工作一直很出色。
