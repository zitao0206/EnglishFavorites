### chore
[tʃɔː(r)]

**Description**
n. a boring or unpleasant task, especially housework.
家务活，杂事，差事

**Usages**
- household chores 家务活
- daily chores 日常杂务

**Examples**
1. Doing the dishes is my least favorite chore. 洗碗是我最讨厌的家务活。
2. He helps with household chores on weekends. 他周末帮忙做家务。
