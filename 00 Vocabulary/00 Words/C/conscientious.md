### conscientious
[ˌkɑːnʃiˈenʃəs]

**Desp**
adj. careful, thorough, and responsible in one's work.
adj. 认真尽责的，一丝不苟的

**Extension**
名词：conscience 良心
名词：conscientiousness 尽责性

**Usages**
- a conscientious worker 尽责的员工
- conscientious research 严谨的研究

**Examples**
1. She is a conscientious student who never misses homework. 她是个认真负责的学生，从不缺交作业。
2. He made a conscientious effort to finish on time. 他尽心尽力按时完成。
3. Conscientious employees double-check their work. 尽责的员工会反复核对工作。
