### compensate
[ˈkɒmpenseɪt]

**Description**
v. to pay someone money for loss or damage; to make up for something.
补偿，赔偿；弥补

**Usages**
- compensation n. 补偿
- compensate sb for sth 因某事补偿某人

**Examples**
1. The company compensated the workers for their injuries. 公司对受伤工人进行了赔偿。
2. Nothing can compensate for the loss of health. 失去健康是无法弥补的。
3. He received a large compensation from the court. 他从法院获得了大笔赔偿。
