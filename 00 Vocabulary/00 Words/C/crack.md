### crack
[kræk]

**Desp**
n/v. a line on the surface of something where it has broken; to break or make something break without separating completely.
n. 裂缝；v. 破裂；破解

**Usages**
- a crack in the wall 墙上的裂缝
- crack a code 破译密码
- crack down on 严厉打击

**Examples**
1. There's a crack in the ceiling. 天花板上有道裂缝。
2. The glass cracked under the heat. 玻璃在高温下裂开了。
3. The police are cracking down on drug trafficking. 警方正在严厉打击毒品交易。
