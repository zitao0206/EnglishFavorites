### constrain
[kənˈstreɪn]

**Desp**
v. to limit or restrict someone or something.
v. 限制，约束，克制

**Extension**
名词：constraint 限制，约束

**Usages**
- be constrained by 受…限制
- financial constraints 资金限制
- constrain development 限制发展

**Examples**
1. Our research was constrained by a lack of funding. 我们的研究因资金不足而受限。
2. Time constraints prevented a full analysis. 时间限制无法进行完整分析。
3. Don't constrain your children's creativity. 别束缚孩子们的创造力。
