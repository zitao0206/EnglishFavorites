### crunchy
[ˈkrʌntʃi]

**Desp**
adj. Firm and making a loud sound when chewed (thick, crispy-crunchy).
松脆的（厚的、咀嚼嘎嘣响）

**Usages**
- crunchy bread 酥脆的面包
- crunchy texture 松脆的口感

**Examples**
1. The apple is nice and crunchy. 苹果脆生生。
2. Pan-fried beans have a crunchy bottom. 煎豆底部吃着嘎嘣脆。
3. I love the crunchy crust of fresh bread. 我喜欢新鲜面包脆脆的外皮。
