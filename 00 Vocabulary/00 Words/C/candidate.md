### candidate
[ˈkændɪdət]

**Desp**
n. a person who is applying for a job or being considered for a position; a person taking an exam.
候选人；应试者；求职者

**Usages**
- a presidential candidate 总统候选人
- a strong candidate 有力的候选人
- a candidate for... ……的候选人

**Examples**
1. She is the best candidate for the job. 她是这份工作的最佳人选。
2. There are three candidates for the position. 这个职位有三名候选人。
3. All candidates must register before the exam. 所有考生都必须在考试前报名。
