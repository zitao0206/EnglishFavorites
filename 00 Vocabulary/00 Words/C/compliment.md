### compliment
[ˈkɒmplɪmənt]

**Desp**
n./v. A remark that expresses praise or admiration; to praise someone.
称赞，恭维；（v.）赞美，夸奖

**Usages**
- pay sb a compliment 称赞某人
- return the compliment 回敬称赞
- compliment sb on sth 就某事称赞某人

**Examples**
1. She paid me a lovely compliment on my cooking. 她夸我的厨艺，我很高兴。
2. He complimented her on her new dress. 他称赞了她的新裙子。
3. Thank you — that's a great compliment. 谢谢——这是很高的夸奖。
