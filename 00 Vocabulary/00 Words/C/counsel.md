### counsel
[ˈkaʊnsl]

**Desp**
n. advice; a lawyer. v. to advise.
n. 忠告，建议；律师；v. 商议，劝告

**Usages**
- keep one's counsel 保留意见，不表态
- counsel sb to do sth 建议某人做某事

**Examples**
1. She kept her counsel and said nothing. 她保留意见，什么也没说。
2. The doctor counseled him to rest more. 医生建议他多休息。
