### chop
[tʃɒp]

**Desp**
v. To cut into pieces with a knife; n. a piece of meat.
剁，砍，切碎；（n.）肉排

**Usages**
- pork chop 猪排
- lamb chop 羊排
- chop up the vegetables 把蔬菜切碎

**Examples**
1. Chop the onions finely. 把洋葱切碎。
2. He ordered a pork chop for dinner. 他晚餐点了猪排。
