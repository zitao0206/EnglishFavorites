### crush
[krʌʃ]

**Description**
v. to press with force to break; to defeat completely; n. a crowd; an infatuation.
v. 压碎，碾碎；彻底击败 n. 迷恋；果汁饮料

**Usages**
- crush the rebellion 镇压叛乱
- crush the garlic 把蒜压碎
- have a crush on sb 迷恋某人

**Examples**
1. The machine crushes the rocks into gravel. 机器把岩石碾成碎石。
2. The team was crushed in the final. 球队在决赛中被彻底击败。
3. She has a crush on her classmate. 她暗恋她的同学。
