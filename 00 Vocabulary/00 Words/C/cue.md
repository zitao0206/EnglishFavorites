### cue
[kjuː]

**Desp**
n. a signal or hint that tells someone to do something.
暗示，提示，信号

**Usages**
- social cue 社交暗示，信号
- take one's cue from 效仿，看…的暗示

**Examples**
1. Yawning is a social cue that you're bored. 打哈欠是你感到无聊的社交信号。
2. She waited for his cue before speaking. 她等他的暗示才开口。
