### confine
[kənˈfaɪn]

**Description**
v. to limit or restrict; to keep within boundaries.
v. 限制；禁闭 n. confines 范围，界限

**Extension**
名词：confinement 限制，监禁

**Usages**
- be confined to 局限于
- confine yourself to 只涉及
- confined space 密闭空间

**Examples**
1. Please confine your comments to the topic. 请把发言限制在主题范围内。
2. The illness confined her to bed for weeks. 疾病让她卧床数周。
3. The study is confined to urban areas. 该研究仅限于城市地区。
