### contaminate
[kənˈtæmɪneɪt]

**Desp**
v. to make something dirty or polluted.
v. 污染，弄脏，玷污

**Extension**
名词：contamination 污染
形容词：contaminated 受污染的

**Usages**
- contaminated water 受污染的水
- contaminate the food chain 污染食物链

**Examples**
1. The river was contaminated by industrial waste. 这条河被工业废水污染了。
2. Wash your hands so you don't contaminate the wound. 洗手以免感染伤口。
3. The scandal contaminated the brand's image. 丑闻玷污了品牌形象。
