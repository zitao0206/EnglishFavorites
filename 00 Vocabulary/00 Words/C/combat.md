### combat
[ˈkɑːmbæt / kəmˈbæt]

**Description**
n. fighting; v. to fight against something harmful.
n. 战斗，格斗 v. 打击，与…作斗争

**Usages**
- combat crime 打击犯罪
- hand-to-hand combat 白刃战
- combat terrorism 打击恐怖主义

**Examples**
1. The government launched new measures to combat inflation. 政府推出了打击通胀的新措施。
2. Soldiers engaged in fierce combat. 士兵们进行了激烈战斗。
3. Regular exercise helps combat stress. 经常锻炼有助于对抗压力。
