### crunch
[krʌntʃ]

**Desp**
v. to make a loud crushing sound when chewing or stepping; n. the sound.
嘎吱作响；嚼碎；（n.）关键时期

- crunchy [ˈkrʌntʃi] adj.  松脆的（厚的、咀嚼嘎嘣响）

**Usages**
- crunch on 嘎吱嘎吱地嚼
- crunch the numbers 核算数字
- crispy adj.（薄、油炸）酥脆
- crunchy bread 酥脆的面包
- crunchy texture 松脆的口感

**Examples**
1. The fried-chicken skin is crispy. 炸鸡皮酥脆。
2. He crunched an apple loudly. 他咔嚓咔嚓地啃着苹果。
3. The snow crunched under our boots. 积雪在我们的靴子下嘎吱作响。
4. The apple is nice and crunchy. 苹果脆生生。
5. Pan-fried beans have a crunchy bottom. 煎豆底部吃着嘎嘣脆。
6. I love the crunchy crust of fresh bread. 我喜欢新鲜面包脆脆的外皮。
