### crunch
[krʌntʃ]

**Desp**
v. To make a loud crushing sound when chewing or stepping; n. the sound.
嘎吱作响；嚼碎；（n.）关键时期

**Usages**
- crunch on 嘎吱嘎吱地嚼
- crunch the numbers 核算数字
- crispy adj.（薄、油炸）酥脆

**Examples**
1. The fried-chicken skin is crispy. 炸鸡皮酥脆。
2. He crunched an apple loudly. 他咔嚓咔嚓地啃着苹果。
3. The snow crunched under our boots. 积雪在我们的靴子下嘎吱作响。
