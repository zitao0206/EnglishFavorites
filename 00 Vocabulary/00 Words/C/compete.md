### compete
[kəmˈpiːt]

**Desp**
v. to try to be better than others; to take part in a contest.
竞争，对抗；参加比赛

**Usages**
- compete with / against 与…竞争
- compete for 争夺

**Examples**
1. Small shops cannot compete with big supermarkets. 小店无法与大超市竞争。
2. Five teams competed for the championship. 五支队伍争夺冠军。
