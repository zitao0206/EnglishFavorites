### compel
[kəmˈpel]

**Description**
v. to force someone to do something; to cause a strong feeling or reaction.
强迫，迫使；引起（感情等）

**Usages**
- compel sb to do sth 迫使某人做某事
- be compelled to 被迫……
- compel admiration 令人钦佩

**Examples**
1. The law compels companies to report these figures. 法律强制公司报告这些数据。
2. He felt compelled to tell the truth. 他觉得不得不说出真相。
3. Her courage compels respect. 她的勇气令人肃然起敬。
