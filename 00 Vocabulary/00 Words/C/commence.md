### commence
[kəˈmens]

**Desp**
v. to begin or start something.
开始，着手

**Usages**
- commence the meeting 开始会议
- commence doing sth 开始做某事
- commence with 以……开始

**Examples**
1. The ceremony will commence at 10 a.m. 仪式将于上午十点开始。
2. Construction of the bridge commenced last month. 这座桥于上月动工。
3. Let us commence the meeting. 让我们开始开会吧。
