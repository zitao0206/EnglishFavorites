### crisp
[krɪsp]

**Desp**
adj. firm, dry, and brittle; pleasantly clear and cool; brisk and decisive.
脆的，酥脆的；清爽的；干脆利落的

**Usages**
- crisp autumn air 清爽的秋风
- crisp cookies 酥脆饼干
- a crisp reply 干脆利落的回答

**Examples**
1. The autumn morning was cool and crisp. 秋天的早晨清凉清爽。
2. She gave a crisp answer and walked away. 她干脆地回答了一句就走了。
3. These potato chips are perfectly crisp. 这些薯片非常酥脆。
