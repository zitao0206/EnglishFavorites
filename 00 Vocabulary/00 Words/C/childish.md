### childish
[ˈtʃaɪldɪʃ]

**Desp**
adj. Behaving in a silly way typical of a young child (disapproving).
幼稚的，孩子气的

**Usages**
- childish behavior 幼稚的行为
- a childish joke 幼稚的玩笑
- stop being childish 别再孩子气了

**Examples**
1. Quarreling over such a small thing is rather childish. 为这点小事吵架相当幼稚。
2. He apologized for his childish behavior. 他为自己幼稚的行为道了歉。
3. Don't be so childish—share the toys with your brother. 别这么孩子气，把玩具和弟弟分享。
