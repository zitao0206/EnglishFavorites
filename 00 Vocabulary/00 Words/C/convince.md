### convince
[kənˈvɪns]

**Desp**
v. to make someone believe something.
v. 使确信，说服

**Extension**
形容词：convincing 有说服力的
形容词：convinced 确信的

**Usages**
- convince sb of sth 使某人相信
- convince sb to do 说服某人做
- a convincing argument 有说服力的论点

**Examples**
1. She convinced the jury of his innocence. 她使陪审团相信他是无辜的。
2. We tried to convince him to see a doctor. 我们试图说服他去看医生。
3. He sounded very convincing at first. 起初他说得头头是道。
