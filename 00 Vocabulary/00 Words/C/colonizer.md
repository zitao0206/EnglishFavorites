### colonizer
[ˈkɒlənaɪzə(r)]

**Desp**
n. a person or group that establishes a colony in a new area.
殖民者，开拓者

**Extension**
动词形式：colonize [ˈkɑːlənaɪz]
美式拼写：colonizer

**Usages**
- European colonisers 欧洲殖民者

**Examples**
1. By the time European colonisers arrived in the early 1800s, kakapo had become confined to the central North Island and forested parts of the South Island. 到19世纪初欧洲殖民者到达时，鸮鹦鹉已仅存于北岛中部和南岛的森林地区。
2. The colonisers brought new diseases. 殖民者带来了新的疾病。
3. Early colonisers faced harsh conditions. 早期开拓者面临严酷的条件。
