### confront
[kənˈfrʌnt]

**Desp**
v. to face a problem or danger directly; to challenge or question someone.
面对，正视；对抗；质问

**Usages**
- confront a problem 直面问题
- be confronted with 面对……
- confront sb about sth 就某事质问某人

**Examples**
1. We must confront the challenges ahead. 我们必须直面前方的挑战。
2. She confronted him about the lie. 她就那个谎言质问他。
3. The country is confronted with an economic crisis. 这个国家正面临经济危机。
