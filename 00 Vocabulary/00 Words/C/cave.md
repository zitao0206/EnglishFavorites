### cave
[keɪv]

**Description**
n. a large hole in the side of a hill or under the ground; v. to explore caves.
洞穴，山洞；v. 洞穴探险；
- cave in 屈服让步/坍塌，坍塌

**Usages**
- a deep cave 深洞
- cave in 坍塌，塌陷
- go caving 去洞穴探险

**Examples**
1. The explorers found paintings in the cave. 探险者在山洞里发现了壁画。
2. The roof of the tunnel caved in. 隧道顶部坍塌了。
3. Bears sometimes hibernate in caves. 熊有时会在山洞里冬眠。
4. On no account can we cave in to terrorism. 我们绝不能向恐怖主义低头。
