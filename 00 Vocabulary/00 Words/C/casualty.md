### casualty
[ˈkæʒuəlti]

**Desp**
n. a person killed or injured in an accident or war; a victim.
伤亡人员，受害者

**Usages**
- heavy casualties 重大伤亡
- civilian casualties 平民伤亡

**Examples**
1. The accident caused several casualties. 事故造成数人伤亡。
2. Small shops were the first casualties of the crisis. 小店是这场危机的第一批受害者。
