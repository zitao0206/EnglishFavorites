### country


**Description**
n. the countryside (as opposed to the city); adj. rural.
n. 乡村，乡下；adj. 乡下的，农村的

**Usages**
- country music 乡村音乐
- country life 乡村生活
- in the country 在乡下

**Examples**
1. They live in the country now. 他们现在住在乡下。
2. Country music is very popular in the south. 乡村音乐在南方很受欢迎。
