### contract
[ˈkɑːntrækt] n. / [kənˈtrækt] v.

**Desp**
n. A legal agreement between two or more people or organizations; v. to make a formal agreement; to become smaller.
合同，契约；签订合同；收缩

**Usages**
- sign a contract 签订合同
- break a contract 违约
- a two-year contract 两年期合同

**Examples**
1. He signed a two-year contract with the club. 他与俱乐部签了两年合同。
2. Metal contracts when it cools. 金属冷却时会收缩。
3. The company contracted with a local supplier. 公司与当地供应商签订了合同。
