### caution
[ˈkɔːʃn]

**Desp**
n. care to avoid danger; a warning; v. to warn.
n. 谨慎，小心；警告 v. 警告，提醒

**Extension**
形容词：cautious 谨慎的

**Usages**
- with caution 小心谨慎地
- exercise caution 保持谨慎
- throw caution to the wind 不顾一切

**Examples**
1. Handle the chemicals with caution. 处理这些化学品要小心。
2. The police issued a caution to drivers. 警方向司机发出警示。
3. He was cautioned for speeding. 他因超速受到警告。
