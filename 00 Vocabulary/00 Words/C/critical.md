### critical
[ˈkrɪtɪkl]

**Desp**
adj. expressing disapproval; extremely important; very serious or dangerous.
批评的；关键的，至关重要的；危急的

**Usages**
- critical thinking 批判性思维
- a critical moment 关键时刻
- be critical of 对……批评的

**Examples**
1. Timing is critical to success. 时机对成功至关重要。
2. He is critical of the new policy. 他对新政策持批评态度。
3. The patient is in critical condition. 病人情况危急。
