### conform
[kənˈfɔːrm]

**Desp**
v. to behave according to rules or standards.
v. 遵守，符合，顺应

**Extension**
名词：conformity 一致，遵从

**Usages**
- conform to the rules 遵守规则
- conform to standards 符合标准
- conform with expectations 符合预期

**Examples**
1. All products must conform to safety standards. 所有产品必须符合安全标准。
2. He refused to conform to social expectations. 他拒绝迎合社会期望。
3. The building conforms to the new regulations. 这座建筑符合新规定。
