### claim

**Desp**
v. to state something as true; to demand something.
声称，宣称；索取

**Usages**
- lay claim to sth 索要，宣称所有权

**Examples**
1. He claims that he saw the accident. 他声称目睹了那场事故。
2. She claimed compensation from the company. 她向公司索赔。
3. No one laid claim to the lost wallet. 没人认领丢失的钱包。
