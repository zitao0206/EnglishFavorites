### cabin
[ˈkæbɪn]

**Desp**
n. A small wooden house; a room on a ship or plane.
小木屋；船舱，机舱

**Usages**
- a log cabin 原木小屋
- cabin crew 机组人员
- first-class cabin 头等舱

**Examples**
1. The cabin was built of logs. 这间小屋由圆木搭建。
2. We stayed in a cabin by the lake. 我们住在湖边的小木屋里。
3. The cabin crew served dinner. 乘务员提供了晚餐。
