### commercial
[kəˈmɜːʃl]

**Desp**
adj. related to business and trade.
商业的

**Usages**
- commercial success 商业成功
- commercial value 商业价值
- commercial purposes 商业用途

**Examples**
1. The movie was a huge commercial success. 这部电影取得了巨大的商业成功。
2. The building is used for commercial purposes. 这栋楼用于商业用途。
