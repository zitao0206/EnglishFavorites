### characteristic
[ˌkærəktəˈrɪstɪk]

**Desp**
n. a typical feature; adj. typical of a person or thing.
n. 特征，特性；adj. 典型的，特有的

**Extension**
名词：character 性格

**Usages**
- a defining characteristic 决定性特征
- be characteristic of 是…的典型特征

**Examples**
1. Generosity is one of her main characteristics. 慷慨是她的主要特点之一。
2. The bold colors are characteristic of his work. 大胆用色是他作品的典型特征。
3. Each species has distinct characteristics. 每个物种都有独特的特征。
