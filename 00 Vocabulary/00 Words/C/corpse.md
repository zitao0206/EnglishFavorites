### corpse
[kɔːps]

**Desp**
n. A dead body, usually of a person.
尸体

**Usages**
- a decomposing corpse 腐烂的尸体
- identify the corpse 辨认尸体
- the corpse of the victim 受害者的尸体

**Examples**
1. The police found a corpse in the woods. 警方在树林里发现了一具尸体。
2. The detective examined the corpse for clues. 侦探检查尸体寻找线索。
3. The corpse was discovered by a hiker early in the morning. 尸体是清晨一位徒步者发现的。
