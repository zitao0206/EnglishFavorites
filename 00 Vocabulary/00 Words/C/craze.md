### craze
[kreɪz]

**Desp**
n. a sudden widespread enthusiasm.
一时风靡的热潮，时尚

**Usages**
- the latest craze 最新潮流
- fitness craze 健身热潮

**Examples**
1. The latest craze is collecting digital art. 最新潮流是收藏数字艺术。
2. The fitness craze swept the country. 健身热潮席卷全国。
3. Every toy has its moment as a craze. 每款玩具都有风靡一时的时刻。
4. Don't waste your money by following the latest craze.
