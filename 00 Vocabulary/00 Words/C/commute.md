### commute
[kəˈmjuːt]

**Desp**
v. to travel regularly between home and work; n. the journey.
v. 通勤 n. 通勤路程

**Extension**
名词：commuter 通勤者

**Usages**
- commute to work 通勤上班
- a long commute 漫长的通勤
- daily commuters 每日通勤族

**Examples**
1. She commutes to the city by train every day. 她每天乘火车进城上班。
2. My morning commute takes about forty minutes. 我早上的通勤大约要四十分钟。
3. More people work from home instead of commuting. 更多的人在家办公而不用通勤。
