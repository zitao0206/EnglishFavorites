### co-founder

**Desp**
n. one of the founders of a company.
联合创始人

**Usages**
- co-founder and CEO 联合创始人兼首席执行官

**Examples**
1. He is the co-founder and CEO of this tech-startup. 他是这家科技初创公司的联合创始人兼首席执行官。
2. She is the co-founder of a popular app. 她是一款热门应用的联合创始人。
