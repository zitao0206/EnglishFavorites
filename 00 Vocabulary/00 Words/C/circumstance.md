### circumstance
[ˈsɜːkəmstəns]

**Description**
n. A fact or condition that affects a situation; the conditions of your life.
情况，情形；环境，境况

**Usages**
- under no circumstances 无论如何都不
- in the circumstances 在这种情况下
- financial circumstances 经济状况

**Examples**
1. Under no circumstances should you give up. 无论如何你都不应该放弃。
2. He adapted quickly to the new circumstances. 他很快适应了新环境。
3. Due to unforeseen circumstances, the event was cancelled. 由于无法预见的情况，活动被取消了。
4. Under no circumstances will I give you up.
