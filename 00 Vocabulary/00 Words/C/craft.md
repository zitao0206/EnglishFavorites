### craft
[kræft]

**Desp**
n. Skill in making things by hand; an activity involving skill. v. To make something with skill.
手艺，工艺；精心制作

**Usages**
- craft beer 精酿啤酒
- traditional craft 传统手工艺
- craft a strategy 精心制定策略
- craft a message 精心措辞

**Examples**
1. She learned the craft of pottery from her grandmother. 她从祖母那里学会了陶艺。
2. The marketing team crafted a brilliant campaign. 营销团队精心策划了一场出色的活动。
3. Local craftsmen sell their crafts at the market. 当地手工艺人在市场上出售他们的手工艺品。
