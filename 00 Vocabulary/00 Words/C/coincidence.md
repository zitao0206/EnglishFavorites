### coincidence
[kəʊˈɪnsɪdəns]

**Desp**
n. When two things happen at the same time by chance, in a surprising way.
巧合，巧事

**Usages**
- by coincidence 碰巧，出于巧合
- a pure coincidence 纯属巧合
- a strange coincidence 奇怪的巧合

**Examples**
1. By coincidence, he also had a secret stash. 巧合的是，他也有一处私藏。
2. It was pure coincidence that we took the same train. 我们乘同一班火车纯属巧合。
3. What a coincidence to meet you here! 在这儿遇到你真是巧合！
