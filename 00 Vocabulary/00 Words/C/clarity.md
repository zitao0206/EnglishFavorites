### clarity
[ˈklærəti]

**Desp**
n. The quality of being clear and easy to understand.
清晰，清楚；明晰

**Usages**
- with clarity 清晰地
- clarity of thought 思路清晰
- bring clarity to 使……变得清晰

**Examples**
1. She explained the plan with great clarity. 她把计划解释得非常清楚。
2. The report lacks clarity in several places. 这份报告有几处表述不清。
3. Meditation can bring clarity of mind. 冥想可以带来思绪的清明。
