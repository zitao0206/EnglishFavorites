### complement
[ˈkɑːmplɪment]

**Desp**
v. to combine well with; n. something that completes.
v. 补充，与…相辅相成 n. 补充物

**Extension**
形容词：complementary 互补的

**Usages**
- complement each other 相互补充
- a perfect complement 完美的补充
- complementary colors 互补色

**Examples**
1. The wine complements the meal perfectly. 这款酒与这顿饭相得益彰。
2. Her skills complement mine perfectly. 她的技能与我的完美互补。
3. The scarf is a perfect complement to the outfit. 这条围巾是整套穿搭的完美点缀。
