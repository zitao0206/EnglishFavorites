### constitute
[ˈkɑːnstɪtuːt]

**Description**
v. to form or make up; to be considered as.
v. 构成，组成；被视为

**Extension**
名词：constitution 宪法，体质

**Usages**
- constitute a threat 构成威胁
- constitute the majority 占多数
- constitute an offense 构成犯罪

**Examples**
1. Women constitute sixty percent of the staff. 女性占员工的60%。
2. The leaks constitute a serious security risk. 泄密构成严重的安全风险。
3. Twelve months constitute a year. 十二个月构成一年。
