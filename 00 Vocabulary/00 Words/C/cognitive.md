### cognitive
[ˈkɒɡnɪtɪv]

**Desp**
adj. Connected with thinking and mental processes.
认知的，认识的

**Usages**
- cognitive ability 认知能力
- cognitive development 认知发展
- cognitive bias 认知偏差

**Examples**
1. Puzzles help improve children's cognitive ability. 益智游戏有助于提高孩子的认知能力。
2. Reading supports cognitive development in early childhood. 阅读促进幼儿的认知发展。
3. Cognitive biases affect our judgment. 认知偏差会影响我们的判断。
