### conclusion
[kənˈkluːʒn]

**Desp**
n. The final opinion or decision reached after thinking; the end of something.
结论；结束，结尾

**Usages**
- draw a conclusion 得出结论
- come to the conclusion that 得出结论认为
- in conclusion 总之

**Examples**
1. The study drew the conclusion that exercise improves memory. 这项研究得出结论：锻炼能改善记忆力。
2. In conclusion, the plan is both practical and affordable. 总之，这个计划既可行又负担得起。
3. We reached the conclusion that further research is needed. 我们得出的结论是需要进一步研究。
