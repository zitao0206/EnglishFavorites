### concern


**Desp**
v. To relate to; to worry; n. worry; something that is important to someone.
v. 涉及，与…相关；让…担心；n. 担心；关心的事

**Usages**
- main concerns 主要关切
- express concern 表达担忧
- concerning prep. 关于

**Examples**
1. What are your main concerns as a writer? 作为一名作家，你最关心哪些问题？
2. They represented their concerns to the authorities. 他们向当局表达了自己的担忧。
3. This issue concerns all of us. 这个问题关系到我们所有人。
