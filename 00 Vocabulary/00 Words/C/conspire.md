### conspire
[kənˈspaɪər]

**Desp**
v. to secretly plan with others to do something harmful or illegal; (of events) to combine to produce a result.
v. 密谋，共谋；共同导致（某事发生）

**Extension**
名词：conspiracy（阴谋，密谋）
形容词：conspiratorial（阴谋的，共谋的）

**Usages**
- conspire against someone 密谋对付某人
- conspire to do something 密谋做某事
- a conspiracy theory 阴谋论
- events conspire 种种事件共同导致

**Examples**
1. The two men conspired to overthrow the government. 这两个人密谋推翻政府。
2. Circumstances conspired to make the project fail. 种种情况共同导致了项目的失败。
3. She was charged with conspiring to smuggle drugs. 她被控密谋走私毒品。
4. Nobody believes his conspiracy theory about aliens. 没人相信他关于外星人的阴谋论。
