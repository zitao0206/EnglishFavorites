### cozy
[ˈkəʊzi]

**Description**
adj. comfortable, warm, and relaxing.
舒适温暖的；温馨的（= cosy）

**Usages**
- a cozy room 温馨舒适的房间
- cozy atmosphere 舒适的氛围
- cozy up to sb 讨好某人

**Examples**
1. The cabin was small but cozy. 小屋虽小但很温馨。
2. We spent a cozy evening by the fireplace. 我们在壁炉旁度过了一个温馨的夜晚。
3. The café has a cozy atmosphere. 这家咖啡馆氛围舒适。
