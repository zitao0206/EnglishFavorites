### colonize
[ˈkɒlənaɪz]

**Desp**
vt. to take control of a place and settle there.
开拓殖民地，殖民

**Usages**
- colonize a region 在某地区殖民

**Examples**
1. European powers colonized many parts of Africa. 欧洲列强在非洲许多地区殖民。
2. The island was colonized in the 17th century. 该岛在17世纪被殖民。
3. Bacteria quickly colonize a wound. 细菌会迅速在伤口上繁殖。
