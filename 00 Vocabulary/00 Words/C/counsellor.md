### counsellor
[ˈkaʊnsələ(r)]

**Description**
n. a person whose job is to give advice; an adviser.
顾问，咨询师；辅导员

**Usages**
- school counsellor 学校辅导员
- career counsellor 职业顾问

**Examples**
1. The school counsellor helped me choose my major. 学校辅导员帮我选择了专业。
2. She works as a marriage counsellor. 她是一名婚姻咨询师。
