### curriculum
[kəˈrɪkjələm]

**Desp**
n. the subjects taught in a school or course.
n. 课程（体系）

**Extension**
复数：curricula

**Usages**
- the national curriculum 国家课程标准
- design a curriculum 设计课程
- curriculum reform 课程改革

**Examples**
1. The school offers a broad and balanced curriculum. 这所学校开设广泛而均衡的课程。
2. Coding is now part of the national curriculum. 编程现已纳入国家课程标准。
3. Teachers discussed reforms to the curriculum. 教师们讨论了课程改革。
