### complacent
[kəmˈpleɪsnt]

**Description**
adj. too satisfied with yourself to see dangers or improve.
adj. 自满的，自鸣得意的

**Extension**
名词：complacency 自满

**Usages**
- become complacent 变得自满
- a complacent attitude 自满的态度

**Examples**
1. We must not become complacent after one victory. 一场胜利之后我们绝不能自满。
2. The company grew complacent and lost market share. 公司变得自满，丢掉了市场份额。
3. His complacent smile annoyed everyone. 他自鸣得意的微笑让所有人恼火。
