### cushion
[ˈkʊʃn]

**Desp**
n. A soft bag of cloth filled with material; v. to soften the effect of an impact.
n. 垫子，坐垫；v. 缓冲，减轻

**Usages**
- back cushion 座椅靠垫
- seat cushion 座垫
- cushion the blow 缓冲打击

**Examples**
1. The seat cushion is very comfortable. 这个座垫非常舒适。
2. Savings can cushion the blow of losing your job. 积蓄能缓冲失业带来的打击。
