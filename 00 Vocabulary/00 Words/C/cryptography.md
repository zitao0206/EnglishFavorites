### cryptography
[krɪpˈtɒɡrəfi]

**Description**
n. the art or science of writing and decoding secret codes and messages.
密码学，密码术

**Usages**
- public-key cryptography 公钥密码学
- a cryptography expert 密码学专家
- the cryptography of the internet 互联网加密技术

**Examples**
1. Cryptography protects our online communications. 密码学保护着我们的在线通信。
2. He studied cryptography at university. 他在大学学习密码学。
3. Modern cryptography is essential for digital security. 现代密码学对数字安全至关重要。
