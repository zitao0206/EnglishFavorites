### crackle
[ˈkrækl]

**Desp**
v/n. to make a series of small sharp snapping sounds; such a sound.
（使）发出噼啪声；噼啪声响

**Usages**
- a crackling sound 噼啪作响的声音
- crackle with energy 充满活力
- the crackle of the fire 篝火的噼啪声

**Examples**
1. The fire crackled in the fireplace. 壁炉里的火噼啪作响。
2. We could hear the crackle of the radio static. 我们能听到收音机的静电噼啪声。
3. The leaves crackled under our feet. 落叶在我们脚下发出脆响。
