### corresponding
[ˌkɒrəˈspɒndɪŋ]

**Description**
adj. matching or connected with something that you have just mentioned.
相应的，对应的

**Usages**
- the corresponding period 同期
- corresponding changes 相应的变化
- corresponding author 通讯作者

**Examples**
1. Profits rose, and corresponding bonuses increased. 利润上升了，相应的奖金也增加了。
2. Sales in March were higher than the corresponding period last year. 三月份的销售额高于去年同期。
3. Please fill in the corresponding information. 请填写相应的信息。
