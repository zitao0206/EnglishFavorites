### curry
[ˈkɜːri]

**Desp**
n. a spicy dish of vegetables or meat served with sauce.
咖喱饭菜

**Usages**
- chicken curry 咖喱鸡
- a spicy curry 辣咖喱

**Examples**
1. We had chicken curry for dinner. 我们晚餐吃了咖喱鸡。
2. She ordered a spicy curry. 她点了一份辣咖喱。
3. Curry is popular in many countries. 咖喱在很多国家都很受欢迎。
