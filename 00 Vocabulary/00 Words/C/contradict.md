### contradict
[ˌkɑːntrəˈdɪkt]

**Description**
v. to say the opposite; to be contrary to.
v. 反驳，否认；与…相矛盾

**Extension**
名词：contradiction 矛盾
形容词：contradictory 矛盾的

**Usages**
- contradict oneself 自相矛盾
- contradict the evidence 与证据相悖

**Examples**
1. The two reports contradict each other. 两份报告互相矛盾。
2. He contradicted his boss in the meeting. 他在会上反驳了上司。
3. Her actions contradict her words. 她的行为与言语相矛盾。
