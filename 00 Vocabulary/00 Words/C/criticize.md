### criticize
[ˈkrɪtɪsaɪz]

**Desp**
v. To say that something or someone is wrong or bad; to judge the merits of a work.
批评，指责；评论，评价

**Usages**
- criticize sb for sth 因某事批评某人
- be criticized as 被批评为……
- criticize the decision 批评这个决定

**Examples**
1. The manager criticized him for being late. 经理批评他迟到了。
2. The film was criticized for its weak plot. 这部电影因情节薄弱而受到批评。
3. It's easy to criticize others' work. 批评别人的工作很容易。
