### contest
[ˈkɑːntest]

**Desp**
n. a competition, event, or struggle to win something.
比赛，竞赛；争夺
v. [kənˈtest] to oppose or dispute.

**Usages**
- a speech contest 演讲比赛
- enter a contest 参加比赛
- contest an election 参加竞选

**Examples**
1. She won first prize in the speech contest. 她在演讲比赛中获一等奖。
2. Twenty teams entered the photography contest. 有20支队伍参加了摄影比赛。
3. He plans to contest the seat in the next election. 他打算在下届选举中角逐这个席位。
