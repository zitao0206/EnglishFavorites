### creepy
[ˈkriːpi]

**Desp**
adj. causing an unpleasant feeling of fear or unease.
令人毛骨悚然的

**Usages**
- a creepy old house 阴森的老房子
- a creepy guy 令人发毛的家伙
- feel creepy 感到毛骨悚然

**Examples**
1. The abandoned hospital looks creepy at night. 那座废弃的医院夜里看着阴森恐怖。
2. There's something creepy about that stranger. 那个陌生人有点让人发毛。
3. The film's creepy music made my skin crawl. 影片毛骨悚然的音乐让我起鸡皮疙瘩。
