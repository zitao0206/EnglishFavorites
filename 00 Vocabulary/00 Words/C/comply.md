### comply
[kəmˈplaɪ]

**Description**
v. to obey a rule, law, or request; to act in accordance with.
遵从，服从，遵守

**Usages**
- comply with the rules 遵守规则
- comply with the law 守法
- refuse to comply 拒绝服从

**Examples**
1. All staff must comply with the safety regulations. 所有员工必须遵守安全规定。
2. The company failed to comply with the order. 这家公司没有遵从指令。
3. If you don't comply, you'll be fined. 不遵守就要被罚款。
