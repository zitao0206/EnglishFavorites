### chilling
[ˈtʃɪlɪŋ]

**Desp**
adj. frightening; making you feel cold or afraid. (from chill n./v.)
令人寒意刺骨的，阴森的，令人恐惧的

**Usages**
- a chilling effect 令人胆寒的影响
- chilling story 恐怖故事

**Examples**
1. The documentary gave a chilling account of the war. 这部纪录片对战争进行了令人不寒而栗的记述。
2. Her chilling smile made everyone uneasy. 她阴森的笑容让所有人不安。
