### chronic
[ˈkrɑːnɪk]

**Desp**
adj. Lasting for a long time; (of a disease) long-lasting; habitual.
慢性的；长期的；习惯性的

**Usages**
- chronic disease 慢性病
- chronic pain 慢性疼痛
- chronic lateness 习惯性迟到

**Examples**
1. He suffers from chronic back pain. 他患有慢性背痛。
2. Chronic stress can damage your health. 长期压力会损害健康。
3. His chronic lateness annoyed the boss. 他习惯性迟到让老板很恼火。
