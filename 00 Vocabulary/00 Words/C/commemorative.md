### commemorative
[kəˈmemərətɪv]

**Desp**
adj. intended to remember and honor someone or something.
纪念性的

**Usages**
- commemorative value 纪念价值
- a commemorative coin 纪念币
- a commemorative event 纪念活动

**Examples**
1. The diary has high commemorative value. 这本日记纪念价值很高。
2. The museum issued a commemorative stamp. 博物馆发行了纪念邮票。
