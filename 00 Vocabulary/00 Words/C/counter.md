### counter
[ˈkaʊntə(r)]

**Desp**
n. a long flat surface in a shop; a person or thing that opposes. v. to oppose or reply to something.
n. 柜台；对手；v. 反驳，反击，抵制

**Usages**
- over the counter 在柜台（买）
- counter an argument 反驳论点

**Examples**
1. The assistant placed the goods on the counter. 店员把商品放在柜台上。
2. She countered his argument with strong evidence. 她用有力的证据反驳了他的论点。
