### cater
[ˈkeɪtər]

**Description**
v. to provide food or services; to satisfy needs.
v. 提供饮食，承办酒席；迎合

**Usages**
- cater for a wedding 承办婚宴
- cater to sb's needs 满足某人的需求
- catering industry 餐饮业

**Examples**
1. The restaurant caters for weddings and parties. 这家餐厅承办婚宴和派对。
2. TV programs cater to all tastes. 电视节目迎合各种口味。
3. The hotel caters mainly to business travelers. 这家酒店主要面向商务旅客。
