### competent
[ˈkɒmpɪtənt]

**Desp**
adj. Having the ability and skill to do something well.
能干的，胜任的

**Usages**
- be competent at/in 胜任……
- a competent teacher 称职的教师
- competent enough to 足以……

**Examples**
1. She is highly competent at her job. 她非常胜任自己的工作。
2. He's a competent and reliable manager. 他是一位称职可靠的经理。
3. The court ruled that he was not competent to stand trial. 法院判定他没有能力接受审判。
