### chickenpox
[ˈtʃɪkɪnpɒks]

**Description**
n. a common infectious disease causing fever and red spots on the skin.
水痘

**Usages**
- come out in chickenpox 出水痘
- catch chickenpox 感染水痘

**Examples**
1. Children usually get chickenpox once. 孩子一般只会出一次水痘。
2. She came out in chickenpox and stayed home. 她出水痘待在家里。
