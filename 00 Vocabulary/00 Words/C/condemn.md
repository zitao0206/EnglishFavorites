### condemn
[kənˈdem]

**Desp**
v. To express strong disapproval of something; to sentence someone to a punishment.
谴责；判刑

**Usages**
- condemn violence 谴责暴力
- condemn sb to death 判处某人死刑
- be condemned as... 被指责为……

**Examples**
1. The government condemned the attack. 政府谴责了这次袭击。
2. The judge condemned him to ten years in prison. 法官判处他十年监禁。
3. The report condemns the use of child labor. 这份报告谴责使用童工的行为。
