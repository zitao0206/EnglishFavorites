### cherish
[ˈtʃerɪʃ]

**Desp**
v. to hold something dear; to keep a hope or memory in one's mind deeply.
珍视，珍惜；怀有（希望、想法）

**Extension**
同义词：treasure, value
形容词：cherished

**Usages**
- cherish the memory 珍藏回忆
- cherish a hope 怀有希望
- cherish every moment 珍惜每一刻

**Examples**
1. I cherish the memories of our time together. 我珍藏着我们共度时光的回忆。
2. She cherishes every moment with her children. 她珍惜和孩子们在一起的每一刻。
3. He still cherishes the hope of returning home someday. 他仍怀有朝一日返乡的希望。
