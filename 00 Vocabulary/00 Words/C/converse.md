### converse
[kənˈvɜːs]

**Description**
adj. opposite in nature.
相反的

**Usages**
- conversely adv. 相反地，反过来
- the converse is also true 反之亦然

**Examples**
1. The two professors conversed about philosophy for hours. 两位教授就哲学交谈了数小时。
2. He argued the converse point of view. 他持相反的观点。
3. Conversely, higher prices may reduce demand. 反过来，高价可能会降低需求。
