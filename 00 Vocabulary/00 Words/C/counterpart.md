### counterpart
[ˈkaʊntərpɑːrt]

**Desp**
n. A person or thing that has the same purpose or does the same job as another in a different place or organization.
对应的人（或物）；职位相当的人

**Usages**
- the Chinese counterpart 中方对应人员
- one's counterpart in 在……中的对应者
- meet one's counterpart 会见同行

**Examples**
1. The minister met his French counterpart. 部长会见了他法国同级别的官员。
2. The CEO discussed the deal with his American counterpart. 首席执行官与美国同行讨论了这笔交易。
3. Each department has a counterpart in the other company. 每个部门在另一家公司都有对应部门。
