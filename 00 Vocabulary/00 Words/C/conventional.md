### conventional
[kənˈvenʃənl]

**Description**
adj. based on what is usually done or believed; traditional.
adj. 传统的，常规的；守旧的

**Extension**
反义词：unconventional 非传统的
名词：convention 惯例，大会

**Usages**
- conventional methods 传统方法
- conventional weapons 常规武器
- conventional wisdom 传统观念

**Examples**
1. Conventional teaching methods don't suit every child. 传统教学方法不适合每个孩子。
2. The house runs on conventional electricity. 这栋房子使用常规电力。
3. Conventional wisdom says opposites attract. 传统观念认为异性相吸。
