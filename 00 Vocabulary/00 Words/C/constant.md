### constant
[ˈkɑːnstənt]

**Desp**
adj. Occurring continuously over a period of time; unchanging. n. A quantity that remains the same.
持续的，不断的；恒定的

**Usages**
- constant change 不断变化
- constant noise 持续的噪音
- a constant companion 忠实的伙伴
- remain constant 保持恒定

**Examples**
1. The constant noise from construction is driving me crazy. 施工持续不断的噪音快把我逼疯了。
2. Her constant support helped him through the difficult time. 她持续的支持帮助他度过了难关。
3. The temperature must remain constant during the experiment. 实验过程中温度必须保持恒定。
