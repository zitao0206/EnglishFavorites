### coupon
[ˈkuːpɑːn]

**Description**
n. A piece of paper or code that gives you a discount or a special offer.
优惠券，代金券

**Usages**
- a discount coupon 折扣券
- a coupon code 优惠码
- clip coupons 剪优惠券

**Examples**
1. Use this coupon to get 20% off. 用这张优惠券可享八折。
2. She collects coupons to save money. 她收集优惠券省钱。
3. Enter the coupon code at checkout. 结账时输入优惠码。
