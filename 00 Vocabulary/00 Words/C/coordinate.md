### coordinate
[koʊˈɔːrdɪneɪt]

**Description**
v. to organize things so that they work together effectively; n. a position on a map or graph.
协调，配合；坐标

**Usages**
- coordinate with 与……协调
- coordinate efforts 协调努力
- coordinate an event 组织活动

**Examples**
1. She coordinates the volunteers for the event. 她协调活动的志愿者。
2. We need to coordinate our efforts. 我们需要协调我们的努力。
3. Please coordinate with the other team. 请与另一个团队协调配合。
