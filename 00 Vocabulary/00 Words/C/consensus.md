### consensus
[kənˈsensəs]

**Desp**
n. An opinion that all members of a group agree with.
共识，一致意见

**Usages**
- reach a consensus 达成共识
- a general consensus 普遍的共识
- consensus on/among 关于……的共识

**Examples**
1. The committee finally reached a consensus. 委员会最终达成了共识。
2. There is a growing consensus that we must act now. 越来越多的人达成共识：我们必须现在就采取行动。
3. It's hard to reach a consensus among so many people. 在这么多人之间达成共识很难。
