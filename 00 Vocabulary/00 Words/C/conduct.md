### conduct
[kənˈdʌkt / ˈkɑːndʌkt]

**Description**
v. to carry out or lead; n. behavior.
v. 实施，进行；指挥 n. 行为，举止

**Extension**
名词：conductor 指挥家，售票员

**Usages**
- conduct research 开展研究
- conduct a survey 进行调查
- professional conduct 职业行为

**Examples**
1. The team conducted experiments on mice. 团队用老鼠做了实验。
2. He conducted the orchestra brilliantly. 他出色地指挥了管弦乐队。
3. Her conduct at work was unprofessional. 她在工作中的行为不够专业。
