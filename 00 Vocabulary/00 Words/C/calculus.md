### calculus
[ˈkælkjələs]

**Description**
n. the branch of mathematics dealing with rates of change and areas.
微积分

**Usages**
- differential calculus 微分学
- integral calculus 积分学
- study calculus 学习微积分

**Examples**
1. She is taking a calculus course this semester. 她这学期在上微积分课。
2. Differential calculus deals with rates of change. 微分学研究变化率。
3. Integral calculus helps calculate areas under curves. 积分学用于计算曲线下面积。
