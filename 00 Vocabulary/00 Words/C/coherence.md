### coherence
[kəʊˈhɪərəns]

**Desp**
n. the quality of being logical, consistent and easy to understand.
连贯性，一致性

**Extension**
形容词：coherent

**Usages**
- coherence and cohesion 连贯与衔接
- logical coherence 逻辑连贯性

**Examples**
1. A good essay should have coherence and cohesion. 好的文章应当连贯且衔接。
2. The argument lacks coherence. 这个论点缺乏连贯性。
3. Coherence is one of the IELTS writing assessment criteria. 连贯性是雅思写作评分标准之一。
