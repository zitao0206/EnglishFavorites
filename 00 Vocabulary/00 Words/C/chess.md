### chess
[tʃes]

**Desp**
n. a board game played by two people, using 16 pieces each, on a board of 64 squares.
国际象棋

**Usages**
- play chess 下棋
- a chess match 国际象棋比赛
- chess master 象棋大师

**Examples**
1. My grandfather taught me how to play chess. 爷爷教我下国际象棋。
2. She is the chess champion of our school. 她是我们学校的象棋冠军。
3. Life is like a game of chess. 人生就像一盘棋。
