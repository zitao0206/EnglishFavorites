### consolidate
[kənˈsɑːlɪdeɪt]

**Desp**
v. to make stronger; to combine into one.
v. 巩固；合并，统一

**Extension**
名词：consolidation 巩固，合并

**Usages**
- consolidate one's position 巩固地位
- consolidate debt 合并债务
- consolidate the gains 巩固成果

**Examples**
1. The company consolidated its three offices into one. 公司把三个办事处合并为一个。
2. The win consolidated their lead in the league. 这场胜利巩固了他们在联赛中的领先地位。
3. The regime consolidated power after the coup. 政变后该政权巩固了权力。
