### concrete
[ˈkɒŋkriːt]

**Desp**
adj. definite and specific; n. a building material made of cement.
具体的，明确的；混凝土

**Usages**
- concrete solutions 具体可行的方案
- concrete evidence 确凿的证据
- concrete examples 具体的例子

**Examples**
1. We need concrete solutions, not empty talk. 我们需要具体可行的方案，而不是空谈。
2. There is no concrete evidence against him. 没有对他不利的确凿证据。
3. The floor is made of concrete. 地面是混凝土做的。
