### cope
[koʊp]

**Description**
v. to deal successfully with something difficult.
v. 应付，妥善处理

**Usages**
- cope with stress 应对压力
- cope with the workload 应付工作量
- cope well 处理得当

**Examples**
1. She copes well with pressure at work. 她能很好地应对工作压力。
2. How do you cope with so many deadlines? 你怎么应付这么多截止日期？
3. The hospital struggled to cope with the influx of patients. 医院难以应对大量涌入的病人。
