### criteria
[kraɪˈtɪəriə]

**Desp**
n. standards or principles by which something is judged or decided.
标准，准则（criterion 的复数形式）

**Extension**
单数形式：criterion

**Usages**
- meet the criteria 符合标准
- selection criteria 选拔标准
- assessment criteria 评估标准

**Examples**
1. The candidate did not meet the criteria for the position. 这位候选人不符合该职位的标准。
2. What are the selection criteria for this scholarship? 这项奖学金的选拔标准是什么？
3. Applicants must satisfy all the criteria. 申请者必须满足所有标准。
