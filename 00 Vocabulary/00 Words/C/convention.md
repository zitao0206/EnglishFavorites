### convention
[kənˈvenʃn]

**Description**
n. A large formal meeting of people with the same interest; a way of behaving that is generally accepted.
大会，会议；惯例，常规；公约

**Usages**
- a trade convention 贸易大会
- social conventions 社会习俗
- by convention 按照惯例

**Examples**
1. She is attending a convention in Shanghai. 她正在上海参加一个大会。
2. It's a convention to shake hands when you meet someone. 见面握手是一种社交惯例。
3. The convention was signed by 190 countries. 该公约由190个国家签署。
