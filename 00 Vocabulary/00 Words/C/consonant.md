### consonant
[ˈkɒnsənənt]

**Desp**
n. A speech sound that is not a vowel; a letter representing such a sound.
辅音，辅音音素

**Usages**
- voiceless consonants 清辅音
- voiced consonants 浊辅音
- initial consonant 词首辅音

**Examples**
1. The word 'sky' begins with a consonant cluster. sky 一词以辅音连缀开头。
2. Voiceless and voiced consonants are distinguished by vibration of the vocal cords. 清浊辅音以声带是否振动来区分。
