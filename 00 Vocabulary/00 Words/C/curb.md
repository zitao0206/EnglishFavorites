### curb
[kɜːrb]

**Desp**
v. to control or limit something; n. a control; the edge of a sidewalk.
v. 控制，抑制 n. 抑制；路缘

**Usages**
- curb inflation 抑制通胀
- curb one's temper 控制脾气
- put a curb on 限制

**Examples**
1. New rules were introduced to curb pollution. 出台了新规定来控制污染。
2. She fought to curb her anger. 她努力压住怒火。
3. Measures to curb spending proved effective. 控制开支的措施很有效。
