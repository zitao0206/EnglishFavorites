### comprise
[kəmˈpraɪz]

**Desp**
v. to consist of or be made up of; to constitute or make up.
包含，包括；组成，构成

**Extension**
同义词：constitute, consist of, encompass
易混：compose（组成，be composed of）

**Usages**
- be comprised of... 由……组成
- comprise several parts 包含几个部分
- comprise the majority 占多数

**Examples**
1. The committee comprises members from five countries. 委员会由来自五个国家的成员组成。
2. Women comprise sixty percent of the workforce. 女性占劳动力的百分之六十。
3. The book comprises twelve chapters. 这本书共有十二章。
