### chaotic
[keɪˈɒtɪk]

**Desp**
adj. In a state of complete confusion and disorder.
混乱的，无序的（→ chaos n. 混乱）

**Usages**
- a chaotic situation 混乱的局面
- chaos n. 混乱
- descend into chaos 陷入混乱

**Examples**
1. The office was chaotic during the move. 搬家期间办公室一片混乱。
2. Traffic was chaotic after the accident. 事故后交通一片混乱。
3. The whole city fell into chaos. 整座城市陷入混乱。
