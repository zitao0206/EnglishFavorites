### cling
[klɪŋ]

**Description**
v. to hold on tightly; to stay close; to stick to.
v. 紧紧抓住；依附；坚持

**Extension**
过去式：clung

**Usages**
- cling to the hope 抱着希望不放
- cling together 紧靠在一起
- cling to old ideas 抱残守缺

**Examples**
1. The child clung to her mother's hand. 孩子紧紧抓住妈妈的手。
2. Wet clothes cling to your body. 湿衣服贴在身上。
3. He still clings to the hope of reconciliation. 他仍然抱着和解的希望。
