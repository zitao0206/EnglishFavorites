### capacity
[kəˈpæsəti]

**Desp**
n. The ability to do something; the amount that something can hold or produce.
能力；容量，容积；职位

**Usages**
- in one's capacity as 以……的身份
- capacity to learn 学习能力
- the capacity of the stadium 体育场的容量

**Examples**
1. The stadium has a seating capacity of 60,000. 这座体育场可容纳六万人。
2. She has a great capacity for hard work. 她非常能吃苦。
3. He spoke in his capacity as chairman. 他以主席的身份发言。
