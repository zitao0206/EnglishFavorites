### be true of
[bi truː əv]

📌 ReviewCount: 1

**Desp**

phr. To apply to; to be valid or accurate for (someone or something).
正是如此，也是如此；对……同样成立

**Usages**

- the same is true of ……也是如此
- be true of most cases 适用于大多数情况
- equally true of 同样适用于

**Examples**

1. The rule applies to all employees, and the same is true of the managers. 这条规则适用于所有员工，对管理者也是如此。
2. Prices vary by region, and this is true of housing costs too. 各地价格不同，住房成本也是如此。
3. What he said about kids is equally true of adults. 他关于孩子的说法对成年人同样成立。
