### be pointing to

🕒 Updated: 2026-08-16
📅 Created: 2026-08-16
📌 ReviewCount: 1

**Desp**

phr. To direct attention toward; to indicate, suggest, or prove something.
指向，表明，暗示，证明

**Usages**

- the arrow is pointing to the exit 箭头指向出口
- evidence pointing to sth/sb 指向某事/某人的证据
- everything points to sth 一切都表明……

**Examples**

1. The arrow is pointing to the exit. 箭头指向出口。
2. The evidence points to him as the thief. 证据表明他就是小偷。
3. All signs are pointing to a market rebound. 一切迹象都表明市场即将反弹。
