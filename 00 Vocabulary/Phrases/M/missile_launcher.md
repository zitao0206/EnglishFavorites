### missile launcher
[ˈmɪsl ˈlɔːntʃər]

📌 ReviewCount: 1

**Desp**

phr. A device or vehicle used to fire missiles.
导弹发射器，导弹发射装置

**Usages**

- a portable missile launcher 便携式导弹发射器
- fire a missile launcher 发射导弹
- an air-defense missile launcher 防空导弹发射器

**Examples**

1. The soldiers carried a shoulder-fired missile launcher. 士兵们携带了一具肩扛式导弹发射器。
2. Satellite images revealed a new missile launcher site. 卫星图像显示了一处新的导弹发射装置阵地。
3. The missile launcher was hidden under a camouflage net. 导弹发射器被藏在伪装网下。
