### the Trump administration
[ðə trʌmp ədˌmɪnɪˈstreɪʃn]

📌 ReviewCount: 1

**Desp**

phr. The US government under President Trump.
特朗普政府，特朗普当局

**Usages**

- during the Trump administration 在特朗普政府期间
- the Trump administration's policy 特朗普政府的政策
- officials in the Trump administration 特朗普政府的官员

**Examples**

1. The trade war began during the Trump administration. 贸易战始于特朗普政府时期。
2. The Trump administration imposed new tariffs on steel. 特朗普政府对钢铁加征了新关税。
3. She served as an advisor in the Trump administration. 她在特朗普政府中担任顾问。
