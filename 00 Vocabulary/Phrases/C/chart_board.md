### chart board
[tʃɑːrt bɔːrd]

🕒 Updated: 2026-08-16
📅 Created: 2026-08-16
📌 ReviewCount: 1

**Desp**

phr. A board or panel displaying charts; similar to a dashboard.
表格/图表板，

**Extension**
dash board 仪表盘

**Usages**

- a chart board for sales data 销售数据图表板
- display on the chart board 展示在图表板上
- update the chart board 更新图表板

**Examples**

1. The manager checks the chart board every morning. 经理每天早上查看图表板。
2. All KPIs are shown on one chart board, just like a dashboard. 所有关键指标都显示在一块图表板上，就像仪表盘一样。
3. You can add new metrics to the chart board in settings. 你可以在设置中向图表板添加新指标。
