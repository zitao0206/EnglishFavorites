### show off
[ˈʃəʊ ɒf]

**Desp**
n. a person who behaves in a way intended to attract attention; showing off.
炫耀，卖弄；爱炫耀的人

**Usages**
- a big show-off 很爱炫耀的人
- just to show off 纯粹为了炫耀

**Examples**
1. She only bought that sports car to show off and prove she could afford one. 她买跑车纯粹为炫富。
2. He's such a show-off at parties. 他在派对上特别爱炫耀。
