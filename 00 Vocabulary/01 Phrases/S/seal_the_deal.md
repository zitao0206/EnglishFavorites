### seal the deal

**Desp**

phr. to finalize an agreement; to make an arrangement certain.
敲定交易，达成协议

**Usages**

- seal the deal with sb 与某人敲定交易
- seal the deal on sth 就某事达成协议
- a handshake to seal the deal 握手敲定交易

**Examples**

1. A free upgrade was enough to seal the deal. 免费升级就足以促成这笔交易。
2. They sealed the deal over dinner. 他们在饭局上敲定了交易。
3. The contract was signed, sealing the deal. 合同签了，交易正式达成。
