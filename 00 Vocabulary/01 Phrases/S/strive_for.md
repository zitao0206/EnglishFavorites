### strive for

**Desp**
phr&v. to try very hard to achieve something.
努力争取，为……而奋斗

**Usages**
- strive for the honor of ... 为……的荣誉而奋斗
- strive for excellence 追求卓越
- strive for a goal 为目标而奋斗

**Examples**
1. Athletes strive for the honor of their country. 运动员为国家荣誉而奋斗。
2. We should constantly strive for excellence in our work. 我们应当在工作中不断追求卓越。
3. He strove for years to achieve financial independence. 他奋斗多年以实现经济独立。
