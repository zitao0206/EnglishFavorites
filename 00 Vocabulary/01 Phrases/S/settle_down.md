### settle down

**Desp**
phr&v. to begin living a stable, settled life; to become calm after excitement.
安定下来，定居；（激动后）平静下来

**Usages**
- settle down and get married 成家立业
- settle down in the countryside 在乡下定居
- settle down, kids! 孩子们，安静下来！

**Examples**
1. After years of traveling, they finally settled down in Chengdu. 多年旅行后，他们最终在成都定居下来。
2. It took the children a while to settle down after the party. 聚会过后，孩子们过了好一会儿才安静下来。
3. He wants to settle down and start a family before thirty. 他想在三十岁前成家立业。
