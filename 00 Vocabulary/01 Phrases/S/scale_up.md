### scale up

**Desp**
phr. to increase in size or amount. ↔ scale down 缩减规模
扩容，扩大规模

**Usages**
- scale up production 扩大生产
- scale down 缩减规模

**Examples**
1. We need to scale up the large-language-model for global users. 我们需要为全球用户扩大大模型的规模。
2. The company plans to scale up its operations. 公司计划扩大运营规模。
3. They had to scale down the project due to budget cuts. 由于预算削减，他们不得不缩减项目规模。
