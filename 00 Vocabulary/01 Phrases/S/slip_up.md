### slip up

**Desp**
phr&v. to make a careless mistake; to err.
犯错，失误，出岔子

**Extension**
同义：make a mistake, mess up, blunder
名词：slip-up（失误）

**Usages**
- slip up on the details 在细节上出错
- a costly slip-up 代价高昂的失误
- sorry, I slipped up 对不起，我搞错了

**Examples**
1. I slipped up and sent the email to the wrong person. 我失误了，把邮件发错了人。
2. One little slip-up can ruin the whole plan. 一个小失误就可能毁掉整个计划。
3. The accountant slipped up on the tax forms. 会计在税表上出了差错。
