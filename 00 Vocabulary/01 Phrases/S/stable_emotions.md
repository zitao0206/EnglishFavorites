### stable emotions

**Desp**
phr. a calm, steady emotional state.
稳定的情绪

**Usages**
- keep stable emotions 保持情绪稳定
- stable emotional state 稳定的情绪状态

**Examples**
1. We need to keep stable emotions in our daily life. 日常生活中我们要保持情绪稳定。
2. Stable emotions help us make better decisions. 稳定的情绪有助于我们做出更好的决定。
