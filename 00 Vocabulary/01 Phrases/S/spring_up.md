### spring up

**Desp**
phr. to appear suddenly in large numbers.
突然出现，涌现

**Usages**
- cafes spring up everywhere 咖啡店到处涌现
- spring up like mushrooms 如雨后春笋般涌现

**Examples**
1. New cafes have sprung up everywhere. 到处新开了很多咖啡店。
2. High-rise buildings are springing up across the city. 城市里高楼大厦如雨后春笋般涌现。
3. New ideas spring up during discussion. 讨论中常有新想法冒出来。
