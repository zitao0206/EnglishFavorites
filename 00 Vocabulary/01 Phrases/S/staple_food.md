### staple food

**Desp**
phr. The basic food eaten regularly in a place.
主食

**Usages**
- a staple food 主食
- staple diet 日常主食

**Examples**
1. Fish is a staple food in the diet of many Africans. 鱼是很多非洲人的主食。
2. Rice is the staple food in many Asian countries. 大米是许多亚洲国家的主食。
