### stand up for

**Description**
phr. to defend or support someone or something.
支持，维护

**Usages**
- stand up for your rights 维护你的权利
- stand up for sb 为某人辩护

**Examples**
1. She always stands up for what she believes in. 她总是维护自己相信的东西。
2. Stand up for yourself and don't be bullied. 要为自己撑腰，不要被欺负。
3. He stood up for his friend in the argument. 他在争论中为朋友辩护。
