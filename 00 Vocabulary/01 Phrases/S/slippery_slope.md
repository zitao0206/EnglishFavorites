### slippery slope
[ˌslɪpəri ˈsloʊp]

**Desp**

phr. a dangerous chain reaction: a course of action that seems harmless at first but leads to serious consequences.
滑坡效应，灾难性连锁反应（常用于政治、辩论等场景）

**Usages**

- a slippery slope argument 滑坡式论证
- be on a slippery slope 处于危险的滑坡中
- start down a slippery slope 迈出危险的第一步

**Examples**

1. Censoring one book is a slippery slope toward controlling all ideas. 审查一本书是走向控制一切思想的滑坡。
2. Once you start lying, you're on a slippery slope. 一旦开始撒谎，就会陷入恶性连锁。
3. Opponents argued the new law was a slippery slope to higher taxes. 反对者称新法案是通向更高税收的滑坡。
