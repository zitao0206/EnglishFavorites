### star power

**Desp**

phr. the influence and popularity of famous people (stars).
明星实力，明星影响力，群星云集


**Usages**

- full of star power 大牌云集

**Examples**

1. The game will be full of star power. 这场大赛将大牌云集，星光十足。
2. The film relies on star power to attract audiences. 这部电影靠明星影响力吸引观众。
3. Her star power helped the brand sell out in days. 她的明星效应让品牌几天内售罄。
