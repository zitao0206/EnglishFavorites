### in the quicksand of

**Desp**

idiom. trapped inside a messy hard to escape situation
字面含义：流沙之中，一旦踩进去会越挣扎陷得越深。
陷入……的泥潭；困于棘手、越陷越难脱身的局面 (政治、外交新闻高频比喻。)

**Usages**
‑ be caught in the quicksand of … 深陷……泥潭
‑ sink into the quicksand of … 堕入……的泥潭
‑ become mired in the quicksand of … 越陷越深，难以抽身

**Examples**
1. The United States is caught in the quicksand of the Middle‑East conflict.
美国陷入了中东冲突的泥潭。
2. The government sank into the quicksand of endless political arguments.
政府陷入无休止政治争论的泥潭。
3. The firm became mired in the quicksand of costly legal battles.
该公司在代价高昂的法律纠纷泥潭中越陷越深。

💡同义高阶替换
‑ in the morass of …
‑ bogged down in …
