### in the aftermath

**Desp**
phr. following an event, especially a disastrous one.
在……之后（尤指灾难之后）

**Usages**
- in the aftermath of 在……之后
- in the aftermath of the war 在战争之后

**Examples**
1. In the aftermath of the storm, many trees were uprooted. 风暴过后，许多树被连根拔起。
2. The country struggled in the aftermath of the earthquake. 地震之后，这个国家艰难挣扎。
3. In the aftermath of the decision, shares plummeted. 决定出台后，股价暴跌。
