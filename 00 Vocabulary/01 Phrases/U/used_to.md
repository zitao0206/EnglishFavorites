### used to

**Desp**
phr&v. used to describe a past habit or state that no longer exists.
过去常常做…（现在不再做）

**Usages**
- used to do sth 过去常做某事
- didn't use to 以前并不

**Examples**
1. I used to play basketball after school. 我以前放学后常打篮球。
2. I didn't use to like coffee, but now I love it. 我以前不喜欢咖啡，现在却很喜欢。
3. There used to be a park here. 这里以前有个公园。
