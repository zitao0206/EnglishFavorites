### under my administration

**Description**
phr. within one's administration or management.
在我的管辖范围内

**Usages**
- under one's administration 在某人的管辖下

**Examples**
1. All schools under my administration follow the same rules. 我管辖范围内所有学校都遵循同样的规则。
2. The reform happened under his administration. 改革发生在他的任期内。
