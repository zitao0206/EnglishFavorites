### dive into

**Desp**
phr&v. to plunge into water; to start doing something with enthusiasm and depth.
深入，潜入；潜心钻研，深入探讨

**Usages**
- dive into the pool 跳进泳池
- dive into a book 埋头读书
- dive into the data 深入研究数据

**Examples**
1. She dived into the research and forgot the time. 她潜心钻研研究，忘记了时间。
2. Let's dive into the details of the proposal. 我们来深入探讨一下方案的细节。
3. He dived into the pool to cool off. 他跳进泳池凉快一下。
