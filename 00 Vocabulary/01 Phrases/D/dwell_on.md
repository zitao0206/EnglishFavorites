### dwell on

**Description**
phr&v. to think or talk too much about something, especially something unpleasant.
老是想着，纠结于，（不快的事）念念不忘

**Extension**
反义：let go of（放下）、move on（向前看）
动词：dwell（居住；存在于）

**Usages**
- dwell on the past 纠结于过去
- dwell on mistakes 老想着错误
- don't dwell on it 别再想了

**Examples**
1. Don't dwell on your failure—learn from it and move on. 别纠结于失败——从中吸取教训，继续前行。
2. She kept dwelling on the argument they had. 她一直想着他们那次争吵。
3. Let's not dwell on the problem; let's find a solution. 我们别老盯着问题了，找个解决办法吧。
