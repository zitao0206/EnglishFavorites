### work around

**Description**
phr&v. to find a way to avoid or bypass a problem; to accommodate someone's schedule or constraints.
变通处理（找到规避办法，绕过）；迁就某人，配合某人时间

**Usages**
- work around a problem 绕过问题变通处理
- work around your schedule 迁就你的时间安排
- work around the clock 昼夜不停地工作

**Examples**
1. The system is slow, but we can work around it. 系统很慢，但我们可以绕过它变通处理。
2. I can work around your schedule—just tell me when you're free. 我可以配合你的时间安排，告诉我你什么时候有空就行。
3. They worked around the clock to meet the deadline. 他们昼夜不停地赶在截止日期前完成。
