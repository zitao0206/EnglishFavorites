### with good grace
 
**Description**

phr. willingly and politely, without complaining
欣然地；大度体面地；毫无怨言地（接受不愉快的事：失败、批评、损失）

💡【反】**with bad grace** 不情愿地，勉勉强强地
💡【同】 **gracefully** adv. 优雅地、体面地

**Usages**

‑ accept sth with good grace 大度地接受（失败、批评）
‑ lose with good grace 输得有风度

**Examples**

1. Although he lost the election, he accepted the result with good grace.
虽然输掉选举，他还是大度体面地接受了结果。
2. You should learn to take criticism with good grace.
你应当学会坦然大度地接受批评。
3. She admitted her mistake with good grace.
她爽快大方地承认了自己的错误。
