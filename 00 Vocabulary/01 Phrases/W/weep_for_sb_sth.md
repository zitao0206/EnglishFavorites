### weep for sb/sth

**Description**
phr&v. to cry because of sadness for someone or something.
为……哭泣

**Usages**
- weep for the lost 为逝去者哭泣
- weep for joy 喜极而泣
- weep for one's fate 为命运悲泣

**Examples**
1. People wept for the victims of the disaster. 人们为灾难中的遇难者哭泣。
2. She wept for her lost homeland. 她为失去的祖国而哭泣。
3. He wept for joy at the good news. 听到好消息，他喜极而泣。
