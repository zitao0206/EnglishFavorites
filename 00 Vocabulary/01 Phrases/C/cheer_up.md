### cheer up

**Desp**
phr&v. to become happier; to make someone happier.
振作起来，使高兴起来

**Usages**
- cheer sb up 让某人振作
- cheer up! 打起精神！

**Examples**
1. Cheer up! Things will get better. 振作起来！一切都会好起来的。
2. A funny video always cheers me up. 有趣的视频总能让我开心起来。
