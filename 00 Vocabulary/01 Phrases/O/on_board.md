### on board
[bɔːrd]

**Desp**
phr. on a ship, plane, or train; involved in a project or organization; agreeing to support something.
在船/飞机/车上；参与，加盟；同意，支持

**Usages**
- welcome sb on board 欢迎某人加入
- get/come on board 加入，参与
- bring everyone on board 让所有人达成一致
- all the passengers on board 机上/船上的所有乘客
- baby on board

**Examples**
1. We welcome the new manager on board. 我们欢迎新经理加入。
2. It took a while to get the whole team on board with the plan. 花了一些时间才让整个团队认同这个计划。
3. Please remain seated while we are on board the aircraft. 在飞机上时请保持就座。
4. Once she came on board, the project moved quickly. 她加入后，项目进展迅速。
