### on the tip of my tongue

**Desp**
phr. About to be said but not quite remembered or recalled.
话到嘴边（想不起来）

**Usages**
- be on the tip of one's tongue 就在嘴边
- it's on the tip of my tongue 我一时想不起来

**Examples**
1. His name is on the tip of my tongue—wait a minute. 他的名字就在嘴边，等我想想。
2. The answer is on the tip of my tongue, but I can't recall it. 答案就在嘴边，可我就是想不起来。
