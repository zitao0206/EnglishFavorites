### our peers

**Desp**

phr. people who are the same age or status as us.
我们的同辈，与我们同龄/同地位的人

**Usages**

- compared with our peers 与同辈相比
- pressure from our peers 来自同辈的压力
- learn from our peers 向同辈学习

**Examples**

1. Chinese students outperformed their peers in math. 中国学生的数学成绩超过了同辈。
2. We often judge ourselves against our peers. 我们常拿自己与同辈作比较。
3. Teenagers care a lot about what their peers think. 青少年很在意同辈的看法。
