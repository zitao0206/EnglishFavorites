### on target

**Desp**
phr. on course to achieve a goal; making expected progress.
达到目标，按计划推进

**Usages**
- be on target 达到目标
- on target to reach 按计划达到

**Examples**
1. After the first five years of the Recovery Plan, the population was on target. 在恢复计划实施头五年后，种群数量达到了目标。
2. The project is on target to be completed by June. 该项目按计划将在六月前完成。
3. Sales are on target for the quarter. 本季度销售额达标。
