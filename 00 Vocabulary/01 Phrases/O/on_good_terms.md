### on good terms

**Description**
phr. having a friendly or harmonious relationship with someone.
关系融洽，相处友好

**Extension**
反义：on bad terms（关系不好）
相关：on speaking terms（仅打招呼的交情）

**Usages**
- be on good terms with someone 与某人关系融洽
- remain on good terms 保持友好关系
- part on good terms 好聚好散

**Examples**
1. I'm on good terms with all my colleagues. 我和所有同事都相处融洽。
2. Even after the divorce, they remained on good terms. 即使离婚后，他们仍然保持着友好关系。
3. It's important to stay on good terms with your neighbors. 和邻居保持融洽的关系很重要。
