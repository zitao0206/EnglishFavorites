### on track

**Desp**
phr. making progress in the right way; likely to achieve the desired result.
步入正轨；进展顺利

**Usages**
- get back on track 回归正轨
- let the world get back on track 让世界重回正轨
- keep the discussion on track 让讨论回归正题

**Examples**
1. We must let the world get back on track. 我们必须让世界重回正轨。
2. Try to keep the discussion on track. 尽量让讨论不偏离正题。
3. The project is back on track after the delay. 延误之后，项目重新步入正轨。
