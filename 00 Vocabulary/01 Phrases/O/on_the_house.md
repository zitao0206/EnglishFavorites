### on the house

**Desp**

phr. (of drinks or food in a bar or restaurant) provided free by the establishment.
（酒水、食物）店家免费赠送、请客，免单

**Usages**

- drinks are on the house 酒水免单
- dessert on the house 甜点店家赠送
- it's on the house 这算店家请的

**Examples**

1. The wine is on the house. 葡萄酒是店家免费赠送的。
2. The manager said our coffee was on the house. 经理说我们的咖啡免单。
3. They gave us a round of drinks on the house. 他们免费请了我们一轮酒。
