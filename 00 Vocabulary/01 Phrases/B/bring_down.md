### bring down

**Desp**
phr. to reduce something; to make someone or something fall.
使…降低；打倒

**Usages**
- bring down prices 降低价格
- bring down the government 推翻政府

**Examples**
1. The sale brings down the price of the phone. 促销降低了手机价格。
2. The scandal brought down the minister. 丑闻让部长下台。
3. We need to bring down costs. 我们需要降低成本。
