### be deemed as

**Desp**
phr. to be regarded or considered as something (formal, written).
被视为……（书面正式）

**Usages**
- be deemed as goals 被视作目标
- be deemed as illegal 被视为非法
- be deemed as the best 被公认为最佳

**Examples**
1. They were deemed as goals. 这些被视作目标。
2. Such conduct may be deemed as a breach of contract. 此类行为可被视为违约。
3. The area was deemed as unsafe for tourists. 该地区被视为不适宜游客前往。
