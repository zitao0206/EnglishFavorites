### be preoccupied with

**Desp**

phr. to be completely absorbed in one's thoughts about something.
全神贯注于…，一心想着…


**Usages**

- be preoccupied with work 一心扑在工作上

**Examples**

1. She was too preoccupied with her phone to notice me. 她太专注于手机，没注意到我。
2. He is always preoccupied with the future of the company. 他总是一心想着公司的未来。
3. Don't call him; he's preoccupied with the exam. 别给他打电话，他正全神贯注备考。
