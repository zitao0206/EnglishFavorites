### be used to doing

**Desp**
phr&v. to be accustomed to doing something.
习惯于做某事

**Usages**
- be used to doing sth 习惯于做某事
- get used to sth 逐渐习惯

**Examples**
1. I am used to getting up early. 我已经习惯早起。
2. She is used to working under pressure. 她习惯在压力下工作。
3. You'll soon get used to the new routine. 你很快会习惯新的作息。
