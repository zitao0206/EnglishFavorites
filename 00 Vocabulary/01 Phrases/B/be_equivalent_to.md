### be equivalent to
[ɪˈkwɪvələnt]

**Desp**
phr. to be equal to something in value, amount or meaning.
等同于，相当于

**Usages**
- be equivalent to sth 等同于某物
- equivalent to saying... 相当于说……
- the equivalent of... ……的等价物

**Examples**
1. One mile is equivalent to about 1.6 kilometers. 一英里约等于1.6公里。
2. His silence is equivalent to an admission of guilt. 他的沉默等同于认罪。
3. This certificate is equivalent to a college degree. 这份证书相当于大学学位。
