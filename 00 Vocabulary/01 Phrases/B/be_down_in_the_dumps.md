### be down in the dumps

**Desp**
phr. To feel sad or depressed.
闷闷不乐，情绪低落

**Usages**
- feel down in the dumps 情绪低落
- get out of the dumps 走出低谷

**Examples**
1. He's been down in the dumps since he lost his job. 失业以来他一直闷闷不乐。
2. Don't be down in the dumps — brighter days are ahead. 别垂头丧气，好日子在后头。
