### boarding school

**Desp**

phr&n. a school where students live and study during the term.
寄宿学校

**Usages**

- attend a boarding school 上寄宿学校
- send someone to boarding school 送某人去寄宿学校
- boarding school education 寄宿学校教育

**Examples**

1. She was sent to a boarding school at the age of twelve. 她十二岁就被送去了寄宿学校。
2. He enjoyed his years at boarding school. 他很享受在寄宿学校的那些年。
3. Many British boarding schools have long traditions. 许多英国寄宿学校有着悠久的传统。
