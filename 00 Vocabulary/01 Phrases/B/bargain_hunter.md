### bargain hunter

**Description**

phr. People who look for goods at low prices; shoppers who seek out deals and discounts.
淘便宜货的人，捡漏顾客，薅羊毛的消费者

**Usages**

- attract bargain hunters 吸引捡漏顾客
- a paradise for bargain hunters 捡漏者的天堂
- savvy bargain hunters 精明的薅羊毛达人

**Examples**

1. Black Friday draws bargain hunters to the malls in droves. 黑色星期五把成群的捡漏顾客吸引到商场。
2. Bargain hunters snapped up the discounted electronics within minutes. 薅羊毛的消费者几分钟内就把打折电子产品抢光了。
3. The flea market is a paradise for bargain hunters. 跳蚤市场是捡漏者的天堂。
