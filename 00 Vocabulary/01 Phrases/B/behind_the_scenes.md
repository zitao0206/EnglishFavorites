### behind the scenes
[siːn]

**Desp**

phr. In or into the background; done secretly or out of public view.
在幕后，在暗中

**Usages**

- work behind the scenes 在幕后工作
- behind the scenes of the election 选举的幕后
- a behind-the-scenes look 幕后揭秘

**Examples**

1. A lot of hard work goes on behind the scenes before a product launches. 产品发布前有大量幕后工作要做。
2. He is the real decision-maker, working behind the scenes. 他是真正的决策者，在暗中操盘。
3. The documentary gives a behind-the-scenes look at the film studio. 这部纪录片揭示了电影制片厂的幕后。
