### be destined to do / for sth

**Desp**

phr. to be certain to happen or to be meant to do or have something.
注定要做某事；注定会……

**Usages**

- be destined to do sth 注定要做某事
- be destined for sth 注定要拥有/走向某事物
- be destined for greatness 注定成就非凡

**Examples**

1. He was destined to play these roles. 他注定要扮演这些角色。
2. The project was destined to fail from the start. 这个项目从一开始就注定要失败。
3. She always felt destined for something greater. 她总觉得注定了要做更大的事。
