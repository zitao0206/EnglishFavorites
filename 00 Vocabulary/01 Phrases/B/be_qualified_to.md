### be qualified to

**Desp**
phr. To have the necessary skills, qualifications or credentials for something.
有资格做某事；胜任（担任某职位）

**Usages**
- be qualified to do sth 有资格做某事
- be qualified for the position 胜任这个岗位
- be qualified to practice law 有资格执业法律

**Examples**
1. He is qualified for the position. 他胜任这个岗位。
2. Only certified engineers are qualified to sign the report. 只有持证工程师才有资格签署这份报告。
3. She is qualified to teach at university level. 她有资格在大学任教。
