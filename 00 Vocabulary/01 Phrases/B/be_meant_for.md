### be meant for

**Description**

phr. to be intended or designed for someone or something.
原本是给…的；专为…准备的


**Usages**

- be meant for beginners 专为初学者准备

**Examples**

1. This gift is meant for you. 这份礼物是特意给你的。
2. The course is meant for absolute beginners. 这门课程专为零基础的人准备。
3. The tools are meant for fixing small devices. 这些工具是用来修理小设备的。
