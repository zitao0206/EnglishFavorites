### be subject to

**Desp**

phr. to suffer or experience something; to be controlled or affected by something; to depend on a condition.
遭受，经受；受…约束；以…为条件


**Usages**

- be subject to approval 须经批准

**Examples**

1. This data is subject to proprietary protection. 这份数据受专利保护。
2. All flights are subject to weather conditions. 所有航班都要视天气情况而定。
3. The contract is subject to the board's approval. 合同须经董事会批准。
