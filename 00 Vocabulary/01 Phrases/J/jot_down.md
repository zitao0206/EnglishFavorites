### jot down
[dʒɒt daʊn]

**Description**
phr. to write something down quickly.
随手记下，草草记录

**Usages**
- jot down notes 随手记笔记

**Examples**
1. Let me jot down your phone number. 让我记下你的手机号。
2. She jotted down the key points during the meeting. 她在会议中快速记下要点。
3. Jot down any ideas that come to you. 把想到的点子随手记下来。
