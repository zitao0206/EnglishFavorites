### right as rain

**Desp**
phr. Perfectly healthy or fine.
（身体）痊愈，恢复健康；完全正常

**Usages**
- be right as rain 完全康复
- feel right as rain 感觉好极了

**Examples**
1. Don't worry, she's right as rain now. 别担心，她已经完全康复了。
2. A good night's sleep made me right as rain. 睡个好觉让我完全恢复了。
