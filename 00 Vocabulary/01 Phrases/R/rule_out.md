### rule out

**Description**
phr&v. to exclude something as a possibility; to prevent something from happening.
排除，不考虑；阻止

**Usages**
- rule out foul play 排除蓄意加害
- rule out a possibility 排除一种可能
- rule out doing sth 排除做某事

**Examples**
1. The police ruled out foul play. 警方排除了蓄意加害。
2. Doctors ruled out any serious illness. 医生排除了重病的可能。
3. The heavy rain ruled out the outdoor ceremony. 大雨使户外典礼无法举行。
