### read the room

**Desp**

phr. To understand and adapt to the mood, attitudes, and unspoken cues of the people present.
读懂现场，察言观色（感知环境中人的情绪、态度、潜台词等；look at emotions）

**Usages**

- read the room before speaking 开口前先察言观色
- fail to read the room 没读懂气氛
- learn to read the room 学会察言观色

**Examples**

1. He completely failed to read the room and told that joke at a funeral. 他完全没察言观色，在葬礼上讲了那个笑话。
2. A good speaker knows how to read the room and adjust the tone. 优秀的演讲者懂得读懂现场、调整语气。
3. She read the room and decided not to bring up the sensitive topic. 她察觉气氛不对，决定不提那个敏感话题。
