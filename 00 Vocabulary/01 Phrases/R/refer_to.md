### refer to

**Desp**
phr&v. ① To mean or concern something ② To look at something for information.
① 指的是，涉及，意思是（=mean）② 查询，参考

**Usages**
- What do you refer to? 你指的是什么？
- refer to the staff at front-desk 咨询前台工作人员
- referring to = 说到，关于

**Examples**
1. If confused, refer to the staff at front-desk. 如果有疑问，请咨询前台工作人员。
2. Referring to your last question, I'll explain briefly. 关于你上一个问题，我简单说明一下。
3. The figures refer to last year's sales. 这些数字指的是去年的销售额。
