### rip out

**Desp**
phr. to remove something by pulling.
拔除，拆除

**Usages**
- rip out the old wiring 拆除旧电线

**Examples**
1. They ripped out the old carpet. 他们拆掉了旧地毯。
2. He ripped out the page and started over. 他撕下那页重新开始。
3. The builders ripped out the entire kitchen. 施工队把整个厨房拆了。
