### go Dutch

**Desp**
phr. To share the cost of a meal or outing equally among the participants; each person pays for their own share.
AA制，各付各的

**Usages**
- Let's go Dutch. 我们AA制吧。
- go Dutch on a date 约会时AA制
- go Dutch on dinner 晚餐各付各的

**Examples**
1. Let's go Dutch — I'll pay for mine and you pay for yours. 我们AA制吧——我付我的，你付你的。
2. In many Western countries, it's common to go Dutch on a first date. 在许多西方国家，第一次约会AA制很常见。
3. She insisted on going Dutch even though he offered to pay. 尽管他主动买单，她还是坚持AA制。
