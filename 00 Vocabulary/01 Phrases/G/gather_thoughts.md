### gather thoughts

**Description**
phr&v. to collect your thoughts; to think before acting.
集中思绪，整理思路

**Usages**
- gather one's thoughts 理清思路
- give sb a minute to gather thoughts 给某人一点时间思考

**Examples**
1. Just give me a minute to gather my thoughts. 给我一分钟捋捋思路。
2. She paused to gather her thoughts before answering. 她停顿了一下整理思路再回答。
