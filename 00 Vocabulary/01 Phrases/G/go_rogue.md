### go rogue 
[roʊɡ]

**Desp**

phr. to start behaving in an uncontrolled, rebellious or unpredictable way.
搞反叛的，失控的，桀骜不驯


**Usages**

- go-out-of-control 失控

**Examples**

1. The agent went rogue and refused to follow orders. 那名特工失控叛变，拒绝服从命令。
2. The software went rogue and started deleting files. 软件失控了，开始删除文件。
3. A few players went rogue during the training camp. 几名球员在训练营里变得桀骜不驯。
