### get a grasp of

**Desp**
phr&v. to come to understand something fully; to master.
掌握，领会，理解

**Extension**
同义：come to grips with, figure out
相关：have a good grasp of（很好地掌握）、grasp（v/n. 抓住；理解）

**Usages**
- get a grasp of the basics 掌握基础
- get a good grasp of English 精通英语
- get a firm grasp of the situation 充分了解局势

**Examples**
1. It took me a week to get a grasp of the new software. 我花了一周时间才掌握这个新软件。
2. Students need to get a solid grasp of grammar before writing. 学生在写作前需要扎实掌握语法。
3. Read the report carefully to get a grasp of the situation. 仔细阅读报告以了解局势。
