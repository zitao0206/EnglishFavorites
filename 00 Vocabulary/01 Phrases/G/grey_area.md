### grey area

**Desp**

phr. a situation that is not clearly defined or covered by rules.
灰色地带，模糊不清的范畴


**Usages**

- legal grey area 法律灰色地带

**Examples**

1. The rules don't cover this case, it's a grey area. 规则并未覆盖该案例，这属于灰色地带。
2. Where to draw the line between advice and pressure is a grey area. 建议与施压之间的界限是个灰色地带。
3. The new technology falls into a regulatory grey area. 这项新技术处于监管的灰色地带。
