### fling oneself at sb

**Desp**
phr&v. To throw oneself at someone romantically; to make bold advances toward someone.
主动投怀送抱，示好献殷勤，大胆示爱

**Usages**
- fling oneself at sb 主动投怀送抱
- stop thinking she will fling herself at you 别以为她会主动示好
- fling oneself at every opportunity 见机会就上

**Examples**
1. Stop thinking she will fling herself at you. 别以为她就会主动投怀送抱。
2. He flung himself at her the moment they met. 他们一见面他就大胆示爱。
3. She has no intention of flinging herself at anyone. 她无意对任何人献殷勤。
