### fit in

**Desp**
phr&v. To be accepted by a group; to feel that you belong.
融入（群体），合得来；安排进

**Usages**
- fit in with sb 与某人合得来
- fit in well 融入得很好

**Examples**
1. It took me a while to fit in at the new school. 我花了一段时间才融入新学校。
2. He didn't fit in with the other kids. 他和别的孩子合不来。
