### knock over

**Desp**
phr&v. to hit something so that it falls; to knock a person down.
打倒，撞倒

**Usages**
- knock over a cup 碰倒杯子
- knock sb over 撞倒某人

**Examples**
1. You accidentally knocked over her drink. 你不小心打翻了她的饮料。
2. The dog knocked over the vase. 狗把花瓶撞倒了。
