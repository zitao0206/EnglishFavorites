### the tip of the iceberg
[ˈaɪsbɜːrɡ]

**Desp**
phr. the small, visible part of a much larger problem or situation.
冰山一角（只显露问题/真相的一小部分，背后还有更多）

**Extension**
反义词：the whole picture 全貌

**Usages**
- just the tip of the iceberg 只是冰山一角
- the whole picture 全貌

**Examples**
1. This official who was arrested is just the tip of the iceberg. 被捕的这名官员仅仅只是冰山一角。（背后还有更多涉案人员）
2. The reported losses are only the tip of the iceberg. 报道出来的损失只是冰山一角。
3. We need to see the whole picture before making a decision. 做决定前我们需要看到全貌。
