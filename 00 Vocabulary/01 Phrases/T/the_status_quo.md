### the status quo

**Desp**

phr. The existing state of affairs; the way things currently are.
现状，目前的状况（源自拉丁语 status quo = "原有的状态"）

**Usages**

- maintain the status quo 维持现状
- keep the status quo 保持现状
- challenge the status quo 挑战现状

**Examples**

1. The company prefers to maintain the status quo rather than take risks. 公司宁愿维持现状，也不愿冒险。
2. Many young voters want to change the status quo of politics. 许多年轻选民想改变政治现状。
3. Keeping the status quo may feel safe, but it blocks progress. 保持现状或许安全，却阻碍进步。
