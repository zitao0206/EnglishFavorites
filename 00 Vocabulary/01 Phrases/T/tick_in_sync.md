### tick in sync
[sɪŋk]

**Desp**
phr. to operate or think in perfect rhythm or agreement (out of sync = not synchronized).
步调一致，节奏合拍（out of sync 不同步）

**Usages**
- Our minds tick in sync. 我们不谋而合。
- ticking in sync with each other 彼此节奏合拍
- out of sync 不同步

**Examples**
1. Our minds tick in sync. 我们不谋而合。
2. The two drums ticked in sync throughout the performance. 整场演出中两面鼓节奏完全合拍。
3. Their schedules were out of sync, so they rarely met. 他们的作息不同步，因此很少见面。
