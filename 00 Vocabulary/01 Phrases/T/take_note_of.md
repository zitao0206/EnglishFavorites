### take note of

**Desp**
phr&v. to pay attention to and remember something; to notice.
注意到，留意，把……记在心里

**Extension**
同义：pay attention to, take notice of
相关：take notes（做笔记）

**Usages**
- take note of the details 注意细节
- take note of the warning 留意警告
- take careful note of... 仔细留意……

**Examples**
1. Please take note of the changes in the schedule. 请注意日程安排的变动。
2. Investors took note of the company's rapid growth. 投资者注意到了公司的快速成长。
3. Take note of what I say—it may save your life. 记住我的话——这可能会救你的命。
