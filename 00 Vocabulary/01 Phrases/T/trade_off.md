### trade off

**Description**
phr&v. to balance two things against each other; n. a compromise between two things you cannot have both of.
权衡，取舍

**Extension**
连字符形式：trade-off（名词用法：权衡，取舍）

**Usages**
- trade off A against B 在A与B之间权衡取舍
- a trade off between A and B 在A与B之间权衡取舍

**Examples**
1. We traded off cost against performance. 我们在成本和性能之间做了权衡。
2. There's always a trade-off between speed and quality. 速度和品质之间总有取舍。
3. Accepting a lower salary is the trade-off for more freedom. 接受较低薪水是换取更多自由的代价。
