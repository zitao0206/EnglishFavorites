### to some extent

**Desp**
phr. partly; to a certain degree.
在某种程度上

**Usages**
- to a large extent 在很大程度上

**Examples**
1. To some extent, I agree with you. 在某种程度上我同意你的看法。
2. Her success is due to luck to some extent. 她的成功在某种程度上归功于运气。
3. The weather affects our mood to some extent. 天气在某种程度上影响我们的情绪。
