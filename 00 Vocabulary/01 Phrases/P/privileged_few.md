### privileged few
[ˈprɪvəlɪdʒd fjuː]

**Desp**

phr. a small group of people who have special advantages, wealth, or power.
少数特权阶层

**Usages**

- benefit only the privileged few 只让少数特权阶层受益
- a world ruled by the privileged few 由少数特权阶层统治的世界
- open to the privileged few 只向少数特权者开放

**Examples**

1. Tax cuts should not benefit only the privileged few. 减税不应只让少数特权阶层受益。
2. For centuries, education was reserved for the privileged few. 几个世纪以来，教育只属于少数特权阶层。
3. The club's membership is limited to the privileged few. 这家俱乐部的会员仅限少数特权人士。
