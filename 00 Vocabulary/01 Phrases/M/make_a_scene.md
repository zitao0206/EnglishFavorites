### make a scene

**Desp**
phr&v. To behave noisily and emotionally in public.
当众大吵大闹，闹事

**Usages**
- make a scene in public 当众闹事
- don't make a scene 别闹

**Examples**
1. Don't make a scene in the restaurant. 不要在餐厅当众闹事。
2. She made a scene when they refused to refund her. 他们拒绝退款时她大闹了一场。
3. Please don't make a scene—we can talk calmly. 请别当众闹事，我们可以好好谈。
