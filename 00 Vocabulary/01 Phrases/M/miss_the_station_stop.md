### miss the station / stop

**Desp**
phr&v. To stay on a vehicle past the place where you should get off.
坐过站

**Usages**
- miss one's stop 坐过站
- miss the station 坐过站

**Examples**
1. I missed my stop because I fell asleep. 我睡着了，结果坐过了站。
2. He was so busy on his phone that he missed the station. 他忙着看手机，坐过了站。
