### more of

**Desp**

phr. to a greater degree; used to say something is closer to one thing than another.
更大程度上的，更偏向于，更多是


**Usages**

- more of a problem 更像是一个问题

**Examples**

1. It's more of a suggestion than a rule. 这更像是建议而非规定。
2. He's more of a talker than a doer. 他更偏于是个空谈者而非实干者。
3. The issue is more of a timing problem. 这个问题更多是时机问题。
