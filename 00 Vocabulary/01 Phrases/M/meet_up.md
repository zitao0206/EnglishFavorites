### meet up

**Desp**
phr&v. to meet someone, usually informally or by arrangement.
碰面，相聚，会面（通常非正式）

**Extension**
相关：meet up with someone（与某人碰面）
名词：meetup（聚会，面基）

**Usages**
- meet up with friends 与朋友碰面
- meet up after work 下班后聚一聚
- meet up for coffee 约着喝咖啡

**Examples**
1. Let's meet up for lunch this weekend. 这周末我们约着吃午饭吧。
2. I met up with some old classmates in Shanghai. 我在上海碰到了几位老同学。
3. We meet up every Friday night for board games. 我们每周五晚上聚在一起玩桌游。
