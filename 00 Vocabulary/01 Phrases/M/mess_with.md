### mess with

**Desp**
phr&v. To interfere with or cause trouble for someone or something.
招惹，找麻烦；扰乱某人

**Usages**
- mess with sb 招惹某人
- mess-up 搞砸，弄乱
- Don't mess with success. 别折腾已经成功的局面。

**Examples**
1. Don't mess with success. 别折腾已经成功的局面。
2. You don't want to mess with that guy—he's dangerous. 你不会想招惹那家伙的，他很危险。
3. Someone messed with my files and now everything is a mess-up. 有人乱动了我的文件，现在全搞砸了。
