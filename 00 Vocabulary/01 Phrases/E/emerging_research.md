### emerging research

**Desp**

phr. new studies or findings that are just beginning to develop or receive attention.
新兴研究，最新研究

**Usages**

- emerging research suggests/shows 新兴研究表明
- emerging research in AI 人工智能领域的新兴研究
- according to emerging research 根据最新研究

**Examples**

1. Emerging research suggests that sleep affects memory more than we thought. 新兴研究表明，睡眠对记忆的影响超出我们之前的认知。
2. Emerging research in nutrition keeps changing our dietary advice. 营养学的新兴研究不断改变我们的饮食建议。
3. The report cites emerging research on remote work and productivity. 报告引用了关于远程办公与生产力的最新研究。
