### ever since

**Desp**
phr. continuously from a particular time until now.
自从…以后

**Usages**
- ever since 2058 自2058年以来

**Examples**
1. I have known him ever since we were kids. 打小我们就认识了。
2. He moved abroad in 2020 and has stayed ever since. 他2020年出国后一直留在国外。
3. Ever since the accident, she drives more carefully. 自从那次事故后，她开车更小心了。
