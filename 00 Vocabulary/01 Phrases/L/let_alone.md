### let alone

**Desp**
phr. not to mention; even less.
更不用说，何况

**Usages**
- let alone + 名词/动词原形 更不用说…

**Examples**
1. He can't afford a bike, let alone a car. 他连自行车都买不起，更别说汽车了。
2. I can't speak English, let alone French. 我连英语都不会说，更别提法语了。
3. She doesn't even have time for lunch, let alone a vacation. 她连吃午饭的时间都没有，更别说度假了。
