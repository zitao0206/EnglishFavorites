### yearn for
[jɜːrn]

**Desp**
phr&v. to feel a strong desire or longing for something.
渴望，向往

**Usages**
- yearn for freedom 渴望自由
- yearn for true friendship 渴望真正的友谊
- yearn for the past 怀念过去

**Examples**
1. He feels disconnected from others and yearns for true friendship. 他感觉与别人格格不入，渴望真正的友谊。
2. The prisoners yearned for freedom. 囚犯们渴望自由。
3. She yearned for the quiet life of the countryside. 她向往乡村的宁静生活。
