### apple slices

**Desp**
phr. Apples cut into thin flat pieces.
苹果片

**Usages**
- cut apples into slices 把苹果切成片
- a plate of apple slices 一盘苹果片
- dip apple slices in honey 苹果片蘸蜂蜜

**Examples**
1. She packed some apple slices for the kids' lunch. 她给孩子们的午餐准备了些苹果片。
2. Cut the apple into slices before serving. 上桌前把苹果切成片。
3. Fresh apple slices with peanut butter make a great snack. 新鲜苹果片配花生酱是很棒的零食。
