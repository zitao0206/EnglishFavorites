### align with

**Desp**
phr&v. to be in agreement or alliance with; to adjust so as to match.
与……保持一致，与……结盟；使对齐

**Extension**
同义：be consistent with, conform to
动词：align（使一致，对齐）

**Usages**
- align with the company's goals 与公司目标保持一致
- align with market trends 顺应市场趋势
- closely align with... 与……紧密一致

**Examples**
1. Our strategy must align with the company's long-term goals. 我们的策略必须与公司的长期目标保持一致。
2. The new policy aligns with international standards. 新政策与国际标准接轨。
3. Make sure your actions align with your words. 确保你的行动与言语一致。
