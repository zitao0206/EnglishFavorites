### at stake

**Description**
phr. at risk; in a situation where something may be lost or gained.
濒临险境，处于成败关头，利害攸关

**Extension**
同义：at risk, in jeopardy, on the line

**Usages**
- lives are at stake 生命危在旦夕
- the company's future is at stake 公司前途未卜
- put... at stake 使……处于危险中

**Examples**
1. Thousands of jobs are at stake if the factory closes. 如果工厂关闭，数千个工作岗位将不保。
2. The team's reputation is at stake in this match. 这场比赛关系到球队的声誉。
3. We can't afford mistakes—lives are at stake. 我们容不得失误——这是人命关天的事。
