### as much as possible

**Desp**
phr. to the greatest extent or amount that can be achieved.
尽可能多地，尽量

**Extension**
相关：as soon as possible（尽快）、as far as possible（尽可能远）

**Usages**
- save as much as possible 尽可能多存钱
- practice as much as possible 尽量多练习
- avoid... as much as possible 尽量避免……

**Examples**
1. Try to read English as much as possible. 尽量多读英文。
2. We should reduce waste as much as possible. 我们应该尽可能地减少浪费。
3. She rested as much as possible before the race. 赛前她尽可能多地休息。
