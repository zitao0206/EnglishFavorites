### have feelings for sb

**Desp**
phr. to feel romantic attraction toward someone.
对某人产生好感

**Usages**
- have feelings for sb 对某人有好感
- develop feelings for sb 对某人渐生情愫
- hide one's feelings for sb 掩藏对某人的感情

**Examples**
1. I think he has feelings for you. 我觉得他对你有好感。
2. She slowly developed feelings for her colleague. 她慢慢对同事产生了感情。
3. He tried to hide his feelings for her, but failed. 他试图掩藏对她的好感，但没能做到。
