### help sb walk free from

**Desp**
phr&v. To help someone escape punishment or be released from blame.
帮某人脱罪/逃脱

**Usages**
- help sb walk free from charges 帮某人摆脱指控
- help sb walk free from prison 帮某人脱离牢狱
- walk free from punishment 逍遥法外

**Examples**
1. The clever lawyer helped him walk free from all charges. 能干的律师帮他洗脱了全部指控。
2. A technicality allowed the suspect to walk free from trial. 一个程序漏洞让嫌疑人逃脱了审判。
3. They hoped new evidence would help her walk free from prison. 他们希望新证据能让她重获自由。
