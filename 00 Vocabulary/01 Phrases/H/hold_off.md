### hold off

**Description**

phr. to delay doing something; to stop someone from doing something; to keep someone away.
阻止，拖延，暂缓


**Usages**

- hold off on 暂缓做某事
- hold off the enemy 阻挡敌人

**Examples**

1. Let's hold off on the decision until next week. 我们把决定推迟到下星期吧。
2. The rain held off just long enough for the picnic. 雨迟迟未下，正好让野餐顺利结束。
3. They held off the attackers until help arrived. 他们阻挡住了攻击者，直到援军到来。
