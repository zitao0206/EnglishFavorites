### hand over

**Desp**
phr. to give something to someone else.
交付，移交

**Usages**
- hand over power 移交权力
- hand over the documents 移交文件

**Examples**
1. He handed over the keys to the new owner. 他把钥匙交给了新主人。
2. The CEO will hand over the company to his son. 首席执行官将把公司交给儿子。
3. Please hand over your passport at the counter. 请在柜台交出护照。
