### high heels

**Desp**

phr. shoes with high heels worn especially by women.
高跟鞋


**Usages**

- a pair of high heels 一双高跟鞋

**Examples**

1. She wore high heels to the party. 她穿着高跟鞋去参加聚会。
2. Walking in high heels all day is tiring. 一整天穿高跟鞋走路很累。
3. He bought her a pair of high heels as a gift. 他给她买了一双高跟鞋作为礼物。
