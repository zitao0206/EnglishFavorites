### have sth planned out

**Desp**
phr. To have arranged or organized something in detail beforehand.
把某事全盘规划，周密谋划

**Usages**
- have everything planned out 一切都规划好了
- have the trip planned out 把行程安排妥当
- have a plan planned out for emergencies 备有应急计划

**Examples**
1. Don't worry—I have everything planned out. 别担心，一切都已规划妥当。
2. They had the escape route planned out in advance. 他们事先把逃跑路线周密谋划好了。
3. She had her whole career planned out by age twenty. 她二十岁时就把整个职业生涯规划好了。
