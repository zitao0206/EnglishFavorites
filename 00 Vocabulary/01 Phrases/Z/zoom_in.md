### zoom in

**Desp**
phr&v. to make an image appear larger, or to focus closely on something.
（镜头）拉近，放大；聚焦于

**Usages**
- zoom in on the details 放大看细节
- zoom in on sb/sth 聚焦于某人/某物
- zoom in for a closer look 拉近镜头细看

**Examples**
1. The camera zoomed in on her face. 镜头拉近对准了她的脸。
2. Let's zoom in on this part of the graph. 我们放大看图表的这一部分。
3. The documentary zooms in on the lives of street artists. 这部纪录片聚焦街头艺术家的生活。
