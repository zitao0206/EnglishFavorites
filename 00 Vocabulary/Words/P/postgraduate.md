### postgraduate 
[ˌpoʊstˈɡrædʒuət]

📌 ReviewCount: 1

**Desp**
n. A student who is studying for an advanced degree (such as a master's or doctorate) after completing a bachelor's degree.
研究生；adj. 研究生的

**Usages**
- postgraduate student 研究生
- postgraduate studies 研究生学习/深造
- a postgraduate degree 研究生学位

**Examples**
1. She is a postgraduate at Oxford University. 她是牛津大学的研究生。
2. He decided to continue his postgraduate studies in economics. 他决定继续攻读经济学研究生课程。
3. A postgraduate degree can improve your career prospects. 研究生学位可以改善你的职业前景。
