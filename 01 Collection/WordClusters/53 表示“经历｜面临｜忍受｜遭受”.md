### 表示 “经历｜面临｜忍受｜遭受”

**Description**

- experience [ɪkˈspɪəriəns] v. 体验，经历，体会 -> experienced adj. 有经验的
- face [feɪs] v. 面对，面向，正对，面临
- bear [beə(r)] v. 承受，忍受（bore, borne）
- stand [stænd] v. 容忍，忍受，经受，承受（stood）
- suffer [ˈsʌfə(r)] v. 受折磨，遭受，蒙受 -> suffering n. 苦难
- endure [ɪnˈdjʊə(r)] v. 忍耐，忍受 -> endurance n. 耐力
- encounter [ɪnˈkaʊntə(r)] v. 遭遇，遇到
- go through phr. 经历，经受
- get through phr. 熬过
- confront [kənˈfrʌnt] v. 直面，面对，对抗 -> confrontation n. 对抗

🔍 区别

- **experience**：中性词，**亲历、体验**（好事坏事皆可）
- **face**：**摆在眼前的困难**，必须面对；搭配 **be faced with**
- **bear**：**承受重量、痛苦**（can't bear the noise）
- **stand**：口语**容忍忍受**，多用于否定、疑问（can't stand）
- **suffer**：**遭受痛苦、损失**；搭配 **suffer from**（受……折磨、患病）
- **endure**：**长期顽强忍耐**（endure hardships）
- **encounter**：**意外遭遇**（困难、敌人）——与 14 号"碰到｜遇到｜偶遇"集群交集
- **go through**：**熬过一段（艰苦）经历**（go through a divorce）
- **get through**：**挺过难关**（get through a tough week）
- **confront**：**主动迎面直视**问题、对手（confront the problem）

💡 语感区分小结

- experience：经历过
- face：躲不开、得面对
- bear：扛得住/扛不住
- stand：忍不了（can't stand）
- suffer：吃尽苦头
- endure：咬牙硬扛
- encounter：狭路相逢
- go through：一路坎坷走过
- get through：挺过来了
- confront：迎难而上

**Usages**

- experience failure/success 经历失败/成功
- face a challenge/dilemma 面临挑战/困境
- be faced with difficulties 面对困难
- can't bear the pain/noise 无法忍受疼痛/噪音
- can't stand the heat 忍受不了酷热
- suffer losses/damages 遭受损失/损害
- suffer from headaches 患头痛（受折磨）
- endure hardships/endless waiting 忍受艰难/无尽等待
- encounter difficulties/opposition 遭遇困难/反对
- go through a hard time/a divorce 经历艰难时期/离婚
- get through the crisis/winter 熬过危机/寒冬
- confront the reality/one's fears 直面现实/恐惧

**Examples**

1. The country experienced rapid economic growth in the 1990s.
	该国在 20 世纪 90 年代经历了经济快速增长。
2. She is facing the biggest challenge of her career.
	她正面临职业生涯中最大的挑战。
3. I can't bear to see animals suffer.
	我不忍心看动物受苦。
4. He can't stand being interrupted while working.
	他无法忍受工作时被打断。
5. The company suffered huge losses last year.
	公司去年蒙受了巨额亏损。
6. They endured three years of hardship in the desert.
	他们在沙漠中熬过了三年的艰苦岁月。
7. We encountered several technical problems during testing.
	测试期间我们遭遇了几个技术问题。
8. She has gone through a lot since losing her job.
	失业以来她经历了许多磨难。
9. It was a difficult month, but we got through it somehow.
	那个月很艰难，但我们总算熬过来了。
10. You need to confront the problem instead of avoiding it.
	你需要直面这个问题，而不是逃避。
