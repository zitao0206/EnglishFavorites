
### 表示“投降｜屈服｜认输”

- surrender：投降，屈服
- raise the white flag：举白旗；认输投降（源自军事）
- cry uncle：认输、求饶（美式非正式俚语）
- surrender to despair：向绝望屈服
- bring sb to one's knees / come to their knees/fall to one's knees：迫使某人屈服 / 屈服、屈膝/滑跪，投降
- submit to sth：屈服于；向……低头
- cave [keɪv] in：让步、屈服；塌陷（迫于外界压力妥协）
- succumb to 屈服
- capitulate to 屈服，认输

**Examples** 
1. The champion finally made his opponent cry uncle in the third round. 
2. Don’t surrender to despair. 
3. Economic pressure brought the country to its knees. 
4. He refused to submit to unfair demands.
5. He fell to his knees after hearing the terrible news.
6. The wrestler refused to cry uncle, even when he was pinned to the ground.
7. We kept teasing him until he cried uncle.
8. After months‑long fighting, the group finally raised the white flag.
9. Rather than raise the white flag in the negotiation, they decided to walk away.
10. My parents refused to cave in to my demands for a new phone
