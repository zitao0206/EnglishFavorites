### 表示 “渴望｜想要”

**Description**

- look forward to phr. 期待，盼望（后接 n./doing）
- fancy /ˈfænsi/ v./n. 想要，设想，想做 -> fanciful adj. 想象的
- would like phr. 想要（礼貌表达）
- intend /ɪnˈtend/ v. 打算，计划，想要 -> intention n. 意图
- be eager for phr. 渴望，想得到 -> eagerness n. 渴望
- thirst for phr. 渴望 -> thirsty adj. 渴望的；口渴的
- aspire to phr. 渴望，希求 -> aspiration n. 抱负

🔍 区别

- **look forward to**：**欣然期待**好事即将发生，后接名词或 doing
- **fancy**：口语，**一时兴起想要**（Fancy a cup of tea?）
- **would like**：**礼貌表达想要**（请求、点餐）
- **intend**：**有计划、打定主意**；搭配 **intend to do sth**
- **be eager for**：**急切渴望得到**，热切积极
- **thirst for**：**如饥似渴**（知识、权力、成功）
- **aspire to**：**心怀志向抱负**，追求崇高目标

💡 语感区分小结

- look forward to：翘首以盼
- fancy：心血来潮想要
- would like：客气地要
- intend：打定主意
- be eager for：跃跃欲试
- thirst for：如饥似渴
- aspire to：志存高远

**Usages**

- look forward to seeing you 期待见到你
- I fancy a coffee 我想喝杯咖啡
- fancy doing sth 想做某事
- Would you like some tea? 您要来点茶吗？
- intend to do sth 打算做某事
- be eager for success/knowledge 渴望成功/知识
- thirst for knowledge/power 渴求知识/权力
- aspire to greatness/leadership 追求卓越/领导地位

**Examples**

1. I'm looking forward to the summer holiday.
	我盼着暑假的到来。
2. Do you fancy going out for dinner tonight?
	今晚你想出去吃晚饭吗？
3. I would like to book a table for two.
	我想预订一张双人桌。
4. We intend to expand our business into Southeast Asia.
	我们打算把业务扩展到东南亚。
5. The students were eager for the results of the contest.
	学生们急切盼望比赛结果。
6. Young graduates thirst for practical experience.
	年轻毕业生渴望获得实践经验。
7. She aspires to become a professional pianist.
	她渴望成为职业钢琴家。
