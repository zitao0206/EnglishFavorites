**大学/研究生**

1. 美式最常用（雅思、留学通用）
大一：freshman
大二：sophomore
大三：junior
大四：senior
注意：freshman /‑sophomore /‑junior /‑senior 只适用于本科 4 年制

2. 研究生（美式）
研一：first‑year graduate student
研二：second‑year graduate student

英式英语（英国、澳洲、雅思写作推荐，更稳妥）
本科：
‑ undergraduate 大一/大二/大三/大四
研究生：
‑ postgraduate 研一/研二

**Examples** 
1. He is a freshman at Peking University. 
	他是北京大学一名大一新生。 
2. She is a second‑year postgraduate majoring in computer science. 
	她是计算机专业的一名研二学生。
3. I was a freshman when I first joined the debate club.
    我大一时第一次加入辩论社团。
4. As a sophomore, she began to look for internship opportunities.
    读大二的时候，她开始寻找实习机会。
5. Most students start to prepare for postgraduate exams in their junior year.
    大多数学生大三开始准备考研。
6. He finished his final‑year project as a senior.
    他大四时完成了毕业设计。
7. She is a first‑year graduate student majoring in artificial intelligence.
    她是一名人工智能方向的研一学生。
8. This second‑year postgraduate has published two research papers.
    这位研二学生已经发表了两篇研究论文。
9. He is a Year‑2 undergraduate studying finance.
    他是一名金融学专业的大二本科生。
10. As a Year‑1 postgraduate, she is still choosing her research topic.
    身为研一学生，她还在确定自己的研究课题。
