
### 表示 “超过｜超越｜胜过｜赶超”

- outperform：（绩效、表现）优于，胜过
- excel：擅长；表现出众（本身能力拔尖）
- overtake：追上并反超（排名、数量、速度，从落后位置赶超）
- surpass：超过（水平、目标、预期，通用高级词）
- outdo：胜过；做得比… 更好
- outshine：光彩胜过，比… 耀眼
- beat：打败，胜过（口语高频）
- exceed：超过（限额、数值、上限）
- transcend：超越（抽象边界、局限、世俗）
- go beyond：超出（范围、限度）

**Examples**

1. Our new product outperformed all its rivals in market tests.
2. She excels in logical‑thinking tasks.
3. China’s solar‑panel output overtook Europe’s several years ago.
4. Sales this month surpassed our original expectations.
5. He tried hard to outdo his competitor in the final round.
6. Her wonderful speech outshone every other presentation.
7. Our team beat the champion in yesterday’s match.
8. Drivers must not exceed the speed limit on this highway.
9. True friendship can transcend cultural differences.
10. The problems we are facing have gone beyond our initial plan.
