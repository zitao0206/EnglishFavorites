### 表示 “确保｜担保｜保证”

**Description**

- assure /əˈʃʊə(r)/ v. 保证，确保，使确信 -> assurance n. 保证
- insure /ɪnˈʃʊə(r)/ v. 投保，给……保险 -> insurance n. 保险
- ensure /ɪnˈʃʊə(r)/ v. 确保，保证，担保
- guarantee /ˌɡærənˈtiː/ v./n. 保证，担保
- make sure phr. 确保，务必，务请
- promise /ˈprɒmɪs/ v./n. 答应，保证 -> promising adj. 有前途的
- commitment /kəˈmɪtmənt/ n. 许诺，保证；投入 -> commit v. 承诺；犯（罪）

🔍 区别

- **assure**：**向某人保证以打消顾虑**；搭配 **assure sb of sth / assure sb that...**
- **insure**：**给……上保险**（商业投保）
- **ensure**：**确保结果发生**（ensure safety/success），后面接事不接人
- **guarantee**：**打包票式的担保**（退款、质量承诺）
- **make sure**：**核实弄妥、务必做到**；搭配 **make sure to do / make sure that...**
- **promise**：**口头答应、承诺**；搭配 **promise to do sth**
- **commitment**：名词，**需兑现的郑重许诺**；投入

💡 语感区分小结

- assure sb：宽人心
- insure：买保险
- ensure：保结果
- guarantee：打包票
- make sure：查仔细、办妥
- promise：许诺
- commitment：一诺千金

**Usages**

- assure sb of one's support 向某人保证给予支持
- rest assured 放心
- insure the house against fire 给房屋保火险
- ensure safety/success 确保安全/成功
- guarantee the quality/results 保证质量/结果
- under guarantee 在保修期内
- make sure (that)... 务必确保……
- make sure to lock the door 记得锁门
- promise to do sth 承诺做某事
- keep one's commitment 履行承诺
- a commitment to quality 对质量的承诺

**Examples**

1. I assure you that the report will be ready by Friday.
	我向您保证报告周五前能准备好。
2. The house is insured against fire and flood.
	这栋房子保了火险和洪水险。
3. Please ensure that all the doors are locked before leaving.
	离开前请务必锁好所有门。
4. The watch is guaranteed for two years.
	这块表保修两年。
5. Make sure you back up your files regularly.
	务必定期备份文件。
6. He promised to call me as soon as he arrived.
	他答应一到就给我打电话。
7. The company has made a strong commitment to environmental protection.
	该公司对环境保护作出了坚定承诺。
